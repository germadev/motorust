# 2026-11-01: The Engine Published

## 1. Header & Context
- **Date:** 2026-11-01.
- **Task:** Motorust Engine as a repository of its own: the first commit of `jgermade/motorust-engine`.
- **Commit:** this repository's first; its history begins here. The engine was split out of Motorust, the game, in steps (its content read from packs, its themes as plugins, its tests on its own content, a Cargo workspace with the engine as a library); that history stays in the game's repository, which also holds what is not open (its models, songs and circuits).

## 2. User Request
> Publish the engine as its own repository, licensed MIT or Apache-2.0, after cleaning the mentions of the game's circuits and works out of its code (comments and test names in generic terms), its folder's files as the first commit, never the game's history.

## 3. Implementation Details
- **What is here:** the `motorust_engine` library (`src/`), its content pack (`assets/`: the demo circuit Bahía GP with no song, the demo roster of the bikes built in code, its identity, the textures, the maps of the Moon, the Earth and the Milky Way, the font and the gamepads' art, credited in `assets/CREDITS.md`), its build script (`build.rs`, embedding the pack in release and web builds), `tools/circuit_layout.py`, and `Cargo.lock`.
- **Nothing of the game's:** its circuits, themes and works are not named in the code, the comments or the tests. The checks a game runs on its own content take it as parameters (`NAME_on(circuits, name)`), and the engine's tests run them on its demo where they mean something there. A circuit renamed keeps its records through its file (`formerly: ["Old name"]`, see `Records::take_former`), not through a name written in the engine.
- **Licence:** dual, MIT (`LICENSE-MIT`) or Apache-2.0 (`LICENSE-APACHE`), as Bevy; `license = "MIT OR Apache-2.0"` in `Cargo.toml`. The content keeps its own licences (`assets/CREDITS.md`).
- **Built on its own:** `.cargo/config.toml` gives it the profiles a game's workspace gives it (its code at `opt-level = 1`, its dependencies at 3), and `.github/workflows/ci.yml` checks format, clippy and tests on every push and pull request.
- **Rules:** `AGENTS.md` (English, records in `RECORD/`, the engine knowing nothing of a game's content, the checks before concluding).

## 4. Verification & Testing
- Built and tested on its own, outside the game's workspace (`cargo test`): 583 passed, 2 ignored.
- In the game's workspace: `cargo test --workspace` (the game's 234 and the engine's 583), `cargo fmt --all --check` and `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings` clean.
