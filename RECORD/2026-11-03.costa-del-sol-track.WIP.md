# 2026-11-03 — Costa del Sol: a ninth track

## User request

The user shared a concept image for a new circuit, "Costa del Sol — Sunset Run".
It has a map and five sectors: 1. Salida / Recta costera, 2. Curvas del acantilado,
3. Túnel de la montaña, 4. Puente costero, 5. Chicane final. The scenery is a
Mediterranean coast with whitewashed towns, palms, cliffs, a lighthouse and
sailboats.

## Implementation

- `assets/circuits/costa_del_sol.ron`: a new circuit, written entirely in RON
  with the engine's existing features (nothing new in the engine). The lap is
  2.63 km and runs clockwise round a bay, so the water is on the right all
  the way. The coast is an `Outline`, with the bay cut in from the south.
  - Start straight north along the west shore (railing, palms, beach parasols,
    the town on the left, an orange "COSTA DEL SOL" gantry).
  - S-bends up the cliffs to about 29 m (5.8 % at the steepest), cut rock on
    the left.
  - A tunnel through the mountain at the head of the bay, `Span(985, 1150)`.
  - Down the east shore to a truss bridge over the bay's mouth,
    `Span(2236, 2410)`. The engine has no plain concrete viaduct, so the
    bridge uses `Truss`.
  - A final chicane (its tightest bend is 29.4 m, over the 28.75 m limit), and
    round onto the straight.
  - Checkpoints at 800 m and 1700 m. Sky `GoldenHour`. Song "Crown of the
    Track", which was already in `assets/music/` but not used: 172.9 s and
    -14.4 LUFS, measured as `music.ron` says.
- `assets/circuits/circuits.ron` and `assets/pack.txt` list the circuit and its song.
- `src/game/checks.rs`: `circuits_have_checkpoints` covers circuit 8.
- `docs/tracks.md`, `README.md` and `docs/gameplay.md` now describe nine tracks.

## Verification

- `motorust-engine/tools/circuit_layout.py`: 2633.9 m, tightest bend 29.4 m
  at 2488 m, steepest grade 5.8 % at 506 m, no crests the bike takes off
  over at top speed.

# 2026-11-03 — Costa del Sol: the grid on a straight, and a jump

## Context

The first `cargo test` with the circuit: 231 passed, 3 failed, all on Costa del Sol.

- `start_faces_down_the_main_straight`: the grid stood behind the line while the
  road was still coming out of the last right-hander.
- `the_grid_gets_away_without_a_touch` (Easy, 1 touch): the same crooked grid.
- `jumps_on_the_track_are_short`: every circuit needs a crest that throws the bike
  into the air flat out, and this one had none.

## Implementation

- The bridge, the chicane and the bay's mouth moved 100 m south. The last
  right-hander now ends about 150 m before the line, so the grid stands on the
  straight: points (0, 110) and (0, 55).
- A crest on the start straight, shaped like Bahía GP's (5.4 m at 120 m, 7.2 m at
  155 m, 4.2 m at 190 m). `circuit_layout.py` reports it at 156 m, 9.2 m up, flown
  from 188 km/h.
- Heights and spans from the east shore on moved by the 100 m added. The bridge is
  now `Span(2336, 2510)`, the chicane 2540–2680 m and the straight from 2700 m. A
  buoy that was under the bridge moved out to sea.
- The lap is 2.83 km. Its tightest bend is 29.6 m at 2587 m; its steepest grade
  is 11.2 %, down off the crest.
