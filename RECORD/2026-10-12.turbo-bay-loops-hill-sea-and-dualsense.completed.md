- **Date:** 2026-10-12
- **Topic:** Rename Green Hill to Loops Hill and take its rings off the road; rename Speed Bay to Turbo Bay and redesign it as a hilly figure of eight on an island with an organic coast, passing over its own tunnel; break up the repeating sea texture; stop the DualSense from accelerating by itself and from popping wheelies with the left stick.

## Implementation Details

### Loops Hill (was Green Hill)

- `src/circuits/green_hill.rs` → `src/circuits/loops_hill.rs`, `GREEN_HILL` → `LOOPS_HILL`, name "Loops Hill" in both languages, start arch "LOOPS HILL". Hedgehog Hill was the first proposal; the user preferred Loops Hill.
- The four lines of rings over the road are gone (`rings: &[]`), and the descriptions no longer mention them. The rings feature stays in the engine (`Scenery::rings`), unused.
- Comments that meant the track now say Loops Hill; those about Sonic's zone keep "Green Hill".

### Turbo Bay (was Speed Bay): the layout

- `src/circuits/speed_bay.rs` → `src/circuits/turbo_bay.rs`, `SPEED_BAY` → `TURBO_BAY`, name "Turbo Bay", start arch "TURBO BAY", described as inspired by Moto Racer. Comments about the original game keep "Speed Bay".
- Designed first with a Python copy of `Track` (spline, 2 m resampling, curvature, grades, clearance between stretches, coast distance), then ported.
- A figure of eight, 2.5 km: the seafront start straight heading north (as before), a crest and a steep banked right-hander down to the water, a 120 m tunnel (505–625 m) east through the ridge at the island's waist, the grand hotel on a headland, a rock pass (CHECKPOINT at 960 m), the east coast road behind a concrete barrier, the forest on the highest ground (44 m; CHECKPOINT at 1600 m), then down the ridge **over the tunnel** (about 18 m above the road inside it), down the bay shore to the red-arch bridge over a river mouth (2170–2255 m), the town and back to the seafront.
- Much hillier: heights from sea level to 44 m (the old track topped out at 24.5 m), steepest grade about 11 %, crests on the seafront and the coast road.
- **Organic coast:** a new `Coast::Outline(&[Vec2])` (`src/world/scenery.rs`): the shore is a smooth closed curve drawn through its own points (`track::closed_curve`), not a distance from the road. The island has a beach off the seafront, a north bay and a south-east bay that pinch its waist, a lighthouse headland, the hotel headland, and a river mouth that tapers up a valley under the bridge, instead of the old rectangular inlet. The land still drops to the sea right beyond the walls where the shore is near, as with `AroundTrack`. Hills raised to 22 m, with rocks at the waist, hills inside both loops and islets offshore.

### The track over its own tunnel (engine)

- `src/track.rs`: `nearest_at`, `road_height_at` and `nearest_index_at` take the height of what is on the track and only consider road within 6 m of it (`LEVEL_REACH`), falling back to the plain nearest point if there is none. A first version added a height penalty to the distance instead, but it biased the nearest point along slopes when a test bike's height did not match the road, so it became a filter. `grade` takes the height; there are new helpers `nearest_among` (a run of segments) and `project`; `nearest_index` is now test-only.
- Bike physics, walls, crashes, rider ragdoll, rejoining the track (`BikeState::parked_at_level`), grass kick-up and the rival AI (`Traffic` now carries `elevation`) use the height-aware lookups, so each bike stays on its own level. Bikes more than 4 m apart vertically never collide (`crash::collide`). The flat-out, AI-lap and jump tests use the height-aware lookups too.
- `src/world/structures.rs`: `Tunnels` knows the tunnels' runs of segments. `Tunnels::beneath` finds where another, higher stretch passes over a tunnel. There the terrain (`src/world/terrain.rs`) stays down at the tunnel's level, up to 7 m short of the edge of its rock (`BURIED`), and the tunnel's rock top (`tunnel_meshes`) carries the road above: just below its asphalt within 13 m of its centre line, then sloping down over 12 m, and never under the ground beside it. Normal tunnels are unchanged: this only applies where a road passes more than the tunnel's height plus 2 m above it.
- On natural rock (`Soil::Rock`), the gentle parts of a tunnel's top are now grass (terrain grass texture and tint) instead of bare rock, so the hill over the tunnel looks like the hills around. Checkerboard tunnels (Loops Hill) are unchanged.
- Tests: the self-crossing test allows a crossing only where the lower stretch is in a tunnel and the upper one is more than 12 m higher; the terrain test skips the road where it rests on a tunnel's rock; new `turbo_bay_is_an_island_with_a_river_under_the_bridge` and `turbo_bay_passes_over_its_own_tunnel` (crossing found, 14 m+ apart, each level found from its height, ground kept down at the tunnel).

### The sea

- `src/world/terrain.rs`: the sea is a 160 × 160 grid (`sea_mesh`) instead of a single plane. Its texture coordinates are turned 0.37 rad and warped by up to 70 m over about 520 m, and the vertices are tinted in broad patches (darker or more turquoise, at 360 m and 1.1 km scales). The texture repeats every 110 m instead of 160 m.
- `src/world/textures.rs`: the broadest swells of the water texture are gentler and there are more small waves, so a tile no longer reads as a blotch.

### DualSense

- **Accelerating by itself:** a gamepad's throttle and brake now ignore the first 15 % of trigger travel (`TRIGGER_REST`, `squeezed()` in `src/bike/mod.rs`) and use the rest as the full range. A trigger that does not quite return to zero (as a DualSense can, with its adaptive resistance on) used to give a little throttle forever. That also switched off the engine braking (`step` only brakes with the engine at exactly zero throttle), so the bike seemed to keep accelerating by itself. Applied in single player and split screen, before the start-of-race arming. Not reproducible without the hardware.
- **Wheelie with the stick:** pulling the left stick back no longer pops a wheelie (it was easy to do while steering). The gamepad wheelie is B, by default (`Controls::default`), and RT with A together, as before. Settings saved with no wheelie button get B when loaded, unless B already does something else (`give_the_wheelie_a_button`). Split screen uses B too.

- Turbo Bay passes every per-track test: drivable curves, slopes under 13 %, terrain hugging the road and the road dry, a rival lap within 90 s and a pack of rivals racing a full lap with at most two falls, a flat-out lap without touching the walls, short jumps that still happen, houses that fit, checkpoints (960 and 1600 m).

Fixed from those renders:
  - the tunnel's rock rising above the road on top;
  - a bare brown slab over the tunnel (now grass);
  - rocks taller than the road on top (the ridge and cuttings were lowered);
  - beach parasols at the water's edge (the beach is wider).
- Rock walls beside the road poke onto the verge just before the tunnel mouth. That comes from how cuttings and the terrain grid already worked and was left as is.
- Not verified on hardware: the DualSense changes (no controller in the container).
