# 2026-10-12: DualSense Gradual Trigger Curve Fix

## 1. Context & Overview
- **Date:** 2026-10-12
- **Topic:** Correction of DualSense adaptive trigger start position and curve to eliminate resistance at the beginning of travel and provide truly gradual feedback towards the end.
- **Language:** English (append-only record).

---

## 2. User Requests

### Request: Zero Initial Resistance & Pure Gradual Profile
> "la resistencia de los gatillos no la percibo gradual, al principio del recorrido, no debería tener ninguna resistencia"

---

## 3. Implementation Details

### 3.1 Trigger Actuator Displacement & Curve Calibration (`src/gamepads/apple.rs`)
- In DualSense hardware, configuring `start_position: 0.10` positioned the physical internal gear stopper arm at 10% of physical stroke length (almost immediately at the top of the pull), causing players to encounter motor drag from the very beginning.
- In addition, dynamically scaling `end_strength` with vehicle velocity sent repeated HID reconfiguration commands while holding the trigger down, creating abrupt steps.
- **Adjustments in `compute_bike_trigger_effects`:**
  - Shifted `start_position` from `0.10` to `0.45`:
    - The first 45% of physical trigger travel is now completely free of motor engagement (0.0 resistance).
  - Configured linear slope ramp from `0.45` to `1.0`:
    - Starts at `start_strength: 0.0`.
    - Smoothly ramps up to a gentle `end_strength: 0.25` at bottom-out.
  - Applied the same clean curve to both left (brake) and right (throttle) triggers.
  - Stable parameters allow the DualSense onboard microcontroller to handle continuous closed-loop position interpolation smoothly without HID reinitialization glitches.
