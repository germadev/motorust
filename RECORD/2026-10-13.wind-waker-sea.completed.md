# A Sea in the Style of The Wind Waker

**Date:** 2026-10-13
**Branch:** `claude/loving-maxwell-vr7uub`

## 1. User Request
"Hablando del mar, no me gusta el actual, qué tal quedaría uno estilo Zelda Wind Waker?" (the current sea is not liked; try one in the style of Zelda: The Wind Waker).

## 2. Implementation Details
- **Material** (`src/world/sea.rs`, `src/world/sea.wgsl`, embedded with `embedded_asset!`): a custom `Material` with no prepass and no shadows. The fragment shader has no lighting and goes through Bevy's `main_pass_post_lighting_processing`, so the distance fog and tone mapping match the rest of the scene.
  - Flat, saturated blues: broad patches between a deep and an open-sea blue, and a lighter turquoise towards the coast.
  - White wave lines: the borders of drifting, wobbling Voronoi cells (domain-warped, points circling slowly), only some cells drawn so the lines come in separate squiggles; anti-aliased with `fwidth`, and faded out between 180 and 420 m so they do not shimmer far away.
  - Foam: a band hugging the shore and two rings rolling out to 13 m every 4.5 s, fading as they go, at least a pixel wide at any distance.
- **Shore map** (`ShoreMap`): for each circuit, a grid over the track plus 700 m (at most 1024 texels along its longest side, texels of at least 1.5 m) marks the texels where the terrain stands above sea level; two chamfer passes give the signed distance to the shore (half a texel either side of the boundary), stored as an `R8Unorm` texture from −10 to 40 m and sampled with linear filtering. It is worked out in `prepare`, on the background threads that already prepare each circuit's terrain, so it costs nothing on the main thread.
- **Mesh**: one flat 16 × 16 grid out to 7 km, shared by every circuit; each circuit only has its own material (its shore map). The old sea (a 160 × 160 grid with per-vertex tints and warped UVs) and its procedural `water` texture, built at every startup, are gone.

## 3. Verification & Testing
- New tests: `the_shore_runs_between_dry_and_wet_texels`, `with_no_land_the_whole_map_is_open_sea`, `every_circuit_has_shore_near_its_road`.
- `cargo test`: all pass. `cargo fmt --check` clean, `cargo clippy --all-targets` no warnings.
- Ran the game under Xvfb with Mesa lavapipe: the welcome flyby over the Turbo Bay seafront shows the blue sea with white wave lines and foam on the coast; the aerial views of the three tracks show the turquoise coastal water and foam rings round the islands.
