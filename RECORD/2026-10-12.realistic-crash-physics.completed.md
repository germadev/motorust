# Realistic Bike-to-Bike Contacts, Falls and Instant Rejoin

- **Date:** 2026-10-12
- **Topic:** Rework of bike-to-bike collisions and crashes on physical grounds, fix of the rival AI, and instant repositioning after a fall.
- **Branch:** `claude/awesome-hamilton-hyvwrg`

## User Request

> "No me gusta como han quedado los choques de las motos, son muy poco realistas. Además, ahora los bots conducen fatal.
> Después de una caída los pilotos no pueden volver volando a la moto, prefiero que se reposicione instantáneamente
> Dale un repaso en general, para ellos infórmate de cómo hacer las físicas realistas
> Qué nada de esto afecte a la conducción, me gusta mucho como se siente el manejo ahora"

Summary: make bike collisions and crashes realistic (researching the physics), fix the rival AI, replace the rider "flying back" to the bike with an instant reposition, and do not change how the bike handles.

## Diagnosis of the Previous Implementation

1. **Bots driving badly (root cause):** on every fixed step (120 Hz) in which two bikes touched, `separate_bikes` shifted the rival's *permanent* lane (`Rival::lane`) and its current line by 1.2 m. A one-second rub moved a rival's lane by up to ~140 m, so rivals kept steering towards the walls or the grass for the rest of the race.
2. **Contacts were teleports, not physics:** `bump` added a fixed 0.55 m sideways displacement, a 0.08 rad heading deflection and a 0.25 rad lean *per step of contact* (66 m/s sideways equivalent), regardless of speeds. Bikes jittered and swerved instead of being pushed.
3. **Crashes were decided by heading angle alone:** any contact with headings more than ~26° apart above 7 m/s brought both riders down. Because `bump` rotated both headings by 0.08 rad per step, a few steps of side contact were enough to "cross" the headings, so ordinary side rubs ended in crashes.
4. **Rider ejection was scripted in bike space:** the rider was moved by a fixed curve relative to the (sliding) bike and then interpolated back onto the seat in the last 20 % of the crash — the "flying back to the bike" the user disliked.

## Physics Research

- **Contact model:** standard impulse-based rigid-body collision response (as used by game physics engines): normal impulse `j = -(1 + e)·v_rel·n / (1/m_a + 1/m_b + (r_a×n)²/I_a + (r_b×n)²/I_b)`, followed by a tangential friction impulse capped by Coulomb's law `|j_t| ≤ μ·j` (only the impulse needed to stop the sliding, never more).
- **Restitution:** vehicle-to-vehicle impacts are largely plastic; a coefficient of restitution of ~0.1–0.3 is typical. Used `e = 0.25`; contact friction between fairings/leathers `μ = 0.35`.
- **Sliding motorcycle drag factors** (SAE accident-reconstruction studies 840250, 950197, 961017, 970963): a motorcycle sliding on its side on asphalt decelerates at roughly 0.3–0.55 g (faired sport bikes at the lower end, because the fairing keeps metal off the road); on grass/dirt the factor is clearly higher and depends on the surface. Used 0.45 g on asphalt and 0.7 g on grass.
- **Rider sliding:** a rider in leathers slides on asphalt at roughly 0.5–0.7 g. Used 0.6 g on asphalt and 0.75 g on grass.
- **Newton's first law for the rider:** nothing holds the rider to the bike, so on impact the bike's velocity changes but the rider keeps (most of) the momentum they had *before* the hit: the rider of a bike that hits something with its front wheel is thrown on past it; a rider struck from the side is carried only partly.
- **When riders fall:** in racing, fairing-to-fairing contact and nudges are common and survivable; falls come from losing the tyres' grip (a sideways jolt bigger than the tyres can absorb → lowside), a spin, or a hit on the front wheel (the front tucks / the rider goes over the bars).

## Implementation Details

### New module `src/bike/crash.rs` (pure, unit-tested)
- **`collide(Body, Body) -> Option<Contact>`:** collision shape is a capsule along each bike (wheel to wheel, 0.45 m radius). Closest points between the two spines give the contact point (the middle of the overlap when bikes run side by side, so a flat side rub gives no spin). Penetration is removed half per bike, then — only if the bikes are approaching — the normal impulse (restitution 0.25) and a capped friction impulse (μ 0.35) are applied with equal masses and a yaw inertia per bike (radius of gyration² 1.0 m² on its wheels, where tyres resist the spin, 0.35 m² when sliding on its side). Momentum is conserved exactly (unit test).
- **`Knock` component:** the change of velocity along the bike goes into `BikeState::speed`; the sideways part and the spin go into a `Knock` that `carry` applies after `advance` and that the tyres scrub off (8 m/s² sideways, spin damped at 10/s). A 2 m/s sideways shove moves the bike ~0.25 m and is gone in a quarter of a second. **When there is no contact the knock is zero and `carry` returns immediately, so `step`/`advance` — the handling — are untouched.**
- **`rider_falls`:** a rider goes down only if the hit gives a sideways Δv > 3 m/s, a spin > 2.5 rad/s, or a deceleration > 4.5 m/s from a hit on the front wheel. Side rubs and nudges from behind are ridden out; T-bones, hard swipes at speed and rear-ending a much slower bike are not; contacts at walking pace never bring anyone down.
- **`fall`:** the bike keeps the velocity it has after the hit, all of it as a knock (no drive), and drops onto its side (on the side it was leaning, or the side it was pushed to if upright). The rider is separated as a `ThrownRider` in world space, starting at the seat (including the bike's lean), with the velocity before the hit plus 40 % of the bike's Δv, a small upward launch, and a tumble proportional to the jolt.
- **`slide` / `ThrownRider::step`:** bike and rider decelerate with their own drag factors on asphalt or grass; the rider flies ballistically, lands (bouncing a little on a hard landing), is laid on their back feet first, tumbles about the body's long axis and settles on back or front; walls stop both (20 % rebound).
- **`CrashState`:** `down`, `elapsed`, `still`, `fall_side`, `rider` and `previous` rider (to interpolate the drawing between fixed steps). The fall is over once bike and rider have been still for 0.5 s (at least 1.5 s after the fall), or after 4 s at most.
- **`rejoin_pose`:** the bike is put back **instantly** — upright and stopped — on the asphalt next to where it ended up (lateral clamped 1 m inside the edge), facing along the track, shifting sideways in 2.5 m steps if another bike is within 2.5 m. No more interpolating the rider back to the seat.

### `src/bike/mod.rs`
- `simulate_bikes`: riding bikes run `advance` (unchanged) and then `crash::carry`; fallen bikes run `crash::slide` and, when the fall is over, rejoin via `rejoin_pose` (previous state reset so interpolation draws no trip; a rival's current line is set to where it rejoined, so it eases back to its lane).
- `separate_bikes`: replaced by `crash::collide` + `crash::fall`. Removed the lane-shifting hack. Haptics: the `CollisionImpact` window and controller rumble are now scaled by the severity of the hit and only sent for a *new* hit (not on every step of two bikes leaning on each other); split-screen players are identified by their `LocalPlayer` index.
- `Knock` and `CrashState` are now part of every spawned bike; `restart_race` clears them.
- Removed the old `bump`, its constants and its tests (replaced by the tests in `crash.rs`).

### `src/bike/ai.rs`
- Rivals that are down release all controls (`CrashState::is_down`). With the lane hack gone, `plan_line` again keeps them in their lane and steers around traffic, fallen bikes included.

### Rider drawing (`src/bike/model/`)
- `update_rider_mount` now runs after `move_chassis` and places the rider's hips where the simulation put them, in world space (interpolated), by undoing the bike → lean → tilt → body transforms (`ChassisRig` now records the lean pivot). The rider faces the bike's heading at the fall, rolled by the tumble and laid back `LIE_ANGLE` (on their back, feet first).
- Procedural rider (`rider.rs`) and imported pilots (`pilot.rs`): when down, hands leave the grips (arms spread) and feet leave the pegs (legs stretched out from the hips), blended over 0.3 s; the torso straightens so it lies flat; head and hang-off ignore the bike's lean. The old "recovery to the seat" blends were removed.

### Camera (`src/camera.rs`)
- While the player is down, the chase camera keeps its yaw (the sliding bike spins) and uses the sliding speed for distance and FOV, so it does not snap. After rejoining it blends back behind the bike as usual.

### Docs
- `docs/gameplay.md` (Other bikes) and `docs/architecture.md` (pure logic list and file table) updated.

## Verification & Testing

- **Handling untouched:** `git diff` shows no change to `step`, `settle`, `advance`, `hit_walls`, `read_player_input` or `BikeTuning`; `crash::carry` returns immediately when the knock is zero (unit test), so without contacts every bike behaves exactly as before.
- **New unit tests (`src/bike/crash.rs`):** side rub pushes both apart without a fall and conserves momentum (parting speed = restitution × closing speed); a nudge from behind trades speed and conserves momentum; ramming from behind much faster throws only the follower off; a T-bone at speed brings both down; crossing at walking pace is only a bump; bikes already moving apart are separated without an impulse; a knock is scrubbed off in a moment and does nothing when idle; the thrown rider ends up ahead of the stopped bike, lands within a second and lies on their back; the fallen bike decelerates at exactly 0.45 g on asphalt; the fall ends and the rejoin pose is on the asphalt, along the track; rejoining avoids a bike in the way; a sliding rider is stopped by the walls; the lying body is oriented back-behind, feet-ahead, chest-up.
- **New AI regression test (`src/bike/ai.rs`):** a pack of the seven grid rivals races 90 s with full contact physics, falls and rejoins: nobody leaves the track, at most two falls, and every rival completes at least one lap. `rival_goes_round_a_stopped_bike_in_its_lane` now uses `collide` and checks there is no contact at all.
- `cargo test`: **175 passed**, 0 failed.
- `cargo fmt --check`: clean. `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings`: 0 warnings.
- **Visual check:** ran the game under Xvfb with Mesa's software Vulkan (lavapipe) and a temporary, uncommitted harness that forced crashes and saved screenshots: (1) a forced fall at speed — the bike drops on its side and slides while the rider is thrown on ahead, lands and slides; (2) a real 50° T-bone with a rival through `collide` — both riders go down, bikes and riders separate and slide (one bike onto the grass), and both bikes are back on the track, upright with their riders, at ~2.5 s with no flying back; (3) a side close-up of a low-speed fall — the rider flies, lands, lies flat on the back with the body straight and slides, then the bike reappears instantly.
