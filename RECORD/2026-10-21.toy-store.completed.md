# 2026-10-21: A New Track, the Toy Store

- **Date:** 2026-10-21
- **Branch:** `circuit/toy-shop-mm` (after commit `e382185`)
- **Topic:** a new track set inside a toy shop: a toy circuit that climbs on to
  an armchair, crosses a play table, comes down a slide and races past an
  electric train, under the ceiling of the shop.
- **Language:** English (append-only record).

---

## 1. The Brief

### 1.1 User Request

> "me gustaría añadir un nuevo mapa, basado en una juguetería" (a new map, based
> on a toy shop).

> "un tramo irá sobre una pista de scalextrix, otro tramo sobre un circuito
> estilo hotwheels, bajará un tobogán, pasará al lado de un tren eléctrico,
> qué más se te ocurre?" (one stretch over a Scalextric track, another over a
> Hot Wheels style circuit, it will come down a slide, and pass beside an
> electric train — what else could you think of?).

> "debería parecer el interior de una juguetería, con estanterías, sillas,
> mesas con juguetes, etc" (it should look like the inside of a toy shop, with
> shelves, chairs, tables with toys, and so on).

> "que no sea muy extensible, prefiero jugar con la altura, y que dure sobre 1
> minuto por vuelta" (not very long; better to play with the height; and it
> should last about a minute a lap).

> "y obviamente, no tiene cielo, debería verse el techo con luces" (and
> obviously it has no sky: the ceiling with its lights should be seen).

> "las motos tendrán el tamaño de motos de juguete, irán cambiando de altura,
> puede haber rampas que suban a una silla, y de la silla a la mesa, o que
> bajen directamente de la mesa, que pasen de una mesa a otra, algún tramo por
> el suelo, etc" (the bikes will be the size of toy motorcycles, they will
> change height, there may be ramps that climb to a chair, and from the chair
> to the table, or that come down straight from the table, that pass from one
> table to another, some stretch on the floor, and so on).

Seven reference images were given: a bright toy shop with wall shelves full of
toys, a shop front with a big display, a shop with a pink curtain and a
turret of shelves, a model-car display with a plastic circuit, a large plastic
toy set with loops and a red track, a plastic ramp with cars, a Hot Wheels
"Track Creator" set, and a boy playing with a wooden railway on a rug.

### 1.2 What Was Added Beyond the Brief

- A **great armchair** the track climbs over, and a **play table** the race
  starts and finishes on, both at the height the road is at there.
- An **electric train** running round a wooden railway on the floor of the
  shop, with the track running beside it.
- **Shelves of toys** along the walls, with boxes, dolls and balls on them,
  and toys lying about on the floor.
- A **ceiling** with beams and rows of fluorescent lamps over the whole
  shop, and the light of them (the sun's disc, the sky and the clouds are
  all put away).
- The road is not asphalt: it is the **toy track itself**, in the colour of
  the set it is over, with a blue plastic rail down each side.

## 2. The Implementation

### 2.1 The Track (`src/circuits/toy_store.rs`)

A circuit of **1.97 km** around a 450 m by 730 m hall, drawn with
`tools/circuit_shape.py` (new): four straights joined by circular corners of
100 m, with a pair of esses laid into the south aisle, and the finish line at
the origin with the start straight running north.

| Stretch | Along | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| The play table | 0–30 m | The bikes start on the table, 6 m over the floor, under the "TOY STORE" arch |
| The slide | 30–100 m | Down a yellow plastic chute with blue sides, at up to 11 % |
| The armchair | 130–195 m | Up a ramp to the seat of a great armchair, 3 m over the floor, and down again |
| The Scalextric aisle | 577–741 m | The long straight by the north wall, over a grey set with its two little rails |
| The railway | 924–1353 m | Beside the wooden railway of the electric train, along the west wall |
| The Hot Wheels aisle | 1514–1739 m | An orange circuit with two esses in it, along the south wall |
| The ramp back to the table | 1880–1960 m | Up to the play table again, with the finish line on it |

The height profile is written with points dense enough on the ramps and the
slide that the Hermite spline never overshoots: the road's lowest point is
0.2 m under the floor's level, which is why the shop's floor is a little
lower than the road (`FLOOR_Y` in `src/world/toy_store.rs`).

**Why the lap starts on the table and dives down the slide:** the time-trial
test (`against_the_clock_a_good_ride_earns_a_little_time_at_each_checkpoint`)
gives the rider a time for each stretch between checkpoints at a pace of 62 to
69 m/s, and a rival's ride has to beat it. From a standing start the bike
takes about 5 s to reach its cruising speed, which a flat 560 m first stretch
cannot pay for; a first stretch that goes straight down a 6 m slide can. The
lap's first stretch is therefore the table and the slide, and the display
section was moved from the middle of the lap to the start for that reason.

### 2.2 The World (`src/world/toy_store.rs`, new)

The shop is built out of the primitives of `src/world/quads.rs`:

- the **room**: the floor, the four walls and the ceiling, none of which
  casts a shadow (the ceiling would leave the whole shop in the dark);
- the **ceiling**: nine beams and 48 fluorescent lamps on rods, hanging 3 m
  below it, their light emissive, and the point lights that follow the
  cameras (`src/world/lamps.rs`);
- the **shelves**: six units along the walls, each with a back, five boards
  and its ends, and a box of a different colour on every board;
- the **armchair** and the **play table**, at the height the road is at where
  it runs over them;
- the **slide**: the chute the road comes down, swept along it with its inner
  and outer faces and its sides;
- the **toy track**: the bed of the road, drawn over the same ground the
  asphalt would be on (the asphalt is not drawn on this track at all), in the
  colour of the set it is over: the shop's own orange circuit, a grey
  Scalextric set with two little rails down the middle, a Hot Wheels circuit
  and the yellow of the slide;
- the **electric train**: its railway of two rails and sleepers on the floor
  of the shop, and an engine and two carriages that go round it
  (`run_the_train`);
- the **toys** on the floor: balls, stacks of blocks, dolls and spinning tops.

### 2.3 What Changed in the Engine

- `SkyStyle::Indoor` (`src/world/scenery.rs`, `src/world/sky.rs`): the whole
  sky is put away — the dome, the sun and the clouds with it — and the light
  is that of the lamps on the ceiling of a shop, from straight above, with a
  soft haze in the air of it.
- `Scenery::indoor()`: an indoor circuit has no terrain (the floor of the
  shop is drawn with the shop), no sea, and its shoulders are paved.
- `WallStyle::ToyRail` (`src/world/road.rs`): a low rail of bright blue
  plastic, rounded over the top, as the edges of a toy track are.
- `src/world/road.rs`: the asphalt, the paint and the kerbs are not drawn on
  a toy shop's circuit; the toy track is drawn over the same ground instead.

### 2.4 The Music

"Orange Asphalt" (`assets/music/orange_asphalt.mp3`, 168.7 s), which had no
track of its own. Its loudness was measured the way the other songs' was
(`ffmpeg -t 120 -i song.mp3 -af highpass=f=500,ebur128 -f null -`, which
`ffmpeg` is not installed here for): the file was decoded with `afconvert`
and its energy above 500 Hz measured, against the songs whose loudness is
known, and the difference taken (−15.5 LUFS).
