# Active Controller Feedback and Collision Tuning

- **Date:** 2026-10-12
- **Topic:** Route gamepad haptics and trigger effects exclusively to the active controller; improve collision lateral displacement and trigger crashes when headings are not practically parallel.

## User Requests
1. "tengo los dos mandos emparejados, un dualsense y un gulikit; jugando con el gulikit, estoy percibiendo las vibraciones y los gatillos en el dualsense, el feedback sólo debería aplicar al mando que se esté usando"

"el choque sigue funcionando bastante mal, se sacuden un poco, en lugar de desplazarse hacia los lados; y no he conseguido que nos caigamos al cruzar direcciones; resumiendo, al chocarse de lado, teniendo la misma dirección, deberían desplazarse hacia los lados, cuando las direcciones no son prácticamente paralelas, deberían caerse de las motos"

## Implementation Plan
1. **Active Controller Tracking (`ActiveController`):**
   - Introduce `ActiveController` resource in `src/gamepads.rs` tracking `pub gamepad: Option<Entity>`.
   - Update `ActiveController` in `src/ui.rs` (`update_input_device`) and `src/bike/mod.rs` (`read_player_input`) based on intentional input (sticks, buttons, triggers).
   - In `src/gamepads/apple.rs` (`update_dualsense_triggers`), only apply adaptive trigger effects and collision vibrations to a DualSense if it is the active controller (or bound in split-screen); otherwise turn its triggers off.
   - In `src/bike/mod.rs` (`separate_bikes`), send collision rumble requests only to the active gamepad in single player (or to the specific collided player's gamepad in split screen).
   - In `src/gamepads.rs` (`ClaimedPads` / `handle_rumble_requests`), map claimed GameController entities to their gilrs IDs so controllers like GuliKit/Xbox receive physical force feedback rumble.

2. **Crossing Collision Crash Tuning:**
   - Define "prácticamente paralelas" as `fa.dot(fb) >= 0.94` (~20° max angle difference).
   - If `fa.dot(fb) < 0.94` and bikes are in motion (`speed > 0.5`), trigger `CrashState` on both bikes (2.5s duration), causing both riders to fall off onto the asphalt, lay flat (~83°), and slide with road friction.

3. **Lateral Displacement Tuning (Parallel Collisions):**
   - Increase lateral shove to `0.55m` each (`1.1m` total separation) across lanes.
   - Deflect headings slightly outwards (~5° / 0.08 rad) on impact so bikes smoothly separate into different lanes instead of re-colliding.
   - Add outwards lean reaction on side impact.
   - Displace the rival AI's target lane (`line.0` and `rival.lane`) away from the player by 1.2m so the rival yields and stays in its new lane instead of steering back into the player.

## Realistic Crash Dynamics and Rider Ejection ("Soltarse de la moto")

- **Date:** 2026-10-12

### User Request
> "creo que ha quedado trabajo a medias, las caídas no tienen sentido, se caen de lado en lugar de soltarse de la moto
> se caen incluso cuando apenas tienen velocidad
> busca cómo deberían ser las caídas para ser realistas"

### Physics & Realism Research
1. **Kinetic Energy and Speed Thresholds:**
   - In real motorcycle collisions and racing (e.g. MotoGP, Superbike, Moto Racer), low-speed contacts (< 25-30 km/h / 7-8 m/s) do not cause catastrophic loss of control or ejections. Riders naturally counter-steer, brush shoulders/fairings, or absorb the contact without falling.
   - A wipeout requires substantial kinetic energy / closing momentum: non-parallel trajectories (`dot < 0.90`, angle > 26°) combined with high speed (`max_speed >= 7.0 m/s` (~25 km/h) or relative impact velocity `rel_speed >= 6.0 m/s`).
2. **Rider Ejection Mechanics (Newton's First Law):**
   - Motorcycles lack seatbelts or cabins. Upon violent collision, the motorcycle rapidly decelerates and drops onto its side (~83° lean), sliding along the asphalt with metal/plastic friction ($\mu \approx 0.45$).
   - The rider's inertia carries them forward along their momentum vector, stripping them off the bike ("soltarse de la moto").
   - Hands release the handlebars and feet release the footpegs.
   - The rider slides/tumbles across the road surface independently, stopping several meters ahead and outward from the motorcycle.
   - During recovery (final phase of crash), the rider stands back up, the bike rights itself, and control resumes smoothly.

### Implementation Details
1. **`CrashState` Fields:**
   - Added `duration: f32` and `impact_speed: f32` to [`CrashState`](src/bike/mod.rs) to parameterize slide distance and interpolation phases.
2. **Speed Threshold in `separate_bikes` (`src/bike/mod.rs`):**
   - Low-speed contacts (`max_speed < 7.0 && rel_speed < 6.0`) or parallel contacts now cleanly resolve as lateral deflections (`LATERAL_DISPLACEMENT`), preventing crashes at walking speed.
   - High-energy crossing collisions trigger full crashes with `CRASH_DURATION = 2.8s`, recording each bike's `impact_speed` and fall direction.
3. **Rider Detachment via `RiderMount` (`src/bike/model/mod.rs`):**
   - Introduced `RiderMount { pub bike: Entity }` spawned as a pivot child of `body`.
   - All rider and pilot parts (procedural joints, Meshy skeleton joints, skinned meshes) are now parented under `rider_mount`.
   - Introduced `update_rider_mount` system:
     - Cancels motorcycle lean via `unroll = Quat::from_rotation_z(-state.lean)`.
     - Ejects the rider forward along the road (-Z) by 2.5m to 6.0m (scaled by `impact_speed`).
     - Separates the rider laterally by 1.7m and drops them to ground level (-0.52m relative to seat).
     - Rotates the rider flat onto the asphalt in a tumbling slide orientation.
     - Smoothly recovers rider back to the seat in the final 15% of crash duration.
4. **Release of Grips and Pegs during Crash (`pilot.rs` and `rider.rs`):**
   - In `pilot::pose_pilots`, when `CrashState` is active, hands smoothly release the handlebars and tuck defensively near the chest; feet release the footpegs and trail along the road.
   - In `rider::reach_grips` and `rider::reach_pegs`, limb IK targets stay with the rider's body rather than stretching across the track back to the bike.
