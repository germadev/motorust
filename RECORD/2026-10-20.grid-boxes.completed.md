# The starting grid's boxes drawn round the bikes

## 1. The bikes stood ahead of their boxes

**Date:** 2026-10-20. **Branch:** `claude/sleepy-galileo-s35zhl`. (The "false start" item of `2026-10-20.pending-requests.WIP.md`.)

### User request
Earlier: "When there is a false start, the bikes should go back to the starting position." I had checked that after a false start the bikes are already put back on their slots at once, and asked what was meant. The answer: "About the false start, I think it is not that they are not put back, but that in the normal position the bikes are ahead of the mark."

### Cause
A bike stands on its slot (`Track::grid_slot`) at its middle: the model's origin is halfway between the axles. The front axle is 0.70 m ahead of it, so the front tyre reaches about 1.0 m ahead, and the rear tyre about 1.0 m behind. The box painted on the road (`starting_grid` in `src/world/road.rs`) only ran from 0.9 m behind the slot to its front line 0.4 m ahead. Every bike stood with its front wheel about 0.6 m over the line, after a false start too.

### Implementation
- **`src/bike/model/mod.rs`:** `NOSE` (`-FRONT_AXLE.z + WHEEL_RADIUS`, 1.01 m) and `TAIL` (`REAR_AXLE.z + WHEEL_RADIUS`, 0.99 m): how far a bike reaches ahead of where it stands and behind it. A test holds every design to them, imported ones within the 3 cm their measurements allow (the Falcon's rear tyre reaches 1.009 m).
- **`src/world/road.rs`:** the box is drawn round the bike. The front line runs from `NOSE` + 0.06 m to 0.18 m further ahead (`GRID_LINE_BACK`, `GRID_LINE_FRONT`). The sides run back from it to `TAIL` + 0.1 m behind the slot (`GRID_BOX_BACK`), 0.85 m to each side as before (`GRID_BOX_HALF_WIDTH`). The bikes and the race are untouched: the slots, the distance to the line and the false start stay as they were. Only the paint moved.

### Verification
- New test `a_bike_on_the_grid_stands_inside_its_box`. It reads the grid's mesh round each of the 8 slots. Everything crossing the middle of the box (the front line) is ahead of `NOSE`, and less than 0.4 m ahead of it. The sides reach back past `TAIL`, and less than 0.4 m past it. With the old box the front line, at 0.4 m, would fail it.
- New test `every_bike_reaches_no_further_than_its_nose_and_tail`.
