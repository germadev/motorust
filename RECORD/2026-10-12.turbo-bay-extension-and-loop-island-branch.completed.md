# Turbo Bay Extension, Loop Island and Its Branch with a Rideable Loop

- **Date:** 2026-10-12
- **Topic:** Make Turbo Bay longer with climbs in the new part; rename Loops Hill to Loop Island and give it a vertical loop the bikes can ride, on an alternative branch rather than on the lap; keyboard/D-pad release rate defaulting to medium.
- **Branch:** `claude/pensive-bohr-11hpa8`

## Entry 1 — Track engine, loop physics, rename (WIP checkpoint)

### Implementation Details

- `src/track.rs`: `Layout::branches` with `Branch` (fork and join points on the lap, control points of the road to the loop and of the road back) and `VerticalLoop` (mouth, heading, height, radius, sideways shift). `Track` now holds open side roads (`closed: false`, per-point `walls`, `lap_along`, `apart`) and `Loop`s. `Nearest::road` says which road a point is on; heights, edges, crests, surfaces, poses and `along` dispatch on it. While a side road shares the lap's road its surface is the lap's (blended at query time by `apart`, so the two are one surface); once its walls part from the lap's it goes to its own height and banking over 30 m. Its walls close in to the loop's running surface over the last 70 m (a funnel). Side roads are narrower (10 m of asphalt).
- `src/bike/loop_ride.rs` (new): bikes ride a loop on rails. Engine, brake and resistances as on a flat road, plus gravity along the surface. Past the side, too slow to be pressed against the surface (v²/R < −g·cos θ), the bike falls off (`crash::fall_off`, bike and rider drop to the road below); too slow to reach the side, it comes back down and can reverse out of the mouth. `BikeState::ride`. Collisions skip bikes on a loop; grass kick-up too. Interpolation wraps the pitch. The chase camera turns over with the bike round the loop and comes back upright off it.
- `src/bike/crash.rs`: a fallen bike that is above the road (off a loop) drops onto it under gravity instead of snapping down; `rejoin_pose` works on side roads.
- Loops Hill → Loop Island (`src/circuits/loop_island.rs`, arch "LOOP ISLAND"). Its branch forks right just after the start-straight crest (215 m), cuts inside the first long corner north-east to a 16 m loop, and rejoins on the S-bends (615 m). The decorative loop at 430 m, the rock ledge at 205–290 m and the props in the branch's way were moved or removed.
- `src/pause.rs`: keyboard/D-pad "al soltar la tecla de girar" defaults to "media"; "rápida" (the old default, renamed from "rápido (actual)") and "lenta" are unchanged.

### Verification & Testing

- New tests: branch junctions on the lap's surface and heading, rideable side roads clear of the lap once apart, bikes on a branch finding its road; loop riding (going round, rolling back, falling off, entering at the mouth).
- `cargo test`: 211 passed. `cargo fmt --check`: clean.

## Entry 2 — Drawing the branch and the loop, rivals on the branch, Turbo Bay's mountain, docs

### Implementation Details

- **Roads off the lap, drawn** (`src/world/road.rs`): asphalt, white lines, centre dashes, curbs and walls are built per road (`Road { track, number }`), with the walls at each road's own distance (the funnel into the loop) and the wall style of the stretch of lap the road stands in for. Where two roads meet, a road draws no walls between the other's walls and no lines or curbs on the other's asphalt, and a branch's asphalt is not drawn where it lies wholly on the lap's (it sits 1 cm above the lap's where they overlap). The approach and exit of a loop have curbs over their last and first 30 m, like the running surface.
- **The loop, drawn** (`src/world/landmarks.rs`, `spawn_ridden_loops`): asphalt with yellow and blue curbs and white lines on the running surface, low walls of checkerboard earth along its edges carrying on those of the roads in and out, all on a 2.4 m ring of checkerboard earth; 120 pieces round, three meshes per loop. Its footprint keeps trees off it. Rows of props skip points on or right beside a branch (`row_points`). The minimap draws the branches, slightly thinner.
- **Rivals on the branch** (`src/bike/ai.rs`): `Rival::loops` (two rivals of the grid take the branches); the way ahead is a list of `Waypoint`s along the lap, onto the branch at its fork, straight into the loop and back onto the lap from the road out. Round a loop a rival holds the throttle open. A rival that does not take the branches keeps to the lap where the two share the road, but follows a branch it has been pushed onto. `Loop::fork`/`join` give the lap points where the roads leave and join.
- **Turbo Bay, longer and hillier** (`src/circuits/turbo_bay.rs`): designed with the Python mirror of `Track`, then ported. After the east coast road, a new mountain road climbs north in S-bends between rock walls (with a concrete barrier and the cliffs above the sea on the right), dips, and climbs again to a summit at a new northern cape, 52 m above the start (the old top was 44 m), round a long left-hander with the lighthouse, moved there, on its own rocky cape below. It comes back down the west side through the forest, over a hump, a dip and a rise, onto the old ridge descent over the tunnel. The lap goes from 2.5 to 3.1 km (+550 m); steepest grade 11.5 %; tightest corner 40 m. The coast outline grows a northern lobe; a crag stands inside the bends. Everything after the new section moved 550 m on (spans, signs, cuttings, the second checkpoint now at 1980 m); new chevrons before the climb, the summit and the kink onto the ridge. The tunnel crossing is unchanged.
- **Docs:** `README.md` (tracks, new image `loop-island-loop.jpg`), `docs/tracks.md` (Turbo Bay lap in order with two new images, Loop Island section with the branch and two new images, "Branches and loops" for track authors, tests), `docs/gameplay.md` (branches and loops, debug options table with both keyboard rates and their defaults), `docs/architecture.md` (code map, loops on rails, test table, 212 tests), `docs/building.md` (test count). Old `loops-hill*.jpg` removed.

### Verification & Testing

- New tests: a rival that takes the branches goes round the loop and back; the pack test now runs on the Bay and on Loop Island (with the two loop riders), with loop riding handled as in the game. On Turbo Bay the pack test sees four falls (at the grid and in the first corner); the same four happen on the original commit (`af74ad2`), so they are not from this change, and the test stays off Turbo Bay.
- Loop numbers: off the throttle a bike needs about 36 m/s (130 km/h) at the mouth to get round; slower it falls off (or below about 20 m/s comes back down). With the throttle open the arcade engine (over 1 g) carries it round from much slower. A grip limit on the drive round the loop was tried and dropped: it changed nothing.
- Visual checks under Xvfb with Mesa's software Vulkan (lavapipe), through a temporary harness (not committed) with fixed and following cameras: the fork from above, the funnel and the mouth, the loop from the side with two rivals riding it (one upside down at the top), the ride itself from behind the bike (the camera turns over), the merge, and on Turbo Bay the mountain from the air, the climb, the summit with the lighthouse and the forest descent.
- `cargo test`: 212 passed. `cargo fmt --check`: clean. `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings`: clean.

## Entry 3 — Falling from high up, final checks

### Implementation Details

- A new test of a bike falling off a loop showed it "landing" in mid-air: the height-aware lookups (`Track::nearest_at`, `nearest_index_at`) took any stretch at the bike's level, however far, so something falling from 25 m found the high side of the track hundreds of metres away. A stretch at its level now only counts if it is at most 23 m (`LEVEL_SPREAD`, twice the wall distance) further than the nearest road; where the track passes over itself the two stretches are much closer than that. Bikes on the road behave as before (all the tunnel-crossing tests pass unchanged).
- Test: `falling_off_a_loop_bike_and_rider_drop_to_the_road` (25 m in about 2.3 s, as a falling body; the rider lands too; the bike ends up on its side).

### Verification & Testing

- `cargo test`: 213 passed. `cargo fmt --check`: clean. `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings`: clean. `cargo check --target wasm32-unknown-unknown`: builds.
- Not verified on hardware or at full frame rate: the renders were at about 1 FPS in software, so how the loop feels at speed (the camera turning over in about 1.5 s) still needs a try in the real game.
