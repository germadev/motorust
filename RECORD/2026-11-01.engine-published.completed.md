Tests: the engine's (`a_renamed_circuit_keeps_the_records_of_its_former_names`, on its demo file with two former names) and the game's (`it_keeps_the_records_it_set_as_tsushima_isle`).
- **Comments**: about 120 mentions of the game's circuits and works (Fellowship Road, Moria, the Argonath, Rivendell, the Shire, Ghost Island, Tsushima, Gold Mine, Turbo Bay, Loop Island, Moon Base, the toy store, Hot Wheels, Speed Bay, West Way and Moto Racer, Sonic, The Wind Waker, the Meshy bikes' names and their sheets) rewritten in generic terms ("a circuit with a dam", "great halls dug under a mountain", "an arcade island", "the toy cars' orange track", "a cel-shaded cartoon sea"). ## 4. The Game over the Engine's Repository (2026-11-02)

### 4.1 User Request
> "Usamos las build de motorust-engine en motorust?" (do we use the engine's builds in the game?) Answered: no, and never: the engine's builds are its demo, executables, not something a crate links; a game builds the engine from its sources, and what it takes of a release is its tag. Proposed: the game depending on the engine by its repository and that tag, and this repository's `motorust-engine/` gone. "Sí, hazlo" (yes, do it).

### 4.2 Implementation Details
- **`Cargo.toml`**: no workspace any more; `motorust-engine = { git = "https://github.com/jgermade/motorust-engine", tag = "v0.0.18" }` in `[dependencies]`, and the same with its `testing` feature in `[dev-dependencies]` (the game's checks). `Cargo.lock` names its source (`?tag=v0.0.18#00e57511`). Bevy and serde stay the game's dependencies too, at the engine's versions (one of each in the lock).
- **`motorust-engine/` removed** (`git rm -r`): its files are the engine's repository's, as they were at `v0.0.18` (the same code, but for the records written after it).
- **`tools/engine_dir.sh`** (new): the folder of the engine's sources as Cargo builds the game with them (`cargo metadata`: its checkout at the tag, or the clone under a local `[patch]`), read with jq or, without it, Python 3; on Windows in its Unix form (`cygpath`). Used by CI for the engine's credits (`CREDITS-ENGINE.md` in every package, desktop, `.app` and web) and by `tools/toy_store_layout.py` to import the engine's `tools/circuit_layout.py`.
- **CI** (`.github/workflows/build.yml`): `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` and `cargo test` (the engine's own tests run in its repository); the credits from `tools/engine_dir.sh`. `make test` is `cargo test`.
- **Working on both at once** (`docs/building.md`, "The engine"): the engine's clone beside this one and a `[patch."https://github.com/jgermade/motorust-engine"]` to `../motorust-engine` in `.cargo/config.toml`, which `.gitignore` now leaves out, never committed (nor `Cargo.lock` while it names the clone). A new engine: released in its repository, the tag changed in both places, `cargo update -p motorust-engine`.
- **Docs**: `docs/architecture.md`'s overview says the engine is a dependency from its repository, and that **paths starting with `motorust-engine/` in the docs are in the engine's repository** (the many such paths are left as they are); the tests' count is the game's 234; `tools/engine_dir.sh` listed. `AGENTS.md`: the checks without `--workspace`, and "The engine is a dependency" (changes made, recorded and released in its repository, taken here by the tag; nothing of the game's into it). `README.md`, `assets/CREDITS.md` and `assets/pack.txt` point at the engine's credits and pack in its repository; `build.rs` no longer watches the folder; `src/main.rs`'s doc.
