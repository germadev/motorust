## 3. Implementation Details

### 3.1 Collision Displacement Physics (`src/bike/mod.rs`)
- Enhanced `bump`:
  - When two bikes overlap (`distance < BIKE_SPACING`), calculates normal and static overlap separation (`push = overlap / 2.0`).
  - Added physical collision displacement impulse `BUMP_DISPLACEMENT` (0.12 m):
    - `a.position += normal * (push + BUMP_DISPLACEMENT)`
    - `b.position -= normal * (push + BUMP_DISPLACEMENT)`
  - Bikes now visibly and physically shove and recoil away from each other on contact, avoiding interpenetration and feeling tangibly responsive.
  - `bump` now returns a boolean indicating whether contact occurred.

### 3.2 Impact Tracking & Rumble System (`CollisionImpact`)
- Added `CollisionImpact` component (`timer: f32`, `intensity: f32`) attached to every bike entity during `spawn_grid`.
- In `separate_bikes` (`FixedUpdate`):
  - Decays active collision timers per physics step (`imp.timer = (imp.timer - dt).max(0.0)`).
  - When bikes collide, calculates normalized impact intensity proportional to speeds (`0.35..=0.75`) and activates a 0.20-second impact window on both bikes.
  - If a player-controlled bike is involved, sends a soft rumble pulse via `MessageWriter<GamepadRumbleRequest>` with weak motor bias (160 ms) for all standard gamepad rumble actuators.

### 3.3 DualSense Adaptive Trigger Soft Collision Vibration (`src/gamepads/apple.rs`)
- In `compute_bike_trigger_effects`:
  - Receives `Option<&CollisionImpact>`.
  - If an active collision impact is present (`imp.timer > 0.0`):
    - Both left and right triggers switch to a soft haptic vibration pulse (`amplitude: 0.20..=0.40`, `frequency: 0.50`, `start_position: 0.05`).
  - Once the impact window expires, both triggers immediately revert to their gradual `SlopeFeedback` curve with 0 resistance at the start, subtle resistance at the end, and zero continuous vibration.
