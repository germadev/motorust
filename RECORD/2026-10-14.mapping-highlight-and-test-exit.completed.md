## 2. The Highlight and the End of the Test

### 2.1 User Requests
> "En Opciones > Control > Asignación, solo debe mostrarse el highlight cuando esté en modo test y en modo de reasignación"
> "ESC o SELECT+START para salir del modo test del teclado o mando"

### 2.2 Implementation Details
- **`src/mapping.rs`:** `lights_up` (held, and `Mapping::capturing`: testing or remapping) decides whether a key or button held is lit, on the keyboard (`shade`) and on the gamepad (`tint`). Moving round the view, the keys and buttons pressed (arrows, Enter, the D-pad) no longer light up, and the status line no longer lists what they do (that is the test's). The keyboard's keys that do something keep their lighter grey.
- **The end of the test:** `Step::Test` has no timer any more (the five seconds untouched are gone): Esc ends it, or Select with Start on a gamepad. That is the fullscreen shortcut, which `read_shortcut` takes out of the buttons before the view reads them, so `ControlsTest` (`src/settings.rs`) carries it: `gamepad_shortcuts` sets `ended` instead of switching fullscreen while `on`; `use_options_menu` hands it to the view as `Held::select_start`; `mark_controls_test` (after the options' systems, every frame) sets `on` from the view's step and clears `ended`. The status line says "Esc / Select+Start" with the buttons' names.

### 2.3 Verification
- New tests: `what_is_held_lights_up_only_testing_or_remapping`, `select_with_start_ends_a_test_and_waiting_does_not` (replacing the test of the timeout), `trying_the_controls_select_with_start_ends_the_test_instead` (fullscreen without a test, the end of the test with one).
- `cargo test`: 390 passed. `cargo fmt --check` and `cargo clippy --all-targets` clean.
- In the game (Xvfb, lavapipe): the right arrow held while moving round the view lights nothing and the line under the keyboard stays empty; in a test the line says "Press keys, together too, to try them out (Esc / Select+Start: done)", D held lights up with "D > Steer Right" and Steer Right lit in the list, and Esc ends it. Select+Start could not be pressed here (no gamepad): it is covered by the tests.
