# Gold Mine mountain, lake and forks; tyre and music fixes

- **Date:** 2026-10-13
- **Branch:** `claude/hopeful-bell-eknth2` (after commit `d66253e`)

## 1. Translucent tyres

### 1.1 User Request
- "los neumáticos de la moto se ven translúcidos" (the bike's tyres look translucent).

### 1.2 Implementation Details
- `src/bike/model/tyre.rs`: the procedural tyre's triangles were wound clockwise seen from outside (at the crown, the edge across the section is +X and the edge round the wheel is +Z, so `a, a+1, b` faces −Y while the tyre's outward normal there is +Y). With back faces culled, the outside was not drawn and the inside of the far half showed through. The indices are now `a, b, a+1, b, b+1, a+1`, counter-clockwise from outside. The vertex normals were already right.

### 1.3 Verification & Testing
- New test `every_face_of_the_tyre_is_drawn_from_outside`: the front of every triangle faces the way its corners' normals point. The eight tyre tests pass.

## 2. Music too quiet on Gold Mine and Bahía GP

### 2.1 User Request
- "la música de la Mina Dorada y la de Bahía GP tienen poco volumen" (the Gold Mine and Bahía GP music is quiet).

### 2.2 Implementation Details
- Measured with ffmpeg's `ebur128`. The four songs were normalised to the same integrated loudness (−24 LUFS) and matched it. But over the whole song the bands differ: Gold Mine and Bahía GP carry their loudness in the bass, which the engines' drone covers, and Loop Island in the mids and highs. Measured above 500 Hz over the first two minutes (as long as a race), the songs played at −27.5 (Bahía GP), −27.9 (Turbo Bay), −26.4 (Loop Island) and −28.9 LUFS (Gold Mine). In short-term loudness during a race, Bahía GP and Gold Mine sat 4–5 dB under Loop Island.
- `Song::loudness` is now that measure (`ffmpeg -t 120 -i song.mp3 -af highpass=f=500,ebur128 -f null -`): Bahía GP −16.1, Turbo Bay −16.0, Loop Island −13.3, Gold Mine −16.8 LUFS. `LOUDNESS` is −25.4 LUFS above 500 Hz: Loop Island's level plus 1 dB. Change in gain: Gold Mine +3.5 dB, Bahía GP +2.1, Turbo Bay +2.5, Loop Island +1.0.
- Docs: `docs/gameplay.md`, `docs/tracks.md`, `docs/architecture.md`.

### 2.3 Verification & Testing
- Margin of each song's loudest second (first 2 minutes, full-band RMS) under the player's engine flat out, before the change: 13.4 dB (Bahía GP), 14.1 (Turbo Bay), 15.5 (Loop Island), 13.9 (Gold Mine). After it, every song still clears the 10 dB the test `the_music_stays_well_under_the_player_s_engine` asks for (the smallest margin is Gold Mine's, about 10.4 dB). The 17 sound tests pass.

## 3. MotoGP tyres: rounder, no tread

### 3.1 User Request
- "las ruedas de moto gp son más redondeadas y con menos dibujo, documéntate" (MotoGP tyres are rounder and have less tread; look it up).

### 3.2 Research
- In the dry, MotoGP races on slicks (Michelin Power Slick), with no tread pattern at all, built to grip at lean angles up to 64°. The compound is marked on the sidewall: in Michelin's 2016 code, white for the soft, no marking for the medium, yellow for the hard (intermediates grey, soft wets blue). Sources: crash.net ("Know the Michelin MotoGP tyre markings"), racer.com ("Michelin reveals tire color codes", 2016), tyrepress.com (2014 colour-coded sidewalls), Wikipedia ("Motorcycle tyre", on profile and lean). Whether the colour code is still the same today was not confirmed; motogp.com could not be reached from the container.

### 3.3 Implementation Details
- `src/bike/model/tyre.rs`: the crown of the cross-section is now an ellipse (`SQUARENESS` 2.6 → 2.0), round from shoulder to shoulder, and the tread covers ±82° of it (was ±75°), with short sidewalls. The four tread patterns (slick, sport, touring, sipe) with their grooves and normal maps are gone: `Compound::{Soft, Medium, Hard}` slicks, smooth rubber scrubbed lighter in the middle, with a white band (soft), none (medium) or a yellow band (hard) round the sidewall. Soft: Kraken RS, Stiletto V4, Azura RZ; medium: Shogun TZ, Falcon KRZ, Ronin YS; hard: Legend 88, Cobra XT. Rubber with a satin sheen (roughness 0.62). The grain noise blends round so the texture repeats without a seam.
- `src/bike/model/mod.rs`: one material per compound, shared.
- Docs: `docs/models.md`, `docs/architecture.md`.

### 3.4 Verification & Testing
- New and changed tests: `slicks_are_smooth_with_their_compound_on_the_sidewall`, `the_crown_is_round_from_shoulder_to_shoulder` (an ellipse at the shoulder; past 60° of lean still on the tread), `the_texture_repeats_seamlessly_round_the_tyre`, `the_imported_bikes_wear_every_compound`. The nine tyre tests pass.
- In game (Xvfb + lavapipe): close-ups of the eight bikes on the grid; the tyres are solid, round and smooth, with their bands.
