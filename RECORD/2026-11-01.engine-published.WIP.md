# Step 6 of the Engine's Split: the Engine Published (2026-11-01)

Step 6 of "The engine apart from the game" (`ROADMAP.md`; the plan in `RECORD/2026-10-31.engine-split-plan.WIP.md`): `motorust-engine/` a repository of its own, [jgermade/motorust-engine](https://github.com/jgermade/motorust-engine), starting a new history; and, still to do, this project private, depending on it.

## 1. The Engine's First Commit

### 1.1 User Request
> Asked how to publish (two questions): the licence, **"MIT o Apache-2.0"** (dual, as Bevy); and the mentions of the game's circuits and works left in the engine's code, **"Limpiarlas primero"** (clean them first): the comments and tests' names rewritten in generic terms ("a circuit with a dam", "a theme of Japan") here, and then the folder published as the first commit (a new history, never this repository's).

### 1.2 Implementation Details
- **The engine's tests' names, generic**: the checks of the engine's modules that named a circuit of the game were renamed for what they check (`turbo_bay` → `with_a_dam`, `gold_mine` → `with_a_mine`, `moria` → `with_great_halls`, `moon_base` → `split_circuit`, `turbo_bay_s_dam_holds_its_lake...` → `a_dam_holds_its_lake_a_few_metres_under_the_crest`, `only_loop_island_leaves_a_blue_halo` → `only_a_circuit_with_a_halo_leaves_one`, `the_toy_store_s_helix_is_taken_flat_out` → `a_banked_helix_is_taken_flat_out`, and others), and `src/game/checks.rs` with them.
- **The circuits' names as parameters**: the twelve checks that looked a circuit up by its name (in `structures`, `flowers`, `preview`, `road`, `world/mod.rs`, `terrain` (two), `sea` (two), `wheelie_halo`, `bike/mod.rs`, `ai`, and `shoulders`) take it (`name: &str`); the game's checks pass it ("Fellowship Road", "Ghost Island", ...), the engine's own run on its demo with `""` where the check is of every circuit but the one named. Their `expect`s say `"{name} is a circuit"`.
- **A renamed circuit's records, through its file**: the engine renamed Tsushima Isle's records to Ghost Island's in `Settings::load`. Now a circuit's file says its former names (`formerly: ["Tsushima Isle"]`, `Circuit::formerly`, read by `data::texts`, empty if left out), and `Records::take_former(&circuits)`, called as the `WorldPlugin` is built, keeps their records under its name. Tests: the engine's (`a_renamed_circuit_keeps_the_records_of_its_former_names`, on its demo file with two former names) and the game's (`it_keeps_the_records_it_set_as_tsushima_isle`).
- **Comments**: about 120 mentions of the game's circuits and works (Fellowship Road, Moria, the Argonath, Rivendell, the Shire, Ghost Island, Tsushima, Gold Mine, Turbo Bay, Loop Island, Moon Base, the toy store, Hot Wheels, Speed Bay, West Way and Moto Racer, Sonic, The Wind Waker, the Meshy bikes' names and their sheets) rewritten in generic terms ("a circuit with a dam", "great halls dug under a mountain", "an arcade island", "the toy cars' orange track", "a cel-shaded cartoon sea"). Some comments in Spanish, where touched, are in English now (the liveries, the imported models, `bahia.ron`'s header). Kept: generic and real-world names (Japan, Mount Fuji, a shrine, torii, the Japanese tree species, New Zealand's light, Nintendo's button layout), Meshy (the tool the models are made with) and Bahía GP (the engine's demo).
- **What the engine pointed at in this repository**: the "Step N of the engine's split, `ROADMAP.md`" notes are gone from its modules' docs; the importers (`tools/import_*.py`, the game's) are "the game's importer"; the engine's README no longer links to this repository; the demo circuit's billboard "MOTO RACER" reads "OPEN ROAD" and the examples of a model's folder are `Model("racer")`.
- **`tools/circuit_layout.py` moved to the engine** (`motorust-engine/tools/`, as the plan cut it): its header's paths relative to the engine, its example the demo circuit, run from a game as `python3 motorust-engine/tools/circuit_layout.py assets/circuits/NAME.ron`; `tools/toy_store_layout.py` imports it from there. `AGENTS.md`, `docs/architecture.md` and `ROADMAP.md` updated.
- **The engine's own files**: `LICENSE-MIT` (Copyright (c) 2026 Jesús Germade) and `LICENSE-APACHE` (Apache-2.0, as Bevy ships it); `license = "MIT OR Apache-2.0"` and `repository` in its `Cargo.toml`; its README's Tools and Licence sections; its `AGENTS.md` (this one's rules for the engine: English, records, the engine knowing nothing of a game's content, the checks); `.gitignore`; `.cargo/config.toml` (the profiles this workspace gives it, for when it is built on its own); `.github/workflows/ci.yml` (format, clippy and tests on push and pull request, run only in its own repository); its first record, `motorust-engine/RECORD/2026-11-01.engine-published.completed.md`.
- **The search for anything private** in the folder: no models, songs or circuits of the game (the pack: the demo circuit and roster, Poly Haven's and NASA's images, Russo One, the gamepads' art, all credited); no names of its circuits, themes or works in the code, comments or tests.
- **Published**: the folder's files (but `target/`) copied into the clone of `jgermade/motorust-engine` with this workspace's `Cargo.lock` (trimmed by Cargo to the engine's), committed as its first commit on `main` and pushed.
- `RECORD/2026-11-01.engine-split-step-4.WIP.md` renamed `.completed.md` (step 4 done), and `ROADMAP.md`'s references to it.
