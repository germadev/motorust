# The Last Lap's Word, and the Keyboard's Turn the Other Way as a Slower Swing (2026-10-16)

## 1. User Requests
> "Volvemos a poner el texto 'última', pero no 'actual'"

> "Sobre la velocidad de cambio de dirección con el teclado, el valor medio lo siento lento, sin que cambie en absoluto el comportamiento de rápido, reajusta los valores". Then: "El valor del cambio de sentido medio pasa a ser el lento, el rápido no lo tocamos en absoluto". Then, asked what it applies to: "Sobre el valor de la velocidad de cambio de sentido, a qué aplica? A la velocidad a la que pivota para cambiar la inclinación del piloto?" (it applied to the keyboard's turn, unwound through the middle, not to the lean); then: "Haría que la velocidad de cambio de sentido afecte a cuánto pivota el piloto" and "El comportamiento de rápido no debe cambiar en absoluto". Asked what should swing slower (the bike's lean, the rider's body or both) and whether to keep the turn unwound through the middle, the user chose both, and to take the unwinding out.

## 2. Implementation Details
- **The last lap's word** (`src/race/hud.rs`, `lap_times`): on the first lap the time of the lap under way alone ("0:31.2"); from the second on "ÚLTIMA 1:13.9" / "LAST 1:13.9", the word translucent like every word at the top left, all in gold and bigger while that lap is the one that beat the track's record.
- **The keyboard's turn the other way** (`src/pause.rs`, `src/bike/mod.rs`, `src/bike/model/rider.rs`): the turn unwound back through the middle (record `2026-10-15.grid-start-race-hud-and-keyboard-reverse`) is gone: `DigitalSteer::update` is as it was before it, the turn changing sides at once whatever the option. The option now slows the swing over to the other side: `ReverseRate::slowing` (fast 0, as always; medium 0.4; slow 0.65: 60 % and 35 % of the usual speed), only with a turn from the keyboard or the d-pad (`steer_direct` not nothing), carried by `BikeInput::pivot_slowing` (0 from the stick and the rivals). In `step`, while the lean is going over to the other side (`swinging`: from when the target goes over, while the lean is still on the side it leaves and on its way out on the new one, until it is within a tenth of the target or the target comes back towards the middle; kept in `BikeState::swinging`), the lean's rate is multiplied by `1 - pivot_slowing`; the rider's body hanging off (`rider::hang_off`, its own `swinging`) likewise. With fast the factor is exactly 1: nothing changes. The lean does not steer the bike, so the course is the same at every rate.
- Docs: `docs/gameplay.md` (HUD, Options > Control), `docs/architecture.md` (test counts).
