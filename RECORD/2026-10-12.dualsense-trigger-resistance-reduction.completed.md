# DualSense Trigger Resistance Reduction and Profile Equalization

- **Date:** 2026-10-12
- **Topic:** DualSense adaptive trigger feel tuning: lower resistance, remove notch/step, match right trigger to left

## User Request
"sobre el dualsense, no me gusta la resistencia del gatillo derecho, tiene como un escalón
el del gatillo izquierdo está mejor, pero debería tener algo menos de resistencia
el gatillo derecho igualalo al izquierdo"

## Implementation Details
1. **Reduce Maximum Resistance:**
   - Reduced `end_strength` from `0.25` to `0.16` for both triggers, giving a subtler, softer touch towards the end of travel.
2. **Smooth Transition (Remove Step / Notch):**
   - Started the gradual slope smoothly with `start_position: 0.35` and `start_strength: 0.0`, eliminating any perceived mechanical step or notch when pulling the throttle.
3. **Equalize Right and Left Triggers:**
   - Both left (brake) and right (throttle) triggers now share the exact same smooth, gradual low-resistance profile (`start_position: 0.35`, `end_position: 1.0`, `start_strength: 0.0`, `end_strength: 0.16`).
   - Neither trigger has any vibration motor active on the triggers (`frequency: 0.0`).
