# Stronger Engine Braking

- **Date:** 2026-10-12
- **Topic:** The bike should slow down faster when the throttle is released.
- **Branch:** `claude/awesome-hamilton-hyvwrg`

## User Request

> "Al soltar el acelerador debería decelerar más rápido"

## Implementation Details

- Before: with throttle and brake released the bike lost a constant 3 m/s² (0.5 rolling + 2.5 engine braking) at any speed.
- On a real bike, engine braking grows with revs and the air drag with speed, so closing the throttle at speed slows it much more than at low speed.
- `src/bike/mod.rs`: new `BikeTuning::engine_braking_per_speed` (0.065 /s): when coasting, the engine braking is `2.5 + 0.065·v` m/s². Total deceleration when letting go: ~4 m/s² at 54 km/h, ~7.6 m/s² at 250 km/h (the brake is 28 m/s²). Nothing changes while the throttle or the brake is pressed.
- `docs/gameplay.md`: engine braking bullet.

## Verification & Testing

- New test `letting_go_of_the_throttle_slows_down_harder_the_faster` (15 m/s → 3.5–4.5 m/s² lost in the first second; 70 m/s → 7–8.5 m/s²; well under a third of the brake).
- `rolling_downhill_is_not_felt_as_acceleration` now uses an 80 % slope: its premise (gravity pushing harder than the engine brakes) no longer holds at 40 % with the stronger engine braking; what it checks is unchanged.
- Rival lap tests and flat-out lap tests still pass.
