# Ghost Island: The Small Props Seen by the Road, on Plinths and Over and Over

## 1. Plinths Under the Small Props, and Rows of Them (2026-10-16)

### 1.1 Context
- On the branch `m4-mini-macos`, off `8adb3ae`. Ghost Island's props were in place in `PROPS` (`src/circuits/ghost_island.rs`) but the small ones were hardly seen from the bike.

### 1.2 User Requests
> "estos elementos deberían verse junto a la carretera en Ghost Island" (with a picture of six props made with Meshy: the brazier, the wayside shrine, the guardian lion-dog, the bronze lantern, the memorial stone and the jizō).

> "estos elementos pequeños podrían estar sobre un soporte de piedra o de madera, para que se vean mejor"

> "los elementos pequeños, son susceptibles de ser repetidos con cierta frecuencia"

### 1.3 What Was Wrong
- The props stood on the ground at `WALL_DISTANCE + 1.3` m (12.8 m) from the centre line, just past the wall, which stands 1.1 m over the road (`WALL_HEIGHT`). A jizō is 1.10 m tall and a komainu or a brazier 1.80 m, so the wall hid them whole or nearly. Measured at each spot: the ground falls away from the road at some of them (the jizō at 5160 m stands 1.64 m under the road, its head 0.54 m *under* the road's level).
- Each prop had an opening of the shrine's vermilion fence written by hand in `walls` (a run of 14 m), which does not scale to props repeated along the lap, and one was missing (the stone lantern at 3300 m).

### 1.4 Implementation Details
- **A plinth under each small prop by the road** (`Plinth::Stone` or `Plinth::Wood`, `PropModel::plinth` in `src/world/scenery.rs`): stone under the jizō, komainu, memorial stone and the stone and bronze lanterns; planks under the brazier and the wayside shrine. The big ones (gates, keep, temple, maples, farmhouses, water wheel) stand on the ground as before, and so does every prop standing away from the road, where no wall hides it.
- **How high it stands** (`plinth_top`, `src/world/japan.rs`): its top is `WALL_HEIGHT - 0.5` m over the road beside it, so the prop is seen whole over the wall; never less than 0.3 m over its own ground (where the ground stands higher than the road) nor more than 2.0 m (where it falls away, so it is a plinth and not a tower).
- **How it is built** (`Plinths`): a battered block, square to the prop, as wide as the prop's base and 0.24 m more, sunk 0.4 m into the ground so it never floats on a slope. All the stone ones are one mesh and all the wooden ones another, with the walls' stone texture and the roadside's planks, so each kind draws in one go.
- **Rows of small props** (`PropRow`, `prop_row`, `Japan::rows`): the same prop every so many metres of a span, to one side, as a row of lamps is laid out. They stand as the ones put by hand do, on their plinths; there is none where another road runs, where the ground is under the sea, or where one of `PROPS` already stands (within its footprint and 2 m more). Ghost Island has ten rows: stone lanterns both sides of the castle street (every 55 m), along the coast road's inland side (110 m) and round the lake on the water's side (65 m); jizō over the susuki hills (240 m) and either side down through the bamboo (150 m, one side half a step after the other); braziers up the red ridge (90 m) and both sides of the castle ridge (85 m). About 60 props more.
- **The wall opens on its own** (`prop_fence`, used by `wall_style` in `src/world/road.rs`): where a small prop with a plinth stands by the road, the wall there is the shrine's low vermilion fence, 7 m either way, so that nothing hides it. The seventeen openings written by hand for props are gone from the circuit; the two for the stores, which stand on no plinth, stay, and so do the long runs (the shore before the great torii, the red ridge, the maple's fence, the lake).
- One place says where a row's props stand (`row_alongs`), so that the fence opens exactly where they are, the finish line crossed and all.

### 1.5 Verification & Testing
- New tests: every small prop by the road (those put by hand and those of the rows, more than 60 of them) stands on a plinth that is neither sunk nor a tower, its head over the wall, with the wall there opened into the fence; the rows fill the road without crowding what stands already (more than 50 props, none in the water, each clear of the roads).
- Seen on the M4 mini: at the start of the lap the stone walls now open into vermilion fence at each lantern, and the lantern stands on its stone plinth above the wall, where before the street showed bare walls.
- `cargo fmt --check` and `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` clean; `cargo test` 492 passed, 1 failed: `bike::ai::tests::a_very_hard_pack_races_without_wrecking_each_other`, which fails at `8adb3ae` without these changes too (see `RECORD/2026-10-16.m4-mini-follow-up.WIP.md`).

### 1.6 Still Open
- Whether the small props read well enough from the bike at speed, 12.8 m from the centre line: they may want to be nearer the road, or a little bigger.

## 2. The Kinds Mixed Along Each Stretch (2026-10-16)

### 2.1 User Request
> "veo muchos farolillos pero apenas hay otros objetos pequeños"

### 2.2 What Was Wrong
- Section 1's rows had one kind each, and the three longest stretches (the castle street both sides, the coast and the lake) were all stone lanterns: about 35 of them against no guardian, memorial stone or wayside shrine repeated at all.

### 2.3 Implementation Details
- **A row takes several kinds, each in turn** (`PropRow::models`, `row_steps`): the kind at each step is `models[k % models.len()]`, so a stretch is lined with lanterns, braziers, statues and stones one after another instead of one thing over and over. The wall opens for a step only where that step's kind stands on a plinth.
- **The twelve rows of Ghost Island** now read: the castle street, lanterns with braziers one side and lanterns with jizō the other, half a step apart so they do not face each other in pairs; the coast's inland side, jizō, lanterns and memorial stones; the susuki hills, jizō, wayside shrines and stones; the lake's water side, stone and bronze lanterns; up the red ridge, braziers and stones, with guardians in twos (two rows of the same step either side, as guardians come in pairs); down the castle ridge, braziers with guardians one side and braziers with shrines the other; and through the bamboo, jizō with a shrine one side and jizō with a stone the other.
- **None where its plinth would not show it** (`PLINTH_DROP`, 1.6 m): where the ground beside the road falls further than that under it, a row puts no prop, since not even a plinth of [`PLINTH_MOST`] would lift it over the wall (a brazier on the castle ridge stood in such an embankment). The props put by hand stand where they are put.
- The props of the rows are now 57 in all: 13 jizō, 12 stone lanterns, 8 guardians, 7 braziers, 7 memorial stones, 6 wayside shrines and 4 bronze lanterns.

### 2.4 Verification & Testing
- The rows' test now tallies the kinds: at least six of them, each at least three times and none of them more than two fifths of the row's props.
- Seen on the M4 mini: along the castle street the lanterns, braziers and jizō follow one another on their plinths, each behind its opening of vermilion fence.
- `cargo fmt --check` and `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` clean; `cargo test` 492 passed, 1 failed (the pre-existing `a_very_hard_pack_races_without_wrecking_each_other`).

### 2.5 Still Open
- The fence opens by where a row's steps fall, which does not know about the ground: where a step was left out for a drop, for another road or for the sea, a short stretch of vermilion fence stands with nothing behind it.

## 3. Bigger Props, Facing the Riders, and the Fence Off the Junctions (2026-10-16)

> "esta construcción está escondida, reubícala" (a picture of the temple hall, half hidden behind the corridor's pillars and its trees).

> "quita las vallas que están en medio de la carretera" (a picture of the shrine's vermilion fence standing across the asphalt where the lap splits).

### 3.2 Implementation Details
- **How big a kind stands** (`size`, `src/world/japan.rs`): a prop's mesh is now scaled by its kind as well as by its spot (`Prop::scaled`), so a kind grows everywhere, in the rows too. The wayside shrine and the torii of logs stand 1.8 times the size they were made; everything else as before.
- **Facing the riders** (`Prop::facing_riders`, `RIDERS_BACK` = 110 m): a prop by the road faces the road beside it, as before, or, asked to, the road 110 m back, where the riders are coming up at it. The torii of logs on the susuki hills now reads as a gate against the sky instead of two posts edge on.
- **Clear of the wall** (`stand`): a prop by the road is nudged out so that it never overhangs the wall, however big its kind stands (the shrine of a row, at 1.8, did).
- **The temple and its pagoda before the corridor of torii**: they stood at 3868 m and 3918 m, 41 and 43 m to the right, inside the corridor, where its gates and their trees hid them. The hall now stands on the bank to the left at 3640 m, 40 m out, where the ground is already 2.6 m over the road, and the pagoda by the road to the right at 3780 m, 34 m out, on a terrace 3.5 m over the road (`PAGODA_GROUND`, radius 10 m, skirt 10 m, which ends 14 m from the centre line, clear of the road). Both face the riders coming up the red ridge at them.
- **No railing across another road** (`railing`, `src/world/road.rs`): a wall is not drawn where another road runs over its line (a junction), but a railing was, and the fences the props now open in the wall fall at the castle split, where the lap crosses itself. A railing's post is set only where its own line is clear of another road, and no rail is drawn to a post that is not there; the posts beside such a place go too, so the fence stops short of the junction instead of on its edge. The wall's own check is now `at_a_junction`, which says the same thing for the stretch between two points.

### 3.3 Verification & Testing
- New tests: a prop that faces the riders looks down the road they come up (nearer their way than the road's beside it, and within 0.1 rad of it); no railing of Ghost Island stands on another road (every point of the fence's mesh, built as the world builds it).
- The plinth and row tests now measure each prop at the size its kind stands and where it stands (`stand`, `reach_of`).
- `cargo fmt --check` and `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` clean; `cargo test` 494 passed, 1 failed (the pre-existing `a_very_hard_pack_races_without_wrecking_each_other`).
- Seen on the M4 mini, riding Ghost Island: the torii of logs stands tall on the susuki hills, face on to the road with its rope and streamers, where it was two posts edge on; the pagoda stands by the road on its terrace before the corridor of torii, against the volcano; and from the track menu's view of the island, the temple hall stands in the open on the left of the road and the pagoda on the right, both before the gates, with the fences following the edges of the fork instead of crossing it.

## 4. Starting Part Way Round the Lap (2026-10-16)

### 4.1 User Request
> "añade el atajo de depuración para empezar en un kilometraje dado"

(After a session of riding blind with held keys to reach the stretches the pictures were about: the bike falls over and slides, so each spot took several minutes of sweeping.)

### 4.2 Implementation Details
- **`src/debug_start.rs`**: the game started with `--from <metres>` (`--from=3700` too) or with `MOTORUST_FROM` in the environment says how far along the lap to start; read once, and ignored where it is missing or is not a distance (negative, infinite or not a number).
- **`Track::grid_slot_at`**: grid slot `slot` with the grid `along` metres along the lap instead of behind the finish line, in the formation it always has (the pole at the distance asked, three to a row staggered, the rows behind); the distance wraps round the lap.
- **`start_pose` (`src/bike/mod.rs`)**: where a bike starts, on the grid or where `--from` says. Both the grid at the start of a race and the way back to it after a restart go through it, so the rivals start there too and a restart puts everyone back where they began.
- Only for development: the lap and the times are counted from the finish line as ever, so the first lap is short. Written up in `docs/building.md`.

### 4.3 Verification & Testing
- New tests: the distance is read from the command line or the environment (the command line first), and nothing is read without one; the grid can be moved along the lap keeping its formation (the pole just behind the distance asked, every bike on the asphalt and clear of the others, and moved by a whole lap it is the grid as it always was).
- Seen on the M4 mini: `--from 3700` on Ghost Island starts facing the corridor of torii with the pagoda on its terrace to the right, and `--from 3560` starts with the temple hall ahead on the bank to the left, both as section 3 placed them.
