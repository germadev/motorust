# CI: clippy 1.99 asks for `as_chunks`

- **Date:** 2026-10-13
- **Branch:** `claude/hopeful-bell-eknth2` (PR germadev/motorust#11, after commit `3110563`)

## 1. User Request
- The PR's CI failed at `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings`: clippy 1.99's new lint `chunks_exact_to_as_chunks` flags `.chunks_exact(3)` in `src/bike/model/tyre.rs`, which keeps the tyre's triangles near the hub. The local toolchain (1.97) does not have the lint.

## 2. Implementation Details
- `src/bike/model/tyre.rs`: the triangles are now taken with `as_chunks::<3>().0` (arrays of three indices; any remainder is dropped, as with `chunks_exact`). Same behaviour.

## 3. Verification & Testing
- Installed the 1.99 toolchain with clippy (as CI uses) and ran `cargo +1.99 clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings`: clean over the whole crate. `cargo fmt --check` clean; the tyre tests pass.
