# 2026-11-10 — Puerto Neón: an anti-gravity circuit, and the hover bikes

Branch `claude/youthful-davinci-0c70wu`, with the engine's branch of the same
name (`make test ENGINE=same`): the engine's part is recorded there
(`RECORD/2026-11-10.hover-bikes.WIP.md`).

## User request

Three GLBs of hover bikes made with Meshy (blue and white, yellow, and red,
blue and white: the Shogun TZ's, the Azura RZ's and the Falcon KRZ's
colours) and "a circuit in homage to Wipeout". As the work went on:

- the hover bikes float, bobbing a little up and down when stopped, and the
  wheelie's button drifts in the bends instead;
- which props to ask Meshy for, as a markdown with names, sizes, triangles
  and prompts; the five bikes missing will be made in four days;
- a start gate made with Meshy from that list (it fits; the number of red
  lights of the start is configurable, so the model's five are decoration);
- stretches as tubes where the bikes can ride round and upside down, and
  bends banked as a bobsleigh run's (the tubes: a second stage, not yet in);
- the lap should cross itself a couple of times;
- the circuit's song, "Apex Velocity".

## Implementation so far

- **A homage to the genre, not a copy**: its own layout, names and look;
  no track, team, logo or lettering of any game.
- `tools/import_hoverbike.py` (new): a hover bike's GLB into
  `assets/models/NAME/`: `bike.bin` (one part, `chassis`, in the game's axes,
  2 m long, floating 0.22 m over the ground), `base_color.jpg`,
  `metallic_roughness.jpg`, a flat `normal.jpg`, `emissive.jpg` (the bright
  cyan and blue of its lower part: pads and thrusters, found by hue and by
  the height of the surface each texel paints) and `model.ron` (pads as
  axles, grips, pegs, seat, `hover`, `rider`, `emissive`).
- `assets/models/shogun_hover`, `azura_hover`, `falcon_hover`: the three
  imported, each carrying the rider of the bike it is the hover version of.
- `assets/bikes/roster.ron`: each bike's `hover`. The five with none of
  their own yet ride the nearest in colour (Legend 88 and Stiletto V4 the
  Falcon's, Kraken RS and Cobra XT the Azura's, Ronin YS the Shogun's).
- `assets/circuits/puerto_neon.ron` (new, tenth in the menu, `hover: true`):
  a chain of three loops, 3.94 km, that crosses itself twice, each crossing
  a tunnel under a hill the lap comes back over (18 and 22 m up, over 3.5
  and 6 m); its east loop climbs over the sea on a bridge to 28 m. First
  draft of its scenery, with the engine's own pieces.
- `assets/music/apex_velocity.mp3`: its song (178.1 s, -17.5 LUFS above
  500 Hz, measured as `music.ron` says).
- `docs/puerto-neon-props.md`: the props to make with Meshy.

## 2026-11-10 — The first props made with Meshy, and the tunnel's own look

### User request

Sixteen GLBs made from `docs/puerto-neon-props.md`, five at a time (the
start gate, three towers, the grandstand, the screen on its mast, two
airships, the camera drone, the harbour crane, the fusion plant, the
control tower, the container ship, the maglev train, two hover cars), all
attached with the song to the release `Assets-NeonPort`. "As always, what
is made with Meshy must be well in view from the road." The number of the
start's red lights is configurable (the gate's five are decoration).

### Implementation

- `tools/import_neon.py` (new): runs `tools/import_props.py`'s code with
  its own sizes, into `assets/models/neon/` (`props.bin`, and `color.jpg`,
  1024 pixels a prop in a grid of four). Twelve props in: `start_gate`
  (38 m across, so that its legs, which lean in, are clear of the barriers),
  `tower_spire` (200 m), `tower_stepped` (150 m), `tower_twin` (120 m),
  `screen_mast` (14 m), `grandstand` (40 m long), `harbour_crane` (60 m),
  `control_tower` (45 m), `fusion_plant` (35 m), `container_ship` (180 m),
  `airship_cobalt` and `airship_neon` (60 m).
- `src/game/neon.rs` (new): the theme `"neon"`, which places them as the
  circuit's file says (`props`), lit a little of their own in their own
  colours so that their windows and strips show at night; the ship afloat,
  the airships 110 m up.
- `assets/circuits/puerto_neon.ron`: the gate over the line (in place of
  the engine's arch), a grandstand either side of the grid, the control
  tower by it, six screens by the road facing the riders, twelve towers
  inside the loops and round the lap, the power plant, two cranes and the
  ship by the skyway, an airship over each end.
- The tunnels are a race track's (the engine's branch): dark panels and
  rings of cyan light.
- Not in yet: the camera drone, the maglev train and the two hover cars,
  which have to move or hover (code of their own).

### Verification

- Seen under Xvfb all round the lap: the towers stand lit against the
