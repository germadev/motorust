# Wall Collisions: No More Going Through, and Falls on Hard Hits

- **Date:** 2026-10-12
- **Topic:** Fix bikes (and riders) visibly going through the walls; riders go down when they hit a wall hard, with an arcade tolerance.
- **Branch:** `claude/awesome-hamilton-hyvwrg`

## User Request

> "Haz también que caiga al chocar con los muros, en función de cómo choque, le dejaría cierta tolerancia, ya que se trata de un juego arcade
> Arregla las colisiones con los muros, a veces se atraviesan"

## Diagnosis

- Track geometry is fine: probing both circuits, the tightest corner radius is 29 m (above the 11.5 m wall distance, so the inner wall never folds) and different sections are never closer than 55 m (walls never overlap).
- `hit_walls` treated the bike as a 0.45 m circle at its centre, but the bike is ~2 m long: hitting a wall at an angle let the front wheel go up to ~0.6 m into it, and a rider leaning towards a wall put shoulders and helmet through it.
- `separate_bikes` pushed bikes apart after the walls had been checked, so a bike pushed against a wall by another ended inside it.
- A fallen bike sliding (a 2 m bike lying on its side) and a rider lying on the ground (1.6 m) were also kept off the walls only as small circles at their centres.

## Implementation Details

- `src/bike/mod.rs`:
  - `footprint(state)`: the parts of a bike that can touch a wall as circles — the two ends (±0.6 m, 0.45 m radius, as wide as before so riding upright along a wall is unchanged) and the rider's upper body leaning out to the side (`0.9·sin(lean)`, 0.25 m radius), which for a fallen bike is the top of the bike lying on that side.
  - `wall_contact(track, center, parts)`: the deepest part into a wall (normal and depth); one `nearest` look at the centre is enough when far from the walls, so the cost only rises next to a wall.
  - `hit_walls` now pushes the bike out by the deepest part and returns a `WallHit` (normal, velocity before, speed into the wall, contact point). Speed loss and aligning with the wall are unchanged.
  - `advance_hitting_walls` returns the hit; `advance` (now test-only) wraps it.
  - `separate_bikes` keeps any bike moved by a contact out of the walls.
  - Haptics refactor: `CollisionImpact::record` stores a new hit (`unfelt`), and a new `rumble_controllers` system sends the rumble, for bike and wall hits alike. Wall bumps above 2 m/s are felt; scrapes are not.
- `src/bike/crash.rs`:
  - `WALL_FALL = 10 m/s`: a rider rides out going into a wall at up to ~36 km/h (straight into it at that speed, or a ~12° hit at 180 km/h). A real rider would go down with much less; this is the arcade tolerance asked for.
  - `fall_against_wall`: the wall stops the velocity into it (20 % bounce) at the contact point, so an angled hit spins the bike (capped at 5 rad/s); the rider keeps the velocity along the wall and is thrown on.
  - The sliding bike and the thrown rider are kept off the walls with their real footprints (bike: same `footprint`; rider: hips plus feet ahead and head behind as they are laid back).
- Docs: `docs/gameplay.md` (walls).
