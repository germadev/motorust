## 2. Implementation Details
- **Images** (`docs/images/`), taken in game under Xvfb with Mesa's software Vulkan driver (lavapipe) at 1280 × 720, with the top 36 px cropped off to the 1280 × 684 of the other screenshots (which also drops the FPS counter), saved as progressive JPEGs (150–186 KB):
  - `welcome.jpg` (replaced): the new main menu (the 1P/2P cards and the Options and Exit game banners) over the camera's flyby of the Turbo Bay seafront, with the sea, the palms and the new sky. The old one still showed the first menu ("Comenzar / Opciones / Salir").
  - `track-bahia.jpg`: the pack leaning through a curve between a stone wall and a rocky hill, towards a checkpoint arch.
  - `track-turbo-bay.jpg`: bikes leaning through a rock cutting, past the MOTORUST and RACING billboards, towards a checkpoint arch.
  - `track-loop-island.jpg`: two bikes leaning between the checkerboard hills, with a waterfall ahead.
- **How**: a temporary harness, never committed (the patch was kept outside the repository): with an environment variable set, the AI also rides the player's bike (taking Loop Island's branch too) from the moment the race is set up, and the race HUD is hidden. The game was driven with `xdotool` to each track, and 40 frames were grabbed every 4 s with ImageMagick; the shots were picked from contact sheets.
- **`README.md`**: the main menu stays as the first image. The feature list now points to a new **Tracks** section, with a short description and the image of each track and a link to `docs/tracks.md`. The Loop Island loop screenshot (with the old photographed sky) is left to `docs/tracks.md`. Two stale lines fixed: the menus are in Spanish or English (not only Spanish), and two-player split screen is listed among the features.
