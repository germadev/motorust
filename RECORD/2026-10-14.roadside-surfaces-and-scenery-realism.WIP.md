## 3. Implementation Details

### 3.1 What each shoulder is made of (`src/track.rs`, `src/world/shoulders.rs`)
- `Surface` grew from `Asphalt | Grass` to `Asphalt | Grass | Dirt(Dust) | Paved`, with `Surface::is_rough()` (grass or dirt) and `Dust` (`Sand`, `Earth`, `Desert`, `Slate`), which gives the dust its colour.
- `Track` keeps, per road and per point of its centre line, what the shoulder is on the left and on the right (`Track::set_shoulders`, like `set_edges`); `surface_beside` returns it past the curbs (grass when nothing was set, as before, so the pure tests keep their behaviour).
- New `world/shoulders.rs` works it out when a circuit is prepared (off the main thread, next to the edges): the lap's tunnels (concrete walkways) and bridges (concrete or plank decks) are `Paved`; a mine's excavation uses the stone of each hall (`cavern::stone_at`, extracted from `Cave::stone`): planks in the timbered galleries are `Paved`, red sandstone gravel, black slate gravel and beaten clay are `Dirt`; elsewhere it is the terrain *as drawn* (`Terrain::ground_drawn`, the same triangle split and classification as the terrain meshes): grass, sand (`Dirt(Sand)`), rock, and in the Gold Mine's desert the dusty red earth (`Dirt(Desert)`). Three looks across the shoulder (0.6, 1.6, 2.6 m past the curb), majority wins, ties to the nearest.
- `world/mod.rs`: `TrackEdges` became `Roadsides`, storing a `Roadside { edges, shoulders }` per circuit and applying both to the `Track`.

### 3.2 Physics
- `bike::step`: the grass bite (`state.grass`, top speed down to `grass_max_speed`) now follows `ground.surface.is_rough()`: dirt slows the bike like grass; concrete and planks do not.
- `crash::drag_factor`: concrete and planks drag a sliding bike or rider like asphalt, dirt like grass.

### 3.3 Grass blades and dust (`src/bike/grass_kickup.rs`)
- Blades and clumps fly only off `Surface::Grass`.
- On `Surface::Dirt(dust)`, puffs of dust (`DustPuff`): four soft discs (three standing at 60°, one flat) with a procedural billowing texture, every normal up so they are lit like the ground, plus a little emissive so they read in shade. They start dragged along by the bike's wake (`DUST_CARRY`), rise, swell and thin out; transparency steps through 8 shared materials per kind of dust (`DUST_LEVELS`), so they still batch. No shadows. Cleared on race restart with the blades.
- Paved and asphalt throw nothing.

### 3.4 Grass (`src/world/foliage.rs`, `src/world/tufts.rs`, `src/world/textures.rs`)
- The grass texture is now a lawn: 24 000 blades of several greens (and a few dry ones) painted over the darkened grass photo, combed in clumps, colour-corrected to the photo's average so the tracks keep their tone and gain detail.
- Tufts of grass stand on the grass shoulders: crossed alpha-masked cards of blades (procedural texture), tinted with the grass colour and the terrain's own tint where they grow, normals up, gathered in meshes of 48 points of centre line for culling; never on sand, earth, concrete, another road's asphalt, or up the earth before a rock face.
- Alpha-masked textures keep their coverage down the mipmaps (`foliage::keep_coverage`), so leaves and tufts do not vanish in the distance.

### 3.5 Walls (`src/world/road.rs`, `src/world/textures.rs`)
- Walls are swept cross-sections instead of boxes: stone walls lean back a little under a coping that juts out; concrete is a New Jersey barrier with dark joints every 4 m; hedges are rounded and lumpy with smooth normals; checkerboard earth and timber stay blocks.
- Vertex tints: grime at the foot, slow changes of tone along the wall, lighter coping and moss low down on stone, shade inside hedges.
- The stone texture is now a procedural rubble wall (staggered Voronoi stones squashed into courses, rounded and lit edges, lime mortar, lichen, the photo's fine grain) that repeats every 9.6 m instead of every 2.4 m.

### 3.6 Trees (`src/world/props.rs`)
- Each crown is a smaller, darker solid core (ico 2 instead of 3) covered by alpha-masked cards of leaf clusters or pine needles (procedural atlas), scattered over the crown's own shape, turned every way, with normals pointing out of the crown as an ellipsoid's so they are lit as a volume. Each tree is two entities (wood and core, leaves), both shared per variant so they instance.

### 3.7 Clouds (`src/world/sky.rs`)
- Ten shapes of five kinds (`CloudKind`): cumulus, towering cumulus, stratocumulus rolls, clusters of small cumulus and torn fractus. Each heap has its own base, uneven, wavy and curling up at its ends, cut off softly; the underside shading comes from the run of cloud below each point instead of a fixed line. 22 clouds, sized and placed by kind.
