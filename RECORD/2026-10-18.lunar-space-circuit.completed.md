# 2026-10-18 — Lunar Space Circuit (`Moon Base` / `Base Lunar`)

## Context & User Request

The user requested a new space-themed circuit (space station, lunar landscape, etc.). After clarifying preferences with the user:
- **Setting:** Lunar base + space station (`Base Lunar` / `Moon Base`): grey cratered lunar regolith surface, pressurized space station tunnel/corridor, biodomes, lunar rovers, radio dishes/antennas, solar arrays, launchpad/rocket, and a starry black space sky with Earth hanging above the horizon.
- **Track Layout & Scale:** Large circuit (~4.5–5.0 km) featuring a vertical loop branch, an alternative fork route through a crater field, a two-level crossing (over the pressurized station tunnel), a narrow orbital bridge/causeway, banked turns, and straightaway crests where bikes jump.
- **Physics:** Standard arcade physics and gravity consistent with the rest of the circuits.
## Implementation Details

1. **Space Sky & Earth (`src/world/sky.rs`, `src/world/scenery.rs`):**
   - Added `SkyStyle::Space` with deep black-blue zenith, subtle horizon glow matching the distance fog, crisp high-angle sunlight, cool blue-white earthshine in shadows, and zero cloud alpha (`Color::srgba(..., 0.0)`).
   - Implemented `stars_mesh()` (260 deterministic vertex-coloured diamond star quads across the upper sky dome) and `earth_image()` (procedural 128×128 mipmapped texture showing deep blue oceans, fractal continents, polar ice caps, atmospheric limb glow, swirling clouds, and a day-night terminator).
   - Added `space_sky` entity to `SkyParts` and updated `light_the_sky` to toggle starfield/Earth visibility on `SkyStyle::Space` tracks and scale cloud layer alpha by `palette.clouds.alpha()`.
   - Extended `the_sky_meets_the_fog_at_the_horizon` unit test to verify `SkyStyle::Space`.

2. **Lunar Terrain, Shoulders, Mirrors & Station Tunnels (`src/world/terrain.rs`, `src/world/shoulders.rs`, `src/world/mirror.rs`, `src/world/structures.rs`):**
   - Added `Soil::Lunar` to `scenery::Soil`.
   - Implemented `lunar_vertex_color` in `src/world/terrain.rs` to paint cratered silver-grey regolith and dark basaltic highland rock using multi-octave fractal noise, and routed `Soil::Lunar` through `ground_color`, `band`, `meshes`, and `spawn_terrain` (using the rock texture with high roughness and zero grass tufts).
   - Mapped `(Soil::Lunar, _) => Surface::Dirt(Dust::Slate)` in `src/world/shoulders.rs` so off-road riding kicks up slate-grey lunar dust.
   - Mapped `Soil::Lunar` in `src/world/mirror.rs` and `src/world/structures.rs` so tunnels under `Soil::Lunar` use dark grey-blue lunar rock outside, metallic blue-white station lining inside, and cool cyan-white ceiling light strips and light pools.

3. **Lunar Base & Space Station Props (`src/world/space.rs`, `src/world/props.rs`, `src/world/mod.rs`):**
   - Created `src/world/space.rs` with GPU-instanced procedural meshes sharing 3 materials (`hull`, `glow`, `beacon`):
     - `Biodome`: 12-sided geodesic habitat domes with silver hex struts, tinted glass facets, glowing cyan viewport rings, and airlock tunnels facing the road.
     - `Rover`: 6-wheeled lunar rovers with gold-foil chassis, 6 bogie wheels, high-gain dish antenna, mast camera, and glowing headlights (all instances share one hull mesh and one headlight mesh).
     - `RadioDish`: Parabolic deep-space radio dishes on braced pedestals with tripod feed horns and red beacon tips, animated by `turn_dishes` via the `DishSweep` component.
     - `Rocket`: Multi-stage Saturn-style orbital rockets on concrete launchpads with swept fins and red umbilical launch gantry towers.
     - `StationArch`: Slanted metallic structural arches spanning the road with overhead cyan light strips.
     - `solar_arrays`: Tilted blue photovoltaic solar-panel wings on gold-foil tracking masts along `Row` spans.
     - `LunarBoulders`: 3 faceted basalt boulder mesh variants scattered deterministically clear of all roads and structures.
   - Added unit test `biodome_rover_dish_rocket_and_boulder_meshes_have_geometry`.

4. **New Circuit: `Base Lunar` / `Moon Base` (`src/circuits/moon_base.rs`, `src/circuits/mod.rs`, `src/track.rs`):**
   - Created `src/circuits/moon_base.rs` (`4600.2 m` lap) and registered `MOON_BASE` in `CIRCUITS` (`[Circuit; 6]`).
   - Designed a 60-control-point figure-of-eight layout featuring:
     - Start straight with a jump crest (`55 m`) and broad top (`195 m`) where the orbital `VerticalLoop` branch (`110..637 m`, 16 m radius) forks off to the right.
     - Pressurized Space Station Tunnel (`800..1020 m`) through the central `STATION_MASSIF` (`climbed_massif(550.0, -430.0, 210.0, 32.0)`).
     - Eastern mare rim with a `BridgeStyle::Truss` bridge (`1470..1590 m`) over a deep lunar rille and a straightaway jump crest (`1715 m`).
     - Banked 180-degree sweep around Tycho Crater (`1920..2290 m`).
     - 2-way Crater Split (`2340.2..2690.3 m`) where the left branch climbs over a 4.5 m crater-rim hump (`CRATER_LEFT`, `CRATER_LEFT_RISE`).
     - Station Ridge climb crossing north-west 31 m directly above the Space Station Tunnel (`2970..3140 m`), followed by the western straightaway jump crest (`4095 m`).
   - Added unit test `the_lap_goes_through_the_space_station_tunnel_and_then_over_it` in `src/circuits/moon_base.rs`, and scoped the loop-branch-specific tests in `src/track.rs` (`branches_are_rideable_and_clear_of_the_lap`, `branches_climb_and_drop_smoothly_on_their_own`) to `track.loops` so circuits combining both `loops` and `forks` are tested cleanly by both the loop tests and the fork tests.
   - Updated `docs/tracks.md` and `docs/architecture.md`.
