### 1.3 Measuring Stick: Ghost Island
5.97 km; a figure of eight crossing the castle hill twice (through it, then over it); two splits; grades up to 12.4 % (the game's limit is 13 %); a run of tight esses (bends of about 40 m). A rival must lap in under 90 s (Ghost Island takes about 87 s), so about 6 km is again the ceiling.

A lap is the Fellowship's journey from the Shire to the Falls of Rauros and, past the finish, home again (*There and Back Again*). Each stretch has one dominant colour, as at Ghost Island. About 5.9 km.

- **The figure of eight**: the lap crosses the Misty Mountains twice, over them at the Redhorn Pass (stretch 7) and through them by Moria (stretch 8), as the Fellowship tried Caradhras first and then went under it; the pass runs some 30–40 m over the halls.
- **Steep and winding** (the author's taste, as at Ghost Island): up to 12 % up and down Weathertop, down Rivendell's valley and up Caradhras; tight esses down into Rivendell; hairpins up Caradhras; crests where the bikes fly only on straights (the start, the top of Weathertop, the causeway).
- **Light (to be chosen)**: recommended a late-summer afternoon in the Shire, a blue sky with big white clouds (ref 12), the sun warm but higher than Ghost Island's golden hour, haze and spray in the valleys; Moria is dark whatever the sky. The other option: Rivendell's pink-golden light (ref 8).

### 2.2 To Be Modelled with Meshy (by the author)
Requirements as before: one mesh per GLB, Y up, no ground plane or base, no text, runes or drawings on the stone (painted in code), scale free (the importer sets heights); trees 400–600 triangles; landmarks 3–8k; repeated props 300–1.5k. Text prompts, not image-to-3D from the references.
- **Trees** (into the tree pack, whose atlas grows from 4 × 4 to 5 × 5): English oak, weeping willow, silver birch, golden beech in autumn, holly, mallorn (Ginkgo, Momiji, Broadleaf, Tall, Poplar and the fir are reused).
- **Hobbiton and the Shire**: hobbit hole fronts in four colours (the front only, its back flat, to be set into a bank), Bag End, brick chimney stack, the party tree, the great tree over Bag End, the mill (its wheel apart, to turn), the inn by the bridge, Gandalf's cart; mailbox, wheelbarrow with pumpkins, pile of pumpkins, garden table and bench, straw beehives, log pile, garden gate, scarecrow, hay bales.
- **Bree and Weathertop**: the Prancing Pony (its sign a pony, no words), two half-timbered houses, the gate in the palisade (sized by its opening, as the torii), a lantern post, barrels and crates; the ring of ruins of Amon Sûl, a broken column and a fallen arch; the three stone trolls.
- **Rivendell**: the Last Homely House, the domed gazebo, an elven tower, an elven lamp post.
- **Moria, Lothlórien and the Anduin**: the Doors of Durin (a carved doorway with two pillars, plain), Balin's tomb, a flet round a mallorn's trunk, Galadriel's mirror, two kings of the Argonath (each its own, left hand raised), an elven boat with a swan's prow, the seat of Amon Hen.
- The props of this track in a pack of their own, loaded only for it (Ghost Island's pack fills a 5 × 5 atlas).
