### 3.2 Implementation Details
- **The art style**, one for every model so the track holds together, as a block after each prompt: *"Realistic fantasy film set style, hand-built weathered materials, muted natural colours, even lighting, no baked shadows. Single isolated object, no base, no ground, no text."* Each prompt carries its stretch's own materials and colours: the Shire's ochre plaster, red brick, weathered oak and faded paint; Bree's grey fieldstone, dark timber and cream plaster; the grey granite with moss and lichen of the ruins, the trolls and the Argonath; Rivendell's ivory stone, silver-grey wood and Art Nouveau curves; Moria's dark granite and angular edges; Lothlórien's silver bark and pale gold. - **As the boats do**: the road runs down the middle of the Anduin on a low stone causeway, about 1.5 m over the water, with a low parapet (as Ghost Island's boardwalk, its fence 0.75 m), so the river shows on both sides; the two kings stand on rock ledges at the water's edge either side, their feet at the water, about 45 m from the road's centre line (a gap of about 90 m between them, about one and a half times their height, as in the still), facing upstream towards the riders coming, both with the left hand raised.
- **The shot**: the gorge winds down to them in esses between cliffs about as high as the kings, and then the road runs straight at the gap for some 350 m, so the two kings frame the road for several seconds, with the spray of a waterfall between them beyond (Rauros, as in the still), before passing between them; past them the gorge opens onto the lake.
- **Meshy**: the prompts in section 3 already fit: each king is a statue alone, facing Meshy's front (+Z, the importer's front, turned to the riders), with no rock, plinth or water; the ledges, cliffs, river and spray are built in code.
