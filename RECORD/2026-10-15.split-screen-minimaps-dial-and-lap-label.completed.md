# Split-Screen Minimaps, the Dial's Lit Figure and the Lap Label (2026-10-15)

## 1. User Requests
> "En el modo 2P, podría un mini mapa para cada jugador, por legibilidad." Then: "Los mini mapa para 2P, los pondría abajo a la izquierda de la pantalla de cada uno."

> "En el velocímetro, iluminaría en azul o rojo el valor destacado, según corresponda, con text shadow blanco." Then: "Lo del velocímetro, me refería a la cifra del dial, no a la del centro. Y la sombra blanca creo que no haría falta, simplemente azul, y rojo vivo para las cifras >= 300."

> "En lugar de 'Esta vuelta', pondría 'Actual'."

## 2. Implementation Details
- **A minimap for each player** (`src/minimap.rs`): with two players, two maps of 160 px (190 with one), each at the bottom left of its player's half (over the bottom of player 1's half and the bottom of the screen, 16 px in), instead of one shared map on the line between the halves. `HudMap { viewer, size }`; each dot is a dot of a bike on one map (`Dot { bike, map }`). How a dot is drawn on a map is `dot_style`: on each player's map their own dot is the biggest (19 px, white border, their number) and over the rest, the other player's a little smaller under it (15 px, numbered too), the rivals' small (9 px); with one player, theirs is 14 px with a white border, as before. The numbers are sized with their dot.
- **The dial's lit figure** (`src/speedometer.rs`): the figure of the scale the pointer is at lights up true blue (`#3a9bff`), or bright red (`#ff241c`) from 300 km/h, where it was a pale blue (`#a8e0ff`) and a pale red (`#ff6a5c`); its halo keeps its hues, at 0.9 of the strength it had at 1.5 (`GLOW_STRENGTH`), so that the figure shows its colour rather than a white core in a glow. The candidates were rendered side by side over the dial's dark face to choose (a probe test, not kept). A first reading of the request lit the speed in the middle instead, with a white shadow; it was undone at the user's word, and the middle stays white.
- **The lap label** (`src/race/hud.rs`): the time of the lap under way reads "ACTUAL 0:31.2" ("CURRENT 0:31.2" in English), not "ESTA VUELTA" / "THIS LAP".
- Docs: `docs/gameplay.md` (HUD table: speedometer, minimap, the lap label), `docs/architecture.md` (minimap and speedometer rows).
