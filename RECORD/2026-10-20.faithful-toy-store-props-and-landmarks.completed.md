## 3. Implementation Details

1. **Material Color Binding Fix (`spawn_block_canyon_sector`):**
   - Switched from `&plastic_red` to `&toy_vertex_mat` (`base_color: Color::WHITE`), ensuring vertex colors (primary red, blue, yellow, green) are rendered at full, vibrant intensity without being crushed by red tinting.

2. **Hot Wheels Sector Enhancements (`spawn_hot_wheels_sector`):**
   - **Hot Wheels Entrance Gantry Arch (`along = 1435.0`):** Twin blue tubular steel support pylons (10.5 m height), overhead crossbar, and the iconic flaming Hot Wheels header billboard in fiery orange and bright yellow.
   - **Double Vertical Loop-the-Loop Landmark (`(560.0, 0.0, -95.0)`):** Two side-by-side vertical 360-degree spiral looping tracks (radius 16.5 m, 33 m total height) in vivid orange plastic with blue raised guide rails, heavy blue tubular A-frame support legs, and cross connecting struts.

3. **Train Crossing Sector Enhancements (`spawn_toy_train`):**
   - **Complete Train Composition:** Added black coal tender with coal mound, bright red passenger coach with yellow window band and cream roof, and bright green caboose with red observation cupola and red/black wheels behind the steam locomotive.
   - **Turned-Wood Trees:** Added 6 classic turned-wood toy trees with natural wood trunks and 3 stacked conical bright green foliage tiers arranged along the railway baseboard.
   - **Level Crossing Gates ("Paso a Nivel"):** Added two red-and-white striped level crossing barrier gates at the entry and exit road crossings, featuring black-and-white striped stanchion poles, crossbuck X warning signs ("PASO A NIVEL"), dual red flashing signal lamp boxes, counterweight blocks, and 10 m striped horizontal boom gates.

4. **Curva Peluches Enhancements (`spawn_teddy_bear`):**
