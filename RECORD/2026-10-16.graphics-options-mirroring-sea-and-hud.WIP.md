### 1.3 Implementation Details
- **The place, bigger** (`src/race/hud.rs`): the place is drawn at 64 px (`PLACE_SIZE`, was 40, the lap's size), the lap stays at 40 (`LAP_SIZE`), the totals and words at 24, the totals still sitting on the figures' baseline (`after`). Against the clock, where no place is shown, the place's row is not made at all, so it leaves no room above the lap.
- **The name over the place, after the race** (`src/race/hud.rs`): once the race is over for the players the texts at the top left are emptied (the results say it all) and the name of the bike the camera watches come in takes their place. They were emptied, but stayed on the screen: each is drawn from its spans (`show_figures`), and with its last span gone the text has no `Children` left whose change says it must be laid out again (Bevy removes the component, which `Changed<Children>` does not see), so the interface went on drawing what it said: "8º / 8" and "LAP 1 / 1" under "Legend 88 · 2º". The same left the lap's time up after crossing the line. Reproduced on lavapipe (a one-lap race on autopilot, screenshots once the results were up); now, when a text loses its last span, the text itself is marked changed.
- **The sea that mirrors** (`src/world/mirror.rs`, `src/world/mirror.wgsl`, `src/world/sea.rs`, `src/world/mod.rs`): the lake's water (`LakeMaterial`, `lake.wgsl`, in `dam.rs`) became the mirroring water of the whole game, `MirrorMaterial` in a module of its own (`lake.wgsl` renamed `mirror.wgsl`), with the map of what it mirrors worked out for any water (`surroundings_map`, given a `Reach`: a box, a texel, the water's level and how far a line of sight is followed): the ground by what it is, the roads lying on it, and every dam's crest; under the water only its height (nothing is ever met there, nor its colour seen), and nothing at all where it says no line of sight ever gets; on several threads (`terrain::on_cores`, now `pub(super)`), as many as the circuit's preparation has. It also gives the highest point of the map over the water. A dam's lake maps 170 m round its shore every 2 m, a line of sight followed 520 m, as before (`dam::lake_reach`); the sea maps the track and 700 m round it, at most 768 texels a side and none under 2.5 m (2.6–3.8 m on the five tracks), a line of sight followed 1500 m, over the bays to the hills across them (`sea::mirror_reach`), and leaves out land more than 1 km from the sea (`sea::MIRROR_INLAND`, from the shore map, `ShoreMap::distance_at`). Its colour deep down is the reservoir's dark green-blue, as the lake's; a flooded pit's (the Gold Mine's), a dark wine red (`mirror::deep`). The ground is mapped in the colours the circuit's soil draws it with (`Terrain::ground_color`: a desert canyon's earth and rock, `canyon_vertex_color`; the checkerboard earth; the grass's greens), over the texture each is drawn with (a desert's flat ground is dusty earth, `SAND_AVERAGE`, not grass): mapped as grass, the Gold Mine's pit mirrored a bright green, seen on lavapipe on its bridge.
- **The shader** (`mirror.wgsl`): the reflected line of sight is followed only where it can meet something: from where it comes over the map to where it leaves it (`over_the_map`, a box crossed in x and z), no further than the water says (`water.z`) and no higher than the highest of the map (`water.y`); in as many steps as that stretch needs, one about every 3 m, 8 to 64 (64 as before for the lake's), closer together near the water, then narrowed down in 6. Looking down near the camera, or out to the open sea, it gives up in a few steps.
- **The cartoon sea, kept** (`src/world/sea.rs`, `src/world/scenery.rs`, `src/settings.rs`, `src/options.rs`): `Scenery::cartoon_water` (true for Loop Island only) and `Settings::cartoon_water` (Options > Graphics > "Agua": "Con reflejo" / "Mirroring", the default, or "Wind Waker"). Each circuit's sea is built with both its materials (`SeaWater`: the cartoon one always, the mirroring one unless the track always has the cartoon one) and drawn with the one they ask for (`dress_the_sea`, in `PostUpdate`: the cartoon one as it is made, changed before it is first drawn, and at once when the option changes). The dams' lakes always mirror. Preparing the sea's map costs, in the test build, 0.3–3 s on one thread for each track (Ghost Island the most, mostly land within a kilometre of water; 2–9 s before leaving out what is under the water or far inland and before 768 texels), well under a second to 1.6 s on four.
- **Cutting between menus from the road** (`src/camera.rs`, `src/menu.rs`; also logged in its topic's record, `RECORD/2026-10-16.track-view-option.WIP.md`, section 3): with "Vista al elegir pista" on "A pie de pista" (the default), `start_glide` starts no glide between menus and lets go of one under way: the flight over the road goes on into the track screen as it was, and between the track screen and the bikes the camera cuts. From above, as before. The bikes' screen also looks at the bike chosen at once on coming in (`look_afresh`; `OrbitState` is now a resource, reset on entering), not from where it looked the last time (another track's bikes, which made it glide across).
- **Graphics, reworked** (`src/options.rs`, `src/settings.rs`): its list is now Fullscreen (and its kind, on macOS), Resolution, "Límite FPS" / "FPS limit" (was "FPS", under the resolution now; at the screen's rate the row says the screen's rate once known, "De la pantalla (60 Hz)", from `ScreenRate`, kept from the window's monitor by `follow_screen_rate`), "Forzar rendimiento" / "Force performance" (was the performance mode's tick: now "No" / "Off", the default, "60 fps" or "120 fps", `ForcePerformance`), and right under it Resolution scale and Shadows, which say "Auto", faint, while it is on (they said "Rendimiento: bajas" before); Antialiasing; Water; then two pages: "HUD de rendimiento" / "Performance HUD" and "Avanzado" / "Advanced". The performance forced and the pages each begin a group of their own (`starts_group`). Advanced holds Trees, Lighter far trees and Less detail for two players (moved from the list) over the performance log, pipelined rendering and frame latency (those three still not in the browser, where the page now shows the first three). The new page, "OPCIONES > HUD DE RENDIMIENTO", has Show FPS (on by default), Show active resolution and Show coordinates on the map (off). Each page resets its own options; Graphics' own Reset puts everything back, its pages' too.
- **The performance forced** (`src/world/detail.rs`): `Settings::performance_mode: bool` became `Settings::force_performance`; settings saved with `performance_mode: true` come back as 60 fps (`Settings::brought_up_to_date`, which `load` now uses for every change of the saved file). It aims at 60 or 120, no more than the FPS limit lets through: a cap, or at the screen's rate the screen's (`FpsLimit::bound`, `performance_target`), so 120 on a 60 Hz screen does not drive everything to the last rung. The performance log says `force_performance=Off|Fps60|Fps120` (was `performance_mode=`) and `water=mirror|cartoon`.
- **The performance HUD** (`src/hud.rs`, `src/minimap.rs`, `src/race/hud.rs`, `src/race/results.rs`, `src/bench.rs`): at the top right, a column of the frame rate (as before, hidden if the options say so) and, under it, the pixels the world is drawn at ("3D 1920 × 1080", "3D 1344 × 756 · 70 %", `resolution_line`, as chosen or as far as the performance forced has come, never more than the screen's own pixels), in the game's font (Bevy's has no "×"). Who has crossed the line and the results at the top right go down a line when it has two (`under_performance_hud`). Over each race minimap, just above it, its player's place on the track: how far round the lap in metres from the line (the way the circuits measure their spans), the branch if on one, and X, Z and the height Y, in whole metres (`coordinates_said`). The benchmark shows the frame rate and its own label, not the HUD's second line.
- **A frame cap past the screen's rate** (`src/settings.rs`): a fixed cap waited for the screen to show each frame (`PresentMode::AutoVsync`: FifoRelaxed, else Fifo), so it could never go past the screen's rate. On the BC-250, in a gamescope (Steam) session, 120 stayed at 60 while unlimited (Immediate, no waiting) reached 190: the screen's beat the game waited on was 60 Hz there, not the monitor's 180 (most likely the session's own refresh rate for the display, which gamescope keeps at 60 unless allowed to change it; not seen from here, as the BC-250 is not at hand). A fixed cap now hands each frame over to be shown at the next refresh, the newest one then (`PresentMode::Mailbox`), without tearing and without waiting, the pace kept by `limit_frames` as before; on macOS, where Metal has no Mailbox (Bevy would fall back to Immediate, which tears), it stays on the screen's beat (`CAP_ON_MAILBOX`). Where a driver has no Mailbox elsewhere, Bevy falls back to Immediate (tearing, but the cap kept).
- Docs: `docs/gameplay.md` (the race HUD's place and performance HUD, the view while choosing the track and its cuts, Graphics' new list, the FPS limit (its list was out of date: 30, 60, 120, not 144 or 240), the performance forced, the water, the Performance HUD and Advanced pages, what is saved), `docs/architecture.md` (screens, `sea.rs`, `dam.rs`, the new `mirror.rs` and `mirror.wgsl`, `detail.rs`, `hud.rs`, `minimap.rs`, `options.rs`, `settings.rs`, the frame cap's design decision, the test counts, out of date since several records: now 529, 525 off macOS), `docs/tracks.md` (`water` and the new `cartoon_water`).
