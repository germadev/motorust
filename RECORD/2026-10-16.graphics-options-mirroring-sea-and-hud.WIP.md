### 3.2 Implementation Details
- **Why it looked heavy**: each texel the props reached was raised in the ground's own map to the highest of their points, in their average colour: a solid column from the water to the roof, mirrored as opaquely as a hill, so the shrine on stilts showed as a dark red block standing on the water, with nothing under its floor.
- **Maps of their own** (`src/world/mirror.rs`, `PreparedMirror::stand_up`): what stands on the ground no longer touches the ground's map. It goes into two maps over the same area: its lit colour and its top (`Rgba16Float`), and its base and how much of it the water shows (`Rg16Float`); with nothing standing, a texel of nothing (1 × 1) each, and the shader skips them (`water.w`, 1 if anything stands).
- **From where it is solid up**: each texel is looked at in half-metre bands (`STANDING_BAND`), each in 4 × 4 parts (`STANDING_PARTS`); a band is solid where the points cover 3 of the parts or more (`SOLID_PARTS`) with the bands either side (the points of a wall may be up to 1.2 m apart): a floor, a wall, a roof. Where they cover fewer (stilts, posts) it is thin, too thin for a map 2.5 to 5 m a texel to draw, so the line of sight passes under the floor of a shrine on stilts and a post alone stands as nothing. The texel stands from its lowest solid band to the top of its highest, in their colours.
- **Its edge blends into nothing**: the maps are read between texels, and nothing (black, its base at the water) next to a standing texel darkened its edge and dropped it to the water; the texels round it take its colour, base and share, with nothing standing.
- **Faint and broken up** (`src/world/mirror.wgsl`): on its way, the line of sight notes the first thing standing it passes through (`standing_at`: under its top and over its base); that shows over what is behind it (the ground, the sky) at most 60 % (`STANDING_SEEN`), times its own share, times patches drifting slowly across the water (`unbroken`: two octaves of noise about 2.5 m across, `PATCH_SIZE`), down to a fifth of that where they break it (`STANDING_BROKEN`). Then Fresnel mixes it with the water's own colour as before.
- **The trees in colour** (`src/world/props.rs`): almond, momiji, ginkgo and sakura, the species whose sunlit leaves are not green (`in_colour`, from their own colours in `foliage`, so a new species is sorted by its colour). Each variant of theirs gives a few dozen points over its crown (`crown_points`: the merged mesh's vertices not painted bark, every so many, at most 48, `CROWN_POINTS`), and each tree planted gives them, moved where it stands, to `Assets3d::mirrored_leaves`. The sea takes them as leaves: never thin, from the crown's lowest point to its highest, shown half as much as what is built (`LEAVES_SEEN`), light passing through.
- **Under the water, nothing**: the stilt shrine's model has a footing under the water, a slab half a metre to a metre and a half down, solid in every part; it made the shrine stand from under the water up, the whole column again. Points under the water are left out: nothing under it is seen in it.
