# Sea Waves All Going One Way (a Swell)

**Date:** 2026-10-13
**Branch:** `claude/loving-maxwell-vr7uub`

## 1. User Request
"Sobre las olas, en el caso de Turbo Bay, al tener una isla al lado, se ven raras al tener olas en dos direcciones" (on Turbo Bay, with an island next to the coast, the waves look odd going two ways).

## 2. Cause
The crests follow the lines at the same distance from the nearest shore and roll in to it, so in the water between an island and the coast some waves headed for the island and others for the coast, meeting half way.

## 3. Implementation Details
- `src/world/sea.rs`:
  - `ShoreMap::toward`: the way to the nearest shore at each texel, down the slope of the distance (over two texels each way), at most 1 long (shorter where two shores pull apart), its direction averaged over a 7 × 7 box (`smooth_ways`) so the distance's steps do not show.
  - `ShoreMap::swell`: the sea's prevailing swell, the average way to the shore of the water within 150 m of a shore and 500 m of the start of the race (any shore if there is none there): on Turbo Bay, the seafront.
  - The map texture becomes `Rgba8Unorm`, with the way to the shore in blue and alpha, and the material gets the swell as a uniform.
- `src/world/sea.wgsl`: the waves only show where the shore faces the swell (`smoothstep(0.15, 0.6, dot(toward, swell))`). Between an island and the coast only one of them gets waves, so they all go one way; the sheltered faces have none. The foam on the shoreline stays everywhere.
