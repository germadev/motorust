# Keyboard by curves: Bézier-shaped lean and turn, and their panel

## 1. Keyboard by curves, its options and the panel under the performance HUD

**Date:** 2026-10-29. **Branch:** `claude/motorcycle-keyboard-controls-lpfaaj`.

### User request
The user is not happy with how the bike handles on the keyboard and wants a new way to define it, under Settings > Controls > Advanced: an option that turns on a new keyboard mode (off, the current options apply), shaped by Bézier curves as CSS's `cubic-bezier`. After the design discussion they asked to be able to set how much the bike turns for its speed and for how far it leans, how long it takes to lean over, and so on; to have a development view that draws the curves under the performance HUD; and, later, an automatic mode that corrects the curves from how they ride (running wide for too little turn, cutting inside for too much). They agreed to the plan: the key leans the bike and the lean turns it, one shared set of curves, and steps 1 to 3 first (model and curves, the options, the panel), leaving the automatic mode for after they try it by hand.

### Implementation
- **`src/bezier.rs`** (new): `Bezier`, a CSS-style easing from (0, 0) to (1, 1) with control points P1 and P2. `ease(x)` halves along the curve to the point at that x (x grows with t while both control points' x are within the square); `first_reaching(y)` looks at the curve in 64 stretches from the start and halves the first that gets to the height, so a curve that overshoots and comes back gives its first crossing.
- **`src/bike/curves.rs`** (new):
  - `KeyboardCurves`, saved in the settings: A leaning in (curve and seconds to all the way over), B standing up (seconds from all the way over to upright), C unwinding when the other way is pressed (seconds, nought for at once, as always), D the lean kept for the share of top speed (curve and the share kept at top speed), E the turn for the lean.
  - `CurvePreset`: Classic (the default), Smooth, Arcade, Direct. Classic is the keyboard without the curves, at its options' defaults: `DigitalSteer` at medium is 1.875 t + 1.875 t² held (all over in 0.385 s) and 0.25 t + t² back once let go (0.883 s), both polynomials of time, so their Béziers have P1 and P2 on the thirds across (y1 = a/3, y2 = (a+1)/3); its limit with speed (the smoothstep from 15 to 70 m/s, two thirds of the 0.8 limit, so 86.7 % at top speed) is fitted as an easing with both control points at 0.56 across (within 0.06 of the smoothstep, under 0.01 of the lean).
  - `CurveLean::update`: the lean follows A (capped by D), B or C, each from wherever the lean is (the first point of the curve at its height, recomputed on every change of curve or of the key held), so nothing ever jumps; held at D's limit, its place on A is held at the limit so that slowing down goes on from there. Progress is time over the curve's seconds, so the ride is the same at any frame rate; C ending mid-step leans in with the rest of the step.
  - `CurveProbe`: where the first player's bike is on the curves, for the panel.
- **`src/bike/mod.rs`**:
  - `KeySteer` wraps `DigitalSteer` and `CurveLean` for the three places the keyboard and the d-pad are read (one player; WASD, arrows and the d-pad with two): with the curves off, exactly as before; on, the turn is E of the lean, the lean goes on `BikeInput::lean`, and the reverse rate's slowing is not used (C shapes the swing).
  - `BikeInput::lean: Option<f32>`: in `step`, when set, the lean shown is that share of `max_lean`, scaled in from 2.5 to 15 m/s (`CURVE_LEAN_SPEED`: at walking pace the bars turn the bike), followed at 40/s (`CURVE_LEAN_FOLLOW`) so that the curves' times are the ones seen. With no key held and the bike upright it is `None`, and the lean comes from the turn as always (the stick).
  - `read_player_input` writes `CurveProbe` for the first player.
- **`src/settings.rs`**: `keyboard_curves_on` and `show_keyboard_curves` (both off at first) and `keyboard_curves` (classic), saved; older settings load with them off.
- **`src/options.rs`**: Control gets "Advanced" ("Avanzado", "Keyboard by curves >" beside it when on), a page of its own (`Page::ControlAdvanced`, "OPTIONS > ADVANCED"): "Keyboard by curves", "Curves under the performance HUD", "Preset" (the preset the curves are, or "Custom"; stepping from custom goes to the first or last preset), "Curve" (which one the rows below edit, `OptionsMenu::curve`), its number (seconds or the lean at top speed, steps of 0.05; C steps between nought and 0.05) and P1 X/Y, P2 X/Y (steps of 0.05 in the square, `step_point`). The curve rows are faint while the mode is off. Reset on the page puts back only its options; Reset on Control puts them back too, as Graphics' does with its pages.
- **`src/curves_hud.rs`** (new): the panel at the top right under the performance HUD, two columns of five cards, each the curve drawn into an image of its own (300 × 184 px shown at 150 × 92) only when the curves change: a grid, the classic preset's curve in grey, the arms to P1 and P2 dashed in coral, the curve in Control's blue (B and C drawn as what is left of the lean, coming down; D as the lean kept). A dot shows where the first player is on each now (A, B or C while followed; D always; E while leaning). The card being edited in the options is edged in Control's blue, and the panel is drawn over the options (`GlobalZIndex` 60, the options at 50) so that a curve can be watched while it is edited. Its label: letter, short name and number.
- **Docs:** `docs/gameplay.md` (steering, and Control's Advanced page with the table of the curves), `docs/architecture.md` (the three new files in the code map, the tests).
