# Elimination Place Counter, Split-Screen Performance and Tree Levels of Detail

## 1. The Place Counter in Elimination, Lighter Split Screen and Lighter Far Trees (2026-10-15)

### 1.1 User Requests
> "En el modo eliminación, ve actualizando el contador de posición de modo que cuando, por ejemplo, se hayan eliminado 2 corredores, ponga 2° / 6" (in elimination, the place counter should count only those still in: 2nd / 6 once two riders are out).

> "El rendimiento del modo de 2P se ha resentido mucho. Hay que mejorar el rendimiento, los detalles en este modo no tienen que tener tanto detalle. La prioridad absoluta es el rendimiento" (two-player performance has suffered a lot; this mode does not need so much detail; performance is the absolute priority).

> "¿Sería posible hacer versiones con menos polígonos de los árboles para los que están lejos?" (lower-polygon versions of the trees for those far away).

### 1.2 Implementation Details
- **The place counter** (`src/race/hud.rs`, `place_among`): the HUD showed a bike's place over `Standing::order.len()`, every bike of the race. In elimination, a bike still in is now placed among those still in (all of them less `Standing::out`): 2nd / 6 with two of eight out. A bike that is out (a player retired, or disqualified for jumping the start) keeps its place among them all (7th / 8 for the second out), its final classification. Other modes are as before (a player disqualified in a race still counts).
- **Why two players cost so much more:** with the screen split, the world is drawn twice a frame, and Bevy works out the sun's shadow cascades for each view: four cascades of 2048² out to 250 m each, eight shadow passes a frame, every tree within 250 m drawn into each; the grass, flowers and petals out to their full reach in both views; and thousands of trees at full detail twice.
- **Detail for split screen** (`src/world/detail.rs`, new): a `Detail` resource, `Full` with one player and `Split` with two (from `GameMode`), chosen every frame in `PostUpdate`, applied when it changes:
  - Shadows: two cascades from 20 m out to 90 m (four out to 250 m with one view), each 1024 texels wide (2048): four smaller shadow passes a frame instead of eight, with only what is within 90 m drawn into them. `spawn_sun` now takes its cascades from `Detail::Full`.
  - Grass tufts and the ragged edge, flower clumps and falling petals: every `VisibilityRange` is shortened to 55 % with the screen split (`reach_for_detail`; each entity's full range kept in `FullReach` as it comes, so it goes back with one player). Tufts 55 → 30 m, the edge 170 → 94 m, clumps 150 → 83 m, petals 170 → 94 m.
  - Trees switch to their lighter meshes sooner (below).
- **Tree levels of detail** (`src/world/lod.rs`, new; `src/world/trees.rs`; `src/world/props.rs`):
  - `lod::simplify(mesh, target)`: edge collapse with quadric error metrics (Garland and Heckbert). Vertices at the same spot are welded first (the Meshy trees split them along their texture seams: 631 vertices, 225 spots for the stone pine), each vertex gets the planes of its faces, area-weighted, plus a plane across each open edge; edges are collapsed cheapest first from a heap (stale entries skipped by version), each into the best of the quadric's minimum (when it is settled and near the edge, clamped to the mesh's box), its two ends or its middle; a collapse that would turn a face over is skipped. Faces left with two corners in one go, and so does one that becomes the same as another facing the same way (the two sides of a thin sheet stay). Two guards for trees: the top vertex of each part (faces joined by their corners) is pinned, so tips do not sink (a thin spike is cheap to shorten, its vertices sliding along its own faces: the classic cypress lost 16 % of its height without it); and no part goes below two faces (the Meshy fir's top tier is a part of its own and vanished, 14 m down to 9). Each vertex keeps the average colour and normal of those it gathered (or the faces' normal, or out from the middle).
  - A first attempt, vertex clustering on a grid, was dropped: anything thinner than a cell (a cypress's spire, a trunk) collapsed to nothing.
  - `lod::paint_from_texture(mesh, image, mip)`: the modelled (Meshy) trees' colours painted into their vertices from mip 3 of their 2048² atlas (each triangle the average of its middle and halfway to its corners; each vertex the average of its triangles), so the lighter meshes need no texture. They share a vertex-coloured material (`ModelledTrees::painted`, roughness and reflectance as the textured one).
  - `trees::lighter(full)`: two lighter meshes, a third of the triangles (at most 200) and a tenth (at most 60), or as few as each part allows. The fifteen modelled trees go from 5,510 triangles in all to about 1,820 and 700 (the sugi and bamboo, with 34 and more clumps of leaves, stop at about 70); the 24 classic variants from 14,580 to 3,954 and 1,188. The classic ones are made lighter with their variants, off the main thread, while the circuit is prepared (`plan_trees`). Heights kept: every classic variant exactly; the modelled trees at least 92 % (the fir's top tier, 12.9 of 14 m), most 98-100 %.
  - `dress_trees` now also picks each tree's mesh by its distance from the nearest active camera (`lod::level_at`): full nearer than 60 m, the first lighter mesh to 190 m and the second beyond (30 m and 100 m with the screen split), changing only 4 m past each bound so a tree on a bound does not flicker. It runs in parallel, skips trees of hidden circuits (but dresses them when the Options look changes), compares handles before cloning them, and runs after transforms and visibility propagate and before the cameras' visibility is checked. Trees now have `NoAutoAabb`: their `Aabb` is always the full mesh's of their look (set when the look changes), so changing level never recomputes it nor lets culling shrink.
  - The near look is unchanged (the full mesh, as before), and the lighter ones keep the outline, height and colours, so the switch is hard to see (no different look popping in, as the earlier far crowns did).
- **Petals** (`src/world/japan.rs`, `drift_petals`): every petal of every circuit loaded was moved every frame. Now only those of the circuit in sight within reach of a camera (their range, 20 m of fade and their column) move, in parallel; the others are moved as they come near (where each is follows from the time alone).

### 1.3 Verification & Testing
- New tests: `in_elimination_a_place_is_among_those_still_in` (2nd / 6 with two of eight out, those out 7th and 8th / 8, nobody out 2nd / 8, a race unchanged); `two_views_are_drawn_lighter` and `what_is_drawn_so_far_comes_nearer_with_two_views_and_back` (cascades, shadow map and every range shortened with two players, new entities too, and all of it back with one); `a_lighter_mesh_keeps_its_shape_and_colours_with_fewer_triangles` (a ball of 1,280 triangles to 300, 120 and 40, inside its box, its radius kept, green above and red below, normals out), `a_mesh_already_light_enough_stays_as_it_is`, `the_two_sides_of_a_thin_sheet_are_both_kept`, `a_texture_is_painted_in_the_vertices_as_seen_from_afar`, `the_level_changes_only_past_each_bound`; `each_modelled_tree_has_lighter_meshes_painted_in_its_colours` (a third and fewer triangles, at least 90 % as tall, not black nor white) and the classic trees' test now also checks their lighter meshes (at most 200 and 60 triangles, at least 90 % as tall).
- `cargo test`: 440 passed. `cargo fmt --check` and `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` clean.
- Run on lavapipe (Xvfb, 1280 × 720): the track screen's view over Bahía GP shows hundreds of far trees with their lightest meshes, still pines with trunks, cypresses and almonds in their colours; a two-player race starts and draws both halves with the shorter shadows and the trees at every level. Frame rate cannot be judged on a CPU rasteriser (1-3 fps there): the user is to compare two players on their machine.

## 2. The Game Modes Screen as Big as the Start Menu (2026-10-15)

### 2.1 User Request
> "El tamaño del menú de modo de juego es muy grande respecto al menú de inicio, dependiendo de la resolución" (the game modes menu is too big next to the start menu, depending on the resolution).

### 2.2 Implementation Details
- **Cause** (`src/modes.rs`): the cards of the game modes were 72 % of the window's width (600 to 1100 px), while the start menu is as wide as its title ("MOTORUST" at 110 px, `MenuWidth`, about 600 px whatever the window). In a 1280-point window the modes were half as wide again as the start menu; at 1920 or 2560, nearly twice.
- **Now:** the cards are as wide as the start menu (`cards_width`), the same at every resolution, and never wider than 92 % of a narrow window; on screen both menus share their left and right edges. `MenuWidth` (`src/welcome.rs`) is now public for this. The lettering and the rest of the cards keep their proportions, as before. A first try at 1.3 times the start menu (814 px at 1280 × 720) still looked much bigger in a screenshot, so it is 1.0.
- With two players the three cards are in one row, which at that width left no room for their descriptions (Elimination's took three lines over the card's edge): when there is only one row, its cards are as tall as the championship's (a fifth of the width).

### 2.3 Verification & Testing
- New test: `the_modes_are_as_wide_as_the_start_menu_at_any_resolution` (at 1280, 1600, 1920 and 2560, and inside a 600-wide window).
- Screenshots on lavapipe at 1280 × 720: the start menu and the game modes for one player both 627 px wide, edge to edge; the modes for two players, the three cards tall enough for their descriptions.
- `cargo test`: 440 passed; fmt and clippy clean.
