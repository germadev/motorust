## 2. Free Ride's Music

### 2.1 User Requests
> "en el modo de conducción libre, sonará el tema de la pista por defecto. añadiremos una opción in-game centrado abajo, como la leyenda, la indicación de la tecla Y, rotando entre: música del circuito; playlist de las pistas de todos los circuitos, empezando por la del circuito seleccionado; modo chill con las tres pistas que adjunto" (with three MP3s)
> "el volumen en modo chill será algo más bajo"
> "la leyenda del botón de elección de música en el modo libre, se mostrará siempre, independientemente de la opción de mostrar o no la leyenda"

### 2.2 Implementation Details
- **Assets:** `assets/music/chill_velvet_dashboard.mp3`, `chill_sunlight_on_still_water.mp3`, `chill_leaves_on_the_still_pond.mp3` (192 kbps, 44.1 kHz stereo, about three minutes each), measured as the tracks' songs are: -19.7, -18.6 and -17.7 LUFS above 500 Hz.
- **`Song::length`** (s, from `ffprobe`) on every song, so that a list knows when one is over: songs keep looping from memory (`LoopedDecoder`), and the `Music` entity counts the seconds it has played; at its length, in a list, it goes at once (it would start over) and the next one comes.
- **`src/sound/music.rs`:** `Tune` (a track's song, a chill one, or the menus'), `MusicMode` (`Track`, `AllTracks` from the current track's round them all, `Chill`), `FreeMusic` (the mode and the song of it playing; every free ride starts with the track's song: `reset_free_music` on entering the race). `change_free_music`: in free ride, out of the pause menu, Y or the gamepad's top face button (`MUSIC_KEY`, `MUSIC_BUTTON`; Select+Y stays the mute shortcut, which takes Y's press) changes the mode. The chill songs play at `CHILL_LOUDNESS`, 4 dB under the tracks'.
- **The line at the bottom (`src/hud.rs`, `legend::music_prompt`):** in free ride the race legend always carries Y with what plays ("Music: the track's", "Music: every track's · Bahía GP", "Music: chill · Velvet Dashboard"), alone when the controls legend is off.

## 3. The Menus' Song and the Music On and Off

### 3.1 User Requests
> "añade esta pista como música de fondo, bajita, para los menús de inicio y elección de modo y pista" (Asphalt Shift)
> "en la leyenda del menú de inicio, elección de modo y el de pista, añade la opción de activar/desactivar música"

### 3.2 Implementation Details
- `MENU` ("Asphalt Shift", `assets/music/menu_asphalt_shift.mp3`, -19.6 LUFS) plays in the welcome screen, the lobby, the modes and the tracks at `MENU_LOUDNESS`, 8 dB under the tracks'; it fades out choosing the bike and gives way to the track's song in the race.
- `toggle_menu_music`: in the welcome screen, the modes and the tracks (not with the options open), Y or the top face button turns `Settings::music_enabled` on and off, the same as Options > Sound. Their legends end in `legend::music_toggle_prompt` ("Y Música: sí / no"); the welcome and modes screens are rebuilt when it changes.

## 4. The Tracks' Surroundings Choosing the Bike

### 4.1 User Request
> "en la elección de piloto, mejor sonidos ambientales, como el mar y las gaviotas, etc, coherentes con el mapa que haya elegido"

### 4.2 Implementation Details
- **`Circuit::ambience` (`circuits::Ambience`):** how much sea, gulls, small birds, cicadas and wind each track has: Bahía GP sea 0.8, gulls 0.7, birds 0.3, cicadas 0.2, wind 0.2; Turbo Bay sea 0.9, gulls 0.9, birds 0.15, wind 0.35; Loop Island sea 0.55, gulls 0.35, birds 0.9, cicadas 0.25, wind 0.25; Gold Mine birds 0.1, cicadas 0.75, wind 0.7.
- **`src/sound/ambience.rs`, synthesised in the mixer** (`AmbienceParams` in `Controls`, set by `surroundings` from the state: the track's on the bike select screen, nothing elsewhere; each amount fades over `FADE`, 1.5 s):
  - the sea: two layers, one to each side, of noise through two one-pole low-passes whose cutoff follows each wave's swell (`Wave::swell`: it rises, breaks a little past the middle and washes back), waves of 7 to 11 s;
  - seagulls: calls of 2 to 5 harsh notes (three harmonics and a little noise) rising and falling around 1.3–1.8 kHz, now and then, each from somewhere to the side and nearer or farther; their notes fade in and out (an earlier sudden edge clicked: broadband lines in the spectrogram);
  - small birds: quick trills of pure chirps at 3–5.5 kHz;
  - cicadas: noise in a band at 4.3/4.65 kHz beating 48 times a second, swelling every 7 s;
  - the wind: low noise in slow gusts.
  - `LEVEL` 0.35: about as loud as the bike idling on the screen (each track's mix rendered to WAV and measured, -30 to -32 dB RMS against the idle engine's -30 dB at 1800 rpm), a backdrop to it.
