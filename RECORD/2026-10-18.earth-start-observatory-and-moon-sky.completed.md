# 2026-10-18 — Moon Base: Start on Earth, Space Center & Observatory Dome, Longer High-Relief Earth Sector, and Moon in Earth Sky

## Entry 1 — Analysis & Design Plan

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Task:** Start the `Base Lunar` circuit on Earth, make the Earth stretch longer with high altitude variations, place the space launchpad on Earth alongside giant radio antennas and a new telescope observatory dome (`Observatory`), and show the Moon (instead of the Earth) in the sky when on Earth.

### User Request
The user requested:
1. Place the space launchpad (`Rocket`) on Earth, together with more facilities dedicated to space study such as huge radio antennas (`RadioDish`) and a dome with a telescope (`cúpula con telescópio` / `Observatory`).
2. Make the Earth stretch longer (`haz el tramo de la tierra más largo`) and make the circuit start on Earth (`que el circuito empieze en la tierra`).
3. Give the Earth stretch strong altitude variations.
4. From Earth, the Moon must be seen in the sky, not the Earth (`desde la tierra debe verse la luna, no la tierra`).

### Implementation Plan
1. **Sky (`src/world/sky.rs`):**
   - Add procedural `moon_image()` rendering a detailed cratered lunar disc (basalt maria, highland regolith, rayed impact craters, silvery limb glow, and solar terminator).
   - Spawn both `earth_body` and `moon_body` inside `space_sky`.
   - In `follow_camera` (and `light_the_sky`), toggle visibility between `moon_body` and `earth_body` based on `is_earth_realm(camera_xz)`: when the camera is on Earth (`is_earth_realm == true`), `moon_body` is visible and `earth_body` is hidden; when on the Moon (`is_earth_realm == false`), `earth_body` is visible and `moon_body` is hidden.
2. **Space Research Facilities (`src/world/scenery.rs`, `src/world/space.rs`):**
   - Add `Observatory { pub position: Vec2, pub heading: f32 }` and `pub observatories: &'static [Observatory]` to `Space`.
   - Implement `spawn_observatories` and `observatory_meshes` in `src/world/space.rs`: a cylindrical white/titanium astronomical observatory building with buttresses, observation deck walkway, a rotating hemispherical dome with an open shutter slit, and an angled giant optical reflector telescope tube with a glowing cyan primary/secondary mirror assembly aiming into the sky.
   - Scale up `RadioDish` (`dish_meshes`) by 1.45x so the deep-space radio antennas are massive (`antenas enormes de radio`).
   - Update `place_boulders` in `src/world/space.rs` so lunar boulders only spawn in the Moon realm (`!is_earth_realm(position)`).
3. **Earth-First Realm & Longer High-Relief Earth Sector (`src/world/terrain.rs`, `src/circuits/moon_base.rs`, `docs/tracks.md`):**
   - Update `is_earth_realm(point)` in `src/world/terrain.rs` so `point.x < boundary_x` (with `boundary_x` running through Portal #1 at `(764, -430)` / `1120 m` and Portal #2 at `(658, -300)` / `2895 m`) is the Earth realm (`2825 m`, 61.4% of the lap, including the start/finish line at `(0, 0)`) and `point.x > boundary_x` (`1120..2895 m`, `1775 m`, 38.6% of the lap) is the Moon realm.
   - Redesign `ELEVATION` and `MASSIFS` in `src/circuits/moon_base.rs` so the Earth stretch (`2895..4600..1120 m`) has three major mountain climbs and valley drops (`1 m -> 36 m -> 0.6 m`, `46 m -> 4 m`, and `4 m -> 38 m -> 0 m`), while the Moon stretch (`1120..2895 m`) rolls across the lunar bridge, maria, and crater split (`8..22 m`) before climbing to the return portal.
   - Place all `rockets`, `dishes`, `observatories`, `trees`, `forests`, and `houses` on the Earth stretch, and `biodomes`, `rovers`, and `boulders` on the Moon stretch.

## Entry 2 — Implementation & Verification

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Completed

### Implementation Details
1. **Moon in Earth Sky / Earth in Moon Sky (`src/world/sky.rs`):**
   - Added procedural `moon_image()` rendering a 256×256 cratered lunar sphere with dark basalt maria, bright highland regolith, rayed impact craters, a silvery corona halo, and solar terminator shading.
   - Updated `SkyParts` and `spawn_sky` to spawn both `moon_body` and `earth_body` inside `space_sky`.
   - Updated `follow_camera` to toggle visibility dynamically based on `is_earth_realm(camera_xz)`: when the camera is on Earth (`is_earth_realm == true`), `moon_body` is visible and `earth_body` is hidden; when on the Moon (`is_earth_realm == false`), `earth_body` is visible and `moon_body` is hidden.
2. **Space Research Facilities on Earth (`src/world/scenery.rs`, `src/world/space.rs`):**
   - Added `Observatory { pub position: Vec2, pub heading: f32 }` and `pub observatories: &'static [Observatory]` to `Space`.
   - Implemented `spawn_observatories` and `observatory_meshes` in `src/world/space.rs`: a 14.2 m radius cylindrical observatory building with buttresses, observation deck walkway, illuminated control-room windows, a 13.6 m radius hemispherical dome with an open shutter slit bordered by gold rails, and an angled giant optical Cassegrain reflector telescope tube with Serrurier truss struts and a glowing cyan optical lens/mirror aimed at the sky.
   - Scaled up `RadioDish` (`dish_pedestal_mesh` and `dish_reflector_meshes`) to a 22.4 m diameter parabolic reflector (`r_outer = 11.2 m`) on a 12.2 m tall tower so the deep-space radio antennas are massive (`antenas enormes de radio`).
3. **Earth Start, Longer High-Relief Earth Stretch & Space Center Layout (`src/world/terrain.rs`, `src/circuits/moon_base.rs`, `src/circuits/mod.rs`, `docs/tracks.md`):**
   - Updated `is_earth_realm(point)` in `src/world/terrain.rs` (`point.x < boundary_x` with `boundary_x` running through Portal #1 at `(764, -430)` / `1120 m` and Portal #2 at `(658, -300)` / `2895 m`) so the circuit starts on Earth at `(0, 0)` and the Earth stretch covers `2825 m` (61.4% of the 4600 m lap) while the Moon stretch covers `1775 m` (38.6% of the lap).
   - Placed both space launchpads (`rockets`), 5 telescope observatory domes (`observatories`), and 7 giant deep-space radio antennas (`dishes`) on the Earth stretch (`is_earth_realm == true`), and placed `biodomes`, `rovers`, `solar_arrays`, and `boulders` on the Moon stretch (`1120..2895 m`).
   - Redesigned `ELEVATION` (`[(f32, f32); 57]`) and `MASSIFS` so the Earth stretch features three major mountain climbs and plunges (`1.0 m -> 32.0 m -> 0.4 m` over Observatory Peak into the tunnel, `45.5 m -> 5.5 m` from the tunnel overpass into the northern valley, and `5.5 m -> 35.6 m -> 0.0 m` over the Western Mountain Ridge and Jump #3 back to the finish line).
