### 2.2 Implementation Details
- **Assets (`assets/ui/pads/`):** the four SVGs of anygamepad's `apps/web/src/art` as they are (DualSense, DualShock 4, DualShock 3, Xbox; MIT or Apache-2.0, credited in `assets/CREDITS.md`). Each control is a `data-button` group of `.shape` and `.mark` elements; sticks are `data-stick` groups; `data-trigger` says where the triggers go; `data-label` marks the letters printed on the Xbox face buttons.
- **Drawing (`src/pad_art.rs`):** first with resvg, but the project already rasterizes curves with zeno (through swash) for the menus, the speedometer and the clouds, so resvg was dropped before committing and the illustrations are drawn with zeno and swash, with no new dependency:
  - a small reader of their XML (`parse`: elements, attributes, text, comments);
  - the SVG they use: `path`, `circle` and `rect` (with corner radii) as path data, `use`, `transform` (translate, rotate about a point, scale, matrix: `Affine`, composed as SVG does), inherited presentation attributes (`Style`: fill, stroke, width, caps, joins, stroke opacity, font size, letter spacing, weight, anchor), linear and radial gradients over the box of the shape (`Gradient`), the DualShock 4's dotted pattern as the colour its dots average, clip paths, drop shadows (`feDropShadow`: the alpha blurred by three box blurs, moved and tinted) and the DualSense light bar's glow, and the few words (SELECT, START, SHARE, OPTIONS) in the game's font with swash;
  - premultiplied RGBA canvases that keep the box where anything was drawn, so layers, blurs and crops only go over that.
  - Each illustration is drawn once per size (`PadArts`): the whole controller, without the controls no pad of the game has (the Xbox paddles and its extra C and Z), and every button the game uses on its own: its shapes white and its marks dark, without shadows, cropped to it (`lit`), with where it goes; and where the triggers go.
  - `PadArt::of` picks the illustration: PlayStation pads by name (DualShock 3, DualShock 4 — "Wireless Controller" is how it often reports — or else DualSense), the Xbox controller for the rest; on a Switch pad its printed letters are left out and the view writes the pad's own.
- **The view (`src/mapping.rs`, `src/options.rs`):** on the gamepad's page the controller is drawn 270 px tall with the actions listed beside it. Over it, each button's image is tinted: faint blue if it does something, blue while held, orange the one being remapped, yellow the new ones; the triggers stand above it as small caps (LT/RT, L2/R2, ZL/ZR). `show_options_menu` passed Bevy's limit of sixteen parameters, so some are grouped in tuples.
