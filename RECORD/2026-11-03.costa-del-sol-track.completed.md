# 2026-11-03 — Costa del Sol: a ninth track

## User request

The user shared a concept image for a new circuit, "Costa del Sol — Sunset Run".
It has a map and five sectors: 1. Salida / Recta costera, 2. Curvas del acantilado,
3. Túnel de la montaña, 4. Puente costero, 5. Chicane final. The scenery is a
Mediterranean coast with whitewashed towns, palms, cliffs, a lighthouse and
sailboats.

## Implementation

- `assets/circuits/costa_del_sol.ron`: a new circuit, written entirely in RON
  with the engine's existing features (nothing new in the engine). The lap is
  2.63 km and runs clockwise round a bay, so the water is on the right all
  the way. The coast is an `Outline`, with the bay cut in from the south.
  - Start straight north along the west shore (railing, palms, beach parasols,
    the town on the left, an orange "COSTA DEL SOL" gantry).
  - S-bends up the cliffs to about 29 m (5.8 % at the steepest), cut rock on
    the left.
  - A tunnel through the mountain at the head of the bay, `Span(985, 1150)`.
  - Down the east shore to a truss bridge over the bay's mouth,
    `Span(2236, 2410)`. The engine has no plain concrete viaduct, so the
    bridge uses `Truss`.
  - A final chicane (its tightest bend is 29.4 m, over the 28.75 m limit), and
    round onto the straight.
  - Checkpoints at 800 m and 1700 m. Sky `GoldenHour`. Song "Crown of the
    Track", which was already in `assets/music/` but not used: 172.9 s and
    -14.4 LUFS, measured as `music.ron` says.
- `assets/circuits/circuits.ron` and `assets/pack.txt` list the circuit and its song.
- `src/game/checks.rs`: `circuits_have_checkpoints` covers circuit 8.
- `docs/tracks.md`, `README.md` and `docs/gameplay.md` now describe nine tracks.

## Verification

- `motorust-engine/tools/circuit_layout.py`: 2633.9 m, tightest bend 29.4 m
  at 2488 m, steepest grade 5.8 % at 506 m, no crests the bike takes off
  over at top speed.

# 2026-11-03 — Costa del Sol: pixel art, and a mountain road 30 s long

## User request

Add the pixel look to Costa del Sol, and make the lap longer with a new sector
of about 30 s, another scene of its own. Then, on seeing photos of mountain
roads cut into cliffs, the user asked for the new sector to climb and descend
a mountainous area.

## Implementation

- `look: Pixel(lines: 224, levels: 16)` in the circuit's scenery. The field is
  the engine's (jgermade/motorust-engine PR #2), not yet in a release.
- The new sector: after the tunnel and down the bay's east shore, the lap no
  longer runs down to the bridge. It goes east over the headland through a pine
  forest (`forests`, `Tall`) to the open sea, then south up a mountain road
  cut into the cliffs: rock on the right (`cuttings`), a stone parapet over the
  drop to the sea on the left. It climbs at up to 8 % to a summit 58 m up on a
  straight, then comes down at about 7 % in bends round a new cape with the
  lighthouse below, and west along the south coast to the bridge.
- The mountain is a `climbed` massif (radius 240 m, 72 m high), with a mesa of
  bare rock 115 m high and a crag standing over it.
- The coast was pushed out east and south to hold it, and the islet off the
  cape moved offshore.
- The summit is shaped as a crest the bike takes off over flat out, as on the
  start straight: `jumps_on_the_track_are_short` asks for two flights in 90 s
  flat out, and at 4 km a lap the start straight's crest alone came round only
  once. That is why the first run of this sector failed (233 passed, 1 failed).
- Lap: 4.08 km, +1.25 km, about 28 s at race pace. Checkpoints at 800, 1700,
  2700 and 3450 m; `circuits_have_checkpoints` updated.
- Every span from the headland on moved: bridge `Span(3592, 3762)`, chicane
  3792–3932 m, straight from 3950 m. The descriptions, `docs/tracks.md` and the
  README follow.

## Verification

- `circuit_layout.py`: 4084.5 m, tightest bend 29.5 m at 3838 m, steepest
  grade 11.2 % (down off the start straight's crest).
- Crests the bike takes off over: 156 m (9.2 m up, from 188 km/h) and 2500 m
  (58 m up, from 184 km/h), both on straights.
- `tools/with_engine.sh same cargo test` (the engine at PR #2's branch):
  234 passed, 0 failed, 1 ignored.
- With the engine at v0.0.19 (`Cargo.toml`'s tag), the circuit's file does not
  read, because v0.0.19 has no `look`. The game needs the engine released and
  its tag bumped.

# 2026-11-03 — Costa del Sol: a coast highway and a fork before the line

## User request

The user asked whether any stretch of the track simulates the style of the
8- and 16-bit arcade racers. On the proposal (a straight over crests in a row
between dense rows of palms, and a fork), they asked for it with the fork
included. The track's own design, not a copy of any game's stages.

## Implementation

- From the cape on, the lap changed:
  - **Autopista de la costa:** off the cape, a straight west along the south
    coast with two crests in a row (3291 and 3399 m), palms every 12 m on both
    sides, the sea on the left.
  - **The bridge:** the bay's mouth is now a channel to the sea, 70 m wide, and
    the highway crosses it on the truss bridge, `Span(3474, 3584)`.
  - **The fork:** a right turn north, and the road splits before the line,
    `Span(3849, 4199)`, its way to the left over a hump. The lap's points and
    the way's points and hump (`rise`) are Ghost Island's temple split (itself
    Gold Mine's second split), moved here as they are, since both run north.
    That split already satisfies the checks on forks. Banking is a quarter
    (`factor: 0.25`), as there.
  - The chicane is gone; the right turn after the bridge takes its place.
- The coast: the bay's mouth became the channel, and the south coast was moved
  south below the highway (at z ≈ 720).
- Walls, palms, houses, lamps, gantries, chevrons, brake markers and boats
  moved with the lap. The fourth checkpoint is now at 3640 m.
- The first run had one failure: `jumps_on_the_track_are_short`, a flight
  1.31 m over the road against 1.3. The crests' back sides were softened (to
  11.2 m at 3320 m and 10.6 m at 3430 m); they are now flown from 201 and
  191 km/h.

## Verification

- `circuit_layout.py`: 4379.2 m, tightest bend 42.8 m, steepest grade 11.6 %.
- Four crests the bike takes off over, all on straights: 156, 2500, 3291 and
  3399 m.
- `tools/with_engine.sh same cargo test`: 234 passed, 0 failed, 1 ignored. That
  includes `rivals_ride_the_forks_on_their_side_and_back`,
  `no_way_through_a_split_is_clearly_the_quickest`,
  `branches_are_rideable_and_clear_of_the_lap` and the grid's checks.
