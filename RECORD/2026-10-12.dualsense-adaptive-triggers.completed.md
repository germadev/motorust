## 3. Implementation Details

### 3.1 DualSense Detection & Objective-C Integration (`src/gamepads/apple.rs`)
- Connected controllers in `src/gamepads/apple.rs` are inspected for `GCDualSenseGamepad` capabilities via Objective-C class downcasting:
  - When `unsafe { controller.extendedGamepad() }` is an instance of `GCDualSenseGamepad`, retain handles to `leftTrigger` and `rightTrigger` (both `GCDualSenseAdaptiveTrigger`).
  - Stored `DualSenseTriggers` in the `Pad` struct, tracking both left and right trigger handles as well as previous applied effect states (`last_left`, `last_right`).
  - Added clean resource teardown on controller disconnection and implemented `Drop` for `ApplePads` to guarantee that all adaptive trigger motors are safely powered off (`setModeOff()`) on exit.

### 3.2 Adaptive Trigger Haptic Modeling (`compute_bike_trigger_effects`)
- Formulated physical motorcycle telemetry mapping for both triggers:
  - **Right Trigger (Throttle / R2):**
    - **Airborne / Jump (`!state.grounded`):** Turns off motor resistance (`TriggerEffect::Off`) simulating free engine spinning without load.
    - **Off-Road / Grass Wheel-Spin (`state.grass > 0.08` & `state.speed > 1.5`):** Applies motor vibration directly into the trigger paddle (`TriggerEffect::Vibration`) with amplitude and frequency proportional to grass intrusion and wheel velocity, communicating rear wheel slip to the player.
    - **Tarmac Road Acceleration:** Applies progressive slope feedback (`TriggerEffect::SlopeFeedback`) from 5% trigger pull up to full lock, where maximum resistance scales with vehicle speed (up to 70 m/s) and rear suspension squat/wheelie pull.
  - **Left Trigger (Brake / L2):**
    - **Grass / Surface Skid:** High-frequency vibration (`TriggerEffect::Vibration`) simulating loss of braking traction and tire lockup over dirt/grass.
    - **High-Speed Emergency Braking (`input.brake > 0.55` & `state.speed > 18.0`):** ABS pulse vibration on the brake lever.
    - **Normal Braking:** Realistic hydraulic brake lever profile with an initial 15% slack travel before engaging firm progressive hydraulic resistance (`TriggerEffect::Feedback`).
    - **Standstill:** Light lever resistance (`TriggerEffect::Feedback { start_position: 0.2, resistive_strength: 0.25 }`).

### 3.3 Rate Limiting & HID Saturation Protection (`is_substantially_equal`)
- Created `TriggerEffect::is_substantially_equal` with a fuzzy deadband (`0.04`).
- Avoids flooding the Bluetooth/USB HID pipe with redundant packets when the rider is cruising at constant velocity or idling.

### 3.4 ECS Architecture & Multiplayer Routing (`update_dualsense_triggers`)
- Registered `update_dualsense_triggers` in `PostUpdate` within `GamepadsPlugin` (`src/gamepads.rs`).
- Automatically powers down triggers if the game is paused (`Pause::is_open()`) or outside active race states.
- Supports both single-player (all connected DualSense devices mirror Player 1) and split-screen multiplayer (routes telemetry specifically to Player 1 or Player 2 based on `LocalPlayersConfig` gamepad entity bindings).
