# Earth Outcrops, Limbs Against Walls, and Loose (Not Shaking) Limbs

- **Date:** 2026-10-12
- **Topic:** Bikes went through earth outcrops and, a little, through walls (above all the rider's head and limbs when fallen); fallen riders' limbs shook.
- **Branch:** `claude/awesome-hamilton-hyvwrg`

## User Requests

> "Sigue atravesando los salientes de tierra y un poco el muro, el muro especialmente cuando cae, con la cabeza o extremidades"
> "Al caer que no sacuda las manos y las piernas, deben moverse libres, pero no sacudirse"

## Diagnosis

- **Earth outcrops:** physics only knew the walls at 11.5 m. The terrain is a grid with a vertex every 3.5 m: where rock rises behind the wall, its triangles start sloping up already inside the drivable strip. Probing both circuits: on Bahía 123 of 809 centre-line points (closest 10.6 m) and on Speed Bay 142 of 1343 (closest 10.4 m) have earth more than 0.3 m above the road inside the wall line — that is what bikes drove through.
- **Head and limbs through walls:** the thrown rider was kept off the walls as the hips plus points along the body axis; hands and feet spread up to ~0.8 m to the side, and the helmet, were not checked. A fallen bike's handlebar and tail, which stick out to the side it lies on, were not either.
- **Shaking limbs:** the previous pose added 17–23 Hz "asphalt jolts", a 2.2 Hz windmill and a flop tied to the tumble angle (several Hz while rolling fast).

## Implementation Details

- `src/track.rs`: `Track` keeps `edges` (per centre-line point, left and right: where the solid edge is) with `set_edges`, `edge_beside(&Nearest)` (interpolated along the segment) and `closest_edge_near` (for the early exit). Without edges (tests, before the world is built) it is the wall everywhere.
- `src/world/terrain.rs`: `Terrain::track_edges(track)`: from the edge of the asphalt outwards in 0.1 m steps, the first spot where the terrain as drawn (grid interpolation) stands 0.3 m above the road, also checking half way to the neighbouring points; capped at the wall.
- `src/world/mod.rs`: `build` returns the edges; they are cached per circuit (`TrackEdges`) and set on the `Track` resource on start-up and whenever the circuit changes (circuits are cached, the terrain is not).
- `src/bike/mod.rs`: `wall_contact` measures depth against `edge_beside` (wall or earth). The bike `footprint` gains the handlebar/fairing and tail circles, which move out with the lean, and the rider's lean reach goes from 0.9 to 1.05 (helmet over a metre up, hanging off). Upright, the footprint is the same as before.
- `src/bike/crash.rs`:
  - The thrown rider's footprint is now the hips, the helmet and the actual hands and feet, in the world; it is kept off the walls before and after the limbs move.
  - Limbs are now simulated: `ThrownRider::limbs` / `limb_velocity` (body frame). `pose(elapsed)` gives where they reach for (up and out when thrown, slowly drifting; tucked while tumbling; starfish while sliding, trailing behind; resting, slowly stirring), with continuous weights. `swing` hangs each hand and foot on a damped spring (2.5 Hz, 0.45 of critical damping) from that pose and makes it feel the body's jolts the other way (velocity change per step without gravity, capped at 120 m/s²) and the tumble flinging it out; limb speed is capped at 3.5 m/s. So they lag, flop on landing and fling out when spinning, loosely, without shaking. Removed the jolts, the windmill and the tumble flop.
- `src/bike/model/pilot.rs`, `rider.rs`: use `crash.rider.limbs`.
- Docs: `docs/gameplay.md`.
