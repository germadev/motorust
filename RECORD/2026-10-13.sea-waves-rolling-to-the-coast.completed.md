# Sea Waves Rolling In to the Coast

**Date:** 2026-10-13
**Branch:** `claude/loving-maxwell-vr7uub`

## 1. User Request
After checking the sea beside the track on Turbo Bay: "Las olas deberían estar más espaciadas, hay demasiadas, y deberían dirigirse hacia la costa. En la costa suelen verse más por el rompeolas" (the waves should be further apart, there are too many, and they should head for the coast; by the coast more are seen, where they break).

## 2. Implementation Details
- `src/world/sea.wgsl`: the scattered crest shapes (a jittered grid, two cells in three) are replaced by crests that follow the coast: lines at the same distance from the shore, one every 18 m, travelling in towards it (one spacing every 6 s). Each crest is broken into pieces by a noise that differs from crest to crest: out at sea about a quarter of it shows, short and thin; coming in (from 70 m to 6 m off the shore) the pieces grow longer and thicker, and they melt into the shore foam as they break. Waves fade out towards 400 m off the coast and, as before, with distance from the camera. The foam rings that rolled outwards from the shore (the wrong way) are gone.
- `src/world/sea.rs`: the shore map now has two channels (`Rg8Unorm`): red, the signed distance close up (−10 to 40 m) for the foam; green, the distance up to 400 m out, which places the crests. Close to the shore the fine distance (with its wobble) is used, blending into the coarse one past 30 m.
