## 1. The road round a round bay with an islet; the wooden bridge moved to the shore

### 1.1 User Request
- "In Loop Island, we take away the wooden bridge that starts at 1210 m; instead the road will turn right and come down almost to sea level, going round the inlet of water that is now on the right, and joining the current layout again where the end of the bridge would be. For that the inlet will have to be rounded, without making it smaller. And add a little island in the middle. We can add a wooden bridge like the one we took away between 1690 m and 1900 m."
