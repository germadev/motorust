# The Pro Controller's A, B, X, Y on its face buttons again

**Date:** 2026-10-20. **Branch:** `claude/sleepy-galileo-s35zhl`.

## 1. The face buttons' letters where the buttons are

### 1.1 User Request
- "A, B, X, Y are not well placed using a Pro Controller" (a screenshot of Options > Control: the Xbox controller drawn, the letters Y X A B in a small diamond near its top left, halfway to the face buttons).

### 1.2 Implementation Details
- A Switch pad is drawn on the Xbox controller's illustration without its letters (`pad_art::prepared`, `unlabelled`), the Switch's put on it apart (`mapping::spawn_gamepad`, `MappingLook::letters`), each centred on its button's box. Read without the letters, each face button's group of marks is left empty, and usvg gives an empty group a box at the corner of the drawing (0, 0): joined to the button's own, every face button's box ran from the corner to the button (274 × 73 px instead of 27 × 27), and the letters were centred halfway.
- `pad_art::draw`: a group with nothing in it (`is_empty_group`) gives no box; each face button's box is the button's alone, letters or not.

### 1.3 Verification & Testing
- A temporary test (not committed) printed the face buttons' boxes: with the letters, North at (260, 60) 27 × 27; without them, (137, 36) 274 × 73. New test `the_face_buttons_are_where_they_are_with_their_letters_or_without` (with and without the letters, the same boxes within 2 px). `cargo test --release -- pad_art`: 8 passed.
