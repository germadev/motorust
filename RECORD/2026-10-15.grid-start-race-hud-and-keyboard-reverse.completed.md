## 2. Implementation Details
- **The bots crashing at the start** (`src/bike/ai.rs`, `src/bike/mod.rs`, `src/track.rs`): reproduced in a simulation of the whole grid (every track, every difficulty): one or two falls at 1.1–1.2 s on every start. The grid is three a row (4.5 m left, middle, 4.5 m right), and most rivals' lanes are on the other side of the road from their slot (the third of the front row, from the right to 4 m left; the first of the second row, from the left to 4 m right); each rival's line began at its lane, so from the first step it steered straight across the bikes beside it. Now (a) a rival's line begins at its slot's (`Track::grid_lane`, at the start and when the bikes are sent back to the grid), and (b) a rival never moves across into a bike beside it, from 6 m behind to 6 m ahead (`BESIDE`): whatever it wants (its lane, its line, a gap to pass), it goes across only as far as the clearance short of the first bike in the way (`across_to`), and if what it gets to is not clear ahead it follows the bike there. As the grid sets off side by side, rivals keep to their side until there is room, then go to their lanes. The test pack, which allowed two falls a lap on four tracks, now races all five without one; before, the same pack fell 7 times over its four.
- **The dial's lit figure** (`src/speedometer.rs`): no halo at all, just the figure in blue or, from 300, bright red; the halo's colours, strength, spread and the blur that drew it are gone.
- **The race HUD, top left** (`src/race/hud.rs`): the place big (40 px) and how many race after it smaller (24 px, "1º / 8"), on the same baseline (`BASELINE`: Russo One sits 0.28 of its size above the bottom of its line, so the smaller text is lifted by that share of the difference, `after`); the lap as big as the place (40 px) between its word and how many laps, small like the place's total (24 px: "VUELTA 2 / 5", the total left out in free ride); a time with no word (`lap_times`): on the first lap its time so far ("0:50.2"), and from the second on the last lap's instead ("1:13.9", `Progress::last_lap`), in gold and bigger (21 px instead of 17) while that last lap is the one that beat the track's record in this race (`last_is_record`, from `Standing::record`). Two first versions, "ACTUAL … / ÚLTIMA …" side by side and then "ACTUAL …" or "ÚLTIMA …", were changed at the user's word; and the race's whole time ("TOTAL 5:50.2") only with Options > Game > "Mostrar el tiempo total" / "Show the total time" on (`Settings::show_total_time`, off by default). The best lap's line is gone (the last one's took its place). At the top left, what is not a figure ("º", "/", "VUELTA", "TOTAL") is drawn at 55 % of the figures' opacity (`WORDS`): each text there is a `Figured` (its text and colour), laid out by `show_figures` in a span for each run of figures (digits, and a colon or point between two, `figure_runs`) or of what is not, in the text's font, so the record's gold and the total's red pop go through it too. Places read with "º" in both languages (`ordinal`: "1º", "8º", also in the results, the arrivals and the notices).
- **The pause menu** (`src/ui.rs`): "Continuar" has nothing behind it unless it is chosen (then blue, as before); `Row::idle_background` went with it.
- **The keyboard turning the other way** (`src/pause.rs`, `src/bike/mod.rs`, `src/options.rs`): Options > Control > "Teclado: rapidez de cambio de sentido" / "Keyboard: turning the other way": fast (the default, as always: the turn drops at once and the other way starts as from the middle), medium or slow (`ReverseRate`): the turn unwinds back to the middle first, at 5 or 2.5 of the whole turn a second (from full lock, a fifth or two fifths of a second), and from there turns the new way as from the middle (`DigitalSteer::update`'s `unwind`). Like the other steering variants it is not saved and goes back to fast when the game starts.
- Docs: `docs/gameplay.md` (rivals, HUD, Options > Game and Control, pause menu), `docs/architecture.md` (rivals, speedometer, test counts).
