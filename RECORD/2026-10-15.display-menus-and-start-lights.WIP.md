# Display, Menus and the Start Lights

## 1. A Crash on macOS Retina, the Kind of Fullscreen, a Steady Track Card and Lights through Hitches (2026-10-15)

### 1.1 User Requests
> A crash on macOS (HiDPI), fullscreen at the dynamic resolution: `Attachments have differing sizes: the depth attachment's texture view has extent (3200, 1800, 1) but is followed by the color attachment at index 0's texture view which has (3840, 2160, 1)`; "con resolución dinámica en fullscreen me está mostrando 4096x2304, cuando la resolución nativa del monitor es 2560x1440p" (the dynamic resolution shows 4096 × 2304 on a 2560 × 1440 screen).

> "En macOS debería poder elegir el tipo de pantalla completa: Pantalla Completa Dedicada; Espacio Dedicado (Escritorio)" (on macOS, choose the kind of fullscreen).

> "El cuadro de la vista de selección de mapa debería tener siempre el mismo ancho, para que el mapa siempre esté en la misma posición" (the track select card always as wide, so its map stays in place).

> "Me da la impresión de que el tiempo que pasa desde el último rojo del semáforo y el verde no es siempre el mismo lapso" (the time from the last red light to green does not seem always the same).

### 1.2 Implementation Details
- **The crash** (`src/camera.rs`, `follow_window_size`): Bevy reads a camera's target size again only when its window reports a resize, or when the camera's projection changes. Setting a window size (Options › Resolution, `Settings::configure`) changes `Window` at once, but on macOS winit reports the resize only later (`request_inner_size` returns nothing there), or never during a fullscreen animation. The 3D camera, whose projection changes every frame, took the new size (3200 × 1800, 1600 × 900 points at 2×) and the UI camera kept the old one (3840 × 2160); they share the window's colour textures, sized by whichever camera comes first, while the depth texture is the 3D camera's size, so the main pass had attachments of two sizes and wgpu stopped. Now, before the cameras update (`CameraUpdateSystems`), every camera drawing to the window whose size differs from the window's has its projection marked changed, so all take the window's size in the same frame.
- **4096 × 2304**: not a scale applied twice. The Mac is set to a scaled Retina mode ("looks like 2048 × 1152"), drawn at 4096 × 2304 and shrunk to the 2560 × 1440 panel; borderless fullscreen draws at that size. winit reports the monitor and its modes in those backing pixels, with no mark of the panel's own size. Documented under Options › Resolution; a dedicated fullscreen at 2560 × 1440 draws one pixel to each of the panel's.
- **The kind of fullscreen** (`src/settings.rs`, `src/options.rs`, `src/locale.rs`): a setting `fullscreen_kind` (`FullscreenKind::Desktop`, "Espacio dedicado (escritorio)", a Space of its own over the desktop as it is, `WindowMode::BorderlessFullscreen`; or `Exclusive`, "Pantalla completa dedicada", the screen switched to a display mode of its own, `WindowMode::Fullscreen`), shown under Options › Graphics on macOS only (`CHOOSE_FULLSCREEN`). A dedicated fullscreen takes the display mode nearest the resolution chosen, or with "Dynamic" the monitor's own size. Elsewhere it is as before: a fixed resolution in fullscreen switches the screen, a dynamic one does not. Settings saved before the option, with a fixed resolution, keep switching the screen. Reset puts it back with the rest of the section; applying the settings watches it too.
- **The track card** (`src/track_select.rs`): the card under the track select screen was as wide as its widest line, the description's, so the card, centred, moved its map from one track to the next (up to about 120 px). Now it is always 1020 px wide (`CARD_WIDTH`: room for the widest description line, about 713 px), no wider than the window, the map never shrinking and the text taking the rest; the lap record line keeps its height when empty.

- **The start lights** (`src/race/lights.rs`): the hold after the last red light is random on purpose, 0.3 to 1.3 s, as in a Grand Prix, so that the start cannot be timed (the user was asked whether to make it fixed). On top of that, frame hitches changed it: green was timed from when the grid was set up, so a slow frame that showed the last light late shortened the wait, and Bevy lets a frame advance the clock by up to 250 ms. Now the lights advance at most 0.1 s a frame (`MAX_STEP`, so no light is ever skipped and after a hitch they carry on where they were), and green comes its hold after the last light was seen to come on.

### 1.3 Verification & Testing
- New test: the wait after the last light is the hold, give or take a step, at 144, 60 and 24 frames a second and through hitches of 0.4 s; a frame stuck for 3 s moves the lights on 0.1 s.
- The display mode test checks both ways of deciding the kind of fullscreen (chosen, or by the resolution where it is not), a dedicated fullscreen at a dynamic resolution taking the monitor's own size, and the desktop one covering the screen as it is.
- `cargo fmt --check` clean, `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` clean, and the full suite passes, 422 of 422. The crash fix and the fullscreen type cannot be run here (Linux, no Retina screen): they are built and tested as far as the display mode logic goes.
