### 1.3 Implementation Details
- **The lap** (`src/circuits/turbo_bay.rs`), 3068 m to about 4240 m: after the red bridge the road no longer turns west through the old town loop. It runs south up a gorge beside the river (`GORGE`, 2830–3100 m, a third `CHECKPOINT` arch at 2980 m), turns off left below the dam, climbs east along the hillside round a wide right-hand hairpin above the sea and back west, onto the crest round a left-hander (`CREST`, 3600–3758 m: an arch 125 m in radius, level at about 43.5 m), and comes down the far side, 40 m in 450 m, north through the town beside the beach to the seafront. The spans after the bridge moved (`TOWN`, `SEAFRONT`, the hotels, the BEVY billboard), with chevrons for the turn below the dam, the hairpin, the turn onto the crest and the one off it, and brake markers before the hairpin (the old ones by the bridge are gone). The island grew south and east round all of it (`COAST`); three smooth mountains hold the lake in and a smooth hill carries the hairpin (`climbed_massif`s); the gorge's rock wall stands left of the road up it (`Cutting`) and a concrete barrier on its right.
- **`Dam`** (`src/world/scenery.rs`), in the new `Scenery::dams`, with a bridge of the new `BridgeStyle::Dam` over its crest (so the crest has concrete walls and a paved shoulder, and the ground under it is free, as under the other bridges): which side the lake is on, its shore (a smooth closed curve that runs along the crest where it meets the dam), its level (40.5 m, 3 m under the road), the river from under the crest down to the sea with how high the gorge's far wall stands at each point, which bank the road up the gorge runs along, and where a square with a monument stands. `Span` is now `PartialEq`, so a dam finds its bridge.
- **The ground** (`DamGround`, new `src/world/dam.rs`, used by the terrain's `Shaper` past the walls and under the crest, faded in over its first and last 10 m, `CREST_FADE`): first the rim round the lake, holding the water in (0.8 m over it at the shore and a little more further out, for 45 m); then the gorge, cut through it under the dam: the river's bed under the sea (its water is the sea's, coming up to the foot of the dam), the bank on the road's side only dropping to the water, the far wall rising across it, ragged and rugged, and, at the foot of the dam, the valley opening out into a pool and a floor of gravel either side (`POOL`, `VALLEY`, closing in again 35–120 m down the river), so the face shows across the whole valley; last, the lake's bed, shelving under the water.
- **The dam** (`dam_mesh`): the concrete follows the crest, so the arched crest makes an arch dam. On the lake's side its face drops straight into the water; on the other it is nearly sheer under the crest and leans out more and more down to its foot (`FACE_LEAN`, `FACE_FLARE`), as an arch dam's, down to where it meets the ground (`face_depth`), and into the hillsides past the crest's ends (`ABUTMENT`). The crest juts out 1.4 m over the face (`CORBEL`) on a bracket every other point. The concrete is streaked down the face by vertex colours: each 2 m strip a shade of its own, many darker with damp from the crest down, a few rusty, the foot darker. With the lamps on both parapets (lamps on a bridge or a dam now stand on its deck), the lake's water (a fan from the middle of its shore, a dark, glossy blue: `lake_mesh`) and the square with its monument of two slender concrete slabs on a plinth (`square_mesh`, on a `Terrace` level with the crest).
- A first version had a gravity dam with a spillway pouring down its middle; after the pictures of El Atazar it became the arch dam above, with no spillway.

## 2. The Grass in Many Tones, and No Square Cuts on the Slopes (2026-10-16)

### 2.1 User Request
> "sobre la hierba, estaría bien aplicar varias tonalidades de vegetación, evitando los cortes cuadrados, ya sabes"

### 2.2 Implementation Details
- **Why the cuts were square**: since the borders were cut smoothly (`RECORD/2026-10-14.tsushima-track.WIP.md`, section 13) the snow, the tree line, the beaches and the flowers ran smoothly, but rock and grass were still told apart by the slope of each whole triangle, so the rock of every steep face ended in a zigzag of triangles.
- **The rock by the smoothed slope** (`src/world/terrain.rs`): each point of the grid keeps how much its ground faces up (`ups`, the upward part of the normal from the points either side, as the meshes' normals), a little more or less here and there (`CLIFF_RAGGED`), in its `Site`; the look of the ground (`look`) takes the slope from there, so `cut` and `border` find the edges of the rock along the triangles' edges as the others', smooth and ragged. `look`, `cut` and `border` no longer take the triangle's face.
- **`Greens::Mediterranean`** (`mediterranean_tint`), Turbo Bay's: swathes of straw-coloured dry grass, olive and grey-green sage, with patches of straw and dark scrub within them and a fine mottling, all on a warped field so no edge runs straight; fresher green in the hollows and by the water, drier up high and on the slopes turned to the sun, scrub up the steep slopes and bare earth on the steepest. The grass texture is a lush green photo (linear 0.153, 0.359, 0.039 on average), so each tint is the colour wanted over the photo's own, already as dark as it should be: Turbo Bay's `grass_shade` is back to 1.
- `docs/tracks.md` and `docs/architecture.md` describe both.

### 2.3 Verification & Testing
- New test: the Mediterranean greens are many tones (more than one in twenty points straw, and as many scrub) shading into each other (never a step from one metre to the next). The borders test (Ghost Island) passes with the rock told by the smoothed slope.
- Seen on the M4 mini: the rock on the hills by the coast road ends raggedly, no zigzag; the grass is olive and straw by the roads, with lighter and darker patches over the hills and dry straw through the town.

## 3. The Sun on the Dam's Face (2026-10-16)

### 3.1 User Request
> "mueve el sol para que se ilumine la presa"

### 3.2 Implementation Details
- **`MEDITERRANEAN_SUN`** (`src/world/sky.rs`): Turbo Bay's sun is now high in the north-north-west (heading 1.9π, 0.72 rad up; it was in the south-east with the plain day's), so the dam's downstream face, which looks north down the gorge, is in the sun all across its arch. `direction` is now `pub(crate)`.
- The Mediterranean greens dry the slopes turned to that same sun (`mediterranean_tint` takes it from `MEDITERRANEAN_SUN`, not the fixed direction the other greens keep).

### 3.3 Verification & Testing
- New test: the sun shines on Turbo Bay's dam face all across it (the face looks within about 70° of the sun at every point of the crest).
- Seen on the M4 mini: from the road up the gorge the face is pale and sunlit, its streaks standing out; the seafront is lit as before, with the sun now ahead; the lighthouse at the bottom of the dive is a little against the light, still red and white.
- `cargo fmt --check` and `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` clean; `cargo test` 508 passed, 1 failed (the pre-existing `a_very_hard_pack_races_without_wrecking_each_other`, in Loop Island).
