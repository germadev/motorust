## 2. Implementation Details
- **The performance mode** (`src/world/detail.rs`, `src/settings.rs`, `src/options.rs`, `src/render_scale.rs`, `src/camera.rs`, `src/perf_log.rs`, `src/bench.rs`): Options > Graphics > "Modo rendimiento (60 fps)" / "Performance mode (60 fps)", off by default (`Settings::performance_mode`). The shadows' automatic quality became a ladder: `AutoQuality` keeps a `step` down a `ladder` of `Rung`s (a shadows' level and a render scale) at a `Pace` (how long each look at the frame rate, how long to settle, under what share of the target it goes down and from what share it keeps up, whether it goes down several rungs at once). The shadows alone (Shadows: Automatic) keep their three levels and their pace (`SHADOWS_PACE`: 2 s looks, 3 s settling, under 90 % down, from 97 % keeping up). The performance mode: one view, eight rungs (shadows high at 100 %, medium at 100 %, medium at 90 %, low at 90 %, low at 80, 70, 60 and 50 %); two views (shadows already low) the render scale alone, 100 to 50 % in steps of 10; a look every second, 1 s settling, under 95 % of the target down as many rungs as it falls short by (each reckoned 10 % faster, `RUNG_GAIN`; three at most), back up after 10 s keeping up at 98 % (the hold doubling after a step up soon undone, as the shadows'). The target is 60 fps, or the cap if lower (`performance_target`). Changing ladder (the mode on or off, one view or two) starts it again at the top (`AutoQuality::on`). `Detail` gained `scale`, the render scale in use (the options' or the mode's), which the render scale, the split screen's halves and the performance log follow (`render_scale::render_percent`); `scale_the_world` runs before `choose_detail` so that it and the split viewports (in `Update`) use the same frame's detail. The interface camera's multisampling is now told from where the world's cameras draw this frame (an image or the window), so both change together. With the mode on, the Shadows and Resolution scale rows are faint and say what it has chosen ("Performance: low", "Performance: 80 % (…)") and cannot be changed. The benchmark keeps it off. The log notes `performance_mode=`.
- **Very hard** (`src/settings.rs`, `src/bike/ai.rs`, `src/race/mod.rs`): `Difficulty::VeryHard` ("Muy difícil" / "Very hard"), last of `DIFFICULTIES`. Its rivals dare 0.12 more than on medium (`VERY_HARD`), past 1 for most of them: past 1 (only on very hard) a rival brakes later (it plans with `PLANNED_BRAKING + DARING_BRAKING × (skill − 1)` of the brakes, 0.6 + 2 × the excess) and rides the corners harder (the corner speed from `max_lateral_accel × skill`: the bike grips more with the steering near its stop, `CORNER_GRIP`), its top speed the bike's (`max_speed × min(skill, 1)`). Up to 1 every formula gives what it gave, so easy, medium and hard are as they were. A lap of the quickest rival is about 2 % quicker than on hard (Bahía 22.9 s against 23.4, Ghost Island 80.5 against 81.8), with no wall touched nor the road left. Against the clock: 69 m/s, 1 s spare a stretch, 2.5 s more at the start (`CheckpointTimes::on`): a ride as good as its rivals' never runs short and earns 0–3 s a checkpoint, the start giving as much as hard's but the pace less.
- **The options over the race** (`src/options.rs`): the modal was at a local `ZIndex(100)`, under everything of the race drawn in the global order (the start lights at 5, the speedometers at 5, the minimaps and the pause menu at 10). Now `GlobalZIndex(OPTIONS_LAYER)` (50): over all of it, its legend with it, under the results (90).
- **Control's pages** (`src/options.rs`): Control is now Mapping, "Comportamiento Stick" / "Stick behaviour" ›, "Comportamiento Teclado" / "Keyboard behaviour" ›, the legend and rescanning the gamepads. Each behaviour opens a list of its own in the panel (`OptionsMenu::steering`, `Steering`), titled "OPCIONES > STICK" / "OPCIONES > TECLADO": the stick's turn limit; the keyboard's turn limit, sustained turn rate, return rate and turning the other way, their labels without the "Teclado:" before them. Back, Esc or B go back to Control on the page's row (`leave_page`); Reset there puts back only the page's options (`section_is_default` and `reset_section` take the page); changing section or going up to the tabs closes it.
- **Choosing a track before it is ready** (`src/world/mod.rs`, `src/track_select.rs`): entering the tracks menu, the other circuits are prepared one after another on a single background thread, in order (Bahía, Turbo Bay, Loop Island, Gold Mine, Ghost Island: the two heaviest last). Left and right change the circuit at once, and one not built yet was waited for on the main thread (`block_on`), behind every circuit queued before it: from Bahía, one press to the left (Ghost Island) stopped the game until all four were prepared. Now, in the tracks menu, it is not waited for: if its preparation has not begun, it starts at once on a thread of its own (`urgent_pool`, at full speed), the one queued in the background dropped; meanwhile the menu goes on, the old world hidden, and the card says "Cargando el circuito…" / "Loading the track…" (`CircuitLoading`) instead of its length; when it comes, `spawn_prepared_circuit` (now in every state, the chosen circuit first) builds it shown and gives its roadside to the track. Going on to the bikes while it is on its way goes as soon as it is there. Leaving the menu keeps the chosen one's preparation. Outside the menu (a championship going on to its next track) it is still waited for. The test that held the old behaviour (`a_circuit_chosen_before_it_is_ready_is_built_at_once`) became `a_circuit_chosen_before_it_is_ready_comes_without_stopping_the_menu` (an update under a second, then the circuit shown alone with its roadside).
- A first attempt at fitting Graphics' rows (now thirteen on Linux, fourteen on macOS, more than the panel holds at 1280 × 720: Frame latency and Reset/Back fall outside) by making them smaller when they would not fit was undone at the user's word; it is still to be solved.
- Docs: `docs/gameplay.md` (difficulty, Control, Graphics), `docs/architecture.md` (detail, options, test counts).
