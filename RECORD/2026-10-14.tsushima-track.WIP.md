Sky: day. Ambience: wind, birds, some sea and gulls, a few cicadas.

### 5.2 Implementation Details
- **Trees.** `tools/import_trees.py --keep` adds or replaces trees in the existing pack (their meshes and their cells of the texture moved to a 4 × 4 grid) without the other GLBs at hand. Six Japanese species (`Species::Momiji`, `Ginkgo`, `Sakura`, `BlackPine`, `Sugi`, `Bamboo`), with heights in `HEIGHTS` and classic shapes for the old look. Mend: the black pine's foliage (its green texels above its roots) spread three times as deep round its trunk (`MENDS`). Forests now name their species (`forest(span, &[species])`): sugi and momiji up the red ridge, bamboo down from the castle.
- **Props.** `tools/import_props.py` (new) packs 21 Meshy props into `assets/models/props/` (`props.bin`, `color.jpg`, a 5 × 5 atlas), sized by height, length or, for the gates, the opening between the feet of their pillars (11.63 m round the road's walls, 6.6 m round the corridor's). Mends: the great torii's slab of blue water cut away; the corridor's torii slimmed (pillars and depth, `SLIM`), so that one after another they read as gates, not as a wall; the water wheel split into the wheel with its axle, which turns, and its post (`water_wheel_frame`). `src/world/japan.rs` (new) loads them once (`ModelledProps`) and places them: on the lowest of the ground under them, or wading in the sea (the great torii, the shrine on stilts), floating (boats). Tsushima Isle has 43 props (`PROPS`), every one of the 21 models among them.
- **Corridor of torii** (`ToriiCorridor`): along the narrowed stretch of the red ridge a narrow gate every 6 m, and wide ones over the full road before and after. The checkpoint at 4150 m moved to 4230 m, clear of it.
- **Petals and leaves in the air** (`Petals`): red maple leaves up the red ridge and round the great maple, white cherry petals down the castle street. Each a function of time with no state (falls, sways, drifts downwind, starts again), one mesh and one unlit, double-sided material per kind, no shadows, drawn only near the camera. The red ones in *Ghost of Tsushima*'s duels are maple leaves, as Sucker Punch and the PlayStation Blog describe them; cherry petals fall elsewhere in the game.
- **The great maple's clearing** (`MapleGround`): a ragged carpet of red leaves ringed with 30 logs, on a level terrace (`Terrace` gained a `skirt`, how far its edge blends back into the land: wider where the land stands far above or below it, so the bank stays grass). First on the lake's open shore, 160 m from any road, it now lies beside the road round the lake (about 3115 m, 31 m from its centre line, its leaves short of the walls), the great maple on its water side leaning over it, a stone lantern either side. On the lake's side of that road the hedge became a shrine's vermilion fence (`WallStyle::ShrineFence`: posts and rails like the seafront railing, see-through), so the clearing, the flowers and the water show from the road: walls hide what lies on the ground near them.
- **Fields of flowers** (`src/world/flowers.rs`, new). A first version scattered clumps of cards in fields; from 30 m they were specks, and covering 60 % of the island that way would have taken hundreds of thousands. Now a `FlowerMap` (fields in order, the first one deciding; `FlowerArea::Road`, `Round` or `Everywhere`; patches of each kind; gaps of grass only where it is everywhere) tells the terrain where its grass is in flower: those triangles are drawn apart, with the lawn painted over with blossoms (`foliage::meadow`, one 512 × 512 texture per kind), so a field is a carpet of colour from near and from afar for the cost of a texture and a few more draw calls. Moss phlox (`FlowerKind::Phlox`, shibazakura) is painted pale and coloured by the terrain in wavy bands of magenta, pink, lavender and white (`phlox_color`). Near the roads (out to 34 m, clear of the walls), clumps stand up out of the ground in flower (not the phlox, which hugs the ground): two crossed cards and one lying over them from a procedural atlas (`foliage::flowers`), about 10,600 of them, one mesh per 64 m square, drawn up to 150 m, no shadows. Tsushima Isle's fields: white round the great maple, red spider lilies on the lake's open shore and by the castle street, moss phlox all round the lake, susuki over the susuki hills, yellow and white above the coast, and everywhere else broad swathes of all of them; the sunflowers are gone.
- **Performance.** Painting the six meadows takes about 0.5 s and the atlas 0.1 s: they are painted while a circuit with flowers is prepared, in the background thread (`Prepared::meadows`, `FlowerPlan`, as the trees' `TreePlan`), not at startup nor on the main thread; the other circuits paint nothing. The clumps are planned there too and only filtered by what stands on the ground when spawned.
- **The volcano and the sky.** The volcano moved from the south-west to the north, across the strait: a lone cone 900 m high, 1650 m in radius, its flanks steepening to a crater, its foot just behind the great torii, straight ahead down the castle street over the castle and across the lake from the great maple, as Mount Yōtei or Fuji; the lower ranges keep clear of it. Snow (`Ground::Snow`, a white material shaded bluer in ravines and on slopes turned away) lies over 560 m (±110 m along the ravines) on slopes not too steep; no other track's terrain reaches it. The terrain's cells far from the track now grow to 70 m at most, so a peak keeps its shape (about 18 % more vertices in the coarse ring). `SkyStyle::Clear` (Tsushima Isle): the summer day's light with the fog from 1100 to 6500 m instead of 450 to 3800 m, so the volcano stands out sharp; the fog's range now comes from the palette. The island's rolling hills rose from 14 to 22 m.
- **Answers given.** Meshy paints the textures of a model on its UVs; it is not meant for seamless ground textures, and none was needed: the meadows are painted in code. Displacement maps need tessellation, which neither Bevy nor WebGL2 offers; Bevy's parallax mapping fakes depth inside a surface at the cost of many texture reads per pixel, and the terrain is half the screen, seen at a grazing angle from the bike: not for the flowers or the altitude (which is the terrain's real heights); perhaps one day on the stone walls, measured.
- **Docs:** `docs/tracks.md` (the island's scenery, the clearing, the fields, the volcano, `japan`, `ShrineFence`, `Terrace::skirt`, snow, `SkyStyle::Clear`, the tests), `docs/models.md` (Japanese trees, `--keep`, the props importer and its mends), `docs/architecture.md` (`japan.rs`, `flowers.rs`, the importers, snow, the clear sky), `assets/CREDITS.md`, `README.md`.

## 6. Greens, Muted Colours and the Height Profile (after commit `101bfb9`)

### 6.1 User Requests
> "La base de la montaña se ve verde muy vivo, si vamos a usar verde, deberíamos usar un verde oscuro para la montaña. En general, tenemos un sólo tono de verde, que parece césped recién cortado. > "El rosa que se ve en el mapa, también creo que está muy saturado"; "La hierba en general, mejor más oscura en este mapa"; "El gran arce y las hojas a sus pies lo veo también muy saturado" (the pink too saturated; the grass darker on this map; the great maple and its leaves too saturated).

> "Cuando puedas, dame una curva de la altura del trazado" (a curve of the track's height).

### 6.2 Implementation Details
- **What was looked into.** Landscape painting's atmospheric perspective (greens turning cooler, bluer and flatter with distance; dark greens for conifer forest), environment artists' advice on grass (one green reads as a garden or plastic: vary hue, saturation and value by world-space noise, lusher by water and drier where exposed), and Mount Fuji's vegetation (evergreen conifer forest such as Aokigahara on its lower slopes, a tree line, bare volcanic scree above it).
- **Many greens** (`grass_tint`, `src/world/terrain.rs`): broad swathes (about 170 m) of fresh, yellow, olive, deep and bluish greens, patches within them, lusher and deeper by the water, drier and yellower on the heights and slopes facing the sun, earthier on steep banks, and a dark, cool conifer green from 70 m up (all of it from 170 m) and over the wild land: past the fine part of the grid and wherever the circuit's `mountains` rise. The grass in flower takes a gentler version, so the flowers stay bright. The tufts, the grass's ragged edge and the grass over tunnels take the same colour (`Terrain::grass_color`).
- **The volcano**: dark forest at its foot, bare dark scree above the tree line (`TREE_LINE`, 400 m ± 50 m: rock, coloured as volcanic scree) and snow above it.
- **Darker grass on this map**: `Scenery::grass_shade` (1 elsewhere, 0.28 at Tsushima Isle) multiplies the grass's tint, its green a little more, so it is darker and less vivid; the grass in flower half as much. Tried at 0.62, 0.45 and 0.36 first: the renderer's tonemapping keeps more of the light than the multiplier says; at 0.28 a sunlit verge reads (97, 145, 51) where it read (146, 212, 95).
- **Muted colours**: moss phlox's bands paler and greyer; the clearing's carpet of leaves and the falling maple leaves in autumn reds rather than pure red; the great maple's texture muted at import (`MUTED` in `tools/import_props.py`, saturation 0.62; its geometry unchanged, `props.bin` byte for byte).
- **Height profile**: a page (an Artifact) with the lap's height every 8 m from the game (a temporary test dumped it), its grade below on the same distance scale, the stretches, the crests where the bike takes off and the crossing over the tunnel, the figures (5.9 km, 51 m from 1.9 to 53.2 m, 103 m climbed a lap, +9.7 % / −10.2 %, 968 m steeper than 6 %) and a table by stretch.

## 7. A Japanese Light: Graded, Luminous, Not Saturated (after commit `8b72b60`)

### 7.1 User Requests
> "Los colores aún son muy vivos, para Japón no resulta realista. Debe ser algo luminoso y con contraste, pero no saturado. También se puede adaptar el clima y la luz del cielo" (still too vivid for Japan: luminous and contrasted, not saturated; adapt the weather and the sky's light too).

> "Sobre los verdes de los otros mapas, cuando terminemos con este, los revisaré uno a uno, por lo que sería ideal que sean independientes" (the other maps' greens will be reviewed one by one: keep them independent).

### 7.2 Implementation Details
- **Greens per track** (`Scenery::greens`, `Greens::Lawn` or `Greens::Countryside`): the coastal tracks are back on the arcade lawn exactly as it was before section 6 (`lawn_tint`, the old code, and no forest over their wild land); only Tsushima Isle has the countryside's greens. ## 13. Borders That Follow Their Lines, Not the Grid (after commit `af8807b`)

### 13.1 User Requests
> "No me gustan esos cortes cuadrados" (I don't like those square cuts), with pictures of the volcano's snow and forest lines and of the fields seen from above, all in steps; "en general, evitemos esos cortes cuadrados, especialmente cerca de la carretera" (in general, no square cuts, above all near the road), with a picture of the moss phlox meeting the sand of the lake's shore in a zigzag.

### 13.2 Implementation Details
- **Why they were square**: each triangle of the terrain's grid went whole to one mesh, by what the ground was at its middle (grass, rock, sand or snow, and the flowers in the grass), so every border ran round whole triangles: steps of half a cell near the roads and of tens of metres on the volcano, where the cells are big.
- **Cut along the border** (`Terrain::cut`): the ground's look is worked out at the three corners of a triangle (with the triangle's own slope, so the cliffs are as before); where they differ, the border is found along each edge between two looks by halving it five times (`Terrain::border`, to a 32nd of the edge, the same from either end so the triangles either side agree), and the triangle is cut there: off the odd corner in three pieces, or, with three looks, each corner's up to the middle in six. The new points on the borders take their height, normal and colour from the triangle; the grid's points are shared as before. The snow line, the tree line, the beaches, the fields of flowers (and the grass kept clear of the roads) all run smoothly.
- **What is drawn where** (`ground_drawn`, `flowers_drawn`, for the tufts and the clumps of flowers) is the piece of the cut triangle a point is in, so they agree with the meshes.
- **No slower**: what the look turns on but the slope (the snow and tree lines over a point and the flowers in it) is worked out once for each point of the grid, on every core, as the terrain is (`Site`, `on_cores`). Ghost Island's ground takes 5.4 s for the terrain and 2.0 s for its meshes on 4 threads (4.5 s and 2.8 s before); the other tracks as before.

### 13.3 Verification & Testing
- New test: the borders between grounds run smoothly, not in steps. Over the whole of Ghost Island every 9.7 m, what is drawn at a point against what the ground there is: by whole triangles 3764 points were wrong, cut along the borders 324, all within a 32nd of an edge of a border (the test asks for five times fewer); and every cut triangle's pieces cover it exactly, turning its way.
