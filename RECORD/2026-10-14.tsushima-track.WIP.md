### 2.2 To Be Modelled with Meshy (by the author)
Requirements given: one mesh per GLB, Y up, no ground plane, no text or kanji (painted in code), scale free (the importer sets heights); trees 400–600 triangles like the current ones; landmarks 3–8k; repeated props 300–1.5k. Text prompts rather than image-to-3D from the game's captures.
- **Trees** (into the tree pack, whose atlas grows from 3×3 to 4×4): momiji 7 m, ginkgo 14 m, sakura 8 m, Japanese black pine 9 m, sugi 16 m (the tree test allows 4–17 m), bamboo clump 12 m.
- **Landmarks:** five-storey pagoda 30 m, castle keep 35 m, great torii 16 m, ancient maple on boulders 14 m, temple hall 15 m, shrine hall on stilts 12 m.
- **Repeated props:** stone lantern, Jizō, log torii, thatched farmhouse (first batch); bronze lantern, komainu, hokora, stele, brazier, pile of supplies, fishing boat, water wheel (its turning in code).
- A `tools/import_props.py`, after `tools/import_trees.py`, for everything that is not a tree.
