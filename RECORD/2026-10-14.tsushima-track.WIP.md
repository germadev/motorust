## 4. The Layout and Its Heights (after commit `a4e8896`)

### 4.1 User Requests
> "1. Tsushima Isle 2. dale" (the name; go ahead with the layout of the proposal, sectors by colour and a figure of eight round the castle).

> "ya sabes que me gustan las pendientes pronunciadas y las carreteras sinuosas, no? tb estaría bien un tramo que rodee un lago rodeado de flores y algún que otro árbol característico" (steep slopes and winding roads; a stretch round a lake ringed with flowers and a characteristic tree).

> "me gustaría que un tramo pase por el pasillo típico de kioto" (a stretch through Kyoto's typical corridor: taken to be Fushimi Inari's thousand torii).

### 4.2 Implementation Details
- **Design tool.** A scratchpad model of `src/track.rs` in Python (centripetal Catmull-Rom sampled 512 steps a segment, resampled every 2 m; the closed Hermite profile; curvature as the game measures it), checked against Gold Mine (lap 5954 m, first split at 1706 m, both as the game has them). It reported the lap, radii, grades, vertical crests in bends, the clearance between distant stretches and the crossing's height, and drew maps.
- **`src/circuits/tsushima.rs`** (`TSUSHIMA`, fifth in `CIRCUITS`): 5886 m, 81 control points, 60 heights. A figure of eight round the castle hill: the castle street north from the origin into a tunnel (440–620 m), the coast in esses, the shrine's boardwalk straight across an inlet (1422–1622 m, walls 6 m from the middle, with a crest), the susuki hills up in esses at up to 9 % and over two crests on a straight, down at up to 10 % to the lake, three sides of the lake clockwise (175 m from its middle), north through the temple's split (3414–3764 m), up the red ridge at up to 10 % through the corridor of torii (3830–4120 m, walls 6.5 m from the middle), down along the castle ridge through the castle's split (4322–4672 m), over the tunnel 29 m above it (about 4810 m), down through the bamboo in esses and into the castle street.
- **Splits.** Gold Mine's proven shapes in local coordinates, placed with a heading: the temple's split is Gold Mine's second (its lap climbs, as here) turned north, the castle's is Gold Mine's first (its lap comes down, as here) heading west, with their rises. Placing them the other way round first left crests of 0.0035–0.0044 1/m on two ways (the shapes' exits are tuned to the lap's grade); and the lap's own crest where it went over the top of the red ridge straight into the castle's split added to one way's: the top is now before the split, the road coming down at about 4.4 % into it and easing to 3.4 % where it splits. Both splits are hardly banked (factor 0.25), as in Gold Mine.
- **Steepness and crests.** Slopes up to 10.2 %; flat out, crests may throw a bike only on straights: the susuki crests are shaped like the castle street's (Gold Mine's start crest), +4.2 m over 75 m and −1.6 m over 40 m; elsewhere, vertical curvature in bends stays under about 0.0011 1/m.
- **The lake and the island.** `Coast::OutlineWithLakes(&[Vec2], fn(Vec2) -> f32)` (`src/world/scenery.rs`, `src/world/terrain.rs`): an island as `Outline`, with lakes given as a signed distance; the terrain's coast is the nearer of the two, so the land drops to a lake right beyond the walls as it does to the sea (with `Coast::Function`, as at Gold Mine, the ground stays at the road's level for some 100 m and the lake would show only far from the road). The lake: radius 100 m, its shore wandering 8 m, an islet of 16 m for the characteristic tree. The island's outline brings the sea within some 45 m of the coast road and into an inlet under the boardwalk.
- **Placeholder scenery** from the other tracks: climbed massifs (castle hill over the tunnel, castle ridge, red ridge, susuki hills, bamboo slopes), crags and sea stacks, cuttings at the tunnel's mouths, stone/hedge/timber walls, a vermilion "TSUSHIMA" arch and five checkpoints (1000, 2200, 3150, 4150, 5100 m), almond/pine/broadleaf/tall trees, a dense forest where the bamboo will be, cottages in the castle town and a fishing village, two rows of sunflowers round the lake, a grandstand. `mountains`: ranges past 1900 m from the island's middle and a volcano 620 m high, 1150 m in radius, with a crater, across the strait to the south-west. Sky: day. Ambience: wind, birds, some sea and gulls, a few cicadas.
- **Song:** `assets/music/autumn_gale.mp3`, 175.8 s, −15.9 LUFS.
- **`src/bike/ai.rs`:** `rivals_ride_the_forks_on_their_side_and_back` checked where a rival was 20 s after leaving a point before each fork; with Tsushima Isle's splits 560 m apart, a rival from the temple's split is inside the castle's at that moment. It now rides until it is a little past the join (50–150 m) and checks it is on the lap there, having taken the way its lane says. The pack race includes Tsushima Isle.
- **Docs:** `docs/tracks.md` (five tracks, the section, `Coast::OutlineWithLakes`, the narrows, the tests), `README.md`, `docs/gameplay.md` (arcade of five, splits, narrow stretches), `docs/architecture.md`, `docs/building.md` (music size, test count).
