# Juguetería: wider spirals, gentle esses, the train on the outside and the glass ahead

## 1. The helix and the slide wider, the esses gentle, the train table's one wide bend

**Date:** 2026-11-02. **Branch:** `ccr-703edef7-3dm1sq`.

### User request
"The Toy Store circuit is too hard: the zigzags mixed with the banking feel like bumps; the two spiral climbs grip too much, so that riding onto them the bike turns too much and crashes into the inside of the bend: the bends should be quite a lot bigger. The passage by the entrance barely lets you see outside. The electric train is hard to see, being on the inside of the bend."

### Findings
- **The spirals.** The helix was 36 m round and gripped 2.4 times as hard (`Banking::grip`), as a step at its ends: the bike's yaw for the same steering jumped 2.4 times riding onto it, and it turned into the inside wall. The slide was 30 m round, two turns and a quarter.
- **The esses.** Across the first table (bends of 35 m), over the start straight (60 m), down the east side (40 m) and on the train table (35 m), each banked raised: the outside of one bend after the other rose on its risers and sank again, side to side, felt as bumps (the middle sinks to nothing where the bank changes sides).
- **The train and the glass.** On the train table the road turned right in a tight bend (32 m) 12 m short of the glass and ran along it in esses; the train's oval was in the middle of the table, on the inside of that bend; the glass was ahead only along the short straight before the bend, and then to the side.
