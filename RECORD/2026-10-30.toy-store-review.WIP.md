# Juguetería: a good going-over — the banking's bumps, the helix flat out, the shop's glass and the train

## 1. Bumps off the middle, the helix, the road at the doors and by the train

**Date:** 2026-10-30. **Branch:** `claude/motorcycle-keyboard-controls-lpfaaj`.

### User request
"Go over the Toy Store's circuit, give it a good going-over. The banked bends feel like bumps. The snail climbs, very tight, cannot be taken at top speed. A stretch should pass near the door and another near the train, so that both the outside and the train are seen well: with the borders they hardly show now."

### Findings
- **The bumps, measured** (a scratch test, kept as a test below): how sharply the road rises and falls ridden at 30 m/s, in the middle and 4 m either side. Every other track stays under 0.8 g anywhere; Juguetería had over 50 points past 1 g and up to 3 g, all off the middle (the middle itself only past 1 g on its ramps and kicker, which are meant). The bank was to blame: its raised bends bank 1.5 times a road's, so nearly all of them stopped dead at `MAX_BANK` (a kink where they reach it and leave it), and through its esses the bank swung side to side with corners at the arcs' ends. The outside of the road, raised on its risers, rose and fell with it.
- **The helix:** 36 m round. At top speed (80 m/s) the bike grips enough for no less than about 70 m (with the grip of a bend taken hard), so climbing it flat out was impossible; a 70 m helix does not fit the shop beside the slide.
- **The glass and the train** (screenshots in the game): the road on the train table ran about 40 m from the shop's glass, and the table's train went round the middle of the table, more than 25 m from the road.

### Implementation
- **`src/track.rs`:**
  - On a lap that banks raised anywhere (the toy store only: every other track's bank is as it was), the bank comes to its most softly (`tanh` instead of stopping dead) and is eased over 12 points either way more (`RAISED_BANK_SMOOTHING`), all round (only where raised, it jumped where its kinds of banking meet, up to 4 g at the helix's ends).
  - `Banking::grip` (1 on every track's spans but the helix's): where more than 1, the road grips the tyres that much harder across (`Track::grip_beside`, `grip_at`), and may bank as much more steeply (`MAX_BANK × grip`).
  - New test `the_banking_puts_no_bumps_off_the_middle_of_the_road`: 4 m either side of the middle, never more than 0.6 g from what the middle has (every track well under it; Juguetería now 0.3 g, the least of all, from 3 g). `slopes_and_banking_stay_rideable` allows the steeper bank where (and over the points either side where) the road grips harder.
- **`src/bike/mod.rs`:** `Ground::grip`, multiplying the corner grip. New test `the_toy_store_s_helix_is_taken_flat_out`: round every point of it, at top speed, the turn it needs within what the bike gives short of where it scrubs speed off.
- **`src/bike/ai.rs`:** the rivals reckon with the grip: their steering asks for less where it grips harder, and they plan their speed round a bend with up to 1.6 times its grip (`PLANNED_GRIP`). With all of it (2.4) they ran wide into the walls at the slide's and the helix's entries on the outside lanes at about 70 m/s (13 falls on very hard): their steering turns in late into a bend that tight taken flat out.
- **`src/circuits/toy_store.rs`:**
  - The helix grips 2.4 times as hard (`HOT_WHEELS_GRIP`), banked up to about 23°: climbed flat out. First the slide too: then the bikes came off it so fast that the way round the loop by the north wall, 90 m longer, no longer took about as long as the lap beside it (its booster drives up to 75 m/s, which they nearly had already: 6.9 s against 5.3 s); easing the loop's way out (bends of 60 and 70 m instead of 40) did not make up for it (6.5 s). The user's complaint was the climbs: the slide grips as before.
  - The train table: the road runs on along it 20 m further (`tools/toy_store_layout.py`: `S(60)` at the doors), straight at the shop's glass and round 12 m short of it; the table 146 × 108 m (to the glass's 10 m); its train's oval moved beside the road along it (its track 8 m from the road's walls: closer, the test wants the walls well clear); the brick tower inside the oval. The shop's doors moved east along the glass (x 320): their mat and the alarm's gates, 24 m in from the wall, would otherwise stand on the road; the giant robot and the checkout counter east of them, the trolleys west of them, a plant at either end of the glass. The lap 2968.6 m (40 m more: 20 south and 20 back); the spans past it moved on (`TRAIN_TABLE` to 2838, `HOME` 2838–2928, `DRAG_STRIP` from 2928).
  - Moved for the new road: the T-rex 20 m south with the home straight (its head over it), the teepee 1.5 m out from the start straight.
  - Against the clock, `pace` 0.8 (from 0.75): the helix is quicker.
- **`src/world/toys.rs`:** the risers' test wants over 60 banked points (from 100): through esses the bank now eases out.
- **`tools/toy_store_layout.py`:** the stretch at the doors; `--write` puts the Rust arrays into the circuit in place of its own (checked first to leave the file as it was with the lap unchanged).
- **Docs:** `docs/tracks.md` (the toy store: banking, helix, glass and train, spans, pace), `docs/architecture.md` (the tool's `--write`).
