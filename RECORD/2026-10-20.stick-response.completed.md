# Stick response: fast / medium / slow

## 1. Options > Control > Stick behaviour: "Stick response"

**Date:** 2026-10-20. **Branch:** `claude/sleepy-galileo-s35zhl`. (Item 3 of `2026-10-20.pending-requests.WIP.md`.)

### User request
"The stick's speed is very fast with some pads. Add in Options > Control > Stick behaviour an option of stick response: fast, medium, slow, using the current response as fast."

### Implementation
- **`src/pause.rs`:** `StickResponse { Fast, Medium, Slow }` with `factor()` (1.0, 0.65, 0.4), `name(lang)` ("rápida", "media", "lenta" / "fast", "medium", "slow") and `next(step)` over `STICK_RESPONSES`, like the other steering variants. `DebugOptions.stick_response`, `Fast` by default. Like the other steering variants it lasts for the session and is not saved (the pending note had said "saved"; it follows the page's other options instead).
- **`src/bike/mod.rs`:** `AnalogSteer::update(target, response, dt)`. The stick's steering already followed the stick at a capped rate: 3.8 per second leaning in and 12 per second going back towards the centre. Both rates are now multiplied by the response. Fast is exactly as before: full lean-in in 0.26 s, back from full in 0.08 s. Medium takes 0.40 s and 0.13 s. Slow takes 0.66 s and 0.21 s. Pushing the stick to the other side still drops the previous turn at once, at every response, so the steering never fights the stick. Both stick paths (1P, and each player of 2P) pass `options.stick_response.factor()`.
- **`src/options.rs`:** `ControlItem::StickResponse` on `Page::Stick`, after the turn limit. The row is "Respuesta del stick" / "Stick response" with `<  value  >`, and left/right step through the values. The page's Reset and its faint "already default" state include it, and so does the Control section's.
- **`docs/gameplay.md`:** the steering variants table and a paragraph on the response's timings.

### Verification
- New test `a_slower_stick_response_follows_the_stick_more_slowly_never_past_it`, in the 120 Hz step. Fast gets all the way over in 0.2–0.4 s, as before. Medium takes over 1.3 times as long as fast, and slow over 1.3 times as long as medium, still under a second. No response goes past the stick. `next` steps round the three values. The existing `AnalogSteer` tests pass the fast factor and are otherwise unchanged.
- `options` tests: the Stick page lists both items; resetting the Keyboard page leaves the response alone, and resetting the Stick page puts it back to fast.
- `cargo test --release`: 620 passed, 2 ignored. `cargo clippy --release --all-targets -- -D warnings`: clean. `cargo fmt --check`: clean.
