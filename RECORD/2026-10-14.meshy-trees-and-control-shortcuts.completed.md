### 2.2 Implementation Details
- **What came:** nine GLBs, each one mesh of 337–441 triangles in a unit box with three 2048² JPEG textures (colour, metal-roughness, normal). The species the tracks plant: umbrella pine, cypress, olive, tall, broadleaf, maritime pine, poplar, almond — all covered after the second batch; the fir is extra, used among the tall trees of the forests.
- **`tools/import_trees.py`:** stands each tree with the foot of its trunk (the middle of its lowest vertices) at the origin, scales it to the classic trees' average height for its species (measured from their variants by a temporary test: umbrella pine 7.9 m, cypress 10.4, olive 5.0, tall 16.1, broadleaf 8.4, maritime pine 8.8, poplar 11.8, almond 4.8; the fir 14), and writes `assets/models/trees/trees.bin` (the bikes' MRMESH01 pack) and `color.jpg`: the nine colour textures at 512² with a 2 px edge-repeating margin in a 3×3 atlas (1536²), the texture coordinates moved into each cell. The normal and metal-roughness maps are left out. 560 KB in all.
- **Performance** (the question): triangles go down (about 400 a tree against the classic trees' 650 on average as planted), draw calls stay (one material for all, one mesh per species instead of three variants), the atlas costs about 9 MB of GPU memory with its mipmaps (against roughly 600 MB had the 27 original 2048² maps been loaded), the executable grows by about 0.6 MB, and a texture read per pixel replaces the vertex colours.
- **`src/world/trees.rs`:** `TreeModel` (one per species, and `Fir`: half the tall trees, by the tree's seed), `ModelledTrees` (meshes from the pack, the atlas with mipmaps through the bikes' `imported::read`/`texture`, now `pub(crate)`, and one `StandardMaterial`), `Tree` (each tree's classic mesh and material and its model), `dress_trees` (PostUpdate, before the bounds are worked out: the trees just planted, and all of them when `Settings::modelled_trees` changes, their `Aabb` removed to be recomputed).
- **`src/world/props.rs`:** every tree planted with its `Tree`.
- **Options > Graphics:** "Árboles: Nuevos / Clásicos" ("Trees: New / Classic"), on the web too, reset with the section; it applies at once.

## 5. Styled Checkboxes

### 5.1 User Request
> "podrías darle un poco de estilo a los checkboxes de las opciones"

### 5.2 Implementation Details
- **`src/ui.rs`:** `Row::check: Option<bool>` and `Row::check(label, on)`: an option on or off, drawn by the options menu as a box after its value (if any) instead of the text "[X]" / "[ ]".
- **`src/legend.rs`:** a new symbol, `Glyph::Check` (a tick, two strokes meeting low left of the middle), drawn as the others by its signed distance into a white 64 px image with mipmaps, to be tinted.
- **`src/options.rs`:** Fullscreen, Controls legend, Pipelined rendering and Centre results are `Row::check`; Pipelined rendering keeps "on restart" as its value, before the box. `spawn_check` draws a 22 px box, 2 px frame, 6 px corners; `check_colors` paints it: on, filled with the section's colour with a dark tick; on the chosen row (already in that colour), dark with the tick in the section's colour; off, empty with a faint white frame (dark on the chosen row); faint when the row is. The value and the box go together on the right of the row; the rebuild key includes the check.
- Docs: `docs/gameplay.md` (the boxes), `docs/architecture.md` (options row, test counts: 401, Other 83), `docs/building.md` (401).
