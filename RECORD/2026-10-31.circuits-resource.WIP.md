### 1.3 Implementation Details
- **`Circuits`** (`src/circuits/mod.rs`): a `Resource` holding the circuits as a `&'static [Circuit]` (`Copy`), read from their files by `Circuits::load` (the files in `FILES`, now in the menu's order) and leaked once, as the game starts; it dereferences to `[Circuit]`, so `circuits[index]`, `.len()` and `.iter()` read as the array's did. `get(current)` gives the circuit chosen and `at(index)` any one, both `&'static` (for the work on other threads). `Default` loads them, for an app that has none. The `WorldPlugin` inserts it, and builds the first circuit's `Track` from it. `CurrentCircuit::get` is gone.
- **The tests keep a global of their own**: `CIRCUITS`, a `LazyLock<Circuits>` under `#[cfg(test)]`, read once as the game reads them; the 169 uses in tests stay as they were. Outside the tests there is no global left: the compiler pointed at each use, about 40 in 17 files, moved to the resource:
  - systems take `Res<Circuits>` (or `Option<Res<Circuits>>` where they took the `CurrentCircuit` so) and `circuits.get(*current)`: the halo, the minimap, the fly-over, the performance log, the sky, the portals' realms, the pause menu, the tracks menu, the race, its results, the sound's surroundings and the music;
  - helpers take what they need: `Championship::is_over`, `next_after`, `MusicMode::tunes`, `FreeMusic::tune` the number of tracks; `Tune::song`, `Tune::name`, `FreeMusic::describe`, `gain` the circuits; the modes' `description` the number of tracks; `track_checkpoints` the circuit; `checkpoint_lines` its name;
  - the world: `prepare`, `start_preparing`, `start_previewing` take the `Circuits` (on other threads), `preview::prepare_preview` its `&'static Circuit`, `Basis` keeps the circuit it is of (its rest and what the world builds read its scenery there), the world's `Builder` has the resource, and `menu_distance` takes the number of circuits;
  - the bench keeps the `Circuits` in its `Bench` (taken from the app as its plugin is built, or read if there are none): `tracks`, `minutes`, `rough_length`, `row` and `table` take them or their count.
  - `Track::default` (Bahía's track) is for the tests only now.
  - `choose_track` and `fly_over_track` came to eight parameters: `#[allow(clippy::too_many_arguments)]`, as the other systems with as many.
- **Test apps** that run systems wanting the circuits insert the tests' (`*CIRCUITS`): the music's, the lobby's, the results' and the tracks menu's.
- **Docs:** `docs/tracks.md` (`FILES` and the resource; how to add a track), `docs/architecture.md`, `ROADMAP.md` (the resource done; what stands in the way now: the bikes' `ROSTER`).
