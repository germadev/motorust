### 2.2 Implementation Details
- **Fullscreen resolution (`src/settings.rs`):** fullscreen used to be borderless at the screen's own size whatever the resolution chosen. Now, in fullscreen with a fixed resolution, the screen is switched (exclusive fullscreen, `WindowMode::Fullscreen`) to the monitor's display mode nearest to it in pixels (`fitting_mode`: nearest size, then fastest refresh rate, then deepest colour), taken from the `Monitor` the window is on (`OnMonitor`, or the primary one). "Dynamic" stays borderless. Since the modes are known only once the window is open, a saved fixed fullscreen resolution is applied on the first frames (`apply_settings` waits for a monitor).
- **Loop Island bridge (`src/circuits/loop_island.rs`):** a `Narrowing` over the wooden bridge (1230–1310 m), the fences 6 m either side of the middle (`BRIDGE_WALL`), closing in over 25 m before it, as Gold Mine's. The test of narrow bridges now checks every narrowed track.
- **Modes (`src/race/mod.rs`, `src/modes.rs`):**
  - `Mode::Story` is now `Mode::Arcade` ("ARCADE"): the four tracks in order, 5 laps, points; it always starts from the first track. The championship is no longer saved (`Records::story` removed; older settings files with it still load, a test covers it).
  - A race has 5 laps by default (`DEFAULT_LAPS`).
  - Elimination is no longer a championship: the track is chosen (it goes through the track screen) and it has 7 laps (`ELIMINATION_LAPS`, one fewer than the bikes). The last one is out as soon as all but the last two still in have crossed the line at the end of the lap (the third from last crosses it): of those two, the one further back goes. With two left, they race the last lap.
- **Checkpoints (`src/race/mod.rs`, `src/circuits/gold_mine.rs`):** against the clock the checkpoints are now the track's "CHECKPOINT" gantries (those the minimap shows) and the line, instead of fixed thirds of the lap that did not match them (`checkpoints`, `stretch_after`; `Progress::pass` counts them in order; the lap gates stay for counting laps). Gold Mine had two gantries 1.5–2.6 km apart; it now has five, about a kilometre apart (1200, 2640, 3540, 4440, 5250 m; none in the excavation or on the bridges). The time given for a stretch is now what it takes at 62 m/s plus 2 s (it was 36 m/s plus 4), and the start gives 3 s more for the standing start (`starting_time`). Measured with a rival of skill 0.9 (`checkpoint_times` in `src/bike/ai.rs`): at 36 m/s it banked 14–60 s a lap; now it earns 1–4 s at each checkpoint (test `against_the_clock_a_good_ride_earns_a_little_time_at_each_checkpoint`).
- **End of the race:**
  - **Autopilot (`src/bike/mod.rs`):** a player who crosses the line at the end gets `Autopilot` and a `Rival` (skill 0.85, the lane nearest to where they are, no loops): the rivals' AI rides the bike on, and `read_player_input` no longer writes its input. Restarting the race gives the bike back.
  - **Results (`src/race/mod.rs`, `src/race/results.rs`):** when the players are done the `Outcome` is shown at once as before (2.5 s later), but the race goes on behind it: `follow_the_race` and `eliminate` keep running, and `fill_in_the_results` updates the places every frame, so the others' times appear as they come in (those still racing read "racing", faintly). The race's results stand on the right of the screen, with no dimming, over the race; the standings stay in the middle. Going on ("Continue" instead of "Standings") settles the places (`Outcome::settle`): those still racing are placed where they are with the time they would take at their pace so far (`estimate_finish`, shown with "~"; not in an elimination, where they could still go out), and the championship is scored then (it used to be scored as soon as the player finished, with the others "racing").
  - **Watching (`Watching`, `watch_them_come_in`, `src/camera.rs`, `src/race/hud.rs`):** with the results up, the camera follows the bike nearest to the line of those still racing, cutting to it; once it is in, it stays on it 2.5 s and goes to the next. Top left: its name, place and lap; in the middle, as it crosses the line: "FINISH! Name 4th +12.3 s". The players' own HUD is hidden. In split screen the first camera takes the whole screen for this, which only happens once both players are done (the `Outcome` needs every player finished or out).
  - **"Next race" (`src/race/results.rs`):** going on removed the page resource, so in the same frame the race's results were built again and stayed up while the next track loaded. A `ResultsPage::Leaving` now keeps them away until the state changes (test `the_results_go_away_for_the_next_race`).
- **Start lights (`src/race/lights.rs`, `src/sound/synth.rs`, `src/settings.rs`, `src/options.rs`):** five red lights come on a second apart from 1 s after the grid is set up; 0.3–1.3 s after the fifth (from the real clock's nanoseconds) they go out, flash green and the race starts. Each light beeps (880 Hz), going out beeps higher and longer (1320 Hz). Until then `Race::held` keeps every bike on the grid (`simulate_bikes` skips them) while the players can rev, and the throttle does not start the race. Restarting lights them again. New setting `start: RaceStart` (Lights by default, Throttle as before), saved, in Options > Game as "Race start".
- **Menu background (`src/camera.rs`):** the welcome screen, the lobby and the modes share one flight over the track; the glide started between them froze the camera where it was and set it off again, as if the animation restarted. No glide between those three now.
- **Pause menu (`src/ui.rs`, `src/pause.rs`):** `Row::primary`: "Resume" is the main action, in blue (the 1P card's blue) when chosen and faintly blue when not.
- **Web page (`web/index.html`):** nothing is downloaded when the page opens: it shows the "Jugar online" button (with the size to download, or that it is already kept), and the `.wasm` and even the JavaScript glue (`import()`) are fetched only once it is pressed (or Enter or Space); then the progress bar and "Preparando la pista…", and the game starts.

### 3.2 Implementation Details
- **Layout (`src/options.rs`):** the list of options fills the panel and Back, always last, has `margin-top: auto`, so it stands at the foot of the panel on every tab. Game: language, difficulty, race start. Control: the bindings, reset, the four steering variants (moved from Game), the race legend, rescan. Control's rows are set closer (17 px, 1 px apart) so its fifteen rows fit the 600 px panel. The tips under the controls (sticks, M, fullscreen) left the panel: the sticks' are gone, and the sound and fullscreen shortcuts stand just under the panel, outside it, drawn as legend badges for the device in use (`legend::shortcut_prompts`, `spawn_prompts`); their room is kept on every tab so the panel does not move.
- **Gamepad shortcuts (`src/settings.rs`, `src/sound/mod.rs`, `src/legend.rs`):** Select held with Start switches fullscreen, with Y (the top face button) mutes and unmutes (`ToggleMute`, also read by `toggle_mute` beside M). `gamepad_shortcuts` runs in `PreUpdate` after Bevy's input and takes the presses out of the gamepad (`read_shortcut`): Select goes down unseen and, if no shortcut was made while held, counts as pressed (for a frame) when let go of, so it still restarts the race; Start and Y held with Select neither pause nor accept. The race legend shows Select+Y for the sound on a gamepad.
- **Difficulty (`src/settings.rs`, `src/bike/mod.rs`, `src/race/mod.rs`):** `Difficulty { Easy, Medium (default), Hard }`, saved. Medium is the rivals as they were; easy takes 0.07 off each rival's skill, hard adds 0.045 (at most 1), when the grid is set up. Against the clock (`CheckpointTimes`): easy 54 m/s + 3 s a stretch and 5 s more at the start, medium 62 + 2 and 3, hard 66 + 1.5 and 2. With a rival's ride of skill 0.9 the test checks it never runs short on any of them and earns 0.3–3.5 s a checkpoint on hard, 1–5 on medium, 2.5–9 on easy.
- **Graphics (`src/settings.rs`, `src/main.rs`):** "Pipelined rendering" (on by default; it was always off) and "Frame latency" (1, 2 or 3 frames queued, Bevy's 2 by default; it was 1). Both are read when the game starts (Bevy only uses them then), so the row says "on restart" while the choice differs from the one running (`StartedWith`).
- **Split screen at the end:** the first camera only takes the whole screen once both players are done (the `Outcome` needs every player finished or out), as it already was.
- **Grid order:** waiting on the grid (now visible for six seconds at the lights), the HUD read "0th / 0": the order was only worked out once the race started. It is now worked out on the grid too.

### 4.2 Implementation Details
- **Finishers (`src/race/hud.rs`):** in a race or the arcade, top right under the frame rate, with no panel behind (white text with a shadow), each bike that has crossed the line at the end, in order: "1st   Azura RZ   1:21.2" (`arrival_lines`). It gives way to the results once they are up.
- **Bindings (`src/settings.rs`):** a `Binding` also has `key_combos` and `button_combos` (keys or buttons pressed together; `#[serde(default)]`, so older files load). A combination counts while all of it is held (`key_value`, `button_value`: as much as the least pressed button) and is just pressed when its last one goes down. The `Bound` trait gives the keys and the buttons the same code: `action_of`, `remap(from, to)` (the action of `from`, on its own or the combination it is in, is done with `to` instead, in its place; `to` stops doing anything else; the action keeps its other keys) and `reset::<KeyCode>()` / `reset::<GamepadButton>()` (one device back to the defaults, the other untouched). `bind_key`, `bind_button` and `describe` went; `describe_keys` and `describe_buttons` name one device's, a combination joined with "+".
- **Mapping view (`src/mapping.rs`, `src/options.rs`):** Control's rows of actions and "Reset controls" are replaced by "Mapping", which opens the view in the options panel (`OptionsMenu::mapping`): titled "OPTIONS > CONTROL", with Keyboard and Gamepad in place of the sections (the one in use when it opens, following it if the device changes; across, Q/E or L1/R1 change it). The device is drawn: a keyboard of five rows with the arrows (`KEY_ROWS`, keys 30 px), or a gamepad body with its triggers, shoulders, D-pad, sticks, View/Menu, home and face buttons (`PAD`), marked as on the controller in use (`legend::button_badge`). Keys and buttons that do something are tinted in Control's blue, those held down are white, the one being remapped orange and the new ones yellow; with nothing going on, the line under it names what each key held down does. Under it, every action with that device's keys or buttons, in two columns. "Remap" waits for the key to remap (one that does nothing says so), then for the new key or keys: those pressed are gathered until all are let go of (`combination`), so one key or a combination; Esc or eight seconds call it off; M and Esc cannot be mapped. "Reset" beside it resets that device. Back at the foot, and Esc or B, go back to Control.
- **Docs:** `README.md` (modes, the lights, the finish, mapping), `docs/gameplay.md` (five screens, a "Game modes" section with the difficulty and the finish, the start lights, the HUD table, gamepad shortcuts, the options as they are now, "Mapping" instead of "Rebinding", what is saved, Resume in blue), `docs/architecture.md` (screens, code map with `src/race/lights.rs` and `src/mapping.rs`, pipelined rendering and frame latency as options, the tests table), `docs/tracks.md` (Loop Island's narrower bridge, Gold Mine's five checkpoint arches, checkpoint gantries are the mode's checkpoints), `docs/building.md` (the web page waits for "Jugar online").
