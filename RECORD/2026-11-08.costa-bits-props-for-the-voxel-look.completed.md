# 2026-11-08 — Costa Bits: Meshy's props for the voxel look

## User request

Bring the list of props to make with Meshy for Costa Bits
(`docs/costa-bits-props.md`, on hold since the pixel filter was dropped) up to
the track's look as it is: the ground in voxels, the trees and palms as flat
cards.

## Implementation

- `docs/costa-bits-props.md`:
  - The "on hold" note and the section on the pixel filter are gone. In their
    place, how the track is drawn and what that asks of a model: flat blocks of
    colour, chunky shapes, few triangles.
  - Each prop says how it is drawn (a new column, **Drawn as**):
    - as a **Model**, what stands close to the road or has a clear front (signs,
      the billboard, the buildings by the road, the car, the boulders);
    - as a **Card**, what is far off, round or plant-like (the watchtower, the
      hermitage, the castle, the cactus, the agave, the ibex). It is
      photographed by the engine's `src/world/sprites.rs`, as the trees are.
  - The engine photographs a mesh by its vertices' colours, not its texture,
    so the importer is to bake the cards' textures into their vertices.
  - Triangle targets lowered for the models (boulders 400, signs 200), and for
    the cards, which are drawn as two triangles.
  - The prompts' shared ending asks for chunky faceted shapes and flat areas of
    colour, no longer for a "16-bit" style, which makes Meshy paint pixels into
    the texture. The negative prompt adds pixelated textures, gradients and
    glossy surfaces. A few prompts are made blockier.
  - `palm_retro` is dropped: the palms are already cards.
  - "Sending them" names what is to be written: the importer
    (`tools/import_costa.py`) and a theme in `src/game/`, which may need the
    engine to offer its sprites to the game.
- `ROADMAP.md`: the props are no longer on hold.

## Verification

- Documentation only; no code or data changed, so no build or test was run.
