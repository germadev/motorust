## 2. Implementation Details
- **Graphics > Advanced** (`src/options.rs`): Graphics had more rows than the panel holds at 1280 × 720 (Frame latency and Reset/Back fell outside). Its three last options (Performance log, Pipelined rendering, Frame latency) are now on a page of their own, "Avanzado" / "Advanced" › (`GraphicsItem::Advanced`), the last row of Graphics outside the browser, titled "OPCIONES > AVANZADO" / "OPTIONS > ADVANCED". Control's pages became the menu's pages: `Steering` was renamed `Page` (`Stick`, `Keyboard`, `Advanced`), `OptionsMenu::steering` became `OptionsMenu::page`; each page knows the row it opens from (`Page::row`) and its title. `graphics_items` and `control_items` take the page. Reset on the Advanced page puts back only its three options (`advanced`, compared and reset in `section_is_default` and `reset_section`); Back, Esc or B go back to Graphics on the Advanced row (`leave_page`). Not shown in the browser, where none of the three is.
- **Scrolling the options** (`src/options.rs`): with the Advanced page, Graphics still had eleven rows on Linux (twelve on macOS, with the fullscreen type), one or two more than the panel (600 px tall, as on every tab) holds above Reset and Back at 1280 × 720: Reset and Back were cut by the panel's edge. (Making the panel taller, to 680 px, was tried first and undone for the scrolling the user asked for.) The list of options is now a list of its own (`OptionsList`, with its tab and page) that scrolls (`Overflow::scroll_y`, `ScrollPosition`), filling the panel above the foot, which is now beside it, below, and stays where it is on every tab. The rows keep their size (`flex_shrink: 0`). The list scrolls as little as needed to keep the chosen option in view (`ListScroll::showing`, in logical px, never past either end): as the panel is built again on every change, the list built anew is scrolled from the one shown until then, if it is the same tab's (and page's), measured from its layout (`ListScroll::of`, `row_in`: each row, `OptionsRow`, where it lies down the list); and after the layout of each frame (`follow_the_chosen_option`, in `PostUpdate` after `UiSystems::Layout`) whatever the panel could not tell (coming back from a page to its row, below the fold) is put right the next frame. Beside the list, in the panel's margin, a thin bar (`ScrollBar`) with its thumb (`ScrollThumb`) in the section's colour, as tall as the share of the options shown and as far down as the list is scrolled (`ListScroll::thumb`), only when they do not all fit.
- **Test before Remap, and chosen** (`src/mapping.rs`): the buttons at the foot of the mapping view are now Test, Remap, Reset, Back (`BUTTONS`), and the view opens with Test chosen (`Mapping::open` takes `BUTTONS[0]`), as does going down, or accepting, from the devices' row.
- **Ending the test** (`src/mapping.rs`, `src/settings.rs`, `src/options.rs`): a test now ends only with Esc, or with B (the gamepad's right face button, `END_BUTTON`) held down for 3 s (`END_HOLD`): `Mapping::ending` keeps since when B has been held, cleared when it is let go of, so pressing B to try it out does not end the test. Select with Start, which ended it before, does not any more (`Held::select_start` and `ControlsTest::ended` removed). The line under the device says "Esc / B pulsado 3 s" ("Esc / B held 3 s"), with the gamepad's own name for B.
- **Nothing works while testing** (`src/mapping.rs`, `src/settings.rs`, `src/sound/mod.rs`): the menu was already left alone while testing (the arrows, A, B, the shoulder buttons: `use_mapping` ignores the request in `Step::Test`, and the device shown does not change); the shortcuts now are too. With `ControlsTest::on`, the gamepads' Select with R3 (fullscreen) and Select with Y (sound) do nothing (`gamepad_shortcuts`), nor Alt/Cmd+Enter (`toggle_fullscreen`) nor M (`toggle_mute`, the sound plugin initialising `ControlsTest` too for apps without the settings' plugin). The buttons keep lighting up in the test as ever.
- **Fullscreen on the gamepad: Select + R3** (`src/settings.rs`): `FULLSCREEN_BUTTON` is now `GamepadButton::RightThumb` (the right stick pressed in: "R3" on PlayStation, "RS" on Xbox), instead of Start. Start is no shortcut's any more: with Select held it pauses as ever. R3 does nothing in a race, so held with Select it costs nothing. The legend's prompt takes the button from the constant.
- **Left as it is**: the very hard rivals' crossing at Loop Island's fork (a rival on the lap's right side crossed by one taking the branch to the loop), at the user's word: "quiero probarlo antes, no lo cambies".
- Docs: `docs/gameplay.md` (the options scrolling, Graphics' Advanced page, the gamepad's fullscreen shortcut and why Select's shortcuts leave Start alone, the mapping view's foot and its test), `docs/architecture.md` (options, mapping, settings, test counts).
