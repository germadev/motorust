# La Juguetería (The Toy Store) circuit

**Date:** 2026-10-19
**Status:** Completed and verified
**Commit:** not committed yet

## User request

After a design discussion about a toy-store map at toy scale, the user said
"ok, dale!": implement a new circuit called **La Juguetería** / **The Toy
Store** with Scalextric, Hot Wheels, a slide, an electric train and toy-sized
bikes.

## Implementation

### Approach

The bikes keep their usual size, physics and track dimensions; the world is
built at the scale of the toys (giant shelves, blocks, dice and balls), so the
bikes feel like toy motorbikes riding through a shop.

### New files and changes

- `src/circuits/toy_store.rs`: the new `Circuit`.
  - 20 control points, lap 3524.6 m.
  - Sections: start straight with two take-off ramps, Scalextric sweeper and
    narrow slot lane, train table and tunnel, Hot Wheels, building blocks,
    slide back to the shop floor, plush aisle and checkout.
  - Checkpoints at 880, 1760 and 2640 m.
  - Elevation profile with two toy ramps (2.7 m and 2.2 m) on the start
    straight, a tabletop around 22–30 m, and a banked descent from 2980 m.
  - Scenery: checkered soil, concrete/timber/checkered/railing walls, a
    papier-mâché massif and cuttings, a model water table beside the sweeper,
    start grandstand, billboards, gantries and a giant loop.
  - `TOYS` data: shelf rows, a toy train, block piles, dice and balls.
- `src/world/scenery.rs`: `Toys`, `Shelf`, `ToyTrain`, `ToyBlockPile`,
  `GiantDice` and `GiantBall`; `Scenery::toys: Option<Toys>`.
- `src/world/toys.rs`: `spawn_toys`, batched meshes for shelves, rails, blocks,
  dice and balls, moving `ToyTrainCar` carriages (`roll_trains`), and helper
  geometry (`box_at`, `beside`, `lowest_ground`, `pip_grid`).
- `src/world/mod.rs`: `mod toys;` and the `roll_trains` system.
- `src/world/props.rs`: calls `spawn_toys` after the other scenery.
- `src/circuits/mod.rs`: registers `toy_store`, grows `CIRCUITS` to seven and
  adds the checkpoint expectations.
- All six existing circuits now pass `toys: None`.
- `docs/tracks.md`, `docs/architecture.md` and `README.md` document the new
  track, its toys module and the seven-track count.

### Layout tuning notes

- The first draft was too smooth: it failed the banking test (no corner over
  0.08 rad) and the very-hard-vs-hard AI test (ratio 0.9957, only 0.43 %
  faster instead of more than 0.5 %).
- Two compact tight corners (32.7 m and about 39 m radius) were added: one in
  the final plush section and one in the Hot Wheels area. The final corner is
  the tightest at 32.7 m, above the 28.75 m minimum.
- A first chicane attempt was far too tight (10–23 m) and another S was too
  long and slow, failing the checkpoint-clock test. The final geometry keeps
  the lap fast enough for the clock test while giving the very-hard rivals
  enough braking zones (ratio 0.9929).
- Ramp heights were reduced to 2.7 m and 2.2 m so the bike flies twice but
  never above 1.3 m or for more than 0.75 s.
- Checkpoint spacing was set to roughly a quarter-lap (880/1760/2640 m) so
  every stretch is between 550 m and 1500 m.
- A model water table beside the sweeper provides the shore the sea tests
  expect, and cuttings provide the earth that rises before the walls.
