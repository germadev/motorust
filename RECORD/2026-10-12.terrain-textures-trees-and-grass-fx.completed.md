## 3. Implementation Details

### 3.1 Terrain Texture De-Repetition & Multi-Scale Shading
- **Macro Detrending Filter (`src/world/textures.rs`):**
  - Enhanced procedural and loaded terrain textures with luminance detrending.
  - Calculated average image luminance and applied high-pass/low-pass balance to dampen low-frequency repeating blotches when textures tile across large distances.
  - Fine-tuned grass, rock, and sand color balances to preserve bright 90s arcade aesthetic while avoiding tiled grid artifacts.
- **UV Domain Warping & Organic Vertex Colors (`src/world/terrain.rs`):**
  - Inserted fractional Brownian motion (`fbm`) domain warping into vertex UV coordinates (`Vec2(x + warp.x, z + warp.y)`), breaking straight repetitive tile boundaries across hills and plains.
  - Implemented `ground_vertex_color` calculating per-vertex RGB tinting:
    - **Grass:** Blends between vibrant meadow greens, warm sunlit hillside yellows, and deep natural hollow shades, with steeper inclines showing subtle earthy dry slopes.
    - **Rock:** Horizontal sedimentary strata layers and mineral patches mixing limestone, slate, and neutral rock.
    - **Sand:** Height-based sea-level moisture gradient and wind-blown dune variations.
  - Modified `Builder` in `src/world/terrain.rs` to insert `Mesh::ATTRIBUTE_COLOR`, multiplying vertex colors seamlessly into the standard PBR shading pipeline.

### 3.2 Tree Variety, Multi-Canopy Geometry, and Flora Density
- **Expanded Tree Species (`src/world/scenery.rs`):**

  - Total species count now includes: `UmbrellaPine`, `Cypress`, `Olive`, `Tall`, `Broadleaf`, `MaritimePine`, `Poplar`, `Almond`.
- **Canopy Sculpting & Multi-Branch Geometry (`src/world/props.rs`):**
  - Created `CrownStyle` enum: `Umbrella`, `Spire`, `Cloud`, and `ConicalTier`.
  - Upgraded `crown_mesh` to shape icosahedron spheres into distinct biological profiles with directional sunlight shading and ambient occlusion.
  - Replaced single-cylinder trunks with branched structures (`TreeVariant { wood: Vec<...>, crowns: Vec<...> }`).
  - Updated `TreeAssets` with shared GPU mesh handles per species and variant.
  - Raised default tree density and population limits (from 1,000 to 2,200) across `Bay` and `Speed Bay` circuits (`src/circuits/bay.rs`, `src/circuits/speed_bay.rs`).

### 3.3 Dynamic Grass Kickup Particle VFX (`src/bike/grass_kickup.rs`)
- **Particle System Architecture:**
  - Added `GrassKickup` component attached to every bike entity (`src/bike/mod.rs`).
  - Created dedicated plugin `GrassKickupPlugin` with systems running during `GameState::Race`:
    - `kick_up_grass`: Detects when a grounded bike is riding on off-road `Surface::Grass` at speeds exceeding 2.0 m/s.
    - Rate of emission scales linearly with forward bike speed (`state.speed * 1.85`).
    - 85% of particles spawn behind the rear drive wheel with upward and backward velocity; 15% spawn from the front steering wheel.
    - Ejection velocity scales directly with speed, launching blades higher and farther at high velocities.
    - `update_grass_particles`: Computes parabolic arc under arcade gravity (`-13.5 m/s²`), mild drag, continuous 3D rotation, and cubic shrink-on-fade before despawn.
  - Batched GPU rendering: Uses 4 shared mesh variants (`grass_blade_mesh` variations and `grass_clump_mesh` crossed root clumps) with vertex gradients, sharing a single double-sided `StandardMaterial`.
  - Cleanup on race restart: despawns all existing particles immediately upon restarting the race.

## 5. Follow-Up: Rock Texture De-Repetition & Geological Shading Overhaul

### 5.1 User Request
> "la textura de las rocas aún es muy repetitiva, tiene mucho margen de mejora"

### 5.2 Root Causes Identified
1. **Severe Artifact in `rock.jpg`:** The underlying 512x512 texture contained a high-contrast horizontal fracture spanning across the middle (y ≈ 230) and large dark corner patches. High-pass enhancement had magnified these cracks, turning them into noticeable repeating black bars every 12 meters.
2. **Synchronized Planar Projection:** Rock faces used standard world-elevation projection (`-point.y`), meaning every cliff across the whole circuit repeated the horizontal fracture at the exact same world elevations.
3. **Small Tile Size:** `ROCK_TILE = 12.0m` repeated 5 times on a typical 60m cliff and dozens of times along straights.
4. **Minimal Vertex Color Modulation:** Previous vertex colors only shifted by ±5%, leaving the underlying texture almost raw.

### 5.3 Implementation Details
- **Dual-Phase Seamless Texture Decorrelation (`src/world/textures.rs`):**
  - Integrated dual-phase periodic blending: samples the base pixel `p(x, y)` and a half-period offset pixel `p((x + W/2) % W, (y + H/2) % H)`.
  - Weighted by a continuous harmonic sinusoidal mask `(fx * 2.0).sin() * (fy * 3.0).cos()`, breaking the unbroken horizontal crack into natural, interlocking stone blocks while maintaining 100% C-infinity wrap continuity.
  - Implemented a wide-kernel (17x17) macro detrending step to cancel out the dark corner-to-corner lighting gradients present in the raw photograph.
  - Calibrated the tone curve to warm golden Mediterranean limestone and granite without crushing dark fissures into pure black.
- **3D Continuous Domain Warping & Geological Dip Angle (`src/world/terrain.rs`):**
  - Warped 3D world coordinates before planar projection: `warped_p = p + Vec3(warp_x * 9.0, warp_y * 7.5, warp_z * 9.0)`. Shifting `y` by up to ±3.75m desynchronizes the elevation of rock features across cliffs.
  - Scaled tile size from `12.0m` to `20.0m` (`ROCK_TILE = 20.0`), expanding the surface area per tile by 2.77x (from 144 m² to 400 m²).
  - Applied a 14-degree geological dip angle rotation to UV coordinates, slanting strata and fracture lines naturally along mountain topography.
  - Oriented horizontal projection axes (`face.x > 0.0`, `face.z > 0.0`) in `rock_projection` for continuous texture flow around cliff turns.
- **Multi-Tier Geological Vertex Shading (`src/world/terrain.rs`):**
  - **Macro Massifs (~120m scale):** Low-frequency noise splits circuits into 3 regional rock types: terracotta/ochre sandstone, sun-baked golden limestone, and cool dark slate/basalt.
  - **Sedimentary Strata Bands (~6m - 14m scale):** Inclined strata layers alternating between lighter mineral deposits and darker dense shale.
  - **Directional Sunlight & Crevice AO:** Sun-facing slopes catch warm golden direct highlights, while steep hollows and wall bases receive ambient occlusion shadowing.
  - **Sheltered Lichen / Moss Accents:** Moderate-slope sheltered crevices receive subtle organic green tints.
