# Green Hill: a Third Track Inspired by Sonic the Hedgehog

- **Date:** 2026-10-12
- **Topic:** New track, Green Hill, with the scenery features it needs: checkerboard earth, giant loops, totem poles, sunflowers, spinning rings, waterfalls and a wooden bridge.
- **Branch:** `claude/epic-knuth-fcgte2`

## Implementation Details

### The track (`src/circuits/green_hill.rs`)

- A 2.1 km clockwise lap round an island (`Coast::AroundTrack`), with an inlet where the sea runs in under the bridge. Third in `CIRCUITS` (`src/circuits/mod.rs`).
- Layout designed with a Python mirror of `Track` (centripetal Catmull-Rom, 2 m resampling, curvature, clearance and grade) before porting: tightest corner 53 m, distant stretches at least 88 m apart, steepest grade 9.8 %.
- Elevation as a roller coaster: crests where the bike takes off at about 195 m (start straight), 1410 m (after the bridge) and 1775 m (south shore), and the high ground of the tunnel (18 m). Two extra-banked spans: the long right-hander after the tunnel (×1.6) and the last corner (×1.5).
- Scenery: checkerboard mesas inside the lap, a ridge for the tunnel (770–870 m), cuttings for the waterfalls, hedges on the start straight and checkerboard walls elsewhere, yellow and blue curbs, palms, broadleaf trees, sunflowers, five totem poles, two loops, four lines of rings, two waterfalls, a wooden bridge (1230–1310 m), a blue "GREEN HILL" arch and two checkpoints (700 m and 1420 m).

### New scenery features (all optional, empty for Bahía and Speed Bay)

- `src/world/scenery.rs`: `Soil` (`Rock` or `Checkered`), `BridgeStyle` (`RedArches` or `Wooden`), `WallStyle::Checkered`, and the `Totem`, `Loop`, `Rings` and `Waterfall` specs. New `Scenery` fields: `soil`, `bridge_style`, `sunflowers`, `totems`, `loops`, `rings`, `waterfalls`.
- `src/world/textures.rs`: procedural `soil` (two by two chocolate and caramel squares with a bevel and grain), `falls` (seamless streaks of falling water) and `planks` textures, through a shared `tiling` helper.
- `src/world/terrain.rs`: with `Soil::Checkered`, steep faces use the soil texture with a plain planar projection (straight rows at the same heights on every cliff, no warp or dip) and light-and-shade vertex colours (`soil_vertex_color`).
- `src/world/road.rs`: checkerboard walls; on wooden bridges `wall_style` returns `None`, as the bridge brings its own fence.
- `src/world/structures.rs`: `wooden_bridge_meshes` (planks over the road and round the deck, a fence of posts and two rails, log piers with crossbeams); tunnels through checkerboard earth are lined with it.
- `src/world/landmarks.rs` (new):
  - Loops: a ribbon swept round a circle with a corkscrew shift so its ends sit side by side, grass running surface and lip, checkerboard sides, on a block of earth that stands above the highest ground under it. Oriented in the plane of the road, so they are seen side on.
  - Totem poles, sunflowers: one shared mesh each with vertex colours, turned to face the road.
  - Rings: one shared torus and gold material; the `Spin` component turns them (`spin` system).
  - Waterfalls: one sheet for all of a track's waterfalls, draped a little in front of the rock face from the stream on top to the foot of the wall, with foam puffs; the `flow` system slides the shared material's `uv_transform` so the water runs down.
  - Loops, totems and sunflowers return their footprints so trees are not planted on them.
- `src/world/mod.rs`: registers `landmarks::spin` and `landmarks::flow` in `Update` (transform and single-material updates, no spawning).
- `src/world/props.rs`: `Footprint` and `row_points` shared with `landmarks`.
