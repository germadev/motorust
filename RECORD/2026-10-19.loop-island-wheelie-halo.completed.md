# Loop Island: a blue halo from the rear wheel riding a wheelie

**Date:** 2026-10-19. **Commits:** on branch `claude/happy-ramanujan-ijcsku` (PR germadev/motorust#20), after `aa8e027`.

## User Request
- "Only on Loop Island, make the rear wheel leave a blue halo when doing a wheelie."

## Implementation Details
- **Which circuits** (`Scenery::wheelie_halo: Option<Color>`): the colour of the halo, or `None`; only Loop Island has one (`SPIN_HALO`, Sonic's blue, sRGB 0.2, 0.5, 1.0), every other circuit `None`.
- **The halo** (`src/bike/wheelie_halo.rs`, `WheelieHaloPlugin`, registered with the bikes' plugins): while a bike rides a wheelie (`Chassis::wheelie_lift` over 0.2), on the road, not on a loop, faster than 3 m/s, a soft disc of light is left at its rear wheel's hub (0.68 m behind its middle, 0.31 m up) every 30 cm it goes, back along the way the wheel came in the frame (so that they lie evenly whatever the frame rate), and stays where it was left, swelling from 0.9 to 1.5 m across and fading out over 0.45 s: a glowing trail behind the wheel. Each disc is the dust puffs' mesh, four soft discs crossed (`grass_kickup::puff_mesh`, `puff_image`), its light added over what is behind it, unlit, casting no shadow, fading through eight shared materials (so the discs draw in batches). Its mesh and materials are made on entering a race on a circuit with a halo, and none on the others.
- Tests: `only_loop_island_leaves_a_blue_halo`; `a_disc_glows_at_once_and_fades_out_as_it_swells`; `a_halo_is_left_only_riding_a_wheelie` (not on both wheels, in the air or barely going); `a_bike_riding_a_wheelie_leaves_a_trail_of_discs_behind_its_rear_wheel` (one every 30 cm of the way gone, at the hub's height, behind it).
- Docs: `docs/tracks.md` (Loop Island; the `wheelie_halo` row of the scenery's fields), `docs/architecture.md` (the module).
