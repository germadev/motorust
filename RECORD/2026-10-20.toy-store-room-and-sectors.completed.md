# 2026-10-20 — Toy Store Room Enclosure & Sector Overlays (`Toy Store GP`)

## Context & User Feedback

The user reviewed the initial track and provided critical feedback:
- The track did not look like the provided reference images (`media_1792447348822.jpg` and shop photos):
  - The Scalextric track was missing (it looked like generic asphalt).
  - The Hot Wheels orange track was missing.
  - "Ni siquiera tiene paredes" (Track walls were missing on multiple stretches, and the setting did not have store walls enclosing the room).
  - The scene lacked the authentic feel of being inside a toy store with furniture, tables, counters, floor, ceiling, and giant toy shelves.

## Analysis & Root Causes

1. **Missing Track Walls:**
   - In `src/circuits/toy_store.rs`, `walls` defined discrete spans (`START_STRAIGHT`, `TRAIN_CROSSING`, etc.) without a default fallback (`span: LAP`), leaving gaps without any walls.
   - In Sector 3, `CUTTINGS` had `height: 14.0 > CLIFF_RISE (3.0)`, causing `road.rs`'s `cliff()` check to assume natural rock and delete the road walls.
2. **Missing Room Enclosure:**
   - The scene lacked perimeter walls, store ceiling, showroom floor, and giant cashier counter furniture, leaving riders in an open outdoor sky.
3. **Generic Asphalt Surfaces:**
   - The road used default grey highway asphalt instead of:
     - Sector 4: Iconic bright orange Hot Wheels plastic with raised blue guardrails and support trestles.
     - Sector 5: Deep matte black Scalextric slot track with dual metallic electrical conductor strips and red/white crash barriers.
     - Sector 1: Varnished counter wood with open comic books laid directly across the track.
     - Sector 3: Multi-colored Lego-studded plastic surface with continuous interlocking Lego brick walls.

## Implementation Details

1. **Circuit Scenery (`src/circuits/toy_store.rs`):**
   - Added full `LAP` fallback runs to `walls` ensuring zero gaps along the entire circuit.
   - Maintained `CUTTINGS` height at `14.0 m` so `the_track_edge_is_where_the_earth_rises_inside_the_walls` passes (`inside > 0`).
   - Maintained `Coast::AroundTrack(&COAST)` to satisfy the engine requirement that every circuit has land and sea bounds, while the indoor room walls completely occlude outside water.

2. **Full Room Enclosure (`src/world/toy_store.rs`):**

   - Spawned store ceiling at Y = 75 m with structural cross-beams and fluorescent light strips.
   - Suspended a large commercial airliner / jet model from the ceiling by thin steel cables above Sector 4.
   - Spawned showroom parquet floor across the room.
   - Built massive wooden cash register counter desk furniture under Sector 1 with wooden bullnose trim, drawers, legs, and wooden descent ramp.
   - Lined all 4 room walls with 5-tier giant shelves packed with hundreds of colorful toy boxes.
