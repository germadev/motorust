# 2026-11-10 — Costa Bits: Meshy's props, as they come

On `ccr-e7021c37-ulgd1v` (PR #38).

## User request

The author is sending the props of `docs/costa-bits-props.md` as Meshy makes
them, five at a time, to be looked over before they are imported.

## Looked over

Each GLB checked for its triangles, its axes (one textured mesh in a unit box,
up along +Y, front to +Z, as `tools/import_props.py` reads them) and rendered
from five sides.

- Good as they are: `white_house` (1,939 triangles), `chiringuito` (2,431),
  `espeto_boat` (1,379), `watchtower` (1,580, a card), `lifeguard_tower`
  (981; mostly red, its white stripes only on the railings), `flag_marshal`
  (2,566), `banner_plane` (2,537, the banner blank).
- To be made again: `retro_sports_car`. The strakes on its doors, with the red
  wedge and the black louvred tail, bring it too close to a real Italian sports
  car of the 1980s. The author was given lines for the prompt (smooth doors, no
  side intakes, a rounded tail with round lights) and the negative prompt.
- `curve_sign`: Meshy's arrow came out a smudge.

## Implementation

- `tools/make_curve_sign.py`: builds the bend sign as a GLB written as Meshy's
  are, so the props' importer reads it as one of them: a yellow diamond 0.9 m on
  a side with a thick black border and a bold black arrow, drawn with Pillow
  into its texture, on a grey post 8 cm square; 3 m high, 22 triangles.
  `--left` mirrors the arrow, for the bends to the left (`curve_sign_left`).
- `docs/costa-bits-props.md`: the sign's row says it is not from Meshy, and
  names both; its prompt is gone. `docs/architecture.md` lists the tool.

## Verification

- `ruff check` and `ruff format --check` on the tool: clean.
- Both signs read with `tools/import_trees.py`'s `read_glb` (44 vertices, 22
  triangles, a 512 px texture) and, rendered textured, show the arrow clean
  from the front and at 35°, the back grey.
