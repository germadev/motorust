On `ccr-e7021c37-ulgd1v` (PR #38).

## User request

The author is sending the props of `docs/costa-bits-props.md` as Meshy makes
them, five at a time, to be looked over before they are imported.

## Looked over

Each GLB checked for its triangles, its axes (one textured mesh in a unit box,
up along +Y, front to +Z, as `tools/import_props.py` reads them) and rendered
from five sides.

- Good as they are: `white_house` (1,939 triangles), `chiringuito` (2,431),
  `espeto_boat` (1,379), `watchtower` (1,580, a card), `lifeguard_tower`
  (981; mostly red, its white stripes only on the railings), `flag_marshal`
  (2,566), `banner_plane` (2,537, the banner blank).
- To be made again: `retro_sports_car`. The strakes on its doors, with the red
  wedge and the black louvred tail, bring it too close to a real Italian sports
  car of the 1980s. The author was given lines for the prompt (smooth doors, no
  side intakes, a rounded tail with round lights) and the negative prompt.
- `curve_sign`: Meshy's arrow came out a smudge.

## Implementation

- `tools/make_curve_sign.py`: builds the bend sign as a GLB written as Meshy's
  are, so the props' importer reads it as one of them: a yellow diamond 0.9 m on
  a side with a thick black border and a bold black arrow, drawn with Pillow
  into its texture, on a grey post 8 cm square; 3 m high, 22 triangles.
  `--left` mirrors the arrow, for the bends to the left (`curve_sign_left`).
- `docs/costa-bits-props.md`: the sign's row says it is not from Meshy, and
  names both; its prompt is gone. `docs/architecture.md` lists the tool.

## Verification

- `ruff check` and `ruff format --check` on the tool: clean.
- Both signs read with `tools/import_trees.py`'s `read_glb` (44 vertices, 22
  triangles, a 512 px texture) and, rendered textured, show the arrow clean
  from the front and at 35°, the back grey.

# 2026-11-10 — Costa Bits: the sports car repainted

## User request

Keep Meshy's `retro_sports_car` rather than make it again, repainted yellow,
with the strakes on its doors gone and black stripes along its bonnet and boot.

## Implementation

- Every texel of the car's texture was traced to the point of the surface it
  paints (the triangles rasterized in texture space), so the paint could be
  laid by where it lands on the car:
  - the body's red (its red 185 and over, and the darker reds but for the
    tail lights) is yellow (248, 200, 24), each texel as light as its red was;
  - the sides between the wheels are plain yellow;
  - two black stripes 18 cm wide, 11 cm apart, along the middle of whatever
    faces up on the body: the bonnet, the nose and the boot;
  - the texels between the texture's islands take their nearest island's
    colour, so no red shows at the seams.
- Rendered textured and lit, the strakes seen at first were mostly the rough
  first preview's doing (one colour a triangle, drawn back to front): the
  model's doors are plain, faceted panels. The repaint stands for its colour
  and stripes.
- One-off scripts, not committed. The repainted GLB was given to the author to
  keep with the other sources.
- `docs/costa-bits-props.md`: the car's row says it was repainted.

## Verification

- Rendered textured and lit from the side, the front, three quarters and above:
  yellow all over, the tail lights red, the stripes over the bonnet and the
  boot, no red at the seams.

# 2026-11-10 — Costa Bits: the marshal's flag

## User request

Mend the chequered flag of Meshy's `flag_marshal`: its squares came out
smeared and uneven on half of it.

## Implementation

- As for the car, every texel traced to the point of the surface it paints.
  The flag is the texels of little colour above 0.2 (in the model's unit box)
  on the side of the raised hand; the pole, the brown ones beside it.
- The flag's plane is the one its points lie closest to; its upright is the
  pole's direction in it. Its texels were painted again from where they lie in
  that plane: a 6 × 6 grid of black (20) and white (236) squares, square to the
  pole, the same on both faces. The texture's padding takes its nearest
  island's colour.
- The flag's fold is in its mesh and is kept: it reads as cloth.
- One-off script, not committed; the mended GLB was given to the author.
- `docs/costa-bits-props.md`: the marshal's row says so.

## Verification

- Rendered textured and lit from the front, three quarters, the side and the
  back: the squares even and square over the whole flag, the fold crossing
  them; the overalls, the pole and the hand untouched.
