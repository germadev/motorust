# The resolution at once over the desktop; Arcade's orange more vivid; the performance forced drops the parallax first

**Date:** 2026-10-20. **Branch:** `claude/sleepy-galileo-s35zhl`.

## 1. A fixed resolution in a fullscreen over the desktop

### 1.1 User Request
- "On changing the resolution it does not apply the change on the fly."

### 1.2 Implementation Details
- In a window a fixed resolution sizes the window, and in a dedicated fullscreen it switches the screen's display mode; but in a fullscreen over the desktop (on macOS, Options > Graphics > fullscreen type "Desktop", the default there) the window covers the screen as it is (`WindowMode::BorderlessFullscreen`), and the resolution chosen changed nothing until the game left fullscreen.
- Now there it draws the world at that many pixels at most, the screen's shape kept: `Settings::desktop_resolution` (the resolution, in pixels, only in a fullscreen over the desktop) and `render_scale::capped` (`NativeShare` no more than the smaller share of the two sides the resolution lets through), folded into the share `follow_native_share` keeps, so the world's image, the split screen's halves, the HUD's resolution line, the options' render scale row, the benchmark and the performance log all follow it. Drawn smaller, it is scaled up and sharpened as with the render scale (applied on top of it), and it changes at once, in a race too.
- Not seen on a Mac here (no macOS): the user's own case may be another (a window, or a dedicated fullscreen); this is the case in which the option did nothing.
- Docs: `docs/gameplay.md` (Resolution), `docs/architecture.md` (`render_scale.rs`).

### 1.3 Verification & Testing
- Tests: `over_the_desktop_a_fixed_resolution_draws_the_world_at_that_many_pixels_at_most` (1920 × 1080 over a 3456 × 2234 panel: 1671 × 1080, 836 × 540 at 50 %; bigger than the window or none: as before; with the panel's own share smaller, the smaller); `desktop_resolution` in the fullscreen kinds' test (over the desktop yes; switching the screen, in a window, or dynamic, no).

## 3. The performance forced drops the parallax first

### 3.1 User Request
- "Force performance handles the resolution scale and the shadows automatically. It will also handle antialiasing and relief. What would the engine prioritise to make sure of that rate with the best graphic result?" Answered: the parallax first (the dearest of the relief, up to 8 readings on the grass, half the screen, and the least seen racing), then the shadows and the resolution as before; the normal maps never (taking them away without making the pipelines again saves next to nothing). The antialiasing changes every pipeline (multisampling or not), so in a race what is drawn would vanish while they are made: the user asked to choose between changing it only between races and leaving it out (pending).

### 3.2 Implementation Details
- `detail.rs`: `Rung::parallax` and `Detail::parallax` (whether the parallax may be drawn); `Ladder { performance, split, parallax }` picks the ladder (`ladder`, `ladder_on`); with "Normals and parallax" chosen and the performance forced, a rung without it before the rest (`PERFORMANCE_PARALLAX`, 9 rungs; `PERFORMANCE_SPLIT_PARALLAX`, 7); otherwise as before (a rung that would change nothing is left out). The shadows alone, automatic, leave the relief as chosen.
- `relief.rs`: `show_relief` takes the detail's, `capped` (the parallax down to normal maps when it may not be drawn): a flag of the materials, no pipeline made again.
- Docs: `docs/gameplay.md` (Force performance), `docs/architecture.md` (`detail.rs`).

### 3.3 Verification & Testing
- Tests: `the_performance_ladder_drops_the_parallax_then_the_shadows_and_the_resolution_by_turns` (one change a rung; the parallax, if chosen, first, then the ladder as without it), `the_detail_comes_from_the_performance_forced_or_from_the_options` (with the parallax chosen, from its ladder's top, its first rung down without it; not forced, with it), `the_performance_forced_takes_only_the_parallax_away`.
