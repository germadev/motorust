# Two Players: Second Player's Sound and Numbers on the Minimap; Sea Waves Instead of Cells

**Date:** 2026-10-13
**Branch:** `claude/loving-maxwell-vr7uub`

## 1. User Request
- 2 players: "no suenan los FX del segundo jugador" (the second player's effects are not heard) and "en el minimapa, podría aparecer los números de cada jugador" (the minimap could show each player's number).
- "El mar a pie de pista se ve mal, deberían verse simplemente olas, y no celdas" (from the track, the sea looks wrong: it should just show waves, not cells).

## 2. Implementation Details
### 2.1 Second player's sound (`src/sound/mod.rs`)
- Cause: the sound was placed for one listener, `cameras.iter().next()` (either camera, in no set order), and only the bike with `Player` counted as the player's. Player 2's bike (`LocalPlayer(1)`) was mixed as a rival: at 70 % volume, attenuated with the distance to player 1's camera (about 1 % at 400 m) and with Doppler; and the wind, tyres and grass only came from player 1's bike.
- Now every camera listens, each with its own smoothed velocity for the Doppler: each bike is heard from the nearer camera (`nearest_listener`), and both players' bikes (`Player` or `LocalPlayer`) sound as the player's (full volume, no Doppler). The wind, tyre squeal and grass take, each, the louder of the two players (`rider_noise`).

### 2.2 Minimap numbers (`src/minimap.rs`)
- With two players, each player's dot is a little bigger (19 px) and carries their number (1 or 2) in white with a shadow. With one player nothing changes.

### 2.3 Split bike select (`src/menu.rs`)
- Removed the old "ESC or B: Back to track select · Both players confirm to race" line, which overlapped the shared legend (going back is in the legend; each panel says how to get ready).

### 2.4 Sea waves (`src/world/sea.wgsl`)
- The white lines were the borders of Voronoi cells: from the track they read as a net of cells. They are now separate waves: each cell of a jittered 7 m grid may hold one (two in three do), a short arc like a crest, thick in the middle and thin at the ends, leaning a little its own way; they drift with the wind and each one swells and fades over 5 s, so they come and go. Branch-free, so the anti-aliasing derivatives stay valid; faded out between 160 and 380 m.

## 3. Verification & Testing
- New tests: `sound::tests::each_bike_is_heard_from_the_nearer_camera`, `sound::tests::with_two_players_both_bikes_sound_as_the_player` (player 2, 400 m from player 1's camera, as loud as player 1).
- `cargo test`: all pass. `cargo fmt --check` clean, `cargo clippy --all-targets` no warnings.
- Ran the game under Xvfb with Mesa lavapipe: a two-player race (WASD and arrows) shows the numbered dots on the minimap; the welcome flyby over the Turbo Bay seafront shows the new waves and the shore foam.
