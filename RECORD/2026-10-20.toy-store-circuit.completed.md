# 2026-10-20 — Toy Store Circuit (`Toy Store GP` / `Juguetería`)

## Context & User Request

The user requested a new circuit based on the interior of a toy store ("juguetería"), where bikes have the size of toy motorbikes (miniature perspective).
Key user requirements and references:
- **Setting:** Interior of a giant toy store with ceiling and lights overhead (no open sky), surrounding giant shelves with toys, tables, chairs, and shop displays.
- **5 Thematic Sectors (based on provided "Toy Store GP" track layout infographic):**
  1. *Mostrador de la caja registradora:* Finish line flanked by giant AA battery pillars ("Línea de meta de pilas"), barcode laser scanner, cash register display/keypad, comic book ramp, and giant teddy bear sitting at the "Curva Peluches".
  2. *Paso a nivel del tren:* Wooden/electric toy train track with steam locomotive, wooden trees, and level crossing barriers.
  3. *Cañón de bloques de construcción:* Skyscraper towers made of colorful construction blocks (Lego style), giant dice, and house of cards.
  4. *Pista Hot Wheels:* Suspended bright orange plastic track with blue edge barriers, elevated trestles, and high-speed steep drops / loops with toy cars.
  5. *Pista Scalextric:* Textured black slot-car track with dual metallic electrical rails, start lights, and red/white safety barriers.
- **Audio:** Custom music track provided by the user at `assets/music/plastic_velocity_rush.mp3` (~176.3 s, mastered at -16.0 LUFS).

     - `ComicRamp`: Curved glossy comic book / magazine pages acting as a ramp crest sending bikes airborne over the counter.
     - `TeddyBear`: Chubby plush teddy bear sitting by the corner of the counter ("Curva Peluches").
     - `ToyTrain`: Circular railway layout with wooden cross ties, dual metallic rails, and classic black/red/yellow toy steam locomotive.

     - `Die`: Giant game dice with beveled edges and contrasting pip patterns.
     - `CardHouse`: Leaning playing card structures with white faces, red patterned backs, and horizontal floor dividers.
     - `CeilingLight`: Overhead lighting fixtures with pendant lamp shades and glowing light source elements hanging 55 m above ground.
     - `ToyShelf`: Giant multi-tier wooden perimeter bookcases stocked with stacks of colorful boxed games and toys.
   - Built procedural mesh generation with vertex colors and batched standard materials for 60+ FPS ECS rendering.
   - Integrated into world generation pipeline in `src/world/props.rs` and `src/world/mod.rs`.
