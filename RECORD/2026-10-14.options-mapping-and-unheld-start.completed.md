## 2. resvg for the Gamepad Illustrations

### 2.1 User Requests
> "Es mejor resvg o zeno?"
> "La prioridad es el rendimiento, y he visto que resvg está mejor mantenido"
> "Re está re rasterizado al cambiar la resolución?"

### 2.2 Implementation Details
- **`src/pad_art.rs` rewritten on resvg 0.45** (`default-features = false`, `text`), on the tiny-skia Bevy already has; the hand-written XML reader and SVG subset on zeno are gone. The illustration's text is prepared with roxmltree (re-exported by usvg) before usvg reads it: the controls no pad of the game has are cut out (Xbox paddles, C and Z), the Xbox letters too for a Switch pad, and each game button's group gets an id (`pad-<name>`). It is read three times: whole; the buttons without their marks; only the buttons' marks; the last two without `filter` attributes (no shadows). The whole is rendered with `resvg::render`; each button's shapes and marks with `resvg::render_node` into a pixmap cropped to the union of both nodes' boxes (`draw_in_place` undoes the group's own transform that `render_node` applies and moves its box to the crop), then made white with dark marks (`lit`). The Russo One font is loaded into usvg's font database as the sans-serif family.
- **Benchmark** (a temporary ignored test, not committed; 540 px tall at scale factor 2, best of five, `CARGO_PROFILE_DEV_OPT_LEVEL=3`):

  | Pad | zeno (before) | resvg |
  |---|---|---|
  | DualSense | 62 ms | ~118 ms |
  | DualShock 4 | 279 ms | ~120 ms |
  | DualShock 3 | 234 ms | ~133 ms |
  | Xbox | 337 ms | ~103 ms |

  resvg is faster on three of four and about twice as fast overall; its dependencies build at opt-level 3 in the dev profile too, where the zeno code (in the game's crate) was at opt-level 1. Profiling showed 85–90 % of resvg's time in the filters (the body's drop shadow and the DualSense's glow); without them a pad takes 10–30 ms. `color-interpolation-filters="sRGB"` did not help.
- **Drawn in the background:** `PadArts::get` now returns `Option<&DrawnPad>` and, if missing, spawns the drawing on Bevy's `AsyncComputeTaskPool` (`draw` returns plain pixels, `Drawing`); `finish_pad_arts` (before `show_options_menu`) turns finished drawings into images. `show_options_menu` starts it as soon as the Control tab is open (on the web, whose background is the same thread, only when the gamepad page is shown), so it is ready by the time the page is; meanwhile the page keeps the pad's room (`PadArt::size`, from the viewBox). No frame waits for a drawing.
- **Re-rasterizing:** the drawing is cached by pixel height (logical height × scale factor), so a change of window size does not draw it again; only a change of scale factor does. The view's rebuild key now includes the scale factor and whether the pad is drawn, so it is rebuilt when either changes.
- **Docs:** `docs/architecture.md` (code map), `assets/CREDITS.md`.

### 2.3 Verification
- `pad_art` tests: 7 (new `the_size_of_an_illustration_is_read_from_its_view`, `a_pad_is_drawn_in_the_background_once_for_each_size`; `a_pad_is_drawn_with_each_button_on_it` now on `draw` itself).
- In the game (Xvfb, lavapipe): the Xbox controller drawn, sticks and buttons in place.

## 5. The Start at the Lights Without Holding the Bikes

### 5.1 User Request
> "En Opciones > Juego. En Salida añade una opción, quedando: Semáforo, Semáforo sin bloqueo, Acelerando. Semaforo sin bloqueo, permitiría arrancar antes de que termine el semáforo, y si ocurre, quedaría descalificado"

### 5.2 Implementation Details
- `RaceStart::LightsUnheld` ("Semáforo sin bloqueo" / "Lights, no hold") between Lights and Throttle; `at_lights` and `holds`.
- `Race::lights` (the race starts at the lights, the throttle does not start it) apart from `Race::held` (the bikes stay on the grid); `run_lights` sets both from the setting.
- `jump_starts` (`src/race/lights.rs`): at the lights with the bikes free, a player's bike going over 1 m/s (`JUMP_SPEED`) before they go out is disqualified: `Retired` (it rolls to a stop), and in `Standing::out` and `Standing::disqualified`. The rivals still wait for the lights.
- The HUD says "¡SALIDA ANTICIPADA! DESCALIFICADO" / "JUMP START! DISQUALIFIED"; the results "descalificado"/"disqualified" (`Place::disqualified`); in the arcade they score nothing (`Outcome::settle` leaves them out, the line shows +0). On the grid the order already puts those out last. Restart with someone out (eliminated or disqualified) sets the race up again (`restart_with_some_out`, was `restart_elimination`).

### 5.3 Verification
- New tests: `a_player_who_moves_off_before_the_lights_go_out_is_disqualified`, `with_the_bikes_held_nobody_jumps_the_start`, `a_player_disqualified_says_so_in_the_results`; the scoring test checks no points for a disqualified player; the settings test checks `at_lights`/`holds`.
- In the game (Arcade, Bahía GP, "Lights, no hold"): W held with two lights on: the bike moves (13 km/h), "JUMP START! DISQUALIFIED", the bike rolls to a stop, the lights go on and the rivals leave when they go out; the results show "8th Shogun TZ · YOU — disqualified".

## 7. The Lap Record Told Once; the Camera's Shots Once Everybody Is In

### 7.1 User Requests
> "En la carrera, me está notificando Record de vuelta en todas las vueltas, aunque hayan sido lentas"
> "Al centrar el cuatro de resultados, que la cámara vaya mostrando a los corredores, idealmente en zonas vistosas"

### 7.2 Implementation Details
- **The lap record (`src/race/mod.rs`, `src/race/hud.rs`):** the notice compared the player's best lap with the race's record and showed for a few seconds after the start of every lap, so once a lap had set the record every later lap, however slow, told it again. `Standing::record` is now the lap time and when it was set; the notice shows for `NOTICE` seconds after that, to the player whose best lap it is. A lap counts as a record only if it beats one already kept: the first lap ever ridden on a track is kept, but not announced.
- **The shots (`watch_them_come_in`):** once nobody is still racing, a shot of `SHOT` (6) seconds on each bike in turn. `spot_worth` judges a bike's spot: round a loop (+3), on a road off the lap (+1.5), the tightest bend (smoothed curvature × 40) and the sharpest crest (× 60) in the next `SPOT_AHEAD` (120) m, each up to 1.5, and +0.5 for each bike within `TOGETHER` (20) m, up to 1.5; a bike under 5 m/s is worth a fifth. `next_shot` takes the most worth watching but the one watched and the last `RECENT_SHOTS` (3) shown, while there are others. The camera cuts to it as it already did between arrivals.

### 7.3 Verification
- New tests: `a_lap_record_is_told_once_just_after_the_lap_that_beat_it`, `the_camera_goes_where_there_is_something_to_see` (on the default track, a bike coming up to its tightest bend beats one on its straightest stretch; a group adds; a stopped bike hardly counts), `the_shots_go_round_the_bikes_most_worth_watching_first`.

> "cuántas caras tienen los árboles en promedio?" (a question; answered from a temporary test, not committed)

### 8.2 Implementation Details
- **Where the results stand (`src/race/results.rs`, `Stand`):** at the top right (`Corner`: 40 px from the top, 28 from the right) while the others come in, and after if `Settings::centre_results` is off (the default); with it on, once everybody is in, in the middle above the bike the camera shows (`Above`: 26 % of the height kept below; first written as a percentage of the bottom padding, which Bevy takes of the width, so it stood against the top of the screen, now `Val::Vh`). The standings stay in the middle (`Middle`). The option is in Options > Game ("Centrar resultados al llegar todos" / "Centre results when all are in"), reset with the section.
- **After the results (`Next::Again`, `back_from`):** out of a championship, Enter/A "Repetir" / "Race again" sets the same race up again (the race state entered again), Esc/B "Volver" / "Back" goes to the track screen. In the arcade: "Continuar" to the standings, and Esc/B to the game modes, as before.
- **Start lights (`Settings::start_lights`, `START_LIGHTS` 1–5, `DEFAULT_START_LIGHTS` 5):** `StartLights::new(chance, count)` lights `count` lamps a second apart, out a moment after the last; the panel is built with as many (`spawn_lights` after `set_the_lights`). A restart keeps the count of the race. Options > Game "Luces rojas del semáforo" / "Red start lights", faint with a start at the throttle; reset with the section.
- **Modes (`src/modes.rs`):** for one player Arcade on top, then Race (blue) and Elimination (red), then Checkpoint (purple) and Free ride (green); for two, Race, Elimination and Free ride. The chequered flag slides inwards on the left cards and outwards on the right ones as before.
- **Trees:** 260 triangles a cypress or poplar, 440 a tall tree, 780 an umbrella pine, olive, broadleaf, maritime pine or almond; planted, about 600 on Bahía GP, 575 on Turbo Bay, 780 on Loop Island, none on Gold Mine: about 650 on average.

### 8.3 Verification
- New test: `with_fewer_lights_they_go_out_sooner`; `after_the_results_comes_what_the_mode_says` checks `Next::Again` and `back_from`; the settings test checks the new defaults; the modes' tests walk the new order.
- In the game: the results at the top right while the others come in (Race, Bahía GP, one lap).
