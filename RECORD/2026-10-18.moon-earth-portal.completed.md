# 2026-10-18 — Moon Base: Interdimensional Earth Portal, Removal of Loop, and High-Relief Earth Sector

## Entry 1 — Initial Analysis & Design Plan

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Task:** Remove the vertical loop from `Base Lunar`, add an interdimensional portal connecting the Moon to Earth so that half of the circuit takes place on Earth, and give the Earth half dramatic altitude changes.

### User Request
The user requested:
1. Remove the vertical loop (`VerticalLoop`) from `Base Lunar`, noting that the loop belongs to Sonic (`Loop Island`).
2. Add an interdimensional portal that leads to Earth so that half of the circuit runs on Earth (and then returns to the Moon via a second portal).
3. Give the Earth half of the circuit much greater altitude variation (steep climbs, deep valley drops, mountain passes, and jump crests).

### Implementation Plan
1. **Dual-Realm Terrain (`src/world/terrain.rs`, `src/world/shoulders.rs`, `src/world/mirror.rs`):**
   - Partition `Soil::Lunar` across the interdimensional boundary (`x > 585.0` for the Earth eastern lobe, `x <= 585.0` for the Lunar western/central lobe).
   - In the Lunar realm (`x <= 585.0`), classify non-rock/non-water ground as `Ground::Sand` tinted with `lunar_vertex_color` (grey lunar regolith and dark basalt) and `Surface::Dirt(Dust::Slate)` shoulders with no grass tufts.
   - In the Earth realm (`x > 585.0`), classify meadows as `Ground::Grass` with `textures.grass` and lush green `grass_tint`, `Ground::Rock` with natural Earth rock vertex coloring, and `Surface::Grass` shoulders with 3D grass tufts and grass particle kick-up.
2. **Interdimensional Portal (`src/world/scenery.rs`, `src/world/space.rs`, `src/world/mod.rs`):**
   - Add `Portal` to `Space` in `src/world/scenery.rs` (`pub portals: &'static [Portal]`).
   - Build procedural 3D stargate portals (`spawn_portals` and `spin_portals` in `src/world/space.rs`) featuring a dark titanium/gold chevron outer ring, twin plasma energy pylons, a translucent glowing vortex curtain (`AlphaMode::Blend`), and counter-rotating emissive cyan/violet energy rings (`PortalSpin`).
3. **Circuit Update (`src/circuits/moon_base.rs`):**
   - Remove the `VerticalLoop` branch from `branches`.
   - Place the Moon-to-Earth portal at `1045.0 m` (just outside the Space Station Tunnel exit) and the Earth-to-Moon portal at `2965.0 m` (just before the Station Ridge overpass back onto the Moon).
   - Redesign `ELEVATION` and `MASSIFS` on the Earth half (`1045..2965 m`) with dramatic altitude swings: climbing from `1.5 m` at the portal to `35.5 m` over the high truss bridge, diving to `10.0 m` in the coastal valley with a straightaway jump crest at `1715 m`, and climbing up a mountain pass through the split (`36.0 m -> 48.0 m`) to a `60.0 m` mountain summit (`2830 m`) before descending to `31.6 m` at the return portal.
   - Populate the Earth half (`1080..2920 m`) with trees, forests, and houses while restricting lunar boulders and space props to the Moon half.

## Entry 2 — Implementation & Verification

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Completed

### Implementation Details
1. **Dual-Realm Moon/Earth Terrain (`src/world/terrain.rs`, `src/world/shoulders.rs`, `src/world/mirror.rs`):**
   - Added `pub fn is_earth_realm(point: Vec2) -> bool` in `src/world/terrain.rs` defining the boundary line `boundary_x = 692.0 - 70.0 * ((point.y + 430.0) / 81.0).clamp(0.0, 1.15)` passing directly through the two interdimensional portals (`(692, -430)` at `1048 m` and `(622, -349)` at `2950 m`).
   - Updated `Site`, `site()`, `look()`, `Terrain::ground()`, `Terrain::ground_color()`, `Terrain::band()`, and `spawn_terrain()` so that `Soil::Lunar` circuits render the Moon half (`!is_earth_realm`) as grey regolith (`Ground::Sand` with `lunar_vertex_color`) and dark basalt (`Ground::Rock` with `lunar_vertex_color`), while the Earth half (`is_earth_realm`) renders lush green grass (`Ground::Grass` with `textures.grass` and `grass_tint`) and natural Earth rock (`Ground::Rock` with `ground_vertex_color`), smoothly sliced across triangles by `Terrain::cut` and `Terrain::border`.
   - Updated `terrain_surface` in `src/world/shoulders.rs` and `texel` in `src/world/mirror.rs` so `Ground::Grass` on `Soil::Lunar` produces `Surface::Grass` (enabling 3D grass tufts, ragged grass shoulder strips, and grass kick-up particles on the Earth half while keeping `Surface::Dirt(Dust::Slate)` on the Moon half) and reflects green grass in water.
2. **Interdimensional Portals (`src/world/scenery.rs`, `src/world/space.rs`, `src/world/mod.rs`):**
   - Added `Portal { pub along: f32 }` and `pub portals: &'static [Portal]` to `Space` in `src/world/scenery.rs`.
   - Implemented `spawn_portals`, `portal_frame_meshes`, `portal_ring_mesh`, `portal_vortex_mesh`, `PortalSpin`, and `spin_portals` in `src/world/space.rs` (registered in `WorldPlugin` `Update` systems in `src/world/mod.rs`). Each portal spans the full track width with twin plasma pylons, a dark titanium arch with 13 gold chevron locks, two counter-rotating cyan and magenta emissive energy rings (`PortalSpin`), and a semi-transparent double-sided dimensional vortex curtain (`AlphaMode::Blend`) that riders drive straight through.
   - Updated `place_boulders` in `src/world/space.rs` to exclude `is_earth_realm(position)` so lunar craters and boulders only spawn on the Moon half.
