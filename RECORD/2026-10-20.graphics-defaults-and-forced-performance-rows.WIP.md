# Graphics' defaults; the options the performance forced lowers, in amber and still to be chosen

**Date:** 2026-10-20. **Branch:** `claude/sleepy-galileo-s35zhl`.

## 1. The defaults; the rows of what the performance forced may lower

### 1.1 User Request
- With a screenshot of Options > Graphics (Force performance "No", Resolution scale "100 %", Shadows "Automatic (high)", Antialiasing "FXAA", Relief "No", Relief on the road off): "Set these values as the defaults, and when Force performance is on, do not disable Resolution scale and Shadows; instead, highlight the options that will be lowered automatically (Resolution scale, Shadows, Antialiasing and Relief)."

### 1.2 Implementation Details
- **Defaults** (`settings.rs`): `Antialiasing::Fxaa` (was `Msaa`) and `Relief::Off` (was `Normal`); the shadows were automatic already, the scale 100 %, nothing forced and no relief on the road. Settings saved with them keep what was saved; saved without them, as the game starts now.
- **The rows** (`options.rs`): Resolution scale and Shadows no longer faint and fixed ("Auto") with the performance forced: they can be changed, and what they say is where it starts from, the most it gives (`detail::Ladder::level` and `scale`: `ladder` caps every rung at them and leaves out a rung that comes to the one above it). The rows of what it may lower show in amber (`ui::Row::automatic`, `AUTOMATIC`, the HUD's amber; `detail::lowered`: the resolution scale and the shadows unless at their lowest, the antialiasing with MSAA 4× chosen, the relief with the parallax chosen); the Antialiasing and Relief rows say again what is chosen (the "· Auto" of the one in use, gone).
- **The benchmark** (`bench.rs`): its scenarios are a fixed reference (shadows high, multisampled, normal maps), not the game's defaults, so runs compare with those before.
- Docs: `docs/gameplay.md` (Force performance, Antialiasing, Relief).

### 1.3 Verification & Testing
- Tests: `the_game_starts_with_fxaa_and_no_relief_and_keeps_what_is_chosen`, `the_performance_forced_lowers_from_the_shadows_and_the_scale_chosen` (never above them, no rung that changes nothing, at the lowest the multisampling alone; what the rows mark), `the_detail_comes_from_the_performance_forced_or_from_the_options` (from medium and 70 %: without the multisampling, low, then the resolution), the antialiasing's (FXAA as the game starts), the relief's reset (to none), the bench's settings line.
