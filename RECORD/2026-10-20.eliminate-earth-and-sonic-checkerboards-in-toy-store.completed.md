# 2026-10-20 — Eliminate Earth, Dirt Shoulders and Sonic Checkerboards in Toy Store GP

## User Feedback / Problem Description

The user pointed out:
- "De dónde has sacado que en una juguetería hay tierra?" (Why is there dirt/earth in a toy store?)
- "Y los dameros de Sonic, por qué?" (Why are there Sonic the Hedgehog checkerboards?)

## Root Cause Analysis

1. **`Soil::Checkered` inherited from Loop Island:**
   When `toy_store.rs` was originally drafted, `soil: Soil::Checkered` was copied from `loop_island.rs`. `Soil::Checkered` is the brown-and-tan checkerboard earth texture from Green Hill / Loop Island.
2. **`CUTTINGS` with height 14.0 m in Sector 3 (Block Canyon):**
   `cuttings` generated 14-meter-tall steep cliffs along both sides of the track in Sector 3.
   Because `cuttings` height was > 3.0 m, the engine's `cliff()` logic in `road.rs` removed the track walls and rendered natural cliff rock faces instead.
   With `soil: Soil::Checkered`, these cliffs were drawn as giant 14-meter-tall Sonic checkerboard dirt cliffs!
3. **`COUNTER_MASSIF`:**
   A 2.5 m natural earth hill was raised under the counter straight.
4. **Natural grass and dirt shoulders:**
   The terrain generator textured ground outside the road with grass, and shoulders defaulted to dirt/earth with grass tufts (`tufts.rs`).

## Implementation Details

1. **New `Soil::ToyStore` Enum Variant:**
   - Added `Soil::ToyStore` to `src/world/scenery.rs`.
   - In `src/world/terrain.rs`:
     - Textures ground with `textures.planks` (warm wood parquet / showroom floor) instead of grass or earth.
     - Sets ground vertex color to warm oak parquet tone (`[0.85, 0.72, 0.55]`).
   - In `src/world/structures.rs` & `src/world/mirror.rs`: Handled `Soil::ToyStore`.
2. **Eliminated All Dirt Cuttings & Massifs (`src/circuits/toy_store.rs`):**
   - Changed `soil: Soil::Checkered` -> `soil: Soil::ToyStore`.
   - Set `cuttings: &[]` and `massifs: &[]`.
   - Now Sector 3 ("Cañón de Bloques") is entirely enclosed by the Lego block walls, Lego arch, towers, and dice, with zero dirt or checkerboard cliffs.
3. **Paved Shoulders (`src/world/shoulders.rs`):**
   - Set `Surface::Paved` for all shoulders when `scenery.toy_store.is_some()`.
   - Completely removes dirt dust and grass tufts from the track sides.
4. **Test Adjustments:**
   - In `src/world/terrain.rs`, guarded `assert!(inside > 0)` to only check circuits with cuttings.
   - In `src/world/mod.rs`, adjusted rest-pieces threshold in `a_circuit_is_shown_before_its_rest_comes_and_raced_on_whole` to `> shown + 50` since no grass tufts are spawned on indoor tracks.
