# 2026-10-18 — Base Lunar: Separate Earth and Moon Realms, Seen Only Through the Portals; Radio Dishes Point Up

## Entry 1 — Analysis & Implementation

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Branch:** `circuit/space-pursuit` (on top of `3ae2c47`)

### User Request
1. From the Moon part of the Earth's terrain was visible beside the road (and vice versa); the other world should only be visible through the portals, never over the walls before entering.
2. The radio antennas (dishes) must point at the sky.

### Analysis
- Both realms share one map and one terrain; the realm only changed the ground's colour and type (`is_earth_realm`), so the Earth's grass, trees and houses were plainly visible from the Moon road next to the border (and the other way).
- `turn_dishes` pitched the reflector (which faces local `-Z`) by `-0.52 rad`: it looked ~30° down at the ground.

### Implementation
1. **Realm render layers (`src/world/realm.rs`, new):** layer 1 Moon, 2 Earth, 3 overview (menus see the circuit whole), 4/5 each player's curtains; 0 stays shared (sky, portal frames). `Realm`, `splits(scenery)`, `part_in(mesh, realm)` (keeps the triangles whose middle is in a realm), `RealmScenery` (children get `Propagate<RenderLayers>` by where they stand; `HierarchyPropagatePlugin<RenderLayers>` added in `PostUpdate`), `PortalGate` (curtains added per player), `Gates::realm_at` (near a portal, the side of its curtain; elsewhere the map's realm).
2. **Terrain (`src/world/terrain.rs`):** `Terrain::meshes_as(threads, style, side)` draws all the ground as one realm's (`look`, `cut`, `border`, `band` take the style) and keeps only the grid triangles on `side`; `GroundMesh::layers`. `world::realm_ground` builds the four sets: each realm's own ground on its layers + overview, and the land beyond its border drawn as its own, seen only from it.
3. **World building (`src/world/mod.rs`):** on a split circuit roads/structures are parted by realm, gantries/signs and all props go under a `RealmScenery`, the grass tufts and the sea are the Earth's.
4. **Cameras:** in a race on a split circuit each player's camera sees `{0, its realm, its curtains}`; otherwise `{0, overview}`. The sun shadows only the first player's realm (otherwise invisible trees would cast shadows on the other realm's land).
5. **Portals:** a `PortalView` camera per player at the same place draws the other realm into an image the size of the player's viewport; the curtain (`PortalCurtain`, `src/world/portal.wgsl`) samples it at its own screen position, with a swirl at the rim. To keep the cost down it only draws while a portal is within 700 m and on screen, only over the curtains' screen box (`Camera::viewport` + `SubCameraView`), with its frustum narrowed to that box (`narrow_portal_frusta`, Bevy culls to the whole sub view). The old translucent vortex is kept for the overview only. Images are made with `copy_on_resize = false` (a render target cannot be copied from: the first run quit with a wgpu validation error).
6. **Sky (`src/world/sky.rs`):** the Moon (seen from the Earth) and the Earth (seen from the Moon) are on the realm layers instead of being toggled by the camera's position, so each portal view shows the right one; the dome follows player 1's camera.
7. Portal cameras are left out of `render_scale`, antialiasing, sound listeners and the menu cameras' `Single` queries.
8. **Dishes (`src/world/space.rs`):** pitch `0.85 ± 0.12 rad`, so the reflector faces up (rim stays ~11 m over the base, clear of the pedestal).

### Verification
- New tests in `world::realm`: only Base Lunar is split; along the lap the realm changes exactly twice, at the portals (within one track step); meshes part by realm; layer sets are disjoint between realms; curtain screen box (ahead: small box; behind: none; passing through: whole screen; too far: none).
- `cargo test`: 553 passed, 1 failed — `circuits::tests::circuits_have_checkpoints`, which already fails on `3ae2c47` without these changes.
- Benchmark (`MOTORUST_BENCH_TRACKS=5 MOTORUST_BENCH_SECONDS=2`, Apple M1 Pro) ran the whole lap with 1 and 2 players with no render errors. Base Lunar, average / 1 % low fps:

  | 1 player | 104.0 / 69.3 | 87.0 / 45.3 | 97.1 / 56.8 |
  | 2 players | 75.5 / 51.0 | 68.0 / 38.0 | 75.6 / 43.4 |

  The remaining cost is near the portals (slowest around 0.8 km, the approach to the first one, which was already the slowest spot): the portal view still renders its own shadow cascades. Visual check could not be done from the agent (no screen capture permission).
- `cargo clippy --all-targets` and `cargo fmt --check` clean.

## Entry 5 — Fix: the Moon Looked Like a Long Smear

- **User Report:** the Moon looks like a long smear.
- **Cause:** `body` returned the Moon's transform scaled by its radius while its mesh was already that radius, so it was drawn ~62 times too big (≈3.8 km radius), through the sky dome. The Earth's transform had its scale reset; the Moon's did not.
- **Fix (`src/world/sky.rs`):** `body` returns an unscaled transform and the radius apart. Test `the_moon_and_the_earth_are_as_big_as_they_should_look` checks the transform is unscaled and each body subtends its intended diameter.
- **Verification:** sky tests pass; clippy and fmt clean.

## Entry 6 — The Milky Way Drawn with Stars

- **User Report:** the Milky Way looks blurry; could it be approximated with stars?
- **Cause:** a 1024 × 512 map stretched round the whole sky is about a third of a degree per texel: several pixels on screen.
- **Implementation (`src/world/sky.rs`):** `milky_way_stars_mesh` sows 9000 star quads (one mesh, ~1 px, a few bigger) round the celestial sphere by rejection sampling: each place tried evenly round the sphere is kept as likely as the NASA map is bright there (`star_likelihood`: none in the dark sky or the dust lanes), tinted by the map's colour there (the warm core, the bluer arms), most faint and a few bright, turned with the map (`celestial_turn`), 4385 m out between the glow and the other stars. The map itself stays behind as a faint glow (`MILKY_WAY_GLOW` = 0.4 of its light).
- **Verification:** test `the_milky_way_is_sown_with_stars_along_its_band` (all 9000 sown; over 70 % within 20° of the galactic plane, a third of the sky); sky tests pass; clippy clean; the game ran past the first portal with no render errors. Not seen on screen.

## Entry 7 — The Milky Way as in a Long Exposure

- **User Report:** the Milky Way still looks like a smudge; it should look like an astrophoto (a reference photo of the Milky Way over an observatory in the Atacama: a dense star field everywhere, a high-contrast band with a golden core, bluish edges and sharp dark dust lanes).
- **Cause:** the 1024 × 512 map is a third of a degree per texel, blurring the dust lanes; and the 420 background stars were far too few.
- **Implementation (`src/world/sky.rs`, `assets/textures/milky_way.jpg`, `assets/CREDITS.md`):**
  - The map is now NASA SVS Deep Star Maps 2020 `milkyway_2020_4k_gal.exr` (4096 × 2048, galactic coordinates; checked by the Magellanic Clouds at l ≈ 280°/300°, b ≈ −33°/−44°), converted with `sips` and cut to |b| ≤ 45° (4096 × 1024, 1 MB JPEG, ~22 MB with mipmaps on the GPU), mapped on a band of the sky (`galactic`, `galactic_at`) placed by the same `celestial_turn`.
  - `enhance_milky_way` grades it at load as an astrophotographer would: black point, faint light lifted, darks deepened again for contrast, the source's orange desaturated, blue-violet in the faint glow, cream-gold in the bright clouds, nearly white in the brightest. Tuned against previews written by the ignored test `preview_the_milky_way` (`MILKY_WAY_PREVIEW=out.png cargo test preview_the_milky_way -- --ignored`).
  - 20 000 Milky Way stars sown along the band (3 000 000 tries at most), and 5 000 background stars over the whole sky (were 420), nearly all faint, a few bright, white, blue-white and orange. Shared helpers `push_star`, `star_mesh`. The glow is drawn at full strength again (`MILKY_WAY_GLOW` removed).
- **Verification:** sky tests pass (`the_milky_way_is_sown_with_stars_along_its_band`, `the_milky_way_rises_from_sagittarius_above_the_horizon`); clippy clean. Not seen in game (only the graded map, as an image).

## Entry 8 — The Milky Way Blended into the Sky

- **User Report:** the Milky Way now has too much contrast and looks like a cutout; it should blend into the sky.
- **Cause:** the grading clipped the sky to pure black (black point) and then lifted faint light strongly, so the grey glow ended abruptly where the map crossed the threshold; the contrast was high enough that the band covered the sky's own blue instead of adding to it.
- **Implementation (`src/world/sky.rs`):** `enhance_milky_way` without a black point, a gentler lift (1.6 instead of 2.4), dust deepened smoothly down to nothing, softer tints and saturation, dimmed overall (`MILKY_WAY_LIGHT` = 0.75), and faded to nothing over the outer 40 % of the band either side (no edge at ±45°). The band's mesh (192 × 48) dims it towards the horizon in vertex colours (15 % at the horizon, full from ~24° up), as extinction does. The band's stars: likelihood threshold lowered and smoothed (`((bright − 0.02) / 0.4)^1.5`, so their density has no edge either) and a little fainter.
- **Verification:** sky tests pass; clippy clean; the game ran past the first portal with no render errors. Checked the graded map as an image (soft glow, no edge); not seen in game.

## Entry 11 — Fix: Spawn Ready Start Looks Before Ready First Parts in `spawn_prepared_circuit`

- **Date:** 2026-10-19
- **User Request:** Fix test failure in `world::tests::seen_from_the_road_a_circuit_shows_its_start_first`: `thread 'world::tests::seen_from_the_road_a_circuit_shows_its_start_first' panicked at src/world/mod.rs:1431:13: no look at its start`.
- **Cause:** When a frame takes long enough or background threads finish concurrently such that both the circuit's start look (`Previewing`) and its first part (`Preparing`) are ready in the same frame, `spawn_prepared_circuit` previously polled `preparing` first. When the first part was spawned, it called `previewing.0.remove(&index)` to discard pending look tasks, so the finished look was discarded before ever being placed in the world or shown.
- **Implementation (`src/world/mod.rs`):** In `spawn_prepared_circuit`, moved `previewing` polling before `preparing` polling. A ready look at the start is placed into the world and rendered first; on subsequent frames when the first part is processed, `build` hides the look entity as expected.
- **Verification:**
  - `cargo test seen_from_the_road_a_circuit_shows_its_start_first`: passed (also verified with artificial frame delays).
  - `cargo test world::tests::`: all 8 tests passed.
  - `cargo clippy --all-targets`: clean.
  - `cargo fmt --check`: clean.
