# Record: Populate Toys in Toy Store GP

**Date:** 2026-10-20  
**Status:** Completed

## 1. User Request
"además, está muy vacío, dónde están los juguetes? sólo hay cajas"  
(Translation / Context: "Also, it's very empty, where are the toys? There are only boxes.")

## 2. Diagnosis & Objectives
- **Empty Track Problem:** While the circuit is 2400 meters long, `TOY_STORE_PROPS` in `src/circuits/toy_store.rs` only defined a handful of landmarks (1 teddy bear, 1 train circle, 3 block towers, 2 dice, 1 card house, and 4 distant perimeter shelves). Most of the circuit had no landmarks or toys within view of the racer.
- **"Only Boxes" Problem:** The perimeter `ToyShelf` spawners only generated simple cuboid blocks ("boxes") on distant walls, without recognizable miniature toys or display items.
- **Reference Image Alignment:** The user-uploaded references (`media_1792447049028.png`, `media_1792447057631.png`, `media_1792447064611.png`, `media_1792447348822.jpg`, etc.) show vibrant, iconic toy models:
  1. **Royal Nutcracker Soldiers / Soldaditos de Plomo:** Black shako tall hat with gold visor, uniform red tunic with gold frogging/cross-straps, white breeches, black boots, standing at attention in squads beside the track.
  2. **Matryoshka / Russian Nesting Dolls:** Bulbous curved wooden dolls with bright red babushka shawls, painted faces and decorative aprons, grouped in families of three on round display pedestals.
  3. **Toy Sailboats / Barcos Veleros de Madera:** Wooden/blue boat hulls with cabins, tall masts, and crisp white triangular sails with red/blue racing stripes.
  4. **Rainbow Toy Xylophones / Xilófonos Infantiles:** Wooden angled frame with 7 rainbow tone bars (red, orange, yellow, green, cyan, blue, purple) and round-headed mallets.
  5. **Wooden Stacking Ring Pyramids / Pirámides de Anillos de Madera:** Conical peg with 5 graduated colorful donut rings topped with a wooden sphere.
  6. **Vintage Tin Robots / Robots de Hojalata:** Retro cubical tin body with chest gauge dial, square head with antenna ears and visor, clamp hands, and a turning brass wind-up key.
  7. **Medieval Toy Castle Turrets / Castillo de Juguete:** Stone battlements, arched windows, and conical turret roofs with pennants.
  8. **Suspended Toy Dinosaur / T-Rex Colgante:** Bright green prehistoric dinosaur hanging from ceiling wires over the raceway.
  9. **Wooden Toy Cars / Cochecitos de Madera:** Chunky wooden cars with colorful wooden wheel discs.
  10. **Tiered Round Display Tables (Mesas de Exposición):** Multi-tiered circular display tables positioned at corner apexes and curves, presenting toys at rider eye-level.
  11. **Shelves with Real Toys:** Populate `ToyShelf` with miniature teddy bears, block stacks, boats, and cars in addition to packages.

## 3. Implementation Details
1. **Scenery Definitions (`src/world/scenery.rs`):**
   - Added toy prop structs: `Nutcracker`, `Matryoshka`, `ToySailboat`, `Xylophone`, `StackingPyramid`, `TinRobot`, `ToyCastle`, `SuspendedDino`, `ToyCar`, and `DisplayTable`.
   - Expanded `ToyStore` struct with slices for all newly added toy types.

2. **Procedural Geometry & Spawners (`src/world/toy_store.rs`):**
   - Added procedural cylinder/frustum builder `cylinder` for turning round parts (discs, wheels, rings, spires, doll bodies, hat shakos).
   - Implemented `make_nutcracker_mesh` & `spawn_nutcracker`: black shako with golden visor and chin strap, facial features (rosy cheeks, white mustache, beard), red uniform coat with gold frogging, white breeches, black boots, and golden halberd staff.
   - Implemented `make_matryoshka_mesh` & `spawn_matryoshka`: trio of Russian nesting dolls (Mother, Daughter, Baby) in turned wooden stands with red babushka hoods, painted ivory aprons with floral rosettes, and sweet painted faces.
   - Implemented `make_sailboat_mesh` & `spawn_toy_sailboat`: wooden boat hull cradle, beveled hull, teak deck, deckhouse cabin, mast, boom, mainsail, and jib with racing stripes.
   - Implemented `make_xylophone_mesh` & `spawn_xylophone`: angled pine frame with stub legs, 7 rainbow chime bars (red, orange, yellow, green, cyan, blue, purple) with metal pins, and crossed mallets.
   - Implemented `make_stacking_pyramid_mesh` & `spawn_stacking_pyramid`: wooden base and center peg, 5 graduated donut rings, and round ball finial.
   - Implemented `make_tin_robot_mesh` & `spawn_tin_robot`: tin body with chest gauge meter and needle, box head with visor eyes and antenna ears, clamp claw hands, and large brass butterfly wind-up key.
   - Implemented `make_toy_castle_mesh` & `spawn_toy_castle`: stone battlements with crenellations, wooden drawbridge, corner bastion towers with red conical roofs, and central keep with blue spire and waving pennant flag.
   - Implemented `make_suspended_dino_mesh` & `spawn_suspended_dino`: bright green T-Rex hanging from dual ceiling cables, with counterbalancing tail, toothy open jaw, and legs.
   - Implemented `make_toy_car_mesh` & `spawn_toy_car`: chunky wooden roadster with cabin windows, 4 wooden wheel discs with red hubcaps, and yellow headlights.
   - Implemented `make_display_table_mesh` & `spawn_display_table`: multi-tier circular wooden tables with decorative blue trim, presenting sailboats and stacking ring pyramids directly on top.
   - Upgraded `spawn_toy_shelf`: shelves now display miniature teddy bears, alphabet toy blocks, wooden toy cars, stacking rings, and display-window packaging boxes instead of plain cuboids.
   - Material instancing: all procedural toys share a neutral white material (`toy_vertex_mat`), rendering their rich vertex colors in single draw calls with optimal GPU batching.

3. **Track Props Placement (`src/circuits/toy_store.rs`):**
   - Populated `TOY_STORE_PROPS` across all 5 sectors:
     - Sector 1: Nutcracker honor guard lining start straight, Curva Peluches display tables, sailboats, xylophone, stacking pyramids, and wooden cars.
     - Sector 2: Train crossing gate guards, Matryoshka doll trios, sailboats on pond islands, rainbow xylophone, and display tables.
     - Sector 3: Block Canyon colossal tin robot guardians, toy castle fortress, block towers, dice, card houses, and stacking pyramids.
     - Sector 4: Suspended giant T-Rex dinosaur overhead, spectator display tables, Matryoshkas, and cars below Hot Wheels trestles.
     - Sector 5: Scalextric straight marshals, garden castle fortress, display tables, and xylophones.

4. **Documentation Updates (`docs/architecture.md`, `docs/tracks.md`):**
   - Documented the new toy landmark models and updated the `ToyStore` struct description.
