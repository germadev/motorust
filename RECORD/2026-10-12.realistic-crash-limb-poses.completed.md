# Realistic Arm, Hand and Leg Poses in Falls

- **Date:** 2026-10-12
- **Topic:** The arms and hands of a fallen rider looked odd; legs looked stiff.
- **Branch:** `claude/awesome-hamilton-hyvwrg`

## User Requests

> "El movimiento y posición de los brazos y manos a caer es raro. Busca caídas reales de pilotos para ver cómo es"
> "Las piernas también deben tener cierta libertad, no deben quedar rígidas"

## Research

Searched crash technique and descriptions of real racing crashes (MotoGP blog on leathers and sliding, Riding in the Zone, Wikipedia on lowsiders/highsiders, rider-safety guides). Direct fetches of some guides were blocked by the network, so the poses also follow how MotoGP/superbike crashes look:
- Lowside (most racing crashes): the rider drops onto hip and shoulder and slides, usually on the back or side, **feet first**, legs together and a little bent, elbows by the sides and **hands held up off the asphalt** so only the leathers touch (hands out break wrists and collarbones).
- Highside: the rider is launched; the arms let go loosely **out to the sides**, legs apart; then lands and tumbles with limp, bent limbs.
- Face down: knees on the ground, feet up.
- Once stopped: lie still a moment, arms by the sides.

## Diagnosis (renders under Xvfb, frames every 0.1 s of game time)

- The sliding pose was a starfish (arms straight out at shoulder height on the ground, legs wide): not what racers do; an arm stretched away from the camera even read as pointing at the sky.
- The tumble pose held the hands in front of the face like a boxer's guard.
- The imported riders' hands kept the handlebar grip orientation (fingers forward, palm down) all through the fall.
- Lying face down, limbs posed "in front of the chest" went under the asphalt: the thigh disappeared and the boot looked detached.
- Feet used the same spring as the hands and symmetric, almost straight poses: stiff legs.

## Implementation Details

- `src/bike/crash.rs` (`ThrownRider::pose`): new poses — thrown: arms out to the sides, loose and a little bent, legs apart, slowly drifting; tumble: limp, arms and legs bent close to the body; slide: legs together and loosely bent (one more than the other), hands held up over the chest; rest: arms by the sides, soft knees. Face down, feet poses mirror towards the back (knees to the ground, feet up), blended by how much the chest faces up. Legs drag behind while sliding; the held-up hands follow only 40 %.
- `ThrownRider::swing`: separate springs — arms 2.5 Hz / ζ 0.45, legs 1.7 Hz / ζ 0.35 (heavier, looser) — and each knee sways on its own (0.45 Hz, out of step) when sliding or lying. Hands and feet are kept at least 6 cm above the asphalt.
- `src/bike/model/pilot.rs`: off the bike the wrist goes to the hand target (no grip offset) and the hand hangs from the forearm, bent in a little at the wrist with the palm towards the body (`hand_rotation`, generalised from `grip_rotation`), blended in with `let_go`.
- Docs: `docs/gameplay.md`.
