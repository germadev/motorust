# Crash Watching a Bike on a Road off the Lap

## 1. The Camera's Shots Panicked on Ghost Island (2026-10-16)

### 1.1 User Request
> The game closed with `thread 'Compute Task Pool (0)' panicked at src/track.rs:738:34: index out of bounds: the len is 0 but the index is 0`; "el fallo ocurrió en Ghost Island al terminar de ver cómo terminaban de correr los otros corredores" (on Ghost Island, after the race, while watching the others finish).

### 1.2 Implementation Details
- **Cause** (`src/race/mod.rs`, `spot_worth`, from `main`'s `438e994`): once everybody is in, the camera weighs each bike's spot. For a bike on a road off the lap, `road` is that branch (`track.road(nearest.road)`), and the crests ahead were asked of it with the whole track's `Nearest` (`road.crest(&Nearest { index, ..nearest })`), whose `road` still said 1 or more: the branch, which has no roads of its own, looked for its road 1 (`Track::road`, `self.side_roads[road - 1]`) and panicked. Only on tracks with branches (Loop Island, Gold Mine, Ghost Island), and only while a rival rode one as the camera chose a shot.
- **Fix:** the crest is asked of the whole track (`track.crest(...)`), which finds the road from `nearest`. The other methods that take a `Nearest` (`along`, `closest_edge_near`, `edge_beside`, `half_width_beside`, `height_beside`, `pose_beside`, `surface_beside`) were checked: every other call is on the whole track.
- It had nothing to do with the benchmark, which was suspected first; a full benchmark over the five tracks was run anyway and finished cleanly.

### 1.3 Verification & Testing
- New test: `a_bike_on_a_road_off_the_lap_is_worth_watching`: a bike in the middle of every road off the lap of every track is weighed, finite and at least the bonus of a road off the lap. Without the fix it panics exactly as reported (`src/track.rs:738:34`, "the len is 0 but the index is 0"); with it, it passes.
- `cargo test`: 453 passed. `cargo fmt --check` and `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` clean.
