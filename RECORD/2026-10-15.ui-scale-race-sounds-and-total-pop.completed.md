# Interface Scaled with the Screen, Race Sounds and the Elimination Total

## 1. The Interface Proportional to the Screen's Diagonal, the Total Popping, and Tunes for the Moments of a Race (2026-10-15)

### 1.1 User Requests
> "¿Los menús son proporcionales al tamaño de la pantalla?" and then "Sobre los menús, los haría proporcionales a la diagonal de la pantalla, para eso nos viene bien resvg, ¿no?" (make the menus proportional to the screen's diagonal; resvg should help).

> "Escalaría todos los elementos del interfaz: menú de opciones, leyenda, tablas de resultados y clasificación, etc." (scale every element of the interface: options, legend, results and standings tables, and so on).

> "Sobre el contador del total de eliminados, haría una animación cada vez que el total cambie" (animate the elimination counter's total each time it changes).

> "Estaría bien añadir efectos FX o voces cuando se gane una carrera, cuando se bata un récord, al perder, etc." (sound effects or voices when a race is won, a record is beaten, on losing...).

### 1.2 Implementation Details
- **Why the menus were not proportional:** Bevy lays the interface out in logical points: the start menu is as wide as its title at 110 px (about 627 points), whatever the window, so it took half of a 1280 × 720 window and a quarter of a 2560 × 1440 one; the HUD, the options, the legend and the tables likewise.
- **The scale** (`src/ui.rs`): `scale_with_window` sets Bevy's `UiScale`, which every node in px and every font size is multiplied by, so it scales the whole interface at once: menus, options, legend, lobby, results and standings, the HUD, the speedometer (drawn at its size on the screen) and the minimap. `ui_scale_for(size)`: the window's diagonal over that of 1600 × 900 points (`DESIGN_WINDOW`, where everything is as it was; a 14" MacBook's 1512 × 982 gives 1.0, a 16"'s 1728 × 1117 1.1), but no more than its height over 900, so on an ultrawide screen (3440 × 1440: 1.6, not 2.0) the menus still fit; clamped to 0.4..4 and in steps of 0.05, so that dragging the window's edge does not rebuild the menus at every pixel. 1280 × 720 gives 0.8, 1920 × 1080 1.2, 2560 × 1440 1.6.
- **Sharp art** (the user's point about resvg): text is rasterised by Bevy at its size on the screen. The art drawn by the game (the lettering and helmets of the start menu and the mode cards, with swash, and the gamepads of the mapping, with resvg) was drawn at the window's pixels to a point; it is now drawn at `ui_pixels` = those times `UiScale`, so it is as sharp at any scale. `MenuWidth` is measured in units of the interface already (Bevy's inverse scale factor includes `UiScale`); the modes screen turns the window's width into those units before comparing.
- **The total popping** (`src/race/hud.rs`): the place line is now two texts in a row, the place and the total (" / 8"), so the total can move on its own. When the total changes (in elimination, each time one goes out; not the first time it shows) it pops: at once 60 % bigger and red, back to its size and white over 0.8 s (`pop`: up in 0.06 s, then falling as the square of the time left). It is scaled with `UiTransform` from its left end (moved right by half its growth), so neither the place beside it nor the lines under it move.
- **Tunes** (`src/sound/synth.rs`, synthesised like every other effect, no recordings): `Victory`, C E G up to the high C held over the chord, brassy (five harmonics, softer as they go up), 2 s; `Finished`, G and the D above, held, 1.2 s; `Record`, an E major arpeggio of bells (the note and a fainter one 2.76 times up, out of tune as a bell is) two octaves up, 1.1 s; `KnockedOut`, a sweep from 900 Hz down to a third of it in 0.45 s with a breath of noise; `Defeat`, G, F♯, F like a trombone, the F held and wavering, 1.8 s. Peaks are rounded off (`soft_clip`).
- **When they play** (`src/race/mod.rs`): `cue_the_moments` looks at the race each frame (`Moments`: the players in and their places, those out and whether each is a player, the record lap, the time up, the most laps against the clock) and `cues` sends a `RaceCue` for each change: a player in first → `Won`, in any other place → `Finished`, a rival out → `RivalOut`, a player out (eliminated or disqualified) or the time up → `Lost`, a lap record or the most laps → `Record`. A race set up again (fewer of everything) cues nothing. `src/sound/mod.rs` plays `RaceCue`s as those sounds, at the FX volume.
- **Voices** were not added: they cannot be synthesised well here; with recordings (or a text-to-speech service) they would play the same way, on the same cues.

### 1.3 Verification & Testing
- New tests: `the_interface_grows_with_the_screen` (1600 × 900 at 1.0; 1280 × 720, 1920 × 1080 and 2560 × 1440 at 0.8, 1.2 and 1.6; 1728 × 1117 at 1.1; an ultrawide 3440 × 1440 held at 1.6; steps), `the_total_pops_at_once_and_settles_back`, `the_moments_of_a_race_each_sound_their_own_way` (every tune within ±1 and over; the fanfare going up, the defeat down, the record high, the knock-out short) and `the_moments_of_a_race_are_cued_once_as_they_happen` (a rival out, a player out, a win then a third place, two records, the time up, the most laps; once each, and nothing when the race is set up again).
- The pause tests run the options without Bevy's UI, so `UiScale` is optional where the menus read it.
- `cargo test`: 444 passed. `cargo fmt --check` and `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` clean.
- Screenshots on lavapipe: the start menu 752 px wide in a 1920 × 1080 window (1.2 × 627), 39 % of its width as at 1280 × 720, its lettering and helmets sharp; the options panel at the same share of the screen; a race's HUD at 1920 × 1080 with "8th / 8" in one line from its two texts. The tunes and the pop cannot be heard or seen in motion here (no audio device, 1 fps): the user is to try them.
- Not changed: the minimap is drawn at 256 px, so at the largest scales on a Retina screen it is a little soft.
