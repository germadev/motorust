## 2. Options Menu Tabs and the Frame-Rate Counter

### 2.1 User Requests
> "haz los FPS más legibles, cambian demasiado"
> "los botones de sección de opciones, mejor sin borde / excepcionalmente, la leyenda de los botones L1 y R1 en opciones, la pondría a los lados de las opciones en lugar de abajo en la pantalla"

### 2.2 Implementation Details
- **Frame rate (`src/hud.rs`):** the counter shows the average frame rate of each half second (`FpsMeter`, from real time), instead of Bevy's smoothed value every frame, so it reads steadily. `FrameTimeDiagnosticsPlugin` is no longer needed.
- **Options tabs (`src/options.rs`):** the section buttons have no edge: the one shown is filled with its colour, the others faintly; when the row of tabs is chosen, the one shown is underlined in white. The keys that change the section stand on either side of the tabs (`legend::section_badges` and `spawn_lone_badge`: Q and E on the keyboard, the shoulder buttons on a gamepad: L1/R1, LB/RB, L/R) instead of in the legend at the bottom, which keeps accept and back. `Keys::Tabs` and `Language::hint_section` went with it.

### 2.3 Verification
- New test `the_frame_rate_is_shown_as_an_average_twice_a_second`; the legend test now checks the section badges of the keyboard and the PlayStation pad.
- Screenshots of the Control and Graphics tabs.

## 3. Options Tabs: Lit Focus, Section Keys at the Ends

### 3.1 User Request
> "no me gusta el undeline para el foco del botón de sección de opciones, destácalo sin cambiar el tamaño / las indicaciones de L1 y R1 llévalas a los lados"

### 3.2 Implementation Details
- **`src/options.rs`:** each tab is spawned by `spawn_tab`. When the row of tabs is chosen, the tab shown lights up instead of being underlined: its fill is mixed with white and a soft glow in its colour (`BoxShadow`, no offset, 2 px spread, 14 px blur) surrounds it. Neither changes the layout, so the tab keeps its size and the row does not move when the focus goes to it or leaves it (the underline added 6 px of height).
- The tab row is now `SpaceBetween`: the key for the section before (Q, L1, LB, L) at its left end, the key for the one after at its right end, and the tabs grouped in the middle.

### 3.3 Verification

## 5. Game Modes

### 5.1 User Requests
> "Añadamos modos de juego / 1P: * Modo historia, correr las pistas una detrás de otra, guardando los resultados, siempre a 5 vueltas * Modo libre, cómo ahora, elegir pista y correr sin más * Carrera convencional eligiendo pista, eligiendo el número de vueltas * Modo Eliminación, como el modo historia, pero en cada vuelta se elimina al último * Modo Checkpoint, partiendo de un tiempo inicial, no debe permitir que llegue a 0, recupera segundos en cada checkpoint / como el modo principal es el modo historia, pondría esa opción en grande y las otras cuatro debajo / 2P: * Modo libre * Carrera convencional eligiendo pista, eligiendo el número de vueltas * Modo Eliminación"
> "para los menús de modo de juego, usa algo similar al menú inicial"
> "En la selección de pista, muestra también el modo elegido / En el menú de pausa que se vea el modo elegido y el nombre del circuito"

### 5.2 Implementation Details
- **Flow (`src/menu.rs`, `src/welcome.rs`, `src/lobby.rs`, `src/camera.rs`):** new `GameState::Modes` between the welcome screen (1P) or the lobby (2P) and the track screen. The camera flies over the track there too, and glides between Modes and Welcome, Lobby, Tracks and Select. The bike screen goes back to Modes in a championship (whose tracks are not chosen), to Tracks otherwise.
- **Modes screen (`src/modes.rs`):** built like the start menu: translucent cards of `CardMaterial` (`menu_card.wgsl`) that light up in each mode's colour with the chequered flag sliding across, and slanted `VectorArt` lettering. 1P: Story alone in a big first row (its description says which race a saved championship is on), Free/Race and Elimination/Checkpoint below; 2P: Free, Race, Elimination in one row. Arrows walk the rows and the cards; the small cards share one lettering size (that of the widest name). `CardMaterial` gained `new`, `fade_to` and `is` in `src/welcome.rs` so both screens share the card logic; `IDLE`, `FADE`, `TITLE_SIZE` are public.
- **Rules (`src/race/mod.rs`):** `Mode { Story, Free, Race, Elimination, Checkpoint }` and `Rules { mode, laps }`. Championships (story, elimination) are 5 laps (`CHAMPIONSHIP_LAPS`); a race is 1-9 laps (3 by default); free and checkpoint have no end in laps.
  - **Laps (`Progress::pass`):** a lap counts only after the gates at a third and two thirds of the lap, so riding back and forth over the line does not count, and the grid behind the line does not give a lap. `order` ranks finished bikes by time, then the rest by distance, then those out, last out first.
  - **Elimination (`eliminate`):** each time the leader starts a new lap (not the last), the last one still in is out: a rival is despawned, a player's bike gets `Retired` (`src/bike/mod.rs`: it gets idle input and rolls to a stop). Restarting once someone is out re-enters `GameState::Race` (`restart_elimination`), since rivals that left cannot just be parked on the grid.
  - **Checkpoint:** the clock starts with the seconds to reach the first gate at 36 m/s plus 4, and each gate adds those of the stretch to the next (`checkpoint_bonus`); at nought the race is over. The laps done are the score.
  - **End (`finish_the_race`):** when every player has finished, is out, or the time is up, 2.5 s later an `Outcome` is inserted: the players' bikes are `Retired`, the `RaceControls` set of `src/bike/mod.rs` (reading the controls, restarting) stops running, the pause menu does not open, and a championship is scored (10-8-6-5-4-3-2-1) and, for the story, saved.
  - **Records (`Records` in `Settings`, `src/settings.rs`):** best lap per track (any mode), most laps against the clock per track, and the story championship under way, saved with the settings (`#[serde(default)]`, so older files load).
  - **Championship (`start_championship`):** the story resumes where it was left; otherwise both start from the first track. The track left behind is unloaded when the next race starts (`src/world/mod.rs`, `OnEnter(GameState::Race)`).
- **Race HUD (`src/race/hud.rs`):** per player (or per half of the split screen) top left: place / bikes, lap / laps, clock, best lap; top middle against the clock: time left (red under 10 s) and the last bonus; in the middle: FINISH + place, TIME UP, ELIMINATED, who just went out, lap record. Each text is rewritten only when it changes.
- **Results (`src/race/results.rs`):** a panel over the race: places with time or gap (or "out") and championship points; then the standings; then the next race, the end of the championship (back to the welcome screen), or the track screen. Back goes to Modes.
- **Track screen (`src/track_select.rs`):** under the title a badge with the mode in its colour (`modes::spawn_mode_badge`); on the card the track's record for the mode; in a race, the laps between two chevrons (the font has no arrows: `›` turned ±90° with `UiTransform`), changed with up and down (`laps_after`). Back goes to Modes.
- **Pause menu (`src/pause.rs`):** under PAUSE the mode badge with the track's name, and in a championship which race of how many it is.
