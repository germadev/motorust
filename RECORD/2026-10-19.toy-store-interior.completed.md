# 2026-10-19 - Toy Store interior

## User Request

The toy store had to look like the inside of a real toy shop: shelves,
chairs, tables with toys, and no sky. The checkerboard (Sonic-like) earth
and walls had to go, and a ceiling with lights had to be visible instead of
the sky. Reference images showed a light-wood boutique, a department-store
toy floor with a red carpet, a toy shop with a dark ceiling and display
castle, a model-train shop with fluorescent tubes, and wooden train sets on
a rug.

## Implementation

### Done

- New shop soil (light wooden floor and plaster walls) instead of the
  checkerboard soil. `Soil::Shop` uses `shop_planks` (a new pale oak
  procedural texture) on Grass and Sand ground and `plaster` on Rock.
  `shop_floor_vertex_color` and `shop_wall_vertex_color` tint them warm and
  cream. The shop's shoulders are paved.
- `SkyStyle::Interior`: warm cream palette, a steep soft sun, bright warm
  ambient light and no fog in the room; `light_the_sky` hides the `Sky` root
  when the style is interior, and the clear colour is the cream horizon.
- `Room` (`src/world/room.rs`): cream plaster upper walls, wooden wainscot,
  baseboard, chair rail, crown, corner posts and five ceiling beams; sixteen
  pendant globes (eight are point lights) and four large painted signs
  ("TOY STORE", "PLAY TIME", "PLUSH", "TRAINS"). Everything is a handful of
  batched meshes marked `NotShadowCaster`.
- Wall shelves along all four walls: frames, four boards, coloured toy boxes
  and a toy car per unit.
- `ToyTable`: tables with an oval toy railway, sleepers, a four-car train, a
  small village and trees, chairs and a terrain-conforming rug; two tables
  (blue and red rugs) beside the sweeper and the ramp.
- `WaterTable`: a pond sunk into the shop floor (`Coast::Function` as a
  signed distance to its rim), with a wooden ring at the waterline and three
  floating toy boats. It also gives the sea test both water and land.
- The wall style over the building blocks changed from `Checkered` to
  `Timber`, so no Sonic-style checkerboard is left in the shop.
- The circuit's song is now "Plastic Velocity Rush"
  (`assets/music/plastic_velocity_rush.mp3`).
- Docs updated: `README.md`, `docs/tracks.md`, `docs/architecture.md`.
