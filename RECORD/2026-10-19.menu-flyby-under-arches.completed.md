# The menus' flight over the road comes down under the arches

**Date:** 2026-10-19. **Commits:** on branch `claude/happy-ramanujan-ijcsku` (PR germadev/motorust#20), after `aa8e027`.

## User Request
- "As the view that runs along the circuit in the menus and the track select comes down in the tunnels, make it do so with the start and checkpoint arches too."

## Implementation Details
- **Why**: the camera flies 6 m over the road (`FLYBY_HEIGHT`), and the beam of the arches over it, the start's and the checkpoints' (`Scenery::gantries`), is 5.9 to 7.5 m up: it flew through them.
- **Under the arches** (`welcome::flyby_height`, `FLYBY_ARCH_HEIGHT`, `FLYBY_ARCH_UNDER`, `FLYBY_ARCH_FADE`): it comes down to 3.2 m over the road from 6 m either side of each arch, over the 22 m before that, and back up over as many past it, as it does through the tunnels (the lower of the two where they meet). `road::GANTRY_HEIGHT` and `GANTRY_BEAM` now `pub(crate)` (and `world::road`), for the test.
- Tests: `the_camera_flies_under_the_beam_of_every_arch_over_the_road` (more than 2 m under the beam from 6 m before every arch of every circuit to 6 m past it); the tunnels' test (`the_camera_flies_under_the_rock_through_every_tunnel`) asks for its own height away from the tunnels but by an arch (Loop Island has a checkpoint arch by its tunnel).
- Docs: `docs/gameplay.md` (the trackside view).
