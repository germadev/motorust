### 1.3 Implementation Details
- **The seafront** (`ELEVATION`, `src/circuits/turbo_bay.rs`): the crest at 290 m (7 m up, a vertical radius of 260 m: the bike took off from 182 km/h, just before braking for the banked right-hander) is gone: the road runs on level, 0.5 m at 160 m, 1 m at 300 m, 1.5 m at 420 m, into the bend at the water's edge; the rest of the profile is as it was.
- **A first try, undone**: "curvada hacia abajo" was first read as the arch seen on the race map, and the arch was turned round, bowed towards the gorge with the lake inside the bend, the approach a U round a hill to the east along the lake's shore. The user's next message ("debe estar curvada hacia el agua que contiene") and pictures put it back, bowed towards the lake as before; the question asked then settled the rest. Nothing of the first try remains.
- **Coming down onto the dam** (`CONTROL_POINTS`, `ELEVATION`): the crest is as it was (an arch 125 m in radius bowed towards the lake, `CREST` now 3706–3858 m). Before it, the road no longer climbs to it round a hairpin right beside it: from the top of the gorge (the gorge road now ends about 3016 m, at 9.5 m) it turns left below the dam's near end (radius 44 m) and climbs east-north-east up the hillside above the bay, away from the dam (up to 10 %), to a right-hand hairpin (radius 48 m) high over the east coast, 260 m from the dam, and comes back down the hillside straight at the dam (`DESCENT`, 3480–3706 m): over the top at about 51.5 m, 8 m down onto the crest at 43.5 m, the crest and the lake ahead below. The gorge road no longer climbs out of the gorge to a turn-off at 3110 m; it turns about 100 m sooner. The lap is 4344 m (was 4240 m).
- **Around it**: the old hairpin's hill (which stood between the old approach and the lake) is gone; a hill rises between the legs of the new climb (`climbed_massif(345, 318, 90, 52)`); the coast is pushed out round the hairpin; the lake has a lobe out under the hillside the road comes down, in sight from it; the spans after the crest (`TOWN`, `SEAFRONT`, the hotels, the BEVY billboard) moved 104 m on; chevrons for the turn below the dam, the hairpin and the turn off the crest; brake markers before the turn below the dam and before the hairpin; a railing on the left on the way down, so the lake shows over it.
- **The crest**: level across, not banked (`Banking { factor: 0 }` over `CREST`: banked into the bend, the lake's side stood 0.6 m higher and its parapet hid the water); narrow, its walls 5.5 m from the middle (`Narrowing`, as Gold Mine's bridge, closing in over the 25 m before it), so the asphalt is 10 m wide; the dam's deck now reaches just past the walls wherever they are (`edge`, `src/world/dam.rs`, was a fixed 12.3 m), so the concrete is as narrow, with the lamps on its parapets. On its lake's side a low concrete parapet (`WallStyle::Parapet`, 0.45 m, `PARAPET_HEIGHT`, `src/world/road.rs`: on a dam's crest the lake's side gets it, the other the concrete barrier); and the lake 2.5 m under the road (was 3 m).
- **The gorge's floor**: with the road no longer running beside the foot of the dam, the natural ground there hid the lower half of the face from the road up the gorge; on the road's bank the valley floor now opens out further down the river (`OPEN_FAR_ROAD`, 200 m against 120 m on the far bank).
- **The lake mirrors** (`LakeMaterial`, `src/world/dam.rs`, and `src/world/lake.wgsl`, registered in `src/world/mod.rs` as the sea's material is): the lake's water is no longer a glossy `StandardMaterial` (which, with no environment map, mirrored nothing). Worked out with the rest of the circuit (`prepare_lakes`, off the main thread): a map of what stands round the lake, 170 m out from its shore, every 2 m (`surroundings_map`: the ground's colour by what it is, its grass's greens over the grass texture's average colour, rock or sand, the roads' asphalt and the dam's concrete, lit by the sun; and its height over the water, in half floats, `half_float`, as 8 bits made stairs of the grazing reflections); and a map of the sky's gradient each way (`sky_map`, from `Palette::sky_color`, now `pub(crate)`). In the shader the reflected line of sight is followed over the map (64 steps, closer together near the water, then narrowed down in 6) until it meets the ground, the road or the dam, or else takes the sky's colour that way and the sun's glint; it is mixed with the water's own dark green-blue by Schlick's Fresnel, so the water mirrors more the lower one looks across it, and a faint ripple stirs it near the camera. Unlit, with the fog and tone mapping of everything else, no prepass, no shadows.
- **The other waters**: the sea (and Gold Mine's iron water, and Ghost Island's lakes, which are the sea's water) keep the Wind Waker shader: flat, unlit, mirroring nothing.
- **Tools**: `tools/circuit_layout.py` (new) works a circuit's lap out from its source as `src/track.rs` does (centre line and height profile), draws it from above (coast, hills, walls, a dam's lake and river) and its profile (height, grade, vertical radius), and reports its length, tightest bend and steepest grade against the tests' limits, and the crests the bike takes off over at top speed; listed in `docs/architecture.md`. `AGENTS.md` has a new section 4: tools useful beyond the task at hand, above all for the circuits, live in `tools/`, with a header, kept in step with the code they mirror, listed in `docs/architecture.md`; scratch scripts and temporary harnesses are not committed.
- `docs/tracks.md` (Turbo Bay's lap, the jumps rule, the walls, narrows and dams rows, the single-track tests) and `docs/architecture.md` (`dam.rs` and `lake.wgsl`) describe all of it.
