# Rider Arms and Legs Move During Falls

- **Date:** 2026-10-12
- **Topic:** A rider thrown off the bike no longer falls like a mannequin: arms and legs move as real riders' do.
- **Branch:** `claude/awesome-hamilton-hyvwrg`

## User Request

> "Haz que cuando se caiga de la moto, no lo haga como un maniquí, los brazos y piernas no deben estar quietas
> Investiga como debe ocurrir"
> "Al caerse, los brazos y piernas deben tener cierto movimiento"

## Research

Racing and rider-safety guidance on how riders move in a crash (MotoAmerica, Riding in the Zone, Visordown/Ron Haslam), plus procedural-ragdoll references:
- Let go of the bike and get away from it.
- **Thrown/airborne:** people falling flail involuntarily — arms windmill, legs kick.
- **Tumbling:** tuck the limbs in against the body; arms out to break the fall lead to broken wrists and collarbones.
- **Sliding:** spread arms and legs (starfish) to slow down, spread the load over several contact patches and stop rolling; the asphalt drags the limbs behind.
- **Stopped:** stay down a moment (you are often still sliding when you think you have stopped).
- Plain ragdolls go limp; believable falls need active, phase-dependent limb behaviour.

## Implementation Details

- `src/bike/crash.rs`: `ThrownRider::limbs(elapsed) -> Limbs` (pure): hand and foot targets from the hips, in the body frame with the back straight. It blends four poses with continuous weights so there are no pops:
  - flying (by height above the lying height, or still rising): arms windmill round the shoulders in turn (2.2 Hz), legs pedal;
  - tumbling (by roll rate): tucked — hands by the face, knees to the chest — flopping with each turn;
  - sliding (by speed): spread out like a starfish, jolted by the asphalt (small per-limb oscillations growing with speed);
  - resting: arms by the sides, legs together, slowly stirring an arm or a knee (0.35 Hz).
  On the ground the limbs also trail behind against the sliding direction (velocity taken into the body frame).
- `src/bike/model/pilot.rs` (imported riders, the whole roster) and `src/bike/model/rider.rs` (code-built rider): the fixed crash hand/foot targets were replaced by `limbs()`, still blended in from the grips and pegs by `let_go`.
- Docs: `docs/gameplay.md`.
