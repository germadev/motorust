# Turbo Bay's Red Bridge: Deck Out to the Walls

**Date:** 2026-10-13
**Branch:** `claude/loving-maxwell-vr7uub`

## 1. User Request
"El puente de las cuevas rojas de Turbo Bay tiene huecos a los lados, dentro del puente, por los que caería la moto" (the red bridge on Turbo Bay has gaps at the sides, inside the bridge, where the bike would fall).

## 2. Cause
The asphalt is 14 m wide (±7 m) and the walls stand 11.5 m from the centre line. Everywhere else the ground fills those 4.5 m on each side up to the walls, but under the bridge the ground drops to the river, and the red bridge's deck (`bridge_meshes`) only had its underside and its outer faces: no top between the asphalt and the walls. The physics keeps the bike on the road's height out to the walls, so it did not actually fall, but it rode over a visible hole.

## 3. Implementation Details
- `src/world/structures.rs`, `bridge_meshes`: the deck now has a concrete top on each side, from just under the asphalt's edge (0.3 m in, so no crack shows) to past the walls, at the road's height, with the deck's concrete texture repeating every 4 m. The wooden bridge already lays its planks from fence to fence.

## 4. Verification & Testing
- New test `red_bridges_have_a_deck_out_to_the_walls`: at the middle of every red bridge, there is an upward face at the road's height beside the asphalt and by each wall. It fails without the new top.
- `cargo test`: all pass. `cargo fmt --check` clean, `cargo clippy --all-targets` no warnings.
