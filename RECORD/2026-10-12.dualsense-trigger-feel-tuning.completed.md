## 3. Implementation Details

### 3.1 Resistance Profile Tuning (`src/gamepads/apple.rs`)
- Modified `compute_bike_trigger_effects`:
  - **Right Trigger (Throttle / R2):**
    - Configured with `TriggerEffect::SlopeFeedback`.
    - Zero resistance at the start of travel (`start_position: 0.10`, `start_strength: 0.0`).
    - Gentle progressive ramp-up towards the end of travel (`end_position: 1.0`), scaling subtly with speed (`end_strength: 0.20` to `0.30`).
    - Removed all trigger vibration and chatter.
  - **Left Trigger (Brake / L2):**
    - Configured with `TriggerEffect::SlopeFeedback`.
    - Zero resistance at the start of travel (`start_position: 0.10`, `start_strength: 0.0`).
    - Gentle progressive ramp-up towards the end of travel (`end_position: 1.0`, `end_strength: 0.22` to `0.32`).
    - Completely removed all vibration effects (no ABS shudder, no grass chatter).
  - **Airborne State (`!state.grounded`):**
    - Both triggers immediately release resistance (`TriggerEffect::Off`).
