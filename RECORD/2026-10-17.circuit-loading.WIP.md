# Loading the Circuits Faster, and Partly First

## 1. Why Some Circuits Take a While in the Tracks Menu, and the Ground's Meshes on Every Core (2026-10-17)

### 1.1 User Requests
> "Una cosa, por qué en la selección de circuito a pie de pista algunos tardan en cargar, no se puede hacer una carga parcial y el resto en segundo plano?"

> "Me refería a carga parcial del resto del circuito"

> "Explícame lo de Ghost Island de correr en un solo hilo"

### 1.2 Diagnosis
- How it worked: entering the tracks menu, every circuit not built is prepared in the background, one after another on one thread of its own (`circuit_pool`), in the order of the list, each with a few threads for its terrain (`Urgency::Background`: half the cores less one); one chosen before its preparation has begun starts at once on another thread with every core (`urgent_pool`, `Urgency::Now`), "Cargando el circuito…" shown meanwhile, and the menu does not stop. A circuit is shown only once all of it is prepared and built.
- Timed, stage by stage, in the test build (`opt-level = 1`, so slower than the release build) on this machine's 4 cores, with all 4 (and with 1): Ghost Island about 12 s (terrain 3.5 s, 10.3 s on one thread; the ground's meshes 4.8 s on one thread whatever the cores; the sea's mirror map 1.6 s; the roads and structures 0.6 s; grass 0.5 s, flowers 0.6 s, trees 0.1 s); Gold Mine about 6 s (its roads and structures, the excavation, 2.7 s on one thread; terrain 1.4 s; the sea's map 0.7 s; meshes 0.7 s); Turbo Bay about 3 s (terrain 1.5 s); Bahía GP and Loop Island 1 to 1.5 s. Ghost Island, last in the list, is also the last prepared in the background.
- The ground's meshes (`Terrain::meshes`: the grid of heights made into triangles, each with its colour, its grass or flower field, its rock's texture projection) went row after row on a single thread: on Ghost Island, large and mostly in flower, more than its terrain on 4 cores, and on a machine with more cores (the BC-250's 12 threads) nearly all the wait.

### 1.3 Implementation Details
- `src/world/terrain.rs`: `Terrain::meshes(threads)` makes the meshes in bands of 24 rows of cells (`BAND_ROWS`, `Terrain::band`), the bands dealt out to the threads in turn (so each gets some of the dense middle of the grid and some of its sparse edges) and joined in their order (`Band::append`, `Builder::append`: each kind of ground's mesh to its own, a kind of meadow first met later after the others), so the meshes are the same however many threads make them. Within a band the triangles share their vertices as before (its own table of the grid's points, a band and a row tall); on the rows between two bands the vertices are made twice, in the same place with the same normal, texture coordinates and colour. The normals of the grid's points are worked out on the threads too. `prepare` gives it the threads its urgency allows.
- Docs: `docs/architecture.md` (`terrain.rs`).

### 1.4 Verification & Testing
- Timed with a temporary test, not committed: the meshes on 1 and 4 threads, the same number of triangles and the same meshes by kind of ground and flower on every circuit; Ghost Island 5023 ms → 1598 ms (886 510 triangles, 10 meshes), Gold Mine 827 → 303 ms, Turbo Bay 476 → 213 ms, Bahía GP 198 → 90 ms, Loop Island 188 → 90 ms.
- `cargo fmt --check` clean; `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` clean; `cargo test`: 531 passed, 1 ignored.

## 2. A Circuit Shown First, Its Rest Coming After, and the Nearest Prepared First (2026-10-17)

### 2.1 User Request
> (as in section 1) a partial load, and the rest of the circuit in the background.

### 2.2 Implementation Details
- **Why not by places**: every stage after the terrain (the shore, the roads' edges and shoulders, the sea's map, the grass, where trees, houses, props and flowers go) asks the terrain for heights all over the circuit, so a circuit cannot be prepared a stretch at a time without working its whole terrain out first, which is the longest stage. It is split instead by what can wait.
- **Two parts** (`src/world/mod.rs`): first what the circuit is shown with (`Prepared`, worked out by `prepare` and put in the world by `build`): its terrain and its meshes, the map of its shore and what lies beside its roads, its roads, tunnels and bridges, its lakes, its signs and gantries, and the sea, mirroring only the sky as yet (`mirror::sky_mirror`: the sea's material with a map of one texel of nothing, so the switch to the mirroring sea is not a jump from the cartoon one); then the rest (`Rest`, worked out by `prepare_rest` from what the first part leaves, `Basis`: its track, terrain and shore shared, `Arc`, and what lies beside its roads; put in the world by `finish` under the circuit's root): the sea's map of what stands round it, its grass, where its trees and flowers go, put in the world with its houses and props (`props::spawn_props`), and then the sea's map with them standing (`stand_up`) put in the sea's material in place, the same handle (`Assets::get_mut`). A circuit whose first part is in the world and whose rest is still to come is in `Finishing`, with the work on it if it has started.
- **The work on another thread** (`Work`): one shape for both parts (`start_preparing`, `start_finishing`), with whether it has begun, and `wait` (waited for, or, on the web, where the background work runs on the main thread, done there).
- **In the tracks menu** (`queue_circuits`, every frame while in it, after `spawn_prepared_circuit`): the circuits not built are prepared in the background one piece of work at a time, so the choice of the next is made again each time: the first part of each, the nearest in the menu to the one chosen first (`menu_distance`, going round either way), then the rest of each, nearest first; and nothing new while the chosen one is worked on now, so as not to take its cores (one already under way goes on). Before, all of them were queued at once on entering the menu, in the order of the list, so the last (Ghost Island) came last whatever was chosen.
- **The one chosen** (`rebuild_world`): not shown, its first part is started on a thread of its own with every core if its work has not begun, as before; shown, its rest is started so if it has not begun. Its first part, when it comes, is shown with its roadside given to the track, "Cargando el circuito…" gone, and its rest started at once (`spawn_prepared_circuit`). Going on to bike select does not wait for the rest; going on to a race does (`complete_the_circuit`, on entering it, as the championship's next track and the first circuit at start-up are made whole at once, `complete_now`, `build_world`). Leaving the menu, the work on the others is let go, the chosen one's kept.
- Docs: `docs/gameplay.md` (the track select), `docs/architecture.md` (`world/mod.rs`, `track_select.rs`, the world's tests, the test counts: 538, 534 off macOS).

### 2.3 Verification & Testing
- New tests: `a_circuit_is_shown_before_its_rest_comes_and_raced_on_whole` (Ghost Island chosen at once in the menu: shown with its rest still to come, then, going on to a race, whole, more than a hundred more pieces under its root and nothing left to finish), `the_circuits_nearest_in_the_menu_come_first` (the distance round the menu either way). Updated: `circuits_cache_on_tracks_menu_and_unload_on_exit` (entering the menu, one circuit prepared, the next one in it).
- Timed with a temporary test, not committed, in the test build on this machine's 4 cores (slower than the release build, and with the background work on the same cores): each circuit chosen at once on entering the menu, before (the code at 35787b3, the same measure) and after: Ghost Island whole at 14.5 s → shown at 8.6 s and whole at 11.4 s; Gold Mine 8.1 s → 5.9 s and 6.9 s; Turbo Bay 6.6 s → 5.9 s and 6.3 s (the first one prepared in the background, already under way with few threads when chosen, so it is waited for); Loop Island 1.7 s → 1.2 s and 1.3 s.

## 3. Always the Start First, Seen from the Road, and a Quick Look at It (2026-10-17)

### 3.1 User Request
> "Estaba pensando que cuando se cambie de pista en la selección a pie de pista, siempre se muestre el principio de la pista, de modo que se pueda precargar la zona del principio de cada pista, y mitigar de esta forma los tiempos de carga"

### 3.2 What Makes a Look at the Start Quick
- Timed first with a prototype (not committed): the terrain shaped by the roads only within 400 or 600 m of the start, on the whole grid, saved little (Ghost Island 3.5 → 2.8 s, Turbo Bay 1.4 → 1.1 s; the meshes as long): the search for the nearest road is not the dear part. Within the terrain, working out the heights takes nearly all of it (Ghost Island 3.0 of 3.7 s; the ground's look at each point 0.7 s, the rest little), each point of the grid's heights as dear as any other: what costs is how many points the grid has. So the look has a grid of its own: fine only round the start's stretch, coarser away from it as the grid already is past the circuit, and the land there as it would be with no road (no road is drawn there either).

### 3.3 Implementation Details
- **The camera** (`src/welcome.rs`): seen from the road, whenever another track is chosen, the flight along it sets off 90 m before its line (`FLYBY_RUN_UP`, `flyby_start`), so its start is always seen first. It waits a frame for the new track to be in place (the track is replaced at the start of the next frame, `world::rebuild_world`): at first it set off with the last track's lap length, and on Ghost Island it flew on 1.1 km from its start, which the capture harness's log of the camera showed.
- **The terrain** (`src/world/terrain.rs`): `Terrain::shaped_within(track, scenery, threads, near)` is fine (3.5 m) and shaped by the roads round the box `near` only (and `FINE_MARGIN` round it), the grid growing coarser away from it, the land elsewhere as it would be with no road; its grass coloured as the whole circuit's (the box round the whole track still says where the land goes wild). `on_threads` is it round the whole track.
- **The look** (`src/world/preview.rs`): the stretch from 120 m before the line to 450 m past it (`stretch`; the camera stays in it some 25 s), its terrain as above round the box of the centre line along it, its meshes, the map of its shore and the sea mirroring only the sky; the roads built and cut, triangle by triangle, to the box the land is shaped in (`cut_to`); the tunnels, bridges and dams on the stretch only (`structures::structure_pieces` and `road::spawn_gantries` take a stretch, `Span::overlaps`), no signs, no lakes. It is a `Prepared` with no roadside, put in the world by `build` marked `PreviewRoot` (with the arches on the stretch only). Timed in the test build on 4 cores against the circuit's first part: Bahía GP 0.46 against 0.53 s, Turbo Bay 1.0 against 2.0 s, Loop Island 0.8 against 1.2 s, Gold Mine 0.9 against 5.5 s (its excavation left out), Ghost Island 3.4 against 6.4 s (its grid is a third of the whole one's: the grid is rows by columns, so the fine rows and columns round the start reach across the whole of it, coarse the other way).
- **The lawns in flower** (`preview::meadows`): made once a session, each kind, and cloned after (a tenth of a second or so each; Ghost Island's four kinds 0.44 s), for its look and its first part alike.
- **In the tracks menu seen from the road** (`src/world/mod.rs`, `previews_wanted`: not seen from above, where a track half there would show, nor on the web): the work in the background is a look at the start of each circuit first, the nearest in the menu first (`Previewing`), then the first parts, then the rests. A look comes into the world hidden, or shown if its circuit is the one chosen and not there yet; the one chosen is shown by its look till its first part comes (`rebuild_world`), and the first part takes its place, the look despawned (`PreviewRoots`, kept by `build`). Chosen with no look and nothing begun, a look at its start is started first on its own thread, every core, and its first part after it on the same thread. A look that comes after its circuit is despawned unbuilt. Leaving the menu, the looks but the chosen one's go.
- Docs: `docs/gameplay.md` (the track view's option, the track select), `docs/architecture.md` (`world/mod.rs`, the new `preview.rs`, `terrain.rs`, `welcome.rs`, the tests, the test counts: 544, 540 off macOS).
