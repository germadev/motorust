# Stick response as a curve: normal, linear, quadratic, square root

## 1. The stick response changed from a speed to a curve

**Date:** 2026-10-20. **Branch:** `claude/sleepy-galileo-s35zhl`. Replaces the fast / medium / slow response of `2026-10-20.stick-response.completed.md` (commit 78bc293).

### User request
"About the stick response, the current behaviour is not what I was after: the speed of the response was fine. What I think I want is the curve from the stick's turn to the bike's lean. I would put response types: linear, quadratic, square root, linear being the default. Does that fit?"

I pointed out that the game's curve was not linear. Past an 8 % dead zone it raised the stick's travel to 1.35, so linear as the default would make the middle of the stick livelier than now, the opposite of the complaint about some pads. I offered keeping the current curve as the default, and asked whether to remove the speed option. Answers: the current curve as the default with the three others ("Actual + las tres"), and the speed option removed.

### Implementation
- **`src/pause.rs`:** `StickResponse` (fast / medium / slow) replaced by `StickCurve { Normal, Linear, Quadratic, SquareRoot }` with `exponent()` (1.35, 1, 2, 0.5), `name(lang)` ("normal", "lineal", "cuadrática", "raíz cuadrada" / "normal", "linear", "quadratic", "square root") and `next(step)`. `DebugOptions.stick_curve`, `Normal` by default. It lasts for the session like the page's other variants.
- **`src/bike/mod.rs`:** back as it was before 78bc293 (`AnalogSteer::update(target, dt)`, the steering's rates as always). `stick_steer(x, curve)` raises the travel past the dead zone to the curve's exponent. Both stick paths (1P, and each player of 2P) pass `options.stick_curve`. The curve shapes the steering the stick asks for, and the lean follows it.
- **`src/options.rs`:** the row on the Stick page is still "Respuesta del stick" / "Stick response", now with the curves. Its reset and its "already default" state are as before.
- **`docs/gameplay.md`:** the steering variants table, the response's paragraph (the curves, with what each turns at 30 % of the stick: normal 15 %, linear 24 %, quadratic 6 %, square root 49 %) and a pointer from the Steering section.

### Verification
- New test `every_stick_curve_turns_fully_at_the_edge_and_they_differ_near_the_middle`. Every curve gives nothing in the dead zone and full lock at the edge, is symmetric, and grows without jumps. At 30 % they go from square root (liveliest) to linear, normal and quadratic (finest). Linear is exactly the travel past the dead zone. The default is normal.
- The old `stick_turns_little_near_the_center_and_fully_at_the_edge` test now runs on the normal curve, unchanged.
- The `options` tests use the curve in place of the response (reset back to normal).
- `cargo test --release`: 622 passed, 2 ignored (the speed's test gone, the curves' added). `cargo clippy --release --all-targets -- -D warnings`: clean. `cargo fmt --check`: clean.
