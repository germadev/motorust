# The View When Choosing the Track

## 1. Trackside or Aerial, Trackside at First (2026-10-16)

### 1.1 Context
- On the branch `m4-mini-macos`, with the roadside props of `RECORD/2026-10-16.roadside-props-on-plinths.WIP.md`. Until now the track screen always turned round the whole lap from high above, while the start menu, the lobby and the modes fly along the road: the camera jumped on the way in and out.

### 1.2 User Request
> "Añade en Opciones > Juego, la vista de selección de pista: Aérea, como ahora; A pie de pista, mantiene la continuidad de cámara con el menú de inicio y el de selección de modo. Pondríamos la opción de a pie de pista por defecto."

### 1.3 Implementation Details
- **The setting** (`TrackView`, `src/settings.rs`): `Trackside` (the default) or `Aerial`, with `TRACK_VIEWS` for the option to cycle and a name for each in both languages ("A pie de pista" / "Trackside", "Aérea" / "Aerial"). `TrackView::at_the_road` says which one is from the road, as `RaceStart::at_lights` says whether a start has lights. The field is `Settings::track_view`; the settings carry `#[serde(default)]`, so a file saved before this reads back with the new view at its default.
- **The option** (`GameItem::TrackView`, `src/options.rs`): in Options > Game, under the difficulty, as "Vista al elegir pista" / "View when choosing the track". It is part of the Game section's Reset and of what tells the section from its first state.
- **The cameras**: `aerial_camera` (`src/track_select.rs`) now runs only `from_above`, and `fly_over_track` (`src/welcome.rs`), which already flew over the road in the start menu, the lobby and the modes, runs in the track screen too when the view is `from_the_road`. One condition is the other's negation, so exactly one camera moves it. Flying on, the camera keeps where it had got to along the road, and as the player changes track it goes on over the new one.

### 1.4 Verification & Testing
- New tests: Game offers the view and starts from the road (the default is `Trackside`; both views are offered, each with its own name in both languages, and only one of them is from the road; changed, the section is no longer as it was, and Reset puts it back). The track screen's systems now initialize and run for both views, so the camera from above is still exercised.
- `cargo fmt --check` and `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` clean; `cargo test` 498 passed, 1 failed (the pre-existing `a_very_hard_pack_races_without_wrecking_each_other`, which fails at `8adb3ae` too).
- Not seen in the game yet: the screen went away (the monitor's switch moved to another machine) before it could be looked at.

## 2. The Camera Comes Down Through the Tunnels (2026-10-16)

### 2.1 User Request
> "Creo que habría que bajar un poco la cámara de aerial_camera al pasar por los túneles"

(Seen with the new trackside view. The camera that goes through tunnels is `fly_over_track`, the one that flies along the road in the start menu, the lobby, the modes and now the track screen; `aerial_camera` turns about half the track's size over the ground and never comes near one.)

### 2.2 What Was Wrong
- `FLYBY_HEIGHT` is 6 m over the road and a tunnel's rock stands `TUNNEL_HEIGHT`, 6.5 m, over it: the camera flew half a metre under the ceiling, through the ribs and over the lamps (`LAMP_HEIGHT`, 4.7 m), which passed below it.

### 2.3 Implementation Details
- **`flyby_height`** (`src/welcome.rs`): how high the camera flies at a point of the lap. Its own 6 m, and `FLYBY_TUNNEL_HEIGHT` (2.8 m) through a tunnel, from the scenery's `tunnels`. Each tunnel's span is taken widened by `FLYBY_TUNNEL_FADE` (25 m) at both ends and weighed with `Span::weight` over that same fade, so the camera is already down as it reaches the mouth and comes back up once it is out, instead of diving inside.
- `TUNNEL_HEIGHT` and `world::structures` are now `pub(crate)`, so the camera's rule can be measured against the rock it has to fly under.

### 2.4 Verification & Testing
- New test: the camera flies under the rock through every tunnel of every circuit (all the way through, at least 1.5 m under `TUNNEL_HEIGHT`), is already down at both mouths, and is back at its own height well before and well past them; on a circuit with no tunnel it stays at its own height all the way round.
- Seen on the M4 mini: flying through Turbo Bay's tunnel from the track screen, the camera passes under the ceiling with the lamps overhead and the mouth ahead.
- `cargo fmt --check` and `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` clean; `cargo test` 499 passed, 1 failed (the pre-existing one).
