Step 2 of "The engine apart from the game" in `ROADMAP.md`: what the circuits' files named as functions in Rust, written as shapes with their parameters, so that an editor can change them.

### 1.2 Implementation Details
- **`src/world/mountains.rs`** (new): `Mountains`, a list of shapes (`&'static [Mountain]`), the highest of them at each point and none if there are none; read from a circuit's file, `Scenery::mountains` (none if left out). `Mountain::Range { center, near, far, toward, ragged, facing, clear_of, mesas, relief }`: rolling peaks rising from `near` to `far` metres from the centre (or past a line through it, `toward`), the edge wandering (`Ragged`), only one way (`Facing`), none round a point (`Clear`), cut into mesas (`Mesas`), their height `low + (high - low) * fbm(point / scale + offset)^power` (`Relief`). `Mountain::Cone { center, radius, height, steepness, crater, rim, relief }`: a lone peak with a crater, its flanks' relief a function by name if any (`data::optional_function`). The variants hold their fields themselves, so a file writes `Range(center: …, …)` (an enum of structs would need `Range((center: …))`; RON's `unwrap_variant_newtypes` would read it so, but then `Some((4, …))` and the like, in every file, would have to be written otherwise).
- **Each circuit's mountains in its file**, from its function: Bahía's a range past a line to the north-east; Turbo Bay's and Loop Island's ranges facing one way; Gold Mine's mesas; Base Lunar's ring and its cratered peak; Ghost Island's ranges clear of the volcano, and the volcano a cone whose gullies, lumps and parasitic cone stay a function (`ghost_island_volcano_relief`, `volcano_gullies`, the way out the roadmap allows for what the game adds of its own); Fellowship Road's hills and the Misty Mountains, a ragged range past a line, peaks sharpened (`power: 1.4`); Juguetería none.
- **The functions gone:** the seven `mountains` in Rust, their names in `terrain_function`, `flat`, and `data::function`; `turbo_bay.rs`, left with nothing, removed; `loop_island.rs` and `moon_base.rs`, left with their tests only, compiled for the tests only; Ghost Island's and Fellowship Road's tests read the mountains from their files (a `mountains` of their own in their test modules), `VOLCANO_HEIGHT` with them. `data::TerrainFunction` names the type of the terrain's functions.
- **Docs:** `docs/tracks.md` (the shapes, under `mountains`; what stays in Rust), `docs/architecture.md` (`src/world/mountains.rs`, `src/circuits/`), `ROADMAP.md` (the mountains done; next the coasts and the water; steps 4 and 6 as the user plans them).

### 1.3 Verification & Testing

- New tests in `src/world/mountains.rs`: a range rises from `near` to `far` and only the way it faces; one past a line, its peaks between `low` and `high`; a cone round its crater, and the highest of several shapes; a file writes them as their fields.
- The terrain's tests (the snow and the forest up the gullies, the mountains to the north-east of the bay) and the circuits' (the volcano's gullies, the Misty Mountains under snow, nothing by the road) pass on the shapes.
- `cargo test`: 721 passed, 2 ignored. `cargo fmt --check` and `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` clean.

## 2. The Water

### 2.1 User Request
> "Dale a la segunda mitad del paso 2" (go on with the second half of step 2): the coasts, lakes and rivers as shapes.

### 2.2 Implementation Details
- **`src/world/waters.rs`** (new): `Waters`, how far from the water a point is, positive on the land: the nearest of its `shores` (the sea beyond a straight line, `normal` and `offset`, bent by `waves`: `along`, `amplitude`, `length`, `phase`), `lakes` (`center`, `radius`, `wander`) and `rivers` (`points` with how far the banks are at each, the width changing evenly between them, `wander`), but for its `islets` (`center`, `radius`), land in them; `Wander` (`amount`, `scale`, `octaves`, `offset`) bends a shore in and out by noise; with none, `NO_WATER` (1 km), as the old `dry` was.
- **`Coast`** holds `Waters` where it held functions: `Sea(Waters)` (was `Function`), `OutlineWithLakes(outline, Waters)`, `Inland(Waters)`; `terrain.rs` asks them `distance`. A file writes them through `CoastFile`, whose variants hold their fields (`Sea(shores: […])`, `OutlineWithLakes(outline: […], lakes: […], islets: […])`, `Inland(lakes: […], rivers: […])`, `Inland()` for none), each list empty if left out.
- **Each circuit's water in its file**, from its function's constants: Bahía's west and south coasts, each a shore with two waves; Gold Mine's lake at the bottom of the pit, wandering, and the river out of it; Ghost Island's lake, wandering, and its islet; Fellowship Road's pool, Mirrormere and Nen Hithoel, and the Water, the Brandywine, the Bruinen and the Anduin with their widths (converted from the source with their comments), all wandering alike; Juguetería none.
- **The functions gone:** `bay::coast` (and `bay.rs`), `gold_mine::water`, `ghost_island::lakes`, `fellowship_road::water` and `river_banks`, with their constants; `dry`. `gold_mine.rs` and `fellowship_road.rs`, left with their tests only, compiled for the tests only; the tests that asked the water as a function ask the circuit's `Waters` (`distance`), with the lake's and the islet's middles as constants of their own. Only one function is left for the files to name: Ghost Island's volcano's gullies. `mistakes_are_told` tries an unknown function there.
- **`tools/circuit_layout.py`** draws a file's lakes, islets and rivers from its `coast`, and Ghost Island's outline written as `outline: [...]`; its usage and help name a circuit's file.
- **Docs:** `docs/tracks.md` (`coast`: the shapes; how to add a track; how a file writes its water), `docs/architecture.md` (`src/world/waters.rs`, `src/circuits/`), `src/circuits/data.rs`'s docs, `ROADMAP.md` (step 2 done).

### 2.3 Verification & Testing
- A temporary test compared each circuit's water read from its file with its old function at every point of a grid 6 km across (7 by 11 m): the same to the bit at Gold Mine, Ghost Island and Fellowship Road; at Bahía 0.24 mm at most, the waves' sum done in another order. Removed with the functions.
- New tests in `src/world/waters.rs`: a shore bent by its wave, the sea beyond it; the nearest water but for an islet, a river wider downstream, none far off; a shore wandering no further than it says.
- The circuits' tests (Gold Mine's lake and pit, Ghost Island's lake, islet and boardwalk over the inlet, Fellowship Road dry but on its bridges) and the terrain's (the pit round the lake) pass on the shapes. The plans `tools/circuit_layout.py` draws from the files show each lake, islet and river where its function had it.
- `cargo test`: 724 passed, 2 ignored. `cargo fmt --check` and `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` clean.
