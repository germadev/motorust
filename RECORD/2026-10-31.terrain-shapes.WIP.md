### 1.2 Implementation Details
- **`src/world/mountains.rs`** (new): `Mountains`, a list of shapes (`&'static [Mountain]`), the highest of them at each point and none if there are none; read from a circuit's file, `Scenery::mountains` (none if left out). `Mountain::Range { center, near, far, toward, ragged, facing, clear_of, mesas, relief }`: rolling peaks rising from `near` to `far` metres from the centre (or past a line through it, `toward`), the edge wandering (`Ragged`), only one way (`Facing`), none round a point (`Clear`), cut into mesas (`Mesas`), their height `low + (high - low) * fbm(point / scale + offset)^power` (`Relief`). `Mountain::Cone { center, radius, height, steepness, crater, rim, relief }`: a lone peak with a crater, its flanks' relief a function by name if any (`data::optional_function`). The variants hold their fields themselves, so a file writes `Range(center: …, …)` (an enum of structs would need `Range((center: …))`; RON's `unwrap_variant_newtypes` would read it so, but then `Some((4, …))` and the like, in every file, would have to be written otherwise).
- **Each circuit's mountains in its file**, from its function: Bahía's a range past a line to the north-east; Turbo Bay's and Loop Island's ranges facing one way; Gold Mine's mesas; Base Lunar's ring and its cratered peak; Ghost Island's ranges clear of the volcano, and the volcano a cone whose gullies, lumps and parasitic cone stay a function (`ghost_island_volcano_relief`, `volcano_gullies`, the way out the roadmap allows for what the game adds of its own); Fellowship Road's hills and the Misty Mountains, a ragged range past a line, peaks sharpened (`power: 1.4`); Juguetería none.
- **The functions gone:** the seven `mountains` in Rust, their names in `terrain_function`, `flat`, and `data::function`; `turbo_bay.rs`, left with nothing, removed; `loop_island.rs` and `moon_base.rs`, left with their tests only, compiled for the tests only; Ghost Island's and Fellowship Road's tests read the mountains from their files (a `mountains` of their own in their test modules), `VOLCANO_HEIGHT` with them. `data::TerrainFunction` names the type of the terrain's functions.
- **Docs:** `docs/tracks.md` (the shapes, under `mountains`; what stays in Rust), `docs/architecture.md` (`src/world/mountains.rs`, `src/circuits/`), `ROADMAP.md` (the mountains done; next the coasts and the water; steps 4 and 6 as the user plans them).

### 1.3 Verification & Testing
- A temporary test compared each circuit's mountains read from its file with its old function at every point of a grid 12 km across (37 by 41 m): the most apart, 0.06 mm (Base Lunar), the rest 0.03 to 0.05 mm, as the same sums done in another order round off in `f32`. Removed with the functions.
- New tests in `src/world/mountains.rs`: a range rises from `near` to `far` and only the way it faces; one past a line, its peaks between `low` and `high`; a cone round its crater, and the highest of several shapes; a file writes them as their fields.
- The terrain's tests (the snow and the forest up the gullies, the mountains to the north-east of the bay) and the circuits' (the volcano's gullies, the Misty Mountains under snow, nothing by the road) pass on the shapes.
