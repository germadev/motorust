# Juguetería (Toy Store): an eighth track, indoors

- **Date:** 2026-10-28
- **Task:** A new track inside a toy store, the bikes as small as toys: slot-car, Hot Wheels and slide tracks over the furniture and the floor, under a ceiling of lamps.
- **Branch:** `claude/quirky-ramanujan-q031td`
- **Status:** Work in progress (first version delivered; visual iteration may follow)

## 1. User Request

The user (writing in Spanish) asked for a new map based on a toy store:

- One stretch on a Scalextric (slot-car) track, another on a Hot Wheels style track, down a slide, past an electric train; and asked what else could be added.
- It should look like the inside of a toy store: shelves, chairs, tables with toys on them.
- Not long, playing with height instead, about a minute a lap.
- No sky: the ceiling with its lights.
- The bikes the size of toy bikes, changing height all the time: ramps up onto a chair, from the chair onto a table, or straight down off a table, from one table to another, some stretches on the floor.
- Reference images: toy store interiors, a castle display, a slot-car shop, Hot Wheels playsets (drag strip with a numbered finish gate, Track Creator's orange track on blue supports, a loop), a wooden train on a blue rug, concept images (a dark slot track with red and white borders on blue truss pillars, LEGO castles on green baseplate tables, a pirate ship diorama, trains beside a yellow table, shelves full of boxed toys, ceiling panel lights, hanging planes and helicopters, stacks of tyres).
- Later in the task the user sent the song for the track: "Plastic Velocity Rush" (MP3).

## 2. Implementation Details

### 2.1 Scale and lap design (`tools/toy_store_layout.py`, new)

- Scale about 12 to 1 (a bike about 17 cm long as a toy): the chair's seat 5.5 m, the tables 9 to 15.5 m, the tall shelving unit 39 m, the ceiling 55 m.
- Pace: rivals average about 70 m/s, so a one-minute lap is about 4.4 km. The lap is 4.46 km; a rival's flying lap is about 68 s on medium and 64 s on very hard (about 66 and 70 m/s on average).
- The lap is drawn by a turtle (straights and arcs) with heights marked along the way, worked out as `src/track.rs` does through `tools/circuit_layout.py`, and checked against the tests' limits (radius over 28.75 m, grades under 13 %, distant stretches apart unless one passes more than 12 m over the other in a tunnel span, crests only on straights). The tool also finds the stretches under another (the `tunnels`), estimates a rival's time between checkpoints (`--pace`), prints `CONTROL_POINTS` and `ELEVATION` (`--emit`) and draws the plan with the furniture and the profile.
- The lap, in order: the drag strip (Hot Wheels' orange track on the floor, a booster kicker) north under the diorama table; round to the right (R 70) onto a slot-car track, up a ramp onto the chair (narrow, walls 6.5 m from the middle) and on up onto the first table (14 m), a half turn (R 70) round its far end; across the gap onto the diorama table (15.5 m), past its castles, over the start straight; left (R 65) into the Hot Wheels helix, a turn and a half (R 70) round a blue column up to the top of a tall shelving unit (39 m); along its top; down the helter-skelter's slide, a turn and a quarter (R 62) round its tower to the floor; along the north wall past the electric train on its rug, right (R 70) and south under the first table; up onto the yellow train table (9 m), right and left past its train, straight off its edge down to the floor; and home round a half turn (R 65).
- Iterations: earlier layouts had bends of R 48–50 that held the rivals to about 54 m/s, and the against-the-clock test failed on hard (−0.54 s at the first checkpoint). The layout was redrawn with every bend R 62–70 (single semicircles for the U-turns), and then the first table's half turn widened from R 63 to R 70 (the table made larger, two castles moved) so that a very hard rival reaches the first checkpoint with 0.81 s left (hard 0.97 s), in line with the other tracks (0.77–1.24 s on very hard); the first checkpoint sits at the end of the diorama's straight (1220 m).

### 2.2 The circuit (`src/circuits/toy_store.rs`, new; `src/circuits/mod.rs`)

- `TOY_STORE`, last in `CIRCUITS` (now eight). Name "Juguetería" / "Toy Store", descriptions in both languages, no ambience sounds, song "Plastic Velocity Rush" (`assets/music/plastic_velocity_rush.mp3`, 176.3 s, −14.4 LUFS above 500 Hz over its first two minutes, measured with `ffmpeg -t 120 -i … -af highpass=f=500,ebur128`).
- Banking only round the helix and the slide (factor 2.5), none on the floor and the furniture. Gantries: a blue "TOY STORE" arch at the line and three yellow "CHECKPOINT" arches (1220, 2160 and 3410 m: on the diorama, on top of the shelves and under the first table).
- Scenery: `Coast::Inland`, `Soil::Floor`, `SkyStyle::Indoor`, slot-car red and white curbs, a row of toy blocks along the north wall, the toy tracks as bridges (`BridgeStyle::OrangeTrack` on the drag strip and the helix, `SlotTrack` on the furniture, `Slide`), tunnels where a stretch runs under another, and the store (`ToyStore`): the room, the chair, the first table (green felt), the diorama (a studded baseplate), the tall shelving unit, the train table (yellow), a low table of blue sea with a pirate ship, an aisle of shelving units, the slide's helter-skelter tower and the helix's blue column, two electric trains, three castles, two loops of orange track, a foam mat and a rug, and toys (bear, blocks, balls, duck, robot, spinning top, ship, tyres, hanging planes and helicopter).
- Every other circuit got `toys: None`.

### 2.3 Indoor world support

- `src/world/scenery.rs`: `WallStyle::{SlotBorder, OrangeEdge, ChuteSide, Blocks}`, `Soil::Floor`, `SkyStyle::Indoor`, `BridgeStyle::{SlotTrack, OrangeTrack, Slide}`, `Scenery::toys` and the store's types (`ToyStore`, `Furniture`/`FurnitureKind`, `Tower`/`TowerKind`, `Train`, `Castle`, `ToyLoop`, `Mat`/`MatKind`, `Toy`/`ToyKind`).
- `src/world/sky.rs`: the indoor light: no sun disc and no clouds, warm white light from overhead, a warm ambient fill, a faint warm haze far off, its own colour grade.
- `src/world/terrain.rs`: the floor: flat at the floor's level, hugging the roads on the floor, no rock mounds under the stretches that pass under others, floorboards (`textures.floor`); the edge test lets the floor stand inside the walls.
- `src/world/road.rs`: indoors the road is a toy track (`toy_track`): a slot-car track's plastic with its slots and rails, Hot Wheels' orange track with chevrons, or a slide's chute, and the plastic on out under its borders; no white lines, curbs only on the slot-car track, no skid marks; the toy borders (`SlotBorder` striped, `OrangeEdge`, `ChuteSide`) as wall profiles; indoors a tunnel keeps its walls.
- `src/world/structures.rs`: nothing drawn for tunnels indoors; the toy tracks' decks and supports and the store from `toys.rs`.
- `src/world/textures.rs`: procedural textures for the floorboards, the slot-car track, Hot Wheels' track, the chute, the ceiling tiles, boxed toys (8 kinds), a studded baseplate, foam tiles, lettered blocks and stripes (`lettering::glyph` made `pub(super)` for the blocks).
- `src/world/shoulders.rs`: the floor's shoulders are paved. `src/track.rs`: `BASE_ELEVATION` made public (the store's heights are over it).
- `src/world/toys.rs` (new): the room (walls with bands of colour, a ceiling of tiles with glowing lamps, none casting shadows), the shelves of boxed toys round the walls, the furniture, the toy tracks' decks and supports (blue lattice towers, blue pillars with a cradle, red posts, brackets off a tower round a spiral; each down to whatever stands under it and never on or through a road), towers, castles, loops, mats, toys, rows of blocks, the trains' rails, all in a few big meshes of one material each (vertex colours), worked out with the circuit away from the main thread; and the trains (a locomotive and wagons on shared meshes and one material, `run_trains` in `Update`).
- Tests that picked the last circuit of `CIRCUITS` as an ordinary outdoor one (`world`, `preview`) now pick Fellowship Road by name; the sea's tests leave out the indoor floor.
