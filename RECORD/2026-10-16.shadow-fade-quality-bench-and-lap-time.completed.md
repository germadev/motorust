### 1.2 Implementation Details
- **Why the shadows popped in:** Bevy samples the sun's shadow cascades by view depth and, past the far bound of the last one (250 m), returns no shadow at all (`fetch_directional_shadow` in `bevy_pbr/src/render/shadows.wgsl`): every shadow came in at once as its caster crossed that depth. It blends between cascades, but not at the far end.
- **The fade** (`src/world/shadow_fade.rs`): Bevy's shadow shader is patched as it loads (on `AssetEvent<Shader>` for the shader whose import path is `bevy_pbr::shadows`): before `fetch_directional_shadow` returns, the shadow is mixed towards none with `smoothstep(0.8 * last far bound, last far bound, -view_z)`, so shadows fade out over the last fifth of their reach and come in softly. The patch goes in once (marked), and if the anchor (`return shadow;` before `fn cascade_debug_visualization`) is not found, as with another Bevy, the shader is left alone and a warning says so; when it is applied the game says "The far shadows fade out". Only added where Bevy's PBR is (not in the world's tests). A first idea, pushing the shadows out to 500 m with a coarse last cascade, was dropped: it cost more for a pop that the fade removes at any distance.
- **Shadow quality** (`src/world/detail.rs`, `Detail`, `Level`): High (four cascades out to 250 m, 2048 texels: as always), Medium (three out to 150 m), Low (two out to 90 m, 1024 texels). Nothing but the shadows changes with the level (the grass, flowers and trees keep their reach), so a change of level shows only as the fade moving. Two players (unless "Less detail for two players" is off): Low, and the grass and trees as before.
- **Automatic** (`AutoQuality`, the default "Automatic" in Options > Graphics > Shadows): in a race, not paused, every 2 s the frame rate against the target (60, or the FPS cap chosen, `FpsLimit::target`): under 90 % a level down; at 97 % or more for 10 s a level up, that hold doubling (up to 5 min) each time a step up is undone within 20 s, so it does not see-saw; the first 3 s after any change, the start of a race or the pause are not looked at (pipelines and shadow maps being made). Changing the cascades and the shadow map's size makes no new pipeline, so no hitch. The row shows the level it has come to ("Automáticas (medias)"); the performance log notes the quality chosen and the level in use (`shadows=Auto/Some(Medium)`).
- **Antialiasing is not automatic:** changing MSAA makes every mesh pipeline again, and Bevy draws nothing whose pipeline is not ready, so things would vanish for a moment at each change: a worse pop. It is an option instead (`Settings::antialiasing`): "MSAA 4×" (as always, the default), "FXAA" or "No". All cameras (the interface's too) are multisampled alike or not at all, so none has to copy another's picture; FXAA goes on the world's cameras only, so the interface stays sharp.
- **The benchmark** (`src/bench.rs`, `make bench` = `cargo run --release -- --bench`, or `MOTORUST_BENCH=1`): it rides the tracks (`MOTORUST_BENCH_TRACKS`, as `0,2`, or all) in free ride, the players' bikes on autopilot and the race started without lights, through eight scenarios each: one player as the game starts (shadows high, MSAA, whole trees), then one thing changed at a time (shadows medium, low; FXAA; none; lighter far trees), then two players with less detail and with all of it. Each settles 4 s and is measured `MOTORUST_BENCH_SECONDS` (10 by default), with the frame rate unlimited. A table (fps, slowest 1 %, frame times at 50 and 95 %) is printed and written to `bench-<time>.txt` beside the performance log, and the game closes. The settings tried are marked `transient` (`#[serde(skip)]`) and never saved over the player's. The first scenario is set up in the first frame after start (setting the track during start-up raced with the first world being built: a panic, found by running it on another track first).
- **The lap under way** (`src/race/hud.rs`, `Progress::lap_time`): under the total time, now labelled "TOTAL", "ESTA VUELTA 0:31.2" / "THIS LAP 0:31.2", the time since the bike last crossed the line, until it crosses it at the end.

### 1.3 Verification & Testing
- New tests: `each_level_draws_less_and_two_views_lighter` (each level reaching less far in fewer cascades, nothing else changing with it; High as always; split at the least), `the_automatic_quality_goes_down_while_short_and_back_up_when_it_keeps_up` (45 fps: down a level after settling and a look, then another, no lower; 60 fps: up only after the hold; a step up undone doubles the next hold), `resting_lets_the_first_frames_after_settle`, `what_is_drawn_so_far_comes_nearer_with_two_views_and_back` (now also the shadows chosen low changing only the shadows, and MSAA, FXAA and none on a camera, and back), `the_far_shadows_fade_once`, `every_track_is_ridden_under_each_setting_with_one_change_at_a_time`, `the_tracks_asked_for_or_all`, `the_results_read_as_a_table`, `the_lap_under_way_reads_with_its_time`.
- Run on lavapipe: "The far shadows fade out" logged, the patched shader compiles and a race draws its shadows; the benchmark on Bahía GP alone (3 s each) and on Turbo Bay and Loop Island (2 s each, a track change in the middle) ran its sixteen scenarios, printed and wrote the table and closed with no error, the settings file untouched. Even there (a CPU rasteriser, 0.4-2 fps) the shadows at Low ran about 1.4-1.8 times as fast as High, and two players with less detail about 1.3 times as fast as with all of it.
- `cargo test`: 452 passed. `cargo fmt --check` and `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` clean.
