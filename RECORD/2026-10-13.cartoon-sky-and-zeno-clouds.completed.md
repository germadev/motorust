# A Cartoon Sky with Clouds Drawn with zeno

**Date:** 2026-10-13
**Branch:** `claude/loving-maxwell-vr7uub`

## 2. Implementation Details
- **Sky dome** (`src/world/sky.rs`): the summer-sky photo is gone. The dome is the same 64 × 24 mesh, coloured per vertex from the fog colour at the horizon (`HORIZON`, now defined here and used by `src/camera.rs` for the fog) through a lighter blue just above it to a deep blue overhead, in the flat style of the Wind Waker sea. It is an unlit `StandardMaterial` with no texture.
- **Sun**: a pale disc with a soft glow (a 128 px image computed at startup), on a quad facing the camera in the direction the sunlight comes from.
- **Clouds**: four cloud shapes, each the SVG path of 4 to 6 puffs (circles drawn with two arcs) of different sizes on a block that fills the gaps between them, rasterized once at startup with zeno (`Mask`, the rasterizer the menus already use) into a 512 × 256 image. The outline is cut flat at the bottom, white on top and lavender underneath, with the colour kept where the image is clear so no dark rim shows, and with mipmaps since the clouds are seen at many sizes. The puffs and the block are wound the same way, so their overlaps stay filled under the non-zero rule.
- 18 clouds ring the horizon, 4.2 km away (inside the 4.5 km dome, corners included), from 2° to 14° above it and 650 to 1250 m wide. They are quads that share one mesh and the four materials (unlit, no fog, blended, two-sided), children of one `Clouds` entity that `drift_clouds` turns slowly round the sky.
- **Cost**: at startup, four small rasterizations (the sky tests, which draw all four shapes, take 0.05 s together) instead of decoding a 165 KB JPEG into a large texture; every frame, 19 small blended quads and one rotation. GPU memory drops from the photo's texture to four 512 × 256 images and a 128 × 128 one.
- Removed `assets/textures/sky.jpg`, its line in `assets/CREDITS.md` and its loading in `src/world/textures.rs`; updated `docs/architecture.md` and `docs/models.md`.
