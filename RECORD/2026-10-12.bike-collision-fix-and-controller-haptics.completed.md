## Implementation Details

### 1. Lateral Collision Displacement (Hacia los lados)
- In [`src/bike/mod.rs`](file:///Users/joshua/dev/joshua/motorust/src/bike/mod.rs), updated `bump` to compute the lateral axis perpendicular to the forward direction:
  `let heading_dir = (fa + fb).try_normalize().unwrap_or(fa);`
  `let lateral = Vec2::new(-heading_dir.y, heading_dir.x);`
- Bikes are separated laterally to opposite sides (`LATERAL_DISPLACEMENT = 0.32m`), pushing one to the left and the other to the right across track lanes.
- This prevents longitudinal throttle from eating the shove and avoids bot jittering against the player in a single file.

### 2. Crossing Trajectories & Rider Fall Mechanic (Caída de los pilotos)
- Evaluates trajectory alignment: `forward(state_a.heading).dot(forward(state_b.heading)) < 0.50` determines crossing trajectories (angle > 60°, perpendicular, or diagonal cut).
- When directions cross and bikes are in motion (`speed > 3.0 m/s`):
  - In `bump`: skips bouncy rubber displacement and only resolves mesh interpenetration (`push`).
  - In `separate_bikes`: triggers `CrashState` on both bikes for 2.0s with `fall_side`.
  - In `simulate_bikes`: crashed bikes slide along the asphalt with road friction (`approach(speed, 0.0, 3.5, dt)`), lean flat onto the pavement (`c.fall_side * 1.45 rad`), and right themselves when recovered.
  - In `src/bike/model/rider.rs`: during `CrashState`, the rider disconnects from the seat and slides/tumbles onto the asphalt.
  - In `src/bike/ai.rs`: rivals in `CrashState` release throttle and steering while sliding, and other AI bikes steer around fallen traffic.

### 3. Universal Controller Rumble (DualSense, Xbox, PlayStation, Switch, PC)
- Fixed missing rumble processing: `bevy::gilrs::GilrsPlugin` was disabled in `src/main.rs`, so `GamepadRumbleRequest` had no consumer.
- Implemented `handle_rumble_requests` in [`src/gamepads.rs`](file:///Users/joshua/dev/joshua/motorust/src/gamepads.rs) utilizing `gilrs::ff::EffectBuilder` to drive hardware force-feedback motors across all gamepads on Windows, Linux, and macOS.
- For DualSense on macOS in [`src/gamepads/apple.rs`](file:///Users/joshua/dev/joshua/motorust/src/gamepads/apple.rs): configured `setModeVibrationWithAmplitudes_frequency` with `GCDualSenseAdaptiveTriggerPositionalAmplitudes { values: [amp; 10] }` across all stroke positions with tactile amplitude `0.40 - 0.75` and strong rumble duration (300ms for bumps, 500ms for crashes).
