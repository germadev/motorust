## 1. Meshy's toys, by the road

### User Request

The user (writing in Spanish) sent eleven GLB models made with Meshy, following the big castle already in (`RECORD/2026-10-28.toy-store-track.completed.md`, section 5): the small castle, the pirate ship, the spinning top, the locomotive, the freight wagon, the tin robot, the coach, the teddy bear, the rubber duck, the biplane and the helicopter. Then, as they came in: the objects Meshy made should stand close by the road; and, seeing a rough orthographic preview used to check which way the models faced, asked whether the game would show that much less detail than what they had sent.

### Implementation Details

- **The models.** All are single textured meshes, light enough: 1,042 (helicopter) to 4,753 (small castle) triangles. The ship, the locomotive, the coach, the wagon, the biplane and the helicopter were made facing -X (bow, chimney, nose); the others face +Z.
- **`tools/import_toys.py`:** `TURNED`, the models made facing -X, turned a quarter turn about +Y as they are read so every piece of the pack faces +Z. The coach sized by a length of 10.5 m (Meshy's is long and narrow: at 7 m it would be 1.5 m wide beside a 2.6 m locomotive; at 10.5 m it is as tall and as wide as the others, as a coach is longer than a tank engine). The atlas widened from 2048 to 4096 pixels (the roadmap's step, before it grew past 4096 tall), and `CELLS` (1024-pixel cells) given to the big toys the riders pass close by: both castles, the ship, the bear, the robot, the biplane and the helicopter; the duck, the top and the trains keep 512. The docstring notes that a toy whose cell changes size must be given again (`--keep` cuts each kept toy's cell out of the old atlas at its size in `CELLS`). The pack is now 12 pieces, `color.jpg` 4096 × 3072 (2.2 MB) and `props.bin` 1.8 MB.
- **On the detail question:** the preview was a flat-shaded check of orientation (one colour per triangle), not the game's look. The game draws Meshy's own triangles with their colour texture and smooth normals; what it loses is texture resolution (Meshy's 2048 brought down to 1024 or 512) and Meshy's normal and metal-roughness maps, which no pack of the game uses. Answered in that sense, the big toys raised to 1024 for it.
- **`src/world/scenery.rs`:** `Castle::through` replaced by `Castle::kind` (`CastleKind::{Big, Tower, Through}`); `Train::wagon` (`Wagon::{Coach, Freight}`); `Toy::scale` documented as times the size Meshy's was made at (or times about a metre for those built in code); the toy kinds' docs say which are Meshy's.

  - The code-built bear, duck, robot, top, ship, aeroplane and helicopter dropped (`toy` keeps the blocks, the balls and the tyres; `hanging` and `ship` gone).   - The trains are Meshy's: `spawn_trains` puts the locomotive and `wagons` of the train's `wagon` on their path, each `TrainCar` as long as its piece (`length`), 0.5 m apart buffer to buffer (`CAR_GAP`); `car_pose` turns them half a turn (the pack faces +Z, a heading turns -Z along the way). `locomotive_mesh` and `wagon_mesh` gone. ## 2. Banked bends on plastic risers

### User Request

The user noticed the road was rather flat, without banking: Scalextric uses plastic pieces to bank its curves.

### Implementation Details

- **`src/track.rs`:** `Banking::raised`. Where a span is raised, the road banks as a toy track's banked curve does: its inside wall stays where the road would lie level, and its middle rises by as much as its inside would sink turning about the middle (`Track::lift`), eased in and out (`eased`: the most within 20 points either side, then averaged over 10 points either side twice, so never less than needed and smooth in its grade too, `LIFT_SMOOTHING`). `Track::lift(index)` and `Track::level_beside(nearest)` (the height the road would have lying level) for the world. A road off the lap lies level on its own where the lap beside it banks raised (no supports to draw under the ways off it, which are mostly Hot Wheels' flexible track). Every other circuit's banking spans got `raised: false`: nothing changes there.
- **`src/circuits/toy_store.rs`:** the bends on the floor and the furniture bank raised, half as much again as a road's (`RAISED_BANKING`, 1.5, most bends then at the 0.17 rad maximum); level beside the ways off the lap (the castle split, the loop's split), as those are; the helix and the slide as before.
- **`src/world/terrain.rs`:** indoors, the floor follows the road as it would lie level (`level_beside`), so it stays flat under a raised bend instead of rising with its outside.
- **`src/world/toys.rs`:** `riser`, a slot-car track's banking support: a grey plastic wedge 0.8 m along the road every 5 points (10 m), from where the gap under the deck opens out to the deck's outer edge, its top under the deck and its foot on the table, the chair or the floor, wherever the road banks raised and rests on what is under it on its inside (`resting`); there the blue lattice towers are left out. Off the bridges, on the floor, where the lap is raised, its deck is drawn too, so that its raised outside shows how thick it is (`runs` joins the points into runs, across the finish line too).

### Verification & Testing

- New tests: `track::tests::raised_banking_keeps_the_inside_where_it_would_lie_level` (every raised point: the lower wall no lower than level; where it banks over 0.15 rad, its outside more than 2 m up); `toys::tests::the_bends_on_the_furniture_bank_raised_on_their_risers` (over a hundred points of the lap on the furniture with their outsides more than 1.5 m higher, resting on their insides).
- Changed tests: the road on the furniture is measured at its inside (its lower wall), 0.2 to 1.5 m over the top, more by no more than its lift; the terrain test looks no further than the walls where the road is raised (there is no road past them, its inside wall on the floor) and measures the gap under it at its level height.
- Found on the way: the roads into and out of the loop at first took the lap's raised banking through their junctions (their bank at the straight into the loop not zero) and banked on their own with a lift of their own, which made crests sharper than the tests allow where they leave the lap and on the castle way; the roads off a raised lap now lie level, and the lap with them beside the ways off it.
