# Loop Island: An Easier Way Into the Loop and a Smooth Road Back

- **Date:** 2026-10-12
- **Topic:** Move the fork of Loop Island's branch before the crest of the start straight so the turn towards the loop is gentle, and fix the road from the loop back to the lap, where the bike kept hitting the ground.
- **Branch:** `claude/epic-brahmagupta-3t1you`

## User Request

> "Respecto al looping de Loop Island, haría la entrada más fácil, pondría la entrada antes del cambio de rasante, para que el giro para dirigirse hacia el looping sea más suave. Y en la salida habría que arreglar el tramo hasta que se vuelve a unir al trazado principal, ahora mismo al salir del looping la moto chocar mucho con el suelo"

## Diagnosis

- **Entry.** The branch forked at 215 m, 20 m after the crest at 195 m where the bike takes off flat out, and turned towards the loop with a 45–60 m radius: the bike landed right where it had to turn hard, and at speed it could not take the corner (65 m/s² of grip allows only about 55–60 m/s at that radius).
- **Exit.** A road off the lap got its height by blending the lap's height with the loop's by how far it was from the lap (all of it between 23 and 53 m from the lap's centre line). The road back ran level at the loop's height (6.5 m) for 64 m and then, as it closed on the lap, had to climb nearly 5 m in about 60 m: a valley at the foot of the ramp (vertical curvature −9·10⁻³ 1/m, 11.8 % grade) inside a 60–75 m right-hander, then a crest where it met the lap's own crest at 540 m. A Rust simulation of rivals coming out of the loop at 60–70 m/s showed them braking hard for the corner, the suspension compressing at the foot of the ramp, and the bike taking off and landing three times in 0.2 s on the crest. The approach had the same kind of ramp (11 %) just before the mouth.
- Also, `Track::crest` held the height flat past the ends of a road off the lap, so a road climbing onto the lap looked like a crest at its very last metres (a small hop at the join).

## Implementation Details

### Smooth heights for roads off the lap (`src/track.rs`)

- `Track::side_road`: while a road off the lap shares the lap's road it still lies on the lap's surface. From where it parts (the last point, going out from the loop, whose surface is its own), its own height is now a cubic (`ease`) along the road, from the lap's height and grade there to the loop's height, reached level at the 25 m straight into or out of the loop. The climb or drop is spread over all the road in between instead of the 30 m band where it parts from the lap. Banking is unchanged (the lap's, blended into its own by how far apart it is).
- `Track::crest`: past either end of a road off the lap, the road carries on the way it went (linear extrapolation) instead of being held flat.
- `Branch` doc comment updated.

### New branch layout (`src/circuits/loop_island.rs`)

- Designed with a Python mirror of `Track` (centripetal Catmull-Rom, resampling, `profile_height`, side-road heights and the blend at query time), then ported and checked in Rust.
- The branch now forks at 110 m, on the climb to the crest (195 m), and peels away in a long right-hander (about 160 m radius; never under 119 m) to the north-east, over the shoulder of the crest (8.4 m, where it still shares the lap's road) and gently down to the loop. The loop moved to `(82.2, -258.5)`, still heading north-east, 1 m higher (`height: 5.5`).
- The road back goes north-east, sweeps right (about 200 m radius), runs straight towards the lap and joins it in a last long right-hander (about 340 m radius) at 637 m, climbing gently (no more than 4.8 %, including the lap's own grade at the join). The exit control points were solved so the road lands exactly on the lap, tangent to it.
- The branch is now 520 m long (176 m in, 102 m round the loop, 242 m out) for 527 m of lap left out.
- Scenery: the "MOTORUST" billboard moved from 150 m (now on the branch) to 95 m, just before the fork; a new row of palms inside the first long corner, round the branch (rows skip points on or beside it).

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Fork | 215 m (after the crest) | 110 m (85 m before the crest) |
| Tightest turn into the loop | 45 m | 119 m |
| Tightest turn out of the loop (before the lap's own corner at the join) | 57 m | about 200 m (81 m is the lap's corner at the join) |
| Steepest grade where the branch has its own surface (in / out) | 11.3 % / 11.8 % | 4.2 % / 4.1 % |
| Sharpest dip / crest there (1/m) | −8.3·10⁻³ / 7.6·10⁻³ (in); −9.0·10⁻³ / 5.2·10⁻³ (out) | −3.4·10⁻³ / 5.4·10⁻³ (in, next to the lap's crest); −1.2·10⁻³ / 2.3·10⁻³ (out) |
| Rivals (skill 0.8–1.0) coming out of the loop | brake for the corner, three take-offs and landings | no braking for the corner, no take-off, no landing |

### Tests

- `track::tests::branches_climb_and_drop_smoothly_on_their_own`: where a road off the lap has a surface of its own, its ridden surface (`surface_point_on`) has grades under 6 % and vertical curvature under 6·10⁻³ 1/m (the lap's start-straight crest is about 6.6·10⁻³).
- `circuits::loop_island::tests::the_branch_forks_before_the_crest_and_turns_gently`: the fork is more than 50 m before the highest point of the start straight; the road to the loop never turns tighter than 100 m and the road back never tighter than 75 m.
- `bike::ai::tests::out_of_a_loop_the_road_back_keeps_the_wheels_on_the_ground`: a bike leaving the loop at 30, 50 and 70 m/s, flat out and steered as a rival, stays on the ground all the way to 30 m past the join, and after the first half second its suspension never compresses past 8 cm (the loop leaves it fully compressed, 12 cm). It fails on the old layout (the bike flies).

## Verification & Testing

- `cargo test`: 216 passed (213 before, plus the three new tests). The existing branch tests (junctions on the lap's surface and heading, straight and level into and out of the loop, walls clear of the lap once apart, bikes finding the branch's road, rivals riding round the loop and back, the pack race on Loop Island with at most two falls) pass unchanged.
- `cargo fmt --check`: clean. `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings`: clean.
- Physics checked with a temporary diagnostic test (removed before committing): rivals of skill 0.8, 0.9 and 1.0 on the branch, at 64–77 m/s out of the loop. Before: braking for the corner after the loop, suspension compressing to 7 cm at the foot of the ramp, then three take-offs and landings on the crest. After: no braking for any corner (the slower rivals only ease off to their own top speed), no take-off or landing anywhere on the branch, suspension within ±5 cm once back up from the loop.
- Visual check in-game under Xvfb with Mesa's software Vulkan (lavapipe), through a temporary harness that was not committed (fixed cameras over Loop Island, UI hidden): the fork from above and from the rider's height, the gore between the lap and the branch with its hedge walls, the loop, the road back with its curbs and checkerboard walls, the join, and the scenery round them. One of the views is the new `docs/images/loop-island.jpg` (1280 × 684, progressive JPEG).
- Not checked: `cargo check --target wasm32-unknown-unknown` (no platform-specific code changed), and how it feels at full frame rate with a controller.
