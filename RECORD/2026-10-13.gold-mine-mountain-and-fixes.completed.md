# Gold Mine mountain, lake and forks; tyre and music fixes

- **Date:** 2026-10-13
- **Branch:** `claude/hopeful-bell-eknth2` (after commit `d66253e`)

## 1. Translucent tyres

### 1.1 User Request
- "los neumáticos de la moto se ven translúcidos" (the bike's tyres look translucent).

### 1.2 Implementation Details
- `src/bike/model/tyre.rs`: the procedural tyre's triangles were wound clockwise seen from outside (at the crown, the edge across the section is +X and the edge round the wheel is +Z, so `a, a+1, b` faces −Y while the tyre's outward normal there is +Y). With back faces culled, the outside was not drawn and the inside of the far half showed through. The indices are now `a, b, a+1, b, b+1, a+1`, counter-clockwise from outside. The vertex normals were already right.

### 1.3 Verification & Testing
- New test `every_face_of_the_tyre_is_drawn_from_outside`: the front of every triangle faces the way its corners' normals point. The eight tyre tests pass.

## 2. Music too quiet on Gold Mine and Bahía GP

### 2.1 User Request
- "la música de la Mina Dorada y la de Bahía GP tienen poco volumen" (the Gold Mine and Bahía GP music is quiet).

### 2.2 Implementation Details
- Measured with ffmpeg's `ebur128`. The four songs were normalised to the same integrated loudness (−24 LUFS) and matched it. But over the whole song the bands differ: Gold Mine and Bahía GP carry their loudness in the bass, which the engines' drone covers, and Loop Island in the mids and highs. Measured above 500 Hz over the first two minutes (as long as a race), the songs played at −27.5 (Bahía GP), −27.9 (Turbo Bay), −26.4 (Loop Island) and −28.9 LUFS (Gold Mine). In short-term loudness during a race, Bahía GP and Gold Mine sat 4–5 dB under Loop Island.
- `Song::loudness` is now that measure (`ffmpeg -t 120 -i song.mp3 -af highpass=f=500,ebur128 -f null -`): Bahía GP −16.1, Turbo Bay −16.0, Loop Island −13.3, Gold Mine −16.8 LUFS. `LOUDNESS` is −25.4 LUFS above 500 Hz: Loop Island's level plus 1 dB. Change in gain: Gold Mine +3.5 dB, Bahía GP +2.1, Turbo Bay +2.5, Loop Island +1.0.
- Docs: `docs/gameplay.md`, `docs/tracks.md`, `docs/architecture.md`.

### 2.3 Verification & Testing
- Margin of each song's loudest second (first 2 minutes, full-band RMS) under the player's engine flat out, before the change: 13.4 dB (Bahía GP), 14.1 (Turbo Bay), 15.5 (Loop Island), 13.9 (Gold Mine). After it, every song still clears the 10 dB the test `the_music_stays_well_under_the_player_s_engine` asks for (the smallest margin is Gold Mine's, about 10.4 dB). The 17 sound tests pass.

## 3. MotoGP tyres: rounder, no tread

### 3.1 User Request
- "las ruedas de moto gp son más redondeadas y con menos dibujo, documéntate" (MotoGP tyres are rounder and have less tread; look it up).

### 3.2 Research
- In the dry, MotoGP races on slicks (Michelin Power Slick), with no tread pattern at all, built to grip at lean angles up to 64°. The compound is marked on the sidewall: in Michelin's 2016 code, white for the soft, no marking for the medium, yellow for the hard (intermediates grey, soft wets blue). Sources: crash.net ("Know the Michelin MotoGP tyre markings"), racer.com ("Michelin reveals tire color codes", 2016), tyrepress.com (2014 colour-coded sidewalls), Wikipedia ("Motorcycle tyre", on profile and lean). Whether the colour code is still the same today was not confirmed; motogp.com could not be reached from the container.

### 3.3 Implementation Details
- `src/bike/model/tyre.rs`: the crown of the cross-section is now an ellipse (`SQUARENESS` 2.6 → 2.0), round from shoulder to shoulder, and the tread covers ±82° of it (was ±75°), with short sidewalls. The four tread patterns (slick, sport, touring, sipe) with their grooves and normal maps are gone: `Compound::{Soft, Medium, Hard}` slicks, smooth rubber scrubbed lighter in the middle, with a white band (soft), none (medium) or a yellow band (hard) round the sidewall. Soft: Kraken RS, Stiletto V4, Azura RZ; medium: Shogun TZ, Falcon KRZ, Ronin YS; hard: Legend 88, Cobra XT. Rubber with a satin sheen (roughness 0.62). The grain noise blends round so the texture repeats without a seam.
- `src/bike/model/mod.rs`: one material per compound, shared.
- Docs: `docs/models.md`, `docs/architecture.md`.

### 3.4 Verification & Testing
- New and changed tests: `slicks_are_smooth_with_their_compound_on_the_sidewall`, `the_crown_is_round_from_shoulder_to_shoulder` (an ellipse at the shoulder; past 60° of lean still on the tread), `the_texture_repeats_seamlessly_round_the_tyre`, `the_imported_bikes_wear_every_compound`. The nine tyre tests pass.
- In game (Xvfb + lavapipe): close-ups of the eight bikes on the grid; the tyres are solid, round and smooth, with their bands.

## 4. Gold Mine: a figure of eight through the mountain and over it

### 4.1 User Request
- "el trazado es muy simple, le añadiría subida y bajada de la montaña, haz el lago bastante más grande y el puente de madera bastante más largo / en los caminos bajo la montaña, deberían ser bastante más largos, con cambios de pendiente importantes y que los caminos, no haya uno claramente mejor que las alternativas, haz más bifurcaciones bajo la montaña" (the layout is too simple: add a climb up the mountain and down it, a much bigger lake and a much longer wooden bridge; much longer ways under the mountain, with big changes of grade, none clearly better than the others, and more splits).
- After seeing a map of a first redesign (3.54 km, the climb outside the mountain before the excavation): "Esperaba que el trazado cruzase la montaña de lado a lado, primero por el interior, por las galerías y luego subiéndola y bajándola" (I expected the lap to cross the mountain from side to side, first inside, by the galleries, and then climbing it and coming down it).
- Earlier, with a photo of an open-pit mine: "y que se crucen los caminos" (and the roads should cross). The crossing is done here; the open pit comes next.

### 4.2 Implementation Details
- **Layout** (`src/circuits/gold_mine.rs`, 4.79 km, was 2.2 km at the last commit): a figure of eight round a mountain 70 m high, 1080 m across, centred at (−720, −200). Start straight north through town (0–300 m); a left-hander at its end and south-west up to the east mouth (875 m); through the mountain by its middle, east to west (875–1700 m), down at 5 % to 2.5 m and up at 5.5 % to 21 m; out of the west mouth and round, north, to the lake; a straight bridge of 495 m north-east across it (2105–2600 m); up the north flank, 55 m at 7–8 % (2620–3530 m); over the top, south, at 69 m (3530–3880 m), crossing the excavation at about 3612 m, 65 m above its timbered neck (1265 m); down the south-east flank at 8 % into town. The layout was drawn in a scratchpad script (centripetal Catmull-Rom like the game, radii, grades, crests, the road against the mountain's surface, close stretches and the crossing), and turned 180° about the start so that the start straight faces −Z from the origin, as the tests ask.
- **Splits**: two, each three ways, with the timbered neck between them (where the road over the top crosses): the high road (left, 932–1224 m) over a cosine hump of 1.9 m and the deep road (right) through a dip as deep, in the sandstone and slate halls; the upper gallery (left, 1334–1648 m) over a hump of 2.2 m and the lower gallery (right) through a dip as deep, in the clay and sandstone halls. Each branch is a cosine bump out to 42 m from a straight line, first points at 5 % and 12 % of the way so that it leaves the lap within 0.08 rad of its heading; the lap snakes between them in an S of ±10 m that leaves and rejoins the straight line going straight (`sin 2πu · sin πu`). Each rise is a full cosine over the stretch where the branch is 26 m or more from the lap, as deep as a crest of 0.0024 1/m allows.
- **Branch heights** (`src/track.rs`): `Branch::rise` (pairs of metres along it and metres above the lap beside it, Hermite through them via the new `open_profile`, none at the ends; Loop Island's is `&[]`). A branch without a loop now runs at the height of the lap's surface beside it (with its banking at the clamped lateral) plus its rise; how far along the lap it is beside is smoothed over 8 points either side (fewer towards the ends, where it lies exactly on the lap), which removes the jitter of projecting far points onto the lap's polyline. Earlier attempts (smoothing over 40 points with a separate blend into the parted zone) left crests of up to 0.0059 at the edges of that blend; with the lap's grade steady through each split, the simple form keeps every crest under 0.0030. `FORK_BLEND` (10 m) replaces the junction blend for these branches.
- **Balance**: flat out, the bikes ride the splits near top speed, where slopes hardly matter (a test with a 4 m dip changed the times by nothing), so the times come from length and bends. With the lap straight, the branches were 4–5 % slower; with the lap snaking as long as they are, the times through the first split are 5.90 / 5.72 / 5.87 s (left / lap / right, slowest 3.2 % over the quickest) and through the second 6.27 / 6.14 / 6.25 s (2.0 %).
- **Mountains the road climbs** (`src/world/scenery.rs`, `src/world/terrain.rs`): `Massif::climbed` and `climbed_massif()`. Their smooth slopes (a broad top, ±12 % roughness) are the ground itself: beside a road on them the ground is the road's verge out to 13 m and reaches the mountainside 30 m further (cut into it uphill, built up downhill), and round a tunnel through them at once, as steeply as rock (4–8 m past the verge). Ordinary massifs still rise sheer behind the walls. The mountain has three shoulders (north-west, south and south-east), and seven towers of red rock stand on its slopes and top, all more than 80 m from any road.
- **The crossing** (`src/world/cavern.rs`): the excavation looks for other stretches of the lap that come within 120 m of it (`Cave::others`) and, where one passes over its roof (`Cave::passing_over`), the outside of its rock is level with that road 0.15 m under it out to 13 m from its centre line, with no hump, coming back to its own shape over 20 m more. The ground under it stays down in the excavation, as at Turbo Bay's tunnel (`Tunnels::beneath`).
- **Lake** a circle of 235 m radius at (−1225, −626) with a wandering shore, a river out of it to the north. **Jumps**: the crests in town (185 m) and on the bridge (2390 m) peak on the climb side so that a bike takes off while still rising (`settle` uses twice gravity rising, four times falling). **Scenery** moved to the new lap: arches at 0, 1780 and 3720 m; trestle in the east mouth's cutting (865 m); headframes by the road to the lake and over a shaft on the top; rails in town and on the way to the lake; lantern rows (none over the crossing, where the ground is down in the excavation).
- **Tests**: the flat-out lap's horizon is 80 s (the lap takes about 66 s). Docs: `README.md`, `docs/tracks.md` (Gold Mine rewritten; `climbed_massif`, `rise` and the balance in the branch section; tests), `docs/gameplay.md`, `docs/architecture.md` (292 tests), `docs/building.md`.

### 4.3 Verification & Testing
- New tests: `the_lap_crosses_the_mountain_through_it_and_then_over_it` (mouths far out on the mountain, the middle near its centre; up 50 m and more steadily, down 60 m and more; the road over the top within 3 m of the road through it, more than 50 m above it), `gold_mine_climbs_its_mountain_and_passes_over_its_galleries` (on the mountain the verge is level with the road, the ground 58 m away never towers on both sides and falls away on one side at a third of the points or more; each level of the crossing found by the bikes on it, the ground down in the excavation), `the_rock_over_the_excavation_carries_the_road_over_the_top`, `no_way_through_a_split_is_clearly_the_quickest` (slowest within 5 % of the quickest), and the fork test now checks the grade (< 12 %) and crests (< 0.0035) of the surface as ridden.
- `cargo test`: 292 passed. `cargo fmt --check` clean. `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` clean.
- In game (Xvfb + lavapipe, temporary harness not committed): the halls of both splits, the timbered neck, the bridge over the lake, the climb, the top with its headframe and arch, the descent, and aerial views of the climb and of the crossing, where the road over the top runs on, level, over the rock of the excavation.

## 5. Gold Mine: the great lake at the bottom of a flooded open pit

### 5.1 User Request
- With a photo of an open-pit mine (terraced benches round a reddish lake at the bottom, like the Corta Atalaya at Rio Tinto): "se podría añadir algo así, y que se crucen los caminos" (could something like this be added, and the roads cross). The crossing was done in section 4 (the road over the top passes 65 m above the excavation); this section adds the pit.

### 5.2 Implementation Details
- **Scenery** (`src/world/scenery.rs`): `Pit { benches, step, tread, spoil }` and `Scenery::pit: Option<Pit>`; `Water { Sea, Iron }` and `Scenery::water`. Bahía GP, Turbo Bay and Loop Island: `Water::Sea`, no pit. Gold Mine: `Water::Iron`, and a pit of five benches 3.4 m high and 9 m wide, with 45 m of spoil round the rim.
- **Terrain** (`src/world/terrain.rs`): `pit_ground(pit, land, ground)` shapes the ground by its distance from the water (the coast function, so the benches follow the wandering shore): under the water as it was; from 0.4 m above the water, bench above bench, each face rising one step over 0.6 m per metre (steep), up to the rim (16.4 m); past it, the spoil slopes down to the ground round about over `spoil` metres, or the pit is cut into higher ground. It applies far from the roads, to the ground near them past their verges (blended in over 12 m), and under the bridge (where the ground still stays 2.5 m under the deck).
- **Water** (`src/world/sea.rs`, `sea.wgsl`): the sea's colours are a uniform, `SeaColors { deep, open, shallow, foam }`, from `SeaColors::of(scenery.water)`: the same blues and white foam as before for the sea; wine red (0.36, 0.07, 0.05), rust (0.52, 0.13, 0.06) and orange ochre by the shore (0.82, 0.38, 0.12) with a pale yellow scum for iron. The river out of the lake is red too, like the Río Tinto.
- **Layout** (`src/circuits/gold_mine.rs`): the bridge and its approaches raised to the rim, the deck at 17.6–21.8 m (18 m over the water); the road through the mountain steepened back to about 5 % down from 16.6 m to 2.6 m and about 5 % up to 19.6 m (so that the upper gallery, over its hump, stays under 12 %: the steepest branch is now 11.3 %); the climb up the north flank is now 48 m (from 17.6 to 66 m at 5–7 %), and its test asks for more than 45 m. The track select screen now says "una corta inundada" (a flooded open pit) instead of "un gran lago".
- Docs: `README.md`, `docs/tracks.md` (the land, the lap, `water` and `pit` in the scenery table, the tests), `docs/architecture.md` (295 tests), `docs/building.md`.

### 5.3 Verification & Testing

- `cargo test`: 295 passed. `cargo fmt --check` clean. `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` clean.
- In game (Xvfb + lavapipe, temporary harness not committed): from the bridge and from the air, the benches step down in banded red rock to red water with a pale scum along the shore, all round the pit, the bridge crossing it on its piers and the road to the climb cut through the benches at its far end.
