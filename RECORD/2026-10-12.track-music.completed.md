# Track Music Mixed Under the Bike

- **Date:** 2026-10-12
- **Topic:** Play the music the user added for each track during the race, at a level that leaves the bike's sounds clearly audible.
- **Branch:** `claude/dreamy-brahmagupta-lam887`
- **Follows:** commit `ce2b270` (the user added `assets/music/bahia.mp3`, `turbo_bay.mp3` and `loop_island.mp3`).

## User Request

> "He añadido pistas de música para los circuitos. El volumen de la música debe permitir seguir escuchando los sonidos de la moto"

## Diagnosis

- **The songs are mastered as loud as the engine.** Measured with `ffmpeg -af ebur128`: Bahía −12.6 LUFS, Turbo Bay −12.1 LUFS, Loop Island −10.9 LUFS (true peaks up to +0.9 dBTP, about 3 minutes each, 192 kbps, 44.1 kHz stereo). The synthesised mix of the player's engine with the wind (rendered from `synth::Mixer` to WAV with the gains `place_sounds` and `player_sounds` set, then measured the same way) is −10.7 LUFS flat out at 14,000 rpm, −11.2 at 16,500 rpm, −15.5 at half throttle, −18.4 off the throttle at 11,000 rpm and −29.8 idling on the grid. Played as they come, the songs would cover the bike.
- **Bevy's looping would use a lot of memory.** `PlaybackMode::Loop` wraps the decoder in rodio's `repeat_infinite`, which buffers every decoded sample to replay it: about 60 MB of `f32` per 3-minute song, also on the web.
- **MP3 support.** Bevy's default audio formats only include Vorbis; MP3 needs rodio's `mp3` feature (Symphonia, pure Rust, so it also works on `wasm32`).
- Everything else in the game is embedded with `include_bytes!`, so the songs are embedded the same way.

## Implementation Details

### Each track names its song (`src/circuits/`)

- New `Song { mp3, loudness }` and a `song` field on `Circuit`. Each track embeds its MP3 with `include_bytes!` and records its integrated loudness as measured by `ffmpeg -af ebur128`: Bahía `bahia.mp3` (−12.6), Turbo Bay `turbo_bay.mp3` (−12.1), Loop Island `loop_island.mp3` (−10.9).

### Music playback (`src/sound/music.rs`, new)

- `SongSource`: a Bevy audio source (`Decodable`) over the embedded bytes. Its decoder is rodio's `LoopedDecoder` (gapless), which goes back to the start of the MP3 when it ends and decodes it again, so nothing is buffered and the bytes are not even copied (`Cursor<&'static [u8]>`).
- Level: every song is turned to the same loudness, `LOUDNESS = −24 LUFS` (a gain of 10^((−24 − loudness)/20): −11.4 dB for Bahía, −11.9 dB for Turbo Bay, −13.1 dB for Loop Island). That is about 13 dB under the player's engine flat out, 8.5 dB under half throttle and about 6 dB under it off the throttle at speed, so revs, gear changes, pops, tyres and impacts stay on top. It also scales with the master volume, so **M** mutes it.
- `play_music` (in `Update`, after `toggle_mute`): when the race starts it spawns the current track's song (`AudioPlayer<SongSource>`, `PlaybackSettings::ONCE` at volume 0; the decoder never ends). A fader goes to 1 while racing, to 0.5 (−6 dB) while the pause menu is open, and to 0 when leaving the race; it moves linearly, 2 s from silence to full and 1 s back down, in real time (`Time<Real>`, since the pause stops virtual time). Once faded out, the entity is despawned, which stops the sink and frees the asset. Restarting the race keeps the song going.
- `SoundPlugin` registers the audio source (`add_audio_source::<SongSource>()`) and the system.

### Dependencies

- `Cargo.toml`: `rodio = { version = "0.22", default-features = false, features = ["mp3"] }`, the same version Bevy 0.19 uses, for `Decoder::builder().build_looped()` (Bevy does not re-export rodio's decoder). `Cargo.lock` gains Symphonia's MP3 crates.

## Verification & Testing

- New tests in `src/sound/music.rs`:
  - `every_song_is_turned_to_the_same_loudness`: each song's loudness plus its gain is `LOUDNESS`, and every song is turned down.
  - `songs_decode_and_loop_back_to_the_start`: each MP3 decodes as 44.1 kHz stereo, is over a minute long and not silent, and past its last sample the looped decoder plays exactly the same samples it started with.
  - `the_music_stays_well_under_the_player_s_engine`: the loudest second of each song's first minute, with its gain, is more than 10 dB (RMS) under the synthesiser's mix of the player's engine flat out with the wind. Measured: −23.7 dB (Bahía), −23.8 dB (Turbo Bay) and −25.0 dB (Loop Island) against −9.5 dB.
  - `fades_in_dips_in_the_pause_and_fades_out`: fade times and the pause level.
  - `the_track_s_song_plays_during_the_race_only`: in a headless app with Bevy's audio plugin, no song in the menus, the current track's song at full fader during the race, fading out and despawned after leaving it.
- `cargo test`: the 14 sound tests pass (1.8 s); the whole suite, 221 tests (216 before plus the 5 new ones), passes.
- `cargo fmt --check`: clean. `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings`: clean (`play_music` takes 8 system parameters and carries `#[allow(clippy::too_many_arguments)]`, like the other large systems).
- `cargo check --target wasm32-unknown-unknown`: compiles (Symphonia's MP3 decoder is pure Rust).
- The music has not been listened to in the game here (the container has no audio device): the level comes from the loudness measurements above, and the in-game balance should be confirmed by ear.
