# 2026-10-18 — Moon Base: High-Visibility Observatory Domes, Launchpads, and Space Station Complex

## Entry 1 — Analysis & Design Plan

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Task:** Make the telescope observatory domes, space station structures, launchpads, and radio antennas unmistakably prominent and clearly visible from the road across the entire `Base Lunar` circuit.

### User Request
The user noted that signature elements like the telescope dome (`la cúpula`) and the space station (`la estación espacial`) are poorly visible from the road, even though they are what give the circuit its personality and must be very visible (`deben ser muy visibles`).

### Root Cause Analysis
1. **Sunken bases via `lowest_ground`:** `spawn_observatories`, `spawn_biodome`, and `spawn_rocket` used `lowest_ground(terrain, spot, rotation, half)`. On sloped mountainsides beside the road, the outer corner of a 28–36 m footprint can be 10–25 m below the road, sinking the entire observatory dome or biodome below road level behind the concrete/stone barriers.
2. **Excessive lateral offset & off-axis placement:** Several observatories, rockets, and dishes were placed at raw `(x, z)` coordinates 50–100 m away from the road instead of right beside the road (`WALL_DISTANCE + margin`) and dead ahead on the outside of bends where the camera looks straight at them.
3. **Tree occlusion:** Forest spans (`forests`) planted trees close to the road (`WALL_DISTANCE + 3 m`) with small footprint exclusion radii (`14–19 m`), allowing trees to stand between the road and the observatories/dishes.
4. **Space Station scale:** `StationArch` and `Biodome` lacked a towering overhead/roadside space-station command superstructure (pressurized cylindrical modules, docking rings, solar truss arrays, and command towers) that reads immediately as a monumental space station from the rider's camera.

### Implementation Plan
1. **Road-Level Anchoring & Deep Foundation Plinths (`src/world/space.rs`):**
   - Anchor `Observatory`, `Biodome`, `Rocket`, and `RadioDish` at `road_y.max(terrain.height(spot))` (where `road_y` is the elevation of the nearest road point) with deep `-18 m` structural plinths/foundations, so they never sink below the road on hillsides.
2. **Monumental Observatory Dome (`observatory_meshes` in `src/world/space.rs`):**
   - Increase scale (`r_base = 18.5 m`, `r_dome = 17.8 m`, total height `~32 m` above road level) with glowing cyan observation gallery bands, bright gold shutter arches, and a massive protruding Cassegrain telescope tube (`20 m` long) with glowing cyan optical rings and a visible cosmic laser guide beam / aperture.
   - Place observatories close to the road (`lateral ~ 24–28 m`) and dead ahead at the end of straights and on the outside of bends.
3. **Monumental Space Station Complex (`biodome_meshes` & `station_arch_meshes` in `src/world/space.rs`):**
   - Upgrade `station_arch_meshes` into full overhead **Space Station Modules** spanning over the road: an overhead pressurized cylindrical habitat/docking module with glowing viewport windows, giant radiating solar-panel truss wings on both sides, communication masts, and glowing cyan/orange station ribs.
   - Upgrade `biodome_meshes` so each biodome stands right beside the road wall (`WALL_DISTANCE + r + 3.0 m`) at road elevation, with glowing geodesic ribs, an elevated **Space Station Command Tower** rising above the dome, and a pressurized airlock corridor reaching right to the roadside wall.
4. **Large Sightline Clearings & Line-of-Sight Unit Test (`src/world/space.rs`, `src/circuits/moon_base.rs`):**
   - Return large footprint radii (`42–48 m`) for observatories, launchpads, biodomes, and radio dishes so trees never block the rider's view of them.
   - Add a unit test verifying that the observatories, launchpads, and space station structures stand close to the road and tower well above the road surface.
