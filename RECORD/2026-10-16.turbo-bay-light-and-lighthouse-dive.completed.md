# Turbo Bay: A Realistic Grade, and the Dive to the Lighthouse

## 1. A Photographic Grade, and the Road Down to the Foot of the Lighthouse (2026-10-16)

### 1.1 Context
- On `main`, off `9f15477`. Turbo Bay was lit as the plain summer day (`SkyStyle::Day`, not graded) with the arcade lawn; the mountain road climbed to a 52 m summit and went round the northern cape some 50 m from the lighthouse, which stood lower than the road, on a small massif, behind trees and a concrete barrier.

### 1.2 User Request
> "vamos a mejorar Turbo Bay. haz un etalonaje más realista. baja de forma pronunciada la carretera a la altura del faro, y que se vea muy bien el faro desde la carretera"

### 1.3 Implementation Details
- **A light of its own** (`SkyStyle::Mediterranean`, `src/world/scenery.rs` and `Palette::of` in `src/world/sky.rs`): a clear Mediterranean summer afternoon as a photograph shows it. A deep but less vivid zenith, a pale, milky horizon (the fog's colour) and a faint halo round the sun; the sun a little lower (0.72 rad against 0.834) and warm, 10 % stronger, with less light from the sky (520 against 700) so the shade stays dark and cool; the haze nearer (`MEDITERRANEAN_FOG`, 300 to 4200 m), so the mountains across the sea fade grey-blue.
- **The grade** (`Grade`, `src/world/sky.rs`): two new fields, `warmth` (Bevy's white-balance temperature, a shift of the white point's CIE *x*) and `highlight_saturation` (as film loses saturation in the highlights). Turbo Bay's: post-saturation 0.78, midtone contrast 1.15, +0.15 EV, warmth 0.006, highlights' saturation 0.75. The other styles keep theirs (`..Grade::NONE` for the new fields).
- **The greens** (`src/circuits/turbo_bay.rs`): `Greens::Countryside` (Ghost Island's many greens, dry and olive on the heights) instead of the arcade lawn, and `grass_shade` 0.45.
- **The dive** (`ELEVATION`): the mountain road now tops out at about 40 m near 1420 m, with the lighthouse straight ahead below, and dives 21 m in about 230 m (up to 12.5 %, under the 13 % that `slopes_and_banking_stay_rideable` allows) to about 19 m at the foot of the lighthouse (1620–1680 m), 4 m under the tower's foot,, then climbs back up the west side into the forest (hump, dip and rise as before, lower). The old dip half way up and the 52 m summit are gone; the lap's highest point is now in the forest, about 45 m up.
- **The cape** (`CAPE`, 1560–1700 m): a railing on the right instead of the concrete barrier, so the tower's foot is seen. The small massif under the lighthouse is replaced by a level `Terrace` (radius 12 m, 23 m high, 30 m skirt), so the cape rises gently from the road to the tower instead of in a rock dome that hid it. The lighthouse (`LIGHTHOUSE`) is now 28 m tall (was 24).
- **No trees round a lighthouse** (`lighthouse_clearing`, `src/world/props.rs`): lighthouses are now spawned before the trees and leave a clearing of 1.5 times their height, as `occupied` footprints; before, trees grew right against the tower and hid its base.
- `docs/tracks.md`: the Turbo Bay light, the mountain road and the lighthouse, and the `sky` table row. The summit picture (`docs/images/turbo-bay-summit.jpg`) still shows the old road.

### 1.4 Verification & Testing
- New tests: Turbo Bay dives more than 18 m from the top of the mountain road to the foot of the lighthouse, at over 11 % at its steepest, the lighthouse standing on a cape a little above the road, and the whole tower in sight over the ground from the rider's eyes all the way down the dive and round its foot (`turbo_bay_dives_to_the_foot_of_its_lighthouse_in_full_view`); no tree grows round a lighthouse; the Mediterranean light is graded (less saturated, more contrast, paler highlights, a touch warm), its sky less vivid and its horizon paler than the plain day's, its haze nearer, its sun warm and lower, with less light from the sky.
- Seen on the M4 mini (with a temporary capture harness, not committed: fixed cameras on the road under the benchmark): the lighthouse straight ahead from the top of the dive, then whole, red and white against the sky, from the foot of the cape behind the railing; before and after shots at the same spots show the neon lawn and saturated sky replaced by olive greens, a deeper, quieter sky and grey-blue haze on the far mountains. The screenshots were taken with the first, steeper version of the dive (16 %), before it was eased to stay under 13 %.
